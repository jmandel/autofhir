FHIR-27866: Correct Invoice service period short text

Issue request:
FHIR-27866 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked to replace the former Invoice.date with Invoice.creation 0..1 dateTime, Invoice.period[x] 0..1 date | Period, and Invoice.lineItem.serviced[x] 0..1 date | Period so invoices can identify the applicable billing or service period.

Initial application:
No prior commit in the available history explicitly mentions FHIR-27866. The change appears to have been applied implicitly by git commit a86020d78d769d63c73b763fa178571cd4703ff1, which added Invoice.creation, Invoice.period[x], and Invoice.lineItem.serviced[x] to source/invoice/structuredefinition-Invoice.xml while leaving a temporary deprecated Invoice.date stub. Later commit 71567db3ee039ac6977be5f96a7e013896569a36 removed that deprecated Invoice.date source element. The current fhir.ini keeps active R4-to-current compatibility mappings from Invoice.date to Invoice.period[x].
