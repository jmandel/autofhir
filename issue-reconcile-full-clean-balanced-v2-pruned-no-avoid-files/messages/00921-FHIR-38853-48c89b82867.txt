Initial application:

Additional context:
Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse are active resources in source/fhir.ini. Keep this commit as-is. It is a bounded source correction for the published FHIR-38853 resolution and avoids changing the underlying QuestionnaireResponse nesting model.
