FHIR-29312: Clarify Device identifier relationships

Issue request:
FHIR-29312 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification OO resolution for R5. It asked to clarify the purpose and relationship of Device and DeviceDefinition identifiers, types, UDI identifiers, part numbers, model numbers, serial numbers, and lot numbers. The approved disposition said DeviceDefinition.identifier is typically valued when udiDeviceIdentifier, partNumber, or modelNumber is not valued and represents a different identifier type, while still allowing those identifiers in DeviceDefinition.identifier with the appropriate identifier.type; it also said Device.identifier is typically valued when serialNumber or lotNumber is not valued and represents a different identifier type, while still allowing those identifiers in the identifier element with the appropriate identifier.type.

Initial application:
Commit 004ec6791ef344fdb1e31df772b032f8ea00586e applied FHIR-29312 to source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml and source/devicedefinition/structuredefinition-DeviceDefinition.xml in August 2021. Later DeviceDefinition canonical-resource work for FHIR-42900 replaced the active DeviceDefinition.identifier definition and left the FHIR-29312 text only in a commented-out legacy block. Later FHIR-51403 code-formatting work preserved the active Device.identifier clarification but did not correct its remaining reference to DeviceDefinition.identifier.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists both device and devicedefinition resources, so the edited StructureDefinition files are current build inputs. FHIR-42900 explains why the newer DeviceDefinition.identifier definition should remain canonical-resource oriented; the FHIR-29312 relationship text therefore belongs in an ElementDefinition comment rather than overwriting the current definition. Related UDI identifier discussions in OO/MnM minutes and FHIR-31750/FHIR-34356 show that using DeviceDefinition.identifier or Device.identifier with typed identifiers for secondary/direct-marking/unit-of-use UDI identifiers remained an active modeling path, while Basic UDI-DI later received a dedicated regulatoryIdentifier element.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit restores the approved DeviceDefinition identifier relationship guidance as an active comment on DeviceDefinition.identifier without undoing the later canonical-resource definition, and corrects the Device.identifier comment so serialNumber and lotNumber identifiers are allowed in Device.identifier rather than DeviceDefinition.identifier.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It is a bounded reconciliation of the published FHIR-29312 clarification with later DeviceDefinition canonical-resource changes; related UDI terminology and regulatoryIdentifier work should remain governed by their own Jira issues.
