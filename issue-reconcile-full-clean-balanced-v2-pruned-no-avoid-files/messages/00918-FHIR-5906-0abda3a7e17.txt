FHIR-5906: Clarify Contract signer signature scope

Issue request:
FHIR-5906 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for DSTU1 Contract.signer. It asked that Contract.signer use the Signature datatype, clarify that the Contract is what is being signed, allow Contract.legallyBinding[x] to point to signed contract documentation, and state that any digital signer.signature covers the content or references being signed.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly tagged FHIR-5906 was found on the combined branch or in the local source history. Related historical commits did apply most of the change family: cd16d2a changed Contract.signer.signature to the Signature datatype as part of FHIR-9533, and 268bdbb revised Contract.signer definition/requirements as part of FHIR-9567. The current source already used Signature with 1..* cardinality and described Contract.legallyBinding[x] as the signed source of truth, but Contract.signer.signature still defined the element as DSIG base64 contents and the signer requirements only named the legallyBinding reference, leaving a residual mismatch with FHIR-5906.

Additional context:
Contract is still an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini for this reconciliation branch. The Signature datatype and digital-signature guidance already define base64 signature content and validation of signed resources, so this reconciliation did not duplicate that full guidance. FHIR-20344 is a later unresolved/Triaged request to collapse Contract.signer into Signature alone; it is not a final decision and was not implemented here. Direct Zulip and Confluence searches found no community record that changed the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated source/contract/structuredefinition-Contract.xml so Contract.signer explicitly applies to the Contract itself or the Contract.legallyBinding[x] source-of-truth resource, and so Contract.signer.signature is defined as a Signature over the Contract or its legally binding representation. Added a focused comment that digital signatures should cover the Contract-related content and references being attested to.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. It completes the residual FHIR-5906 clarification without adopting the unresolved broader FHIR-20344 remodel or duplicating the general Signature datatype/signatures page guidance.
