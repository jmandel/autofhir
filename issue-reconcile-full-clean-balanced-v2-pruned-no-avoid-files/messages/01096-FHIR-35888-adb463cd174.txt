FHIR-35888: Include Group root in compartment matrix

Issue request:
FHIR-35888 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked whether a compartment's identity resource belongs to its own compartment, and the approved resolution says every compartment implicitly includes its root resource and that compartment content rendering should include the resource itself.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by git commit c2ddea7415c6952f2988e332315c5e1becdf9bd0, which added the Base Resource narrative to the CompartmentDefinition page, adjusted the compartment template, and changed the Patient resource row to include {def}. Existing Encounter, RelatedPerson, Practitioner, and Device rows already had {def} markers.

Additional context:
FHIR-48863 later added a Group compartment in source/compartments.xml and was already represented on the combined branch by commit 9608dfed9a86ec8e5c5b6ecf3e076a3efb026284. That later application left the Group resource row without a {def} marker in the Group compartment column, making the current source inconsistent with FHIR-35888's general root-resource rule. Group and CompartmentDefinition are active current resources in source/fhir.ini, and source/compartments.xml is the authoritative compartment matrix used for the generated compartment content.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit adds {def} to the Group row's Group compartment column in source/compartments.xml. The change is intentionally limited to the missing root-resource marker and does not broaden Group membership through Group.member, preserving FHIR-48863's distinction that Group members and resources linked only to members are not included in the Group compartment.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It completes FHIR-35888 for the later-added Group compartment while leaving separate Group compartment boundary questions to the already-published FHIR-48863 reconciliation or future trackers.
