FHIR-36388: Correct MedicationDispense V2 separators

Issue request:
FHIR-36388 was a Published/Persuasive technical correction for R5 asking that the MedicationDispense HL7 V2 mapping table use hyphens between segment and field numbers and dots for components, including TQ1-6/TQ1-6.1 and ORC-7/ORC-7.3 in daysSupply, and remove erroneous label hyphens.

Initial application:
Git commit b9257918056a31ebdc080627432cc94db78637e0 cited FHIR-36388 and updated the active MedicationDispense StructureDefinition to remove the erroneous label hyphens from RXD-2, PID-3, RXD-33, RXD-4/RXD-5, RXD-3, RXO-9, and RXE-9 mappings. That partial application left the daysSupply TQ1.6, TQ1.6.1, ORC.7, and ORC.7.3 separator forms unchanged.

Additional context:
MedicationDispense is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, and source/medicationdispense/structuredefinition-MedicationDispense.xml declares the HL7 V2 mapping identity that generates the mapping table. The linked Zulip discussion confirmed the requested convention: hyphen for segment fields and dot for components. Pharmacy meeting minutes for 2022-03-28 record FHIR-36388 as a technical correction with agreement to change.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit corrects the remaining MedicationDispense.daysSupply HL7 V2 mapping source from TQ1.6/ORC.7 forms to TQ1-6/ORC-7 forms while preserving component dots such as TQ1-6.1 and ORC-7.3. No other MedicationDispense V2 mapping separator instances from the issue request remained to change.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. It completes the missing part of the published technical correction in the active authoritative source without broadening into unrelated V2 mapping cleanup.
