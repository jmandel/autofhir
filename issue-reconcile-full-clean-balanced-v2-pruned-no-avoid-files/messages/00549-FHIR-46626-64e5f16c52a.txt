FHIR-46626: Harmonize ParameterDefinition cardinality rules

Issue request:
FHIR-46626 is Applied/Persuasive for R6 and asked FHIR-I to make ParameterDefinition.min/max carry the same cardinality safeguards as ElementDefinition.min/max: min must not be negative, min must not exceed max, and max must be a non-negative integer or "*".

Initial application:
A Jira-tagged source commit, 8e42147627f507755f642e3284bf832f1082b194, was already in the current branch history and on the combined branch, but it applied equivalent min/max harmonization to OperationDefinition.parameter and StructureMap.group.rule.source rather than to source/datatypes/parameterdefinition.xml. Current ParameterDefinition still had min typed as integer and no min/max invariants, so the seed issue remained semantically incomplete.

Additional context:
The reporter's Zulip thread characterized the missing ParameterDefinition cardinality rules as an oversight, and FHIR-I minutes record the 2024-08-19 motion approving FHIR-46626 8-0-0. source/fhir.ini keeps parameterdefinition in the active metadatatypes build area and marks ParameterDefinition normative, while publish.ini copies the FHIR ModelInfo XML that exposes the datatype to CQL users.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated source/datatypes/parameterdefinition.xml so ParameterDefinition.min is unsignedInt, both min and max reference the new min/max invariant, ParameterDefinition.max states "a number or *", and the Invariants sheet defines the Min <= Max and Max-number-or-* rules. Updated source/library/library-fhir-model-definition-content.xml so the build-copied ModelInfo surface also reports ParameterDefinition.min as FHIR.unsignedInt.

Recommendation:
Keep this source-changing reconciliation. Future generated StructureDefinition artifacts should carry prd-1/prd-2 from the spreadsheet source; no additional Jira in the same evidence path needed a separate decision.
