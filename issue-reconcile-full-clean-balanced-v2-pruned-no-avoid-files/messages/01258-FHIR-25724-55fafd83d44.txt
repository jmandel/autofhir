FHIR-25724 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core technical correction for R5 asking that the invalid narrative snippets in the Narrative page linking section be corrected because they omitted the required Narrative.status element.

Initial application:

The XML Bundle/Composition example still omitted the required status element inside the Composition.section.text Narrative, including after the later FHIR-37792 edit that moved the example text under section.text.
