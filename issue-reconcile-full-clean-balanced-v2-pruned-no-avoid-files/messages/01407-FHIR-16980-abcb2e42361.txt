FHIR-16980: Clarify HumanName initials

Issue request:
FHIR-16980 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core datatypes change request for STU3. It asked to clarify the HumanName.given wording because "full name" was ambiguous and the text did not say whether multiple initials could be recorded as separate given-name parts. The approved resolution was to say "full name part" and clarify that initials may be separated but often are not due to practical limitations.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied by git-svn revision 14294, commit f1953dbb3236d9467f106f74e57d97607fd2a23c ("GF#16980 - note about initials"), which changed source/datatypes/humanname.xml. The current source retained that semantic clarification in HumanName.given, but with the typo "paractical"; the active HumanName narrative in source/datatypes.html still used the older ambiguous "full name" wording and omitted the multiple-initials clarification.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists HumanName under [types], and source/datatypes.html hosts the HumanName section with the [%dt HumanName 10%] include, so both edited files are current build inputs. Later Jira FHIR-21226 asked for broader first/middle/initials and v2 round-tripping guidance, but was Resolved - No Change for FHIR core because that guidance belongs in the v2 mapping spec. Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-16980-specific discussion or conflicting work-group record; later Zulip name-modeling questions were background only.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit fixes the typo in source/datatypes/humanname.xml and aligns the source/datatypes.html HumanName narrative with the approved clarification: initials replace full name parts, and multiple initials may be separated into multiple given names but often are not due to practical limitations.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. Do not expand this issue into broader HumanName/v2 mapping guidance; FHIR-21226 records that such guidance belongs outside FHIR core.
