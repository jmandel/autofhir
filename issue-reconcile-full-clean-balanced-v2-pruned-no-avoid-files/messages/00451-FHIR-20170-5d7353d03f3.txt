Issue request:
FHIR-20170 is a Published FHIR-core Claim change request with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. The issue identified "patient health Record (PHR)" in the Claim Boundaries and Relationships text and the final resolution required changing all uses of patient health record to personal health record. The suggested external PHR-S FM link was not part of the final resolution, so this reconciliation only applies the terminology change.
