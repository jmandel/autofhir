FHIR-43499: Align Group determiner narrative

Issue request:
FHIR-43499 was Applied with Persuasive with Modification resolution for R6. The issue started with an unclear Group.characteristic.determinedBy[x] definition; the final decision split that choice into Group.characteristic.formula and Group.characteristic.determiner, with determiner limited to Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Organization, Device, and DeviceDefinition references and a clarifying comment about matching the referenced entity's characteristics.

Initial application:
The current StructureDefinition already contains Group.characteristic.formula and Group.characteristic.determiner with the resolved definitions and target profiles. Git history shows commit bebedf807a5f removed DeviceMetric from the determiner target profiles after OO feedback, and commit 059cec8cb0e added the FHIR-43499 determiner comment. A later FHIR-45508 notes move copied older narrative wording into source/group/group-notes.xml, leaving the active Group narrative saying determiner could reference DeviceMetric and that formula/determiner modified Group.characteristic.code.

Additional context:
The Group resource and the affected formula/determiner elements are active in source/fhir.ini. The Zulip FHIR-I thread for FHIR-43499 records the draft split into formula and determiner and the need for Group notes to explain the elements. FHIR-I minutes on 2025-06-09 sent the DeviceMetric actor question to OO; the 2025-06-16 minutes record that OO agreed to remove DeviceMetric and that FHIR-I approved the final Persuasive with Mod resolution.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates the active Group notes narrative to match the applied StructureDefinition: formula is an expression used to generate the characteristic value, determiner is who or what evaluates the characteristic value, and the narrative lists only Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Organization, Device, and DeviceDefinition reference targets. It removes the stale DeviceMetric advice rather than changing generated diagrams or unrelated Group text.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It reconciles the only discovered current-source inconsistency for FHIR-43499 while preserving the upstream StructureDefinition application and the separate already-published FHIR-45508 reconciliation.
