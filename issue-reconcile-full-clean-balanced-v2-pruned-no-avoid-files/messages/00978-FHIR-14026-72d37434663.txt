FHIR-14026: Add missing photo guidance to person resources

Issue request:
FHIR-14026 was Published/Persuasive for STU3 by Patient Administration. It requested clearer Patient.photo guidance that photos are identification photos rather than clinical photos, should be thumbnail-sized, and should keep byte count low; the Jira resolution also explicitly named Person.photo and Practitioner.photo as parallel elements to clarify.

Initial application:
The Jira-tagged historical commit 6aa19bcefb added the requested guidance to the old Patient spreadsheet, and the 2021 source refactor preserved that guidance on source/patient/structuredefinition-Patient.xml. The same guidance was not present on the current active Person.photo or Practitioner.photo StructureDefinition source, leaving the resolution only partially applied for the parallel person resources.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini has active entries for Patient, Person, and Practitioner, and these StructureDefinition XML files are the current resource sources. The older FHIR-12454 no-change decision confirms the intended photo use is a lightweight thumbnail for quick identification rather than a separate Media clinical image, which is consistent with FHIR-14026. Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later conflicting decision or community discussion changing the FHIR-14026 guidance.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Added the same photo comment already present on Patient.photo to Person.photo and Practitioner.photo so all resources named by the published Jira resolution carry the identification-photo, thumbnail-size, and low-byte-count guidance in active source.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. It completes the published non-substantive clarification without changing cardinality, type, or conformance behavior; no separate decision is needed for FHIR-12454 because that issue was already resolved as no-change and only supports the rationale.
