FHIR-47247: Clarify NutritionIntake not-consumed items

Issue request:
FHIR-47247 was an Applied R6 OO change request asking whether NutritionIntake.nutritionItem.consumedItem.notConsumed should be marked isModifier=true. The final Not Persuasive with Modification disposition said the NutritionIntake reorganization removed the need for modifier treatment and requested a comment on NutritionIntake.nutritionItem clarifying use of the not-consumed element.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source history by commit 05e646d74bb93a1c435d884747dc64da94898435, which explicitly names FHIR-47247 and changed NutritionIntake from the old boolean notConsumed/notConsumedReason shape to the current NutritionIntake.nutritionItem.notConsumedItem backbone. That application removed the modifier concern but did not add the requested usage comment on NutritionIntake.nutritionItem.

Additional context:
FHIR-45063 had already required separating consumed and not-consumed NutritionIntake items, and FHIR-48727 later confirmed the separate notConsumedItem backbone with reason, schedule, and amount. The 2024-10-28 OO HCP minutes record the FHIR-47247 decision that moving notConsumed out of consumedItem meant it was no longer an isModifier candidate. NutritionIntake is active in the current build and assigned to OO in source/fhir.ini.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit adds the missing ElementDefinition.comment to NutritionIntake.nutritionItem in the authoritative StructureDefinition source. The comment explains that consumedItem records nutrition that was consumed or administered and notConsumedItem records nutrition that was intended but refused, held, or otherwise not consumed. No diagram or generated-only artifact was edited.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It completes the applied Jira clarification without changing cardinality, modifier status, or the later notConsumedItem modeling decisions.
