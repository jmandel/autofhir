FHIR-42989: Complete safety implicitRules checklist

Issue request:
FHIR-42989 was an Applied FHIR-core FHIR-I change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking the Clinical Safety checklist to clarify modifier-element handling, clarify modifierExtension handling, and add a separate Resource.implicitRules safety check for every processed resource.

Initial application:
The issue was partially applied in source history. Commit 413371928177b066069986c67e933d242b8234d0 added the approved safety.html and safety-entries CodeSystem language, but the current active CodeSystem no longer contains those FHIR-42989 changes. Commit e963b596c7ad3585b2285ac64ae7f8ee16a644ac later added an implicitRules item to source/safety.html while leaving the modifier and modifierExtension checklist text in its pre-resolution form and without updating the active CodeSystem.

Additional context:
source/hierarchy.xml lists safety.html as a current root page, and source/fhir.ini actively includes source/codesystem/codesystem-safety-entries.xml as safety-entries. The safety page explicitly says the checklist is available as that CodeSystem for formal references, so keeping the CodeSystem in sync is part of the current build-scope behavior. FHIR-I minutes from 2023-10-23 explain the origin in the rendering-styleSensitive/implicitRules discussion, and 2024-04-08 minutes record the final decision to make modifier, modifierExtension, and implicitRules behavior explicit. Zulip searches found no conflicting community rationale.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit completes the current build-scope application by updating source/safety.html to the approved modifier, modifierExtension, and Resource.implicitRules checklist language, changing the implicitRules checklist title/code to implicit-rules, and adding the matching implicit-rules concept plus updated definitions to the active safety-entries CodeSystem.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It restores the intended FHIR-42989 semantics on both authoritative checklist surfaces and avoids broad cleanup of unrelated pre-existing safety checklist drift.
