It started as a request to replace relatedArtifact on Library, ActivityDefinition, and PlanDefinition, but the final CDS decision instead kept RelatedArtifact and required resource-specific constraints and guidance for Library, ActivityDefinition, PlanDefinition, and RequestOrchestration.

Initial application:

Commit 76f561c2361f1ced872d4a1a5a0df756e2fa1471 then removed the successor/predecessor resolve() constraints from the first three resources and converted them to narrative conformance guidance after PR review discussion. PR HL7/fhir#3916 merged those changes as 3cd78c385040041f240053d8481b7ff4797c635b. The remaining RequestOrchestration constraint was present, but its constraint source canonical was copied from PlanDefinition.

Additional context:
FHIR-50010 is the broader Applied/Persuasive RelatedArtifact simplification and usage-guidance issue that directed work groups to decide which relationships are core for each resource and to constrain inappropriate relationship types. The implementer discussion identified FHIR-49782 as the CDS follow-up tracker, alongside separate CQI and terminology trackers. The PR review discussion explains why the resolve() target-type checks should remain narrative guidance rather than executable constraints, so this reconciliation does not reintroduce them.
