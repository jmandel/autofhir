FHIR-47605: Add commercial brand family name type

Issue request:
FHIR-47605 is a Published FHIR-extensions change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, about retiring the Device Commercial Brand extension after R5 and using Device.name.type or DeviceDefinition.deviceName.type for the R6 representation.

Initial application:
The extension-pack side was applied in commit 01a5cbd by adding version-specific-use endFhirVersion=5.0 context markers and text directing R6 users to a Commercial Brand Family name-type value. No matching FHIR-core commit or current CodeSystem concept existed in source/device/codesystem-device-nametype.xml.

FHIR-27765 originally created the R5 device-commercialBrand extension for DeviceDefinition.
