AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Corrected source/observation/lastn-vitalcodes-response.txt so the LOINC 85354-9 display now reads "Blood pressure panel with all children optional" in the Observation $lastn response example.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It completes the same non-substantive typo correction in a current, included Observation example and does not require further work-group review.
