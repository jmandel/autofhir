FHIR-32408: Clarify DeviceMetric-to-Device relationship

Issue request:
FHIR-32408 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It observed that DeviceMetric was listed as a Device-related resource even though Device has no reference to DeviceMetric, and the final OO resolution asked to clarify that the relationship is established through DeviceMetric rather than Device and to add the reverse DeviceMetric boundary wording.

Initial application:
The accepted Device Scope-Boundary-Relationships work was applied in commit 3c23148747 (apply #20574), which included FHIR-32408 with FHIR-32382, FHIR-32277, FHIR-32276, and FHIR-32681. That commit added Device/DeviceMetric boundary text, but the Device entry still said a Device may include multiple metrics without saying that the reference is held on DeviceMetric.

Additional context:
Device and DeviceMetric are active resources in source/fhir.ini, and the current DeviceMetric structure definition requires DeviceMetric.device 1..1 to reference Device. The 2022-06-13 OO HCP minutes record the 10-0-0 motion to accept the Device Scope-Boundary-Relationships document and disposition FHIR-32408 through that document. A Zulip search for FHIR-32408 and DeviceMetric/Device terms found no conflicting discussion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates the active Device and DeviceMetric narrative sources so Device states that multiple metrics are associated through each DeviceMetric.device reference rather than a Device reference, and DeviceMetric states the same relationship from the reverse boundary list item.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It directly resolves the remaining ambiguity identified by FHIR-32408 without changing the Device or DeviceMetric data model and without adding unrelated boundary guidance.
