AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Moved the ElementDefinition.comment parenthetical note from the definition cell into the comment cell in source/datatypes/elementdefinition.xml and put the note-ending periods inside both ElementDefinition.definition and ElementDefinition.comment parenthetical notes. No other ElementDefinition wording or related profiling guidance was changed.
