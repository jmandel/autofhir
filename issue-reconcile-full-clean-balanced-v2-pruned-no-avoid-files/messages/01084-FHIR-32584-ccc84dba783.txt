FHIR-32584: Clarify DocumentReference identifiers

Issue request:
FHIR-32584 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked to remove DocumentReference.content.identifier, explain on DocumentReference.identifier that different business identifier kinds such as XDS DocumentEntry.uniqueId can be distinguished by Identifier.type, and add DocumentReference usage guidance that a system may expose multiple DocumentReference resources pointing to distinct artifacts with common business identifiers, so DocumentReference.identifier is not necessarily unique.

Initial application:
The Jira-linked merge commit e1693d10118242ec2bc17e3e275da8a3a1e462d6 included ca42e22e8898847779efb3f173a39b7bcc9ef204, which removed DocumentReference.content.identifier from source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml and updated DocumentReference.identifier mappings and XDS examples. That application did not leave current usage-note text about Identifier.type or non-unique business identifiers, and a later non-Jira-specific DocumentReference cleanup, 97cf6bb0ede5497d006a53d90f53c1cc2b41248f, replaced the identifier definition with wording that still lacked the FHIR-32584 guidance.

Additional context:
DocumentReference is an active build resource in source/fhir.ini, so the source/documentreference XML files feed the current core build. OO Confluence minutes record the November 2021 discussion to remove the content-level identifier and coordinate with FHIR-I, and the May 2022 vote accepting FHIR-32584 with modification. FHIR-25773 is the earlier same-family issue that moved masterIdentifier into content.identifier; FHIR-32584 supersedes that modeling. FHIR-32791 and FHIR-37444 were checked as nearby DocumentReference content/attachment issues and do not change the identifier guidance.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit keeps the already-applied removal of DocumentReference.content.identifier and adds the missing current-source guidance. source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml now documents Identifier.type and the non-uniqueness caveat on DocumentReference.identifier, and source/documentreference/documentreference-notes.xml now includes the corresponding resource-level implementation note.

Recommendation:
Keep this source change as the bounded reconciliation for FHIR-32584. No Extension Pack or separate related-issue commit is needed; later related issues found in the search path concern attachment URL examples or relation semantics rather than reversing the business-identifier rule.
