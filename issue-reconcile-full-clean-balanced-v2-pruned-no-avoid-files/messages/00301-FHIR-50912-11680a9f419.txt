Issue request:
FHIR-50912 is Applied/Persuasive with Modification. It asked to rename Transport.history, which was easily confused with Transport.relevantHistory, to Transport.priorTransport; change the short display from "Parent (or preceding) transport" to "Prior Transport"; and change the definition to the immediately preceding Transport event.

Initial application:
The FHIR-50912 implementation landed through the Transport PR history. Merge 0637d7939c fixed the definition typo to "immediately" but left the short display mismatching the final Jira resolution.

Additional context:
Transport is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/transport/structuredefinition-Transport.xml is in the current build scope. OO minutes confirm that the accepted concept is a predecessor/prior transport rather than a generic history element; the final Jira resolution and post-application Jira comment identify "Prior Transport" as the correct short display. Follow-up searches found no Zulip discussion or separate Jira issue that changes this conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit changes only Transport.priorTransport.short from "The most recent transport" to "Prior Transport". The id/path rename and corrected definition spelling are already present, no Transport.history element remains in source, and other lowercase uses of "history" refer to relevantHistory, provenance, or generic movement history rather than the renamed predecessor reference.

Recommendation:
