FHIR-39141 + FHIR-47297: Clarify Transport business status boundary

Attribution note: surfaced while reviewing FHIR-36248, but direct Jira backing for this change is FHIR-39141 + FHIR-47297.

Issue request:
FHIR-36248 was a Published/Persuasive-with-Modification OO R5 change request asking the Transport boundaries to distinguish physical patient movement from independent business-state changes such as level of care or patient class. The approved resolution added boundary prose and a Transport.reason element; a later Jira comment recorded that, after PA discussion, a direct EncounterHistory reference was no longer needed.

Initial application:
The Jira-tagged implementation commit 3afd6e72b6 added the requested Transport boundary paragraph and introduced Transport.reason as a CodeableConcept for the transported entity's business status. Follow-up commit ca6f36eb21 adjusted sequencing. Later FHIR-39141 commits removed the duplicate business-status reason element and replaced Transport.reasonCode/reasonReference with the current Transport.reason CodeableReference for why transport is needed, leaving the original boundary paragraph still pointing encounter business status at Transport.reason.

Additional context:
FHIR-39141 and the already-published AutoFHIR FHIR-39141/FHIR-40310 commits establish the current Transport.reason shape and prose: it is repeatable CodeableReference content for why the transport is needed, not the authoritative encounter business-status field. Later PA issue FHIR-47297 added Encounter.businessStatus for current encounter business statuses and says historical business statuses belong in EncounterHistory. Transport is active in source/fhir.ini, so the Transport narrative is current build scope.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates source/transport/transport-introduction.xml to preserve the FHIR-36248 boundary distinction while aligning it with current source: Transport records the physical movement, encounter business status can change with or without movement, Encounter.businessStatus represents the current encounter business status, and Transport.reason records why the transport is needed.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It resolves the stale cross-resource guidance without reintroducing the superseded business-status Transport.reason element. If OO and PA later want Transport to link directly to historical EncounterHistory guidance, that should be handled as a separate work-group decision.
