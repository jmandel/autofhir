Issue request:
FHIR-7928 was a Published FHIR-core HTTP change request asking to remove conditional create support. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution kept conditional create and directed the specification to explain that transactions and conditional operations are complex and not expected on every server, with the same resolution intended for FHIR-7926 and FHIR-7927.
