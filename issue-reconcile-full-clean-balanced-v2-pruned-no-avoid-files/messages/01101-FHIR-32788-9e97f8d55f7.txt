FHIR-32788: Remove blood pressure vital signs supplement guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-32788 was an Applied/Persuasive with Modification OO change request for the Observation vital signs table. The issue reported that the blood pressure row's additional LOINC codes were ambiguous as related Observations versus components; the final disposition directed removal of the blood pressure supplement sentence rather than adding more guidance.

Initial application:
The issue comment says it was addressed by the FHIR-32787 commit f5ab9c158d. That commit updated the active Observation vital signs introduction table and changed the blood pressure text from additional observations to additional component observations, but it did not apply the later FHIR-32788 disposition to remove the supplement sentence.

Additional context:
Observation is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/observation/observation-vitalsigns-profile-introduction.xml feeds the current build. The linked FHIR-32787 body weight issue later received a separate follow-up commit, while the FHIR-32788 blood pressure row still retained the sentence that OO minutes and the Jira resolution say to remove. Community searches found no directly relevant Zulip discussion that supersedes the Jira/OO decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Removed the blood pressure-row sentence listing 8478-0, 8357-6, 41904-4, 8358-4, and 41901-0 as additional component observations, leaving the existing statement that the blood pressure panel has no Observation.value[x] and contains systolic and/or diastolic components.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It implements the final OO/Jira disposition in the active Observation vital signs source without adding substitute guidance for codes whose relationship to Observation.method, Observation.bodySite, or other modeling choices was intentionally left out.
