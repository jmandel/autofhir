FHIR-38957: Clarify CodeableReference constraint mapping

Issue request:
FHIR-38957 was a Published FHIR-core R5 change request resolved Persuasive with Modification. It asked to clarify confusing CodeableReference prose that said constraints apply to CodeableReference.reference or CodeableReference.concept and should not be specified on the child elements; the approved replacement says targetProfile and binding constraints declared on CodeableReference apply to CodeableReference.reference and CodeableReference.concept respectively and SHALL NOT be specified directly on the child elements.

Initial application:
The paragraph was originally introduced by d621ab0bda as part of an MnM changes batch. FHIR-39132 and FHIR-39176 then edited the same paragraph, and 0198f026142817d1711ee92eac43c72f246b77b9 explicitly applied FHIR-38957. That implementation captured the SHALL NOT rule but left the sentence with "or" and "respectively as appropriate", which still obscured the intended one-to-one mapping.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively maps codeablereference to the references page, so source/references.html is current build input. The StructureDefinition constraints sdf-24 and sdf-25 independently require targetProfile and binding constraints to be listed on the CodeableReference rather than on CodeableReference.reference or CodeableReference.concept. FHIR-39176 explains why the narrative must remain SHALL NOT, and the FHIR-I Methodology/Data Types minutes confirm the FHIR-38957 Persuasive with Modification vote. No conflicting Zulip or Confluence evidence was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates source/references.html to match the approved clarification more directly: constraints are declared on CodeableReference, targetProfile and binding map to reference and concept respectively, and those constraints remain prohibited directly on the child elements.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. It is a narrow clarification in an active datatype narrative page and preserves the related SHALL NOT decision from FHIR-39176 while removing the residual ambiguity identified by FHIR-38957.
