FHIR-25800 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and requested that the then-named MedicationUsage.medication[x] element use CodeableReference, with the resolution "Will update." The current resource name is MedicationStatement after a later published rename.

Initial application:

That rename also left the canonical MedicationStatement example using the pre-CodeableReference medicationReference choice element.

No directly relevant Zulip or Confluence record was found for FHIR-25800.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. Treat broader old medicationReference examples on other Pharmacy resources, if desired, under FHIR-25929 or resource-specific reconciliation rather than widening this seed commit.
