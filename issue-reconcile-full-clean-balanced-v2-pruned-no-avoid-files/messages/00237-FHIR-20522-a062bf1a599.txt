FHIR-20522: Add MessageHeader child invariants

Issue request:
FHIR-20522 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core MessageHeader change request for R5. It asked to make MessageHeader.source.endpoint and MessageHeader.destination.endpoint optional because real-world V2-derived messaging does not always know endpoint URLs; the accepted InM motion also required constraints that each present MessageHeader.source or MessageHeader.destination backbone have at least one of its children.

Initial application:
The issue was partially applied by dd765a96be, which changed MessageHeader.destination.endpoint and MessageHeader.source.endpoint from 1..1 to 0..1. The later cd5098720d commit refactored both elements to endpoint[x] with url and Reference(Endpoint) choices and updated examples, but the accepted source/destination child invariants were still absent from the current StructureDefinition.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists messageheader=MessageHeader and assigns messageheader to InM, so source/messageheader/structuredefinition-MessageHeader.xml is in current build scope. Duplicate Jira issues FHIR-33690, FHIR-32369, FHIR-26898, and FHIR-24921 request the same optional endpoint behavior. Zulip discussion and InM minutes confirm the interoperability need and do not supersede the invariant requirement.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Added msh-1 on MessageHeader.destination requiring endpoint, name, or receiver when a destination backbone is present, and msh-2 on MessageHeader.source requiring endpoint, name, software, version, contact, or sender for the mandatory source backbone. Added matching condition references to the child elements involved in each invariant.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It completes the published InM decision without changing endpoint cardinalities or the later endpoint[x] type choice; no separate commits are needed for the duplicate Jira issues.
