FHIR-7404: Align document narrative notes with Composition sections

Issue request:
FHIR-7404 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked whether FHIR document narrative had the same flexibility as CDA and resolved to move narrative into the Composition resource and inline the relevant parts of the List resource into Composition sections.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied through git-svn commits 686889f8a3b83c74a4e58ddddfac7616dd0da4ca and 749c4dd88234688f57191d3d0b4d62bdc525b87d. Those commits refactored Composition sections from section.content/List toward section.text and section.entry, updated examples, and changed the Documents guide presentation list from List resources to section.text narratives.

Additional context:
Older DSTU1 issues such as FHIR-5152 and FHIR-5154 had added List.text-as-attested-content language for the earlier section.content/List model. That model was superseded by FHIR-7404 and later Documents guide text, including the Published FHIR-15723 correction that says additional resources not part of the document presentation are not attested content. Current source still contained stale references to Composition.section.content and List.text as attested document content.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated Composition notes to point attestation review at the subject, Composition, and Composition.section.text narratives; updated Composition.section.title's comment to refer to section narrative and section.entry resources instead of obsolete section.content; and updated List narrative notes to state that document-attested narrative belongs in Composition narrative or Composition.section.text, not only in List.text.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It finishes a bounded cleanup of active source text that still reflected the old List/section.content document model while preserving the current rule that List remains a useful supporting resource outside the attested presentation unless its narrative is incorporated into Composition text.
