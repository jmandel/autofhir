FHIR-54964 later added CG and II as co-responsible work groups; FHIR-54965 remains Triaged for broader roadmap/use-case follow-up and is not a final source-edit decision here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit adds ServiceRequest back to the Diagnostics module Index and removes the duplicate ImagingSelection and ImagingStudy entries from the third Index column. The rest of the page is left as-is because the approved R6 text, FHIR-51647 follow-up, external CG incubator links, Table 1, and acknowledgement content already reflect the later source history.

Recommendation:
Keep this focused source fix. Any broader roadmap or missing-use-case work should wait for a final decision on FHIR-54965 or a successor tracker rather than being folded into this reconciliation.
