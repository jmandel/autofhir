FHIR-53569: Add CapabilityStatement search-name guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-53569 was Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6. It asked to replace the R5 recommendation that custom SearchParameter codes begin with "-" because `_sort` uses a leading hyphen for descending order, and to add Search Tests guidance requiring unfamiliar clients to query CapabilityStatement naming conventions.

Initial application:
Commit 8b4082caae2d890a9164d9119c7db8722a31f172 updated `source/search.html` to replace the hyphen-prefix recommendation with organization-scoped alphanumeric-prefix guidance, but it did not add the approved Search Tests paragraph after the "Search inputs are dereferenced" paragraph.

Additional context:
FHIR-53565 is a duplicate of the same request. The Dec. 8, 2025 FHIR-I minutes and the Dec. 3, 2025 Zulip discussion confirm the accepted scope: avoid recommending punctuation prefixes, include IG authors in the guidance, and point clients to CapabilityStatement-based disambiguation. `source/search.html` is the authoritative current search narrative page, with `source/fhir.ini` also referencing search rule generation against that page.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Added the missing conformance paragraph to `source/search.html` immediately after the Search Tests paragraph that explains CapabilityStatement-based search input dereferencing. The prior alphanumeric-prefix guidance was already present and was left unchanged.

Recommendation:
