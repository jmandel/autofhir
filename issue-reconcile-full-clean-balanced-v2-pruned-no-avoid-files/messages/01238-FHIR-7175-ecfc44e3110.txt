FHIR-7175: Clarify NutritionOrder food preference modifier

Issue request:
FHIR-7175 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for the DSTU1 NutritionRequest/NutritionOrder artifact. The issue asked whether NutritionOrder.foodPreferenceModifier should be combined with allergy or intolerance handling because dislikes and intolerances can be confused, and the final decision kept the clinical distinction because other care-team providers need a way to record food preferences separately.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-7175 was found. The companion FHIR-7176 issue was applied by commit 2308bb93df0b674c1cb64370db6a2540ba2d47b6, which added AllergyIntolerance non-duplication guidance to NutritionOrder.excludeFoodModifier. Later source conversions and resource renames preserved separate AllergyIntolerance, foodPreferenceModifier, and excludeFoodModifier elements, but the current foodPreferenceModifier definition still said preferences could be derived from patient allergies or intolerances.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists nutritionorder=NutritionOrder and assigns it to OO, so source/nutritionorder/structuredefinition-NutritionOrder.xml is in current build scope. FHIR-7176 is already represented on the combined branch by an audit commit, and later FHIR-46700 work clarified that excludeFoodModifier complements but does not duplicate AllergyIntolerance. The current foodPreferenceModifier comment and binding already describe patient preferences, so only the definition needed alignment. Zulip and Confluence searches found no conflicting community discussion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the active NutritionOrder StructureDefinition so NutritionOrder.foodPreferenceModifier reflects patient preferences such as Halal, Vegan, or Kosher instead of saying it can be derived from allergies or intolerances. No AllergyIntolerance or excludeFoodModifier behavior was changed.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It is a narrow clarification that completes the published distinction without duplicating the companion FHIR-7176 exclusion guidance; generated artifacts can be refreshed by the normal build.
