FHIR-8728 was Published as Persuasive for FHIR-core profiles after a DSTU1 report that resource StructureDefinition.differential output omitted ElementDefinition.type for BackboneElement entries, with Patient.contact cited as the example that should have carried a type.

Initial application:

The original Patient.contact example is currently typed as BackboneElement in the active Patient StructureDefinition source.

Keep this source fix.
