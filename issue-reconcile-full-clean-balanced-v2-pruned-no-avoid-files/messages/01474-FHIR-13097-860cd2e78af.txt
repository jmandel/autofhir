FHIR-13097 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core OperationDefinition issue. It asked to update the Boundaries and Relationships text because it said OperationDefinition resources were referenced from two places while the page also identified ServiceDefinition as another referencing resource.

Initial application:

Additional context:
FHIR-13410 later removed ServiceDefinition from the active build in commit 44c2ccc5d64db10ecd5df0b3220bcd20be31e318, which also reverted this OperationDefinition paragraph to two places. In the current active build, ServiceDefinition remains only as dropped/out-of-build source, while OperationDefinition is referenced by active CapabilityStatement, OperationDefinition.base, CarePlan, PlanDefinition, and RequestOrchestration elements.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates the active OperationDefinition introduction to say OperationDefinition resources are referred to from several places, keeps the CapabilityStatement and OperationDefinition.base cases, and adds the current active planning/orchestration references. It intentionally does not restore the obsolete ServiceDefinition link.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It preserves the FHIR-13097 intent without reviving the removed ServiceDefinition resource and aligns the narrative with current active conformance sources.
