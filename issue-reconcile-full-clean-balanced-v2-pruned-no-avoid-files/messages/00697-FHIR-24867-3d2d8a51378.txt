FHIR-24867: Add decision support safety checklist codes

Issue request:
FHIR-24867 was a Published FHIR-core Clinical Decision Support change request, resolved Persuasive, asking for safety-checklist language warning that data from an EHR should not be assumed correct before risk models or other clinical decision support use it. The approved resolution was to add Clinical Safety language for decision support to safety.html and link to it from decision support topics and related resources.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by commit d0cd856afd919b36f213916cc1e9af5c7b0679e8, also linked from Jira via HL7/fhir#2834. That commit added a Decision Support Related Safety Checks section to source/safety.html and added a source/clinicalreasoning-cds-on-fhir.html link to the Implementer's Safety Checklist. Current source still has those page-level changes.

Additional context:
source/hierarchy.xml lists safety.html and clinicalreasoning-cds-on-fhir.html as current pages, and source/fhir.ini actively includes source/codesystem/codesystem-safety-entries.xml as safety-entries. The safety page says the checklist is available as a CodeSystem for formal references, but the active CodeSystem did not include the five decision-support checklist concepts added for FHIR-24867, and three of those new safety page anchors used absolute URL strings instead of local anchor names. A Confluence FHIR-I minute reference only records assignment to CDS for review, and Zulip searches found no conflicting community rationale.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit completes the current build-scope source application by adding the five FHIR-24867 decision-support checklist concepts to source/codesystem/codesystem-safety-entries.xml and normalizing the corresponding safety.html anchors to the local code names. It intentionally does not reconcile broader pre-existing safety checklist/CodeSystem drift from unrelated issues.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It preserves the already-published safety-page and CDS-page wording while making the formal safety-entries CodeSystem expose the decision-support checks that FHIR-24867 added.
