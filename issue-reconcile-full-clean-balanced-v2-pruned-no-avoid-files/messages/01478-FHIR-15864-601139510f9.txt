Issue request:
FHIR-15864 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core extensibility issue raised for STU3. It asked to reconcile the modifier-extension guidance by adding a SHALL statement that implementations must not process data containing unrecognized modifier extensions before the note about environments where modifier extensions cannot occur.

Initial application:

That commit added the requested SHALL sentence to source/extensibility.html, with corrected spelling, and also changed the summary rule line to say "might have carry modifier extensions".
