Initial application:

Later source refactors converted the spreadsheet source to StructureDefinition XML, and FHIR-42886 removed EvidenceVariable.characteristic entirely.

Additional context:
FHIR-42886 is a later Applied/Persuasive with Modification R6 decision, already represented on the combined branch, that replaces EvidenceVariable.characteristic with EvidenceVariable.definition as CodeableReference and explicitly limits definition.reference to Group. The current StructureDefinition and introduction therefore should not reintroduce definitionReference or definitionCanonical. However, the current Evidence examples still referenced the two FHIR-26652 EvidenceVariable example ids after those examples were deleted by the FHIR-42886-era conversion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

No obsolete characteristic or canonical element is reintroduced.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It preserves the later FHIR-42886 model while repairing the remaining FHIR-26652 example references in the active EvidenceVariable and Evidence build scope. Any broader cleanup of unrelated Evidence example references should be handled under separate tracker reconciliation.
