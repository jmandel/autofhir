Initial application:

Additional context:
The original Zulip discussion showed the issue began with invalid SearchParameter FHIRPath and concern about resolve() traversing across resources. FHIR-I's 2025-11-03 minutes reopened the earlier simple-subset resolution because it conflicted with FHIR-49784's use of repeat(), clarified that SearchParameter.expression should not be restricted to a simple subset, and chose guidance plus ballot feedback instead of banning FHIRPath features. SearchParameter is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit completes the approved source application by adding the resolve()/resource-boundary guidance to the SearchParameter.expression element comment. It also corrects the SearchParameter notes examples from Resource.descendents... to the valid FHIRPath Resource.descendants()... form, so the page does not itself publish invalid FHIRPath while resolving a FHIRPath-validation issue.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It applies the final FHIR-I guidance without reintroducing the rejected simple-subset restriction; future tooling or policy changes should wait for ballot feedback or a separate work-group decision.
