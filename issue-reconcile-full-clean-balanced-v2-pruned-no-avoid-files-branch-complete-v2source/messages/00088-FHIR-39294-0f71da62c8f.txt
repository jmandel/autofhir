The final INM resolution was to remove MessageDefinition.parent because PlanDefinition now points to MessageDefinition and ActivityDefinition outcomes are requests rather than message events.

Initial application:

Additional context:

The current PlanDefinition and RequestOrchestration source already describe MessageDefinition as a definitional resource for sending a message. FHIR-32750 records the same removal request and is audited separately; FHIR-15575 is older background that explained the now-removed parent element.

Keep this source fix. It applies the approved INM decision, aligns the direction of references with the CDS PlanDefinition change, and avoids the circular MessageDefinition-to-PlanDefinition/ActivityDefinition relationship that the tracker identified.
