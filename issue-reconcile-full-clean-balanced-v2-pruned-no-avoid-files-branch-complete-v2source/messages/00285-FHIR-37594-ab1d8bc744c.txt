Issue request:
FHIR-37594 was a Published/Persuasive R5 Financial Management clarification to correct the cut-and-paste definition for Claim.item.net, state that net is unit price * quantity * factor, provide the requirement that the line-item amount is needed to know what is being charged, and apply the same change to all .net elements in Claim, ClaimResponse, and ExplanationOfBenefit.

Initial application:
Commit 1521c32f7a applied the approved definition wording to the three Claim .net elements, the three ClaimResponse.addItem .net elements, and the six ExplanationOfBenefit item/addItem .net elements. Commit 569461dff5 later touched the same StructureDefinitions while adding FHIR-32797 traceNumber content, but the .net requirements fields still retained the older duplicated "Provides the total amount claimed" wording.

Additional context:
