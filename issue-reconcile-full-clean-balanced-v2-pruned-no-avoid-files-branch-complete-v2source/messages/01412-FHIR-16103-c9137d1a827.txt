FHIR-16103: Restore mixed normative maturity guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-16103 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I clarification request for the FHIR-core compatibility/versioning page. It asked to move the R4 ballot-intro mixed normative content discussion into the main specification near the FMM maturity-level documentation and to note that operations and profiles may have a lower maturity than the resources they reference, but cannot have a higher maturity.

Initial application:
The request was initially applied by git commit 008e2ec15d026357bc974bb2663f86336cd31665, which moved the Mixed Normative content section from source/ballot-intro.html into source/versions.html under svn revision 14253. Later edits refined the example and notes in that section, including FHIR-47043 and FHIR-47046 changes.

Additional context:
source/versions.html and source/formats.html are active build pages through source/hierarchy.xml and publish.ini. Commit 7466cfc63cd0b162497a42c80a67480eed86c75a later removed the entire Mixed Normative content block from source/versions.html, including the FHIR-16103 operations/profiles maturity note and other notes. The already-published FHIR-25744 reconciliation restored only the shared SearchParameter clarification from that deleted block. FHIR-47043 and FHIR-47046 are already represented on the combined branch as no-change audits because their narrower example/readability requests were not themselves a reason to reintroduce the old block.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit restores a concise current Mixed Normative content subsection in source/versions.html. It also retargets the surviving source/formats.html Mixed Normative content link from the general standards-process anchor to the restored versions.html#mixed anchor.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It restores the published FHIR-I clarification without reviving obsolete example text or the old note-list structure, and it preserves the separate FHIR-25744 shared SearchParameter guidance as an adjacent specialized paragraph.
