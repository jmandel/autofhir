FHIR-25289: Clarify Timing timeOfDay repetition

Issue request:
FHIR-25289 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to remove stale Timing.repeat.dayOfWeek and Timing.repeat.timeOfDay comments that said frequency and period could not be used with those elements, so schedules such as twice a day on Mondays and 10:00 AM every other day remain expressible after FHIR-12460.

Initial application:
The R5 source change in 5cbc14c7d0f361941099bf72066b75c3f74f6d4f mentioned FHIR-25289 and FHIR-23710, removed the dayOfWeek frequency/period prohibition, and changed the timeOfDay exclusivity text to say only that timeOfDay and when cannot both appear. That initial application left the first timeOfDay comment sentence saying the action happens every day, which still conflicted with frequency/period combinations such as every other day.

Additional context:
FHIR-23710 independently approved the timeOfDay comment text that only prohibits using timeOfDay with when. FHIR-12460 loosened the Timing invariant so timeOfDay may be used with frequency and period. The current source/fhir.ini routes datatypes through the active datatypes build area, and source/datatypes/timing.xml is the authoritative Timing spreadsheet. Current source examples include a dosage step with period 2 days plus timeOfDay, confirming that the comment should not unconditionally infer daily repetition.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated source/datatypes/timing.xml so Timing.repeat.timeOfDay says the action occurs at the specified time(s) as otherwise constrained by the repeat timing, while preserving the approved rule that timeOfDay and when are mutually exclusive. No other source files were changed.

Recommendation:
Keep this focused source fix. It completes the FHIR-25289/FHIR-23710 application without changing the underlying constraints, and no additional Timing policy decision is needed for this wording correction.
