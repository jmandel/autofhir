FHIR-35090: Remove guessed birth date guidance from Person

Issue request:
FHIR-35090 was Published/Persuasive for FHIR-core Patient and asked to remove Patient.birthDate guidance that said an estimated year should be guessed when the real DOB is unknown. The approved replacement says partial dates are allowed when the specific date of birth is unknown and preserves the patient-birthTime extension guidance for Patient.birthDate.

Initial application:
The issue was applied to Patient.birthDate by Jira-tagged commit 226cf50c64b4737a64a12f90c243f29a52a0e7df, replacing the guess wording with the approved partial-date wording. Current Patient source still carries that approved text. A source search found the same old guess guidance remained on the active Person.birthDate definition, which appears to have been missed by the initial Patient-only application.

Additional context:
Both Patient and Person are active resources in source/fhir.ini, and Person.birthDate is a parallel birth-date element in the current core build. The patient-birthTime extension now lives in the Extension Pack and explicitly has Person.birthDate in its context, but this reconciliation does not need to change that external definition. PA minutes list FHIR-35090 on the 2022-02-09 agenda, and Zulip searches found no directly relevant conflicting discussion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated source/person/structuredefinition-Person.xml so Person.birthDate no longer tells systems or users to guess a DOB. Patient.birthDate was left unchanged because it already matches the approved FHIR-35090 wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It completes the same non-substantive clarification for the active parallel Person birth-date element while preserving the already-applied Patient wording; no further core change is indicated by the checked Jira, source, git, and community evidence.
