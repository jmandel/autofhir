FHIR-15871: Enforce MedicationRequest initial fill details

Issue request:
FHIR-15871 was Published as Persuasive for the STU3 Pharmacy MedicationPrescription/MedicationRequest artifact. It asked for support for refill duration and refill quantity when they differ from the initial dispense; the approved resolution was to add MedicationRequest.dispenseRequest.initialFill with quantity and duration children, each 0..1, and a constraint that at least one of the two is populated.

Initial application:
The Jira comments point to GForge/SVN build r13840. In git history this is 3ca8aaddb2c50c08732cad49f8ab68f086ba8c83, which added initialFill, initialFill.quantity, and initialFill.duration to the old medicationrequest spreadsheet source. That application also added comment text saying either quantity or duration must be included, but no formal invariant was carried forward.

Additional context:
MedicationRequest is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini, and the source has since been refactored from spreadsheets into source/medicationrequest/structuredefinition-MedicationRequest.xml. Current source already contains the initialFill backbone and the quantity/duration children with the requested 0..1 cardinalities and SimpleQuantity/Duration types. Focused Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later decision or community record reversing the at-least-one requirement.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Added the missing mps-1 error invariant to MedicationRequest.dispenseRequest.initialFill with the FHIRPath expression quantity.exists() or duration.exists(). This keeps the existing elements and narrative comment while making the approved at-least-one rule machine-verifiable in the active StructureDefinition source.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. No separate decisions are made for the other r13840 tracker issues because they concern different MedicationRequest or MedicationDispense/MedicationKnowledge changes and have their own reconciliation paths.
