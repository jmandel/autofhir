FHIR-21633: Clarify DetectedIssue reference authority

Issue request:
FHIR-21633 was a Published/Persuasive CDS change request asking that DetectedIssue be distinguished from the then-new definitional ClinicalUseIssue resource, now ClinicalUseDefinition, and that DetectedIssue may point to that definitional issue for a particular patient case.

Initial application:
The Jira-linked implementation was commit 5a901eb667bfa6f88e04cdebad84c53f8758388f, merged in HL7/fhir#2834, which updated source/detectedissue/detectedissue-introduction.xml to say DetectedIssue represents a patient-specific potential issue and that ClinicalUseDefinition is for general patient-independent knowledge. That left the patient-specific boundary applied, while the DetectedIssue.reference element still described only literature, knowledge-base, or similar references without naming ClinicalUseDefinition.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists both detectedissue and clinicalusedefinition as active current-build resources. Current DetectedIssue narrative already points readers to ClinicalUseDefinition for general knowledge, and DetectedIssue.reference is the existing URI authority element for the literature, knowledge base, or other authority describing the detected propensity. Corrected Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-21633-specific discussion or conflicting community evidence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Added a narrow comment to DetectedIssue.reference stating that the URI may identify a ClinicalUseDefinition describing the general issue being detected in the specific patient context. This completes the missing reference clarification without changing cardinality, type, target profile, or behavior.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as a targeted clarification. No broader DetectedIssue or ClinicalUseDefinition model change is recommended unless the work group later wants a typed FHIR Reference rather than the existing URI authority element, which would be a separate substantive tracker.
