FHIR-46519: Set Flag FMM to normative

Issue request:
FHIR-46519 is an Applied FHIR-core Patient Care change request asking to increase the Flag resource maturity because Flag is used in production by many vendors and applications. The final resolution is Persuasive: in alignment with the September 2025 Patient Care WGM vote, Flag is planned to go normative in R6.

Initial application:
No prior commit on the combined branch carried Issue-Reconcile-Key: FHIR-46519, and git history did not show a FHIR-46519-tagged source commit. The current source had been partially aligned by commits 9987f9c0c3 and 5c20769cc7, which changed the Flag resource and Flag.status terminology standards-status values from trial-use to normative, but source/fhir.ini, the Flag StructureDefinition FMM extension, and the directly bound Flag.status CodeSystem/ValueSet FMM extensions still said 1.

Additional context:
Flag is an active core resource in source/fhir.ini. FHIR versions.html defines FMM 6 as synonymous with normative standards status, and the cited September 2025 Patient Care WGM minutes record the R6 normative-resource discussion and a 31-0-1 motion to support the documented feedback to FMG. The March 2026 Zulip maturity-comment thread supports resolving maturity comments based on the R6 normative intention. Searches for Flag/FHIR-46519-specific Zulip discussion found no conflicting evidence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the authoritative Flag maturity metadata to FMM 6 in source/fhir.ini and source/flag/structuredefinition-Flag.xml. Also updated the required Flag.status terminology artifacts, source/flag/codesystem-flag-status.xml and source/flag/valueset-flag-status.xml, from FMM 1 to FMM 6 because they already carry normative standards-status and are part of the Flag resource's directly required normative content.

Keep this source fix as-is for R6. No broader Patient Care maturity sweep was attempted; co-listed maturity issues concern other artifacts and should remain separate reconciliation items.
