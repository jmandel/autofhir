FHIR-11027: Restore Procedure not-done scope guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-11027 was Published/Persuasive for DSTU2 and asked for a Procedure.notPerformed usage note clarifying the scope of a negated procedure. The approved text said that when the flag is true the procedure did not occur as described, that code/body site/time information may scope the assertion, and that additional details such as performer or outcome are generally inappropriate because they imply the procedure might have occurred at another time, by another clinician, or with another outcome.

Initial application:
Commit 87f224c94cabbf01ee3789f815f26c69d05608d4 applied the issue in the then-authoritative Procedure spreadsheet by adding the requested comment and GF#11027 committee note to Procedure.notPerformed. Later workflow alignment, especially FHIR-14063 in commit 52ac1cc0e7288f26eca9df7e2fb559dcbcbc1efd, removed Procedure.notDone/notPerformed as a separate boolean and represented non-performance with Procedure.status = not-done plus Procedure.statusReason. That refactor carried forward the not-done state but not the FHIR-11027 scope guidance.

Additional context:
Procedure is an active current resource in source/fhir.ini, and source/procedure/structuredefinition-Procedure.xml is the authoritative current Procedure definition. Current Procedure.status already includes the not-done status, while Procedure.statusReason remains for exception-status reasons and has an example binding to procedure-not-performed-reason. The 2017 Zulip implementers thread on negation in resources supports the same distinction: broad questions such as whether a patient ever had a procedure are usually represented by Observation or QuestionnaireResponse, while Procedure.notDone/notPerformed was intended for scheduled or ordered procedures that did not happen. Confluence searches found no directly relevant or conflicting FHIR-11027 record.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Restored the lost FHIR-11027 guidance on the current Procedure.status comment for status not-done, added GF#11027 to the Procedure.status committee notes, and updated the Procedure/Event mapping exception resource comment so the checked-in pattern comparison matches the authoritative Procedure source.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It preserves the approved non-performance scope guidance in the current Procedure.status representation without changing cardinalities, status codes, bindings, or the broader workflow alignment decisions.
