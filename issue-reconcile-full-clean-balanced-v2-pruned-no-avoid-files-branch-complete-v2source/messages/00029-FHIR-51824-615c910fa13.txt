FHIR-51824: Fix MolecularDefinition heading levels

Issue request:
FHIR-51824 is an Applied/Persuasive R6 technical correction for the Clinical Genomics work group. It reported that moleculardefinition.html skipped heading levels by placing the "Support for Molecular Concepts" subsection under an h3 as an h5, producing generated numbering with an unwanted .0 component.

Initial application:
No prior commit mentioning FHIR-51824 was found in the source history or on the combined reconciliation branch. The affected MolecularDefinition notes were rewritten in df11216f35a47b3b75b05dbeef9d7b7b23958dde without a Jira key, and current source still had three peer h5 headings immediately under the h3 "Profiling MolecularDefinition".

Additional context:
FHIR-51822 is the same skipped-heading cleanup family for FHIR-I pages, but it remains Submitted/Unresolved and is not a final decision to reconcile here. The committers Zulip thread for skipped heading levels confirms that h5 nested directly under h3 causes the wonky section numbering and was not intended. MolecularDefinition is current build source: source/moleculardefinition/structuredefinition-MolecularDefinition.xml defines the resource, and source/fhir.ini has active moleculardefinition entries for CG ownership, security category, and FMM.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Changed the three peer h5 headings under "Profiling MolecularDefinition" in source/moleculardefinition/moleculardefinition-notes.xml to h4. This directly removes the h3-to-h5 skip reported for "Support for Molecular Concepts" and keeps the adjacent peer subsections at the same corrected level.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. Do not treat this commit as deciding FHIR-51822; that issue concerns other FHIR-I pages and has not reached a final Applied/Published state.
