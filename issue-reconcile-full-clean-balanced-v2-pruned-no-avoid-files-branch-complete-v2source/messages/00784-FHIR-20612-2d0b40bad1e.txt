FHIR-20612: Relax EpisodeOfCare organization narrative

Issue request:
FHIR-20612 was a Published, Persuasive with Modification Patient Administration change request for EpisodeOfCare. It asked to relax Scope and Usage text that said every involved organization will have its own EpisodeOfCare, while the approved resolution kept EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization at 0..1 and clarified it as the primary organization responsible for care coordination while care teams from other organizations may participate.

Initial application:
The issue was previously applied by git commit 2bd50df5030ce4f128e582f68bb7b3464829ae61, which updated the EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization short, definition, and comment in source/episodeofcare/structuredefinition-EpisodeOfCare.xml. That implementation did not update source/episodeofcare/episodeofcare-introduction.xml, so the active Scope and Usage narrative still contained the absolute "each organization will have its own EpisodeOfCare" wording that triggered the issue.

Additional context:
The EpisodeOfCare resource is active in source/fhir.ini. The linked Zulip thread and the PA WGM minutes show the concern was that the old wording looked like a hard business rule even though the approved resolution intended a narrower clarification around the primary managing organization. Later Jira FHIR-57231 asks to correct the historical EpisodeOfCare.team reference to EpisodeOfCare.careTeam, but it is only Resolved - change required, so it is recorded as related context rather than decided in this default applied/published run. FHIR-57240 is Submitted and asks broader cross-organization modeling questions, so this reconciliation intentionally avoids resolving that broader open topic.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates the active EpisodeOfCare Scope and Usage paragraph to remove the absolute requirement for each organization to have its own resource instance. The replacement mirrors the approved FHIR-20612 scope: a primary managing organization is represented by EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization, care may be provided by care teams from different organizations, and other organizations may have their own EpisodeOfCare instances for tracking their activities.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It completes the published FHIR-20612 clarification without changing managingOrganization cardinality or deciding the broader unresolved FHIR-57240 question. Handle the FHIR-57231 careTeam wording correction separately when that R6 technical correction enters the applicable reconciliation scope.
