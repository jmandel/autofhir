FHIR-30175: Correct otherTherapy indication wording

Issue request:
FHIR-30175 was a Published/Persuasive technical correction asking that the incorrect detailed definitions for contraindication.otherTherapy and indication.otherTherapy be swapped so each backbone describes the correct clinical-use context.

Initial application:
The correction was applied in 447e322ee6dae0af815d4430df0bfbae89dc9209 alongside FHIR-30758, with contraindication.otherTherapy carrying contraindication wording and indication.otherTherapy carrying indication wording. Later ClinicalUseIssue was renamed to ClinicalUseDefinition, and the 2025 FHIR-48631 refactor made indication.otherTherapy the contentReference source while leaving contraindication wording and mapping on that indication element.

Additional context:
ClinicalUseDefinition is the active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, while ClinicalUseIssue is retired/commented there. FHIR-30758 and FHIR-30757 are adjacent split issues for the same otherTherapy area, but this commit only resolves the reverted indication/contraindication wording from FHIR-30175.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated ClinicalUseDefinition.indication.otherTherapy so its short, definition, and IDMP mapping describe the indication context instead of the contraindication context. The contraindication.otherTherapy element already retains contraindication-specific wording and mapping.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is; it restores the accepted FHIR-30175 distinction after the later contentReference refactor without changing cardinality, types, or bindings.
