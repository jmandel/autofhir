FHIR-35941: Clarify QuestionnaireResponse Composition boundary

Issue request:
FHIR-35941 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked for guidance explaining why to use Questionnaire versus QuestionnaireResponse and to establish boundaries with Composition; the resolution also called for guidance distinguishing Questionnaire from QuestionnaireResponse, Composition, StructureDefinition, and ObservationDefinition, plus QuestionnaireResponse guidance differentiating it from Composition and Observation.

Initial application:
The explicit application commit e4f0d156d5 added the expanded Questionnaire Boundaries and Relationships section, including QuestionnaireResponse, ObservationDefinition, Composition, and StructureDefinition comparisons. The current QuestionnaireResponse page already distinguished QuestionnaireResponse from Questionnaire and Observation, but it still had no Composition boundary text, leaving part of the FHIR-35941 resolution unapplied on the active QuestionnaireResponse resource page.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists questionnaire, questionnaireresponse, composition, and observationdefinition as built resources, so the relevant introduction files are in current build scope. The follow-up issues FHIR-37924 and FHIR-37925 were created from the same seed and published/applied the corresponding ObservationDefinition and Composition-side boundary guidance. Confluence minutes record the FHIR SDC 2022-03-03 persuasive-with-modification decision for FHIR-35941. No Zulip search result or later Jira result found a conflicting decision about the QuestionnaireResponse and Composition boundary.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit adds the missing Composition comparison to source/questionnaireresponse/questionnaireresponse-introduction.xml in the Boundaries and Relationships section. The new paragraph explains that both QuestionnaireResponse and Composition can organize content for human presentation, but Composition organizes resources into a contextual document or attested collection, while QuestionnaireResponse organizes answers to Questionnaire questions and should be extracted into appropriate resources when the answers need to be part of a FHIR document or discrete clinical data.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit. It completes the remaining QuestionnaireResponse-side clarification requested by FHIR-35941 without changing the already-applied Questionnaire, Composition, or ObservationDefinition guidance; unrelated spelling cleanup tracked by later issues can remain separate.
