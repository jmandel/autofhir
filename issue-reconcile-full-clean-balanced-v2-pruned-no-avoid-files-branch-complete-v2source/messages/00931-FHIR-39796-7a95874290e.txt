FHIR-39796: Align BodyStructure landmark distance units

Issue request:
FHIR-39796 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked that BodyStructure.includedStructure.bodyLandmarkOrientation.distanceFromLandmark handle any linear unit of measure, replacing centimeter-specific wording with "The measured distance (e.g., in cm) from a body landmark."

Initial application:
The Jira comments say the issue was applied to the build and link PR #2597/commit 369a5bb. The PR merge 0a37fc applied the approved flexible wording to BodyStructure.includedStructure.bodyLandmarkOrientation.distanceFromLandmark.value, but the parent distanceFromLandmark backbone element retained a stale definition saying the distance was "in centimeters."

Additional context:

FHIR-39795 is the adjacent same-element device/value rename decision and explains why the value child exists, but it does not supersede the FHIR-39796 unit-flexibility decision. OO minutes from 2023-01-09 record that FHIR-39796 increased flexibility with no downside noticed.
