FHIR-47624: Capitalize remaining ImagingStudy modal terms

Issue request:
FHIR-47624 was an Applied/Persuasive Imaging Integration technical correction for the R6 ImagingStudy ballot. It asked that lower-case shalls, shoulds, mays, musts, and similar formal terms in the ImagingStudy material be capitalized consistently with FHIR conventions, specifically calling out section 10.5.2 and the generated Requirements text.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in 07c006edb2, which capitalized MAY, SHOULD, and SHALL across ImagingStudy introduction, notes, and StructureDefinition source, and 1ac0ca73d6 followed with a related QA parenthesis correction in the ImagingStudy.identifier requirements text. Those commits covered the original examples, but later ImagingStudy edits changed some nearby text and reintroduced lowercase formal modal wording.

Additional context:
ImagingStudy is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. Later commit d75e2e196f4 for FHIR-47634 changed the ImagingStudy.status comment from "SHALL apply" back to lowercase "must apply", and the matching mapping exception was later aligned with that lowercase wording. Later R6 ImagingStudy narrative also introduced lowercase "must not" and "may" in active introduction and notes text. The remaining lowercase "should never have existed" in the ImagingStudy status CodeSystem mirrors other status CodeSystems and is descriptive code-definition text rather than the resource/modal wording targeted here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit capitalizes the remaining active ImagingStudy modal terms that affect the rendered resource and generated StructureDefinition: the ballot note now says ImagingStudy.series.started MUST NOT predate ImagingStudy.started, the endpoint note says accessing all series MAY require per-series endpoints, and the ImagingStudy.status comment again says one of the defined statuses SHALL apply. The Event mapping exception resource text and reason were kept in sync with the StructureDefinition source.

Recommendation:
Keep this source-changing commit. Future ImagingStudy edits should preserve the uppercase conformance/modal terms or deliberately reword non-conformance possibilities to avoid reintroducing lowercase formal keywords.
