Initial application:

The related FHIR-41456 provision-code search-parameter mismatch was corrected in the immediately preceding AutoFHIR commit. The remaining meaningWhenMissing placeholder is not changed here because the implementation PR left it open and later source history added a constraint requiring decision when provision exists rather than approving a default meaning.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

Other FHIR-53188 checklist items were already reflected in current source or were intentionally left without an approved automatic value.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. If the work group later wants meaningWhenMissing prose for Consent.decision, it should be handled as a separate reviewed decision with approved wording rather than inferred from the unresolved placeholder.
