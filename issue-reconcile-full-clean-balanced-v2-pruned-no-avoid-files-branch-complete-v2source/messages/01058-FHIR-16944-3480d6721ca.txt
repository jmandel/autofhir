FHIR-16944: Fix instant precision wording typo

Issue request:
FHIR-16944 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for the FHIR-core datatypes page. It asked to clarify the instant datatype wording, including replacing "be known" in "The time SHALL be known at least to the second" and replacing "for them" with "for those"; the resolution was to make the text replacements while retaining the regex's exclusion of year zero.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by SVN-backed commit 62bf29513caa81df76251ed0d8e886d5b03e0132, with message "GF#16944 - fix typo" and git-svn revision 14284. That commit replaced "known" with "specified" but accidentally left the active datatype row as "The time SHALL specified at least to the second," creating an ungrammatical remnant of the requested text replacement.

Additional context:
The same instant row already contains the earlier FHIR-7373 clarification that instant is a more constrained dateTime, and the related FHIR-16347 purpose clarification is already represented on the combined branch. Later datatype edits, including the FHIR-24564 UTC-offset cleanup, preserved the affected sentence and did not correct the missing verb. The primitive datatype page is active build source: source/fhir.ini maps primitive datatypes to datatypes.html by default, and source/datatypes.html is the authoritative narrative page containing the row.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Changed source/datatypes.html so the instant row now says "The time SHALL be specified at least to the second and SHALL include a UTC offset." This completes the approved wording replacement without changing the regex, precision rules, UTC-offset requirement, or instant/dateTime relationship.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix as-is. It is a narrow grammar correction to the current active datatype page and completes the FHIR-16944 text replacement without reopening the separate datatype semantics already handled by related issues.
