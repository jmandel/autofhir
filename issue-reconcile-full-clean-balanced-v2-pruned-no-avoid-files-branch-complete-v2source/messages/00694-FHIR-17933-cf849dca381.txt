FHIR-17933: Clarify ClaimResponse subject definition

Issue request:
FHIR-17933 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for STU3 ClaimResponse long description updates, including changing the ClaimResponse patient description to identify the subject of care, fixing payment-date and monetary wording, removing stale points/double-space text, and clarifying insurance/ClaimResponse wording.

Initial application:
The issue was included in the large FM batch commit afb5ec66f68641293bb7950b6e0eeb118fa1444a, which changed the then-authoritative ClaimResponse spreadsheet and examples. The current XML source was later generated from the spreadsheet in the 2021 source rework, preserving most already-applied fixes such as the payment-date search description, monetary total wording, and removal or retirement of the old item category/net text.

Additional context:
ClaimResponse is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. Later Applied issue FHIR-43309 renamed ClaimResponse.patient to ClaimResponse.subject and allowed Patient or Group, while the current ClaimResponse search parameters continue to describe patient/group searches as "The subject of care." That later rename means the FHIR-17933 patient wording now belongs on the current ClaimResponse.subject element rather than a removed ClaimResponse.patient path.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the current ClaimResponse.subject definition to identify the individual or group that is the subject of care, and adjusted the adjacent requirement from patient-only typo-laden wording to subject-based wording. No source changes were made for request parts already reflected in current source, removed from the current resource, or superseded by later model changes.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit. A separate review may consider broader FHIR-43309 wording cleanup for parallel Claim/EOB line-level subject descriptions, but that is outside this FHIR-17933 reconciliation.
