FHIR-39165: Clarify MedicationStatement social-history boundary

Issue request:
FHIR-39165 was a Published Pharmacy change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked how to record tobacco or alcohol in MedicationUsage/MedicationStatement when those agents are not medications and the medication element is mandatory; the resolution removed substance-abuse use from scope, directed non-prescribed alcohol/tobacco use to Observation, and allowed prescribed agents such as nicotine replacement or long term care alcohol in MedicationUsage/MedicationStatement.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in ccd70e13dace3a42cdffade3560070c4ef2af900, which replaced the explicit paragraph allowing substance abuse/tobacco/alcohol in MedicationStatement with a SHALL NOT/UNLESS-prescribed paragraph. That application did not update adjacent scope and boundary text that still described common use as recording recreational drugs and said the resource recorded medications or substances.

Additional context:
FHIR-38682 changed the R5 ballot resource name back from MedicationUsage to MedicationStatement without changing the relevant scope, explaining why the Jira summary uses MedicationUsage while the current active source is MedicationStatement.
