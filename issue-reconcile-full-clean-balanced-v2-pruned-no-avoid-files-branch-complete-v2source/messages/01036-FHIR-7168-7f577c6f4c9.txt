FHIR-7168: Loosen MedicationStatement reporter wording

Issue request:
FHIR-7168 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request from DSTU1. It objected that the MedicationStatement wording limited the record source to a report from the patient or another clinician, and the accepted resolution was to update the wording to say patient, clinician or another person.

Initial application:
The historical source commit e5b1777610 (#7226, 7168) applied the issue to the old MedicationStatement spreadsheet definition by replacing the patient-or-clinician wording with "A record of medication use as reported by a patient, a clinician or another party." Later source refactors preserved the broader meaning in the active StructureDefinition, but current medication-domain narrative rows still repeated the old patient-or-another-clinician limitation.

Additional context:
MedicationAdministration, MedicationDispense, MedicationRequest, and MedicationStatement are active resources in source/fhir.ini, so their introduction XML files are current build inputs. FHIR-12536 later added derived-supporting-information wording to the same MedicationStatement boundary rows; this reconciliation preserves that newer wording while loosening the reporter phrase. FHIR-7226 was applied in the same historical commit and is already represented on the combined branch by an audit commit, so it is related evidence only and not decided here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the active MedicationStatement scope and boundary narrative, plus the copied MedicationStatement rows on MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense, and MedicationAdministration, so they say the information can come from the patient, a clinician or another person while retaining the existing derived-supporting-information clause.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It fixes the remaining current-build narrative surfaces that could still lead implementers to think MedicationStatement reports are limited to patients and clinicians, without reverting later accepted derived-source clarification or changing the already broader StructureDefinition semantics.
