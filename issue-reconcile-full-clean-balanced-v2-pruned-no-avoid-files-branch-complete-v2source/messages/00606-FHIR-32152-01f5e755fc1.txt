FHIR-32152: Fix lifecycle Appointment status link

Issue request:
FHIR-32152 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It requested broader visibility for the Resource Life Cycles page by linking Encounter Status Management and Appointment Statuses and Encounters to lifecycle.html, explicitly identifying Encounter and Appointment as Clinical Workflow Process Life Cycle resources, and updating the lifecycle page's status examples.

Initial application:
The source was initially applied by fb56e0750c8530f97dc1c861248216fc6e310d79 and merged by 22ea3677e1b75deb1b97acdc5ca691d03f57ca88. That commit added the Encounter and Appointment lifecycle prose and added Appointment.status to source/lifecycle.html, but the new Appointment.status link mistakenly targeted appointment-definitions.html#Encounter.status.

Additional context:
Encounter and Appointment are active Patient Administration resources in source/fhir.ini, and source/hierarchy.xml includes lifecycle.html in the built Documentation Index. The PA May 2022 WGM minutes confirm the 11-0-0 Persuasive with Modification vote and the recommendation to leave other resources to separate trackers. No Zulip discussion was found that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit corrects the lifecycle page's Appointment.status example link to appointment-definitions.html#Appointment.status. The visible text and generated <%sclist Appointment.status%> macro were already correct, so no broader source change is needed.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as a narrow source fix. It completes the prior application by repairing a broken/misleading anchor introduced with the FHIR-32152 lifecycle example, without expanding into Observation, DiagnosticReport, or other work-group-owned lifecycle classifications.
