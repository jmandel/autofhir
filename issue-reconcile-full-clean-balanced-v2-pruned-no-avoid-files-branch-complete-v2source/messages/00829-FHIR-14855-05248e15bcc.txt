FHIR-14855: Remove stale immunization model surfaces

Issue request:
FHIR-14855 is a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core issue raised against STU3 for CDS, asking to drop ImmunizationEvaluation and ImmunizationRecommendation as domain-specific CDS resources in favor of a generic recommendation/decision-support approach. The accepted resolution was to work with Public Health and other stakeholders on a general-purpose recommendation approach that at least covered immunization recommendations.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly tagged FHIR-14855 was found. The current source already contains the later removal commit e073fb45ec6f5a738a9f674baf63406bfe90fb78, which removed ImmunizationEvaluation and ImmunizationRecommendation from source/fhir.ini, deleted their resource source directories, removed Immunization.basedOn's ImmunizationRecommendation target, removed them from the core resource list, and converted several narrative links to the immunization incubator Additional Resource IG. That application left a few build-visible parallel surfaces still advertising the deleted resources.

Additional context:
Later Jira comments and Public Health minutes show that the original generic-CDS idea was reconsidered: in 2022 the group discussed replacing ImmunizationRecommendation with a Request-pattern-aligned ImmunizationRequest/RequestOrchestration approach, and in 2023 Public Health clarified that Immunization and ImmunizationRecommendation are focused on Immunization Information System representation while MedicationRequest supports clinical workflow ordering. Subsequent FMG Zulip discussion in 2025 shows ImmunizationEvaluation and ImmunizationRecommendation being moved into an immunization incubator Additional Resource IG, so external incubator links in narrative pages are intentional rather than stale core ownership.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Removed the deleted immunization resources from the active common clinical search-parameter base lists and from the build-visible FHIR/QUICK model-info Library artifacts. The commit also removed the stale ImmunizationEvaluationStatusCodes conversion helper and synchronized embedded Library.data values with the edited content files. It intentionally leaves external links to the immunization incubator Additional Resource IG alone.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. If model-info or common search-parameter artifacts are regenerated, ensure ImmunizationEvaluation and ImmunizationRecommendation are not reintroduced as core FHIR 6.0 resources or common SearchParameter bases; external incubator references should remain governed by the immunization incubator IG.
