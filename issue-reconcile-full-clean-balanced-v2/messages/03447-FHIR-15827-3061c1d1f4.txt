FHIR-15827: Audit datatype text constraint clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-15827 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked that Address and HumanName use consistent text-element guidance: the text may be provided instead of or as well as structured parts, and applications updating the datatype SHALL ensure that when both text and parts are present, nothing appears in text that is not found in the parts.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by b8d38c35d5769d676e3fc2c761c3a38467cdfc08 (GF#15827, svn revision 13567), which updated source/datatypes.html plus the Address and HumanName datatype spreadsheet XML source. That initial narrative patch accidentally said "address" in the HumanName paragraph, but the HumanName spreadsheet row used "name" and the later published FHIR-16963 cleanup corrected the narrative typo in 3fd0ecd870122c5cc18974817f0837553468ad28.

Additional context:
FHIR-12351 explains why HumanName had previously lost the older "text and parts are in agreement, or only one is present" sentence during the HumanName rework. The originating Zulip thread and FHIR-15827 snapshot show the concern was inconsistent Address/HumanName guidance, not a request for server-side validation rules or a new invariant. source/fhir.ini lists HumanName and Address as active datatypes and maps datatype pages to source/datatypes.html, so the checked narrative and spreadsheet XML files are current build inputs. Confluence searches found no additional minutes or conflicting decisions.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source/datatypes.html states the approved rule for both HumanName and Address, and source/datatypes/humanname.xml plus source/datatypes/address.xml carry the same rule in the datatype element comments that feed the detailed definitions.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-15827 unless a future work group decision changes the intended relationship between free-text display values and structured name/address parts.
