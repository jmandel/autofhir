FHIR-29311: Audit Questionnaire group behavior guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-29311 was Published/Persuasive for FHIR-core Questionnaire and asked what readOnly and required mean on Questionnaire group items and nested non-group items. The accepted R5 clarification was to say that readOnly on a group makes all items beneath that group read only, while readOnly on a question affects only that question's answer, and to carry core guidance that a required group must have at least one answered descendant question without making child question items automatically required.

Initial application:
The Jira application comment cites commit 00a5007599ed06ea0a0aa5267dc40a9de405e79e. That commit changed the active Questionnaire StructureDefinition source, adding the readOnly group/question usage note to Questionnaire.item.readOnly and preserving the already-present required-group rule in Questionnaire.item.required.

Additional context:
Questionnaire is an active core resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/questionnaire/structuredefinition-Questionnaire.xml and source/questionnaire/questionnaire-notes.xml are in current build scope. Related published Questionnaire clarifications FHIR-26684 and FHIR-26835 supplied earlier required/group-property context, and FHIR-32151 later added the explicit direct-answer wording for required questions and the group-with-answered-descendant sentence now present in current source. FHIR-32151 is already represented on the combined branch, and Zulip/Confluence searches found no conflicting FHIR-29311-specific evidence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current active source already states that required questions need a direct answer, required groups need at least one answered descendant question, required does not automatically make contained items required, and readOnly on a group makes descendants read only while readOnly on a question does not affect descendant questions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-29311 because the current Questionnaire source already conveys the accepted behavior without requiring redundant prose.
