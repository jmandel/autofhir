The approved resolution was to list tools by category and reference a maintained tooling ecosystem page instead.

Initial application:

That commit removed the inline profiling and testing tool tables from source/implsupport-module.html and replaced them with category prose linking to HL7 wiki pages for profile tooling and testing platforms.
