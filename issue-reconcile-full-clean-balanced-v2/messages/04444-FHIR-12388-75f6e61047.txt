Issue request:

Initial application:

Later source refactors converted the spreadsheet into the current XML CodeSystem, ValueSet, StructureDefinition, and search-parameter bundle while preserving the applied change.

Additional context:
MedicationPrescription is now MedicationRequest, and source/fhir.ini lists medicationrequest as an active resource. Related FHIR-12447 was the companion search-parameter request and is already represented on the combined branch. Later Published Pharmacy decisions refined the same intent value set: FHIR-34218 clarified instance-order as generating a schedule on a medication administration record (MAR), and the already-published FHIR-13999 reconciliation fixed the hierarchy so instance-order is directly under order.
