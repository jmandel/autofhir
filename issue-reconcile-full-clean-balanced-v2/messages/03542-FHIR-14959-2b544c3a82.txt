FHIR-14959: Audit CommunicationRequest requester clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-14959 was a Published Patient Care change request for STU3 asking to clarify the difference between requester and sender in CommunicationRequest. The final Not Persuasive with Modification disposition accepted adding the Scope and Usage sentence that the requester requests the sender to send the payload to the recipient, while explicitly declining to prescribe what to do when requester and sender are the same.

Initial application:
The clarification was applied in commit 722ed5c93f4bc9828e96f0f5b476bedc3486fb92, which changed source/communicationrequest/communicationrequest-introduction.xml to add the accepted requester/sender/payload/recipient sentence. No separate source note forbidding same requester/sender use was applied, matching the Jira comments and resolution.

Additional context:
CommunicationRequest is active in source/fhir.ini and the current source still contains the accepted clarification in the Scope and Usage section. Later CommunicationRequest introduction commits, including J#46539/J#46540, J#46333, and J#51696, edited nearby wording and examples without removing or contradicting the FHIR-14959 clarification. Current element naming has evolved from the older sender element to informationProvider as the source of the communication, but the Scope and Usage sentence remains a generic sender/source explanation and does not create the declined same-party rule.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change because the current active source already carries the final approved clarification and omits the requester-equals-sender prescription that Patient Care declined to add.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Further wording work about informationProvider/sender terminology would be a separate current-spec editorial or work-group decision, not required to reconcile FHIR-14959.
