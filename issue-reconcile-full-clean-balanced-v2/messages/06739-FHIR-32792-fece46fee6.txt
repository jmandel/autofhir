FHIR-32792: Audit QuestionnaireResponse identifier cardinality

Issue request:
FHIR-32792 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I SDC issue raised against R4 and applied for R5. It asked that QuestionnaireResponse.identifier, described in the issue text as part of a broader concern about 0..1 identifier elements such as AdverseEvent and QuestionnaireResponse, allow 0..* identifiers.

Initial application:
The Jira comment linked HL7/fhir PR #1378. That PR merged as 67cd6b4440 and included e6c439d264, which changed source/questionnaireresponse/structuredefinition-QuestionnaireResponse.xml from <max value="1"/> to <max value="*"/> for QuestionnaireResponse.identifier.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively includes both questionnaireresponse and adverseevent resources in the current build. The current QuestionnaireResponse source still has QuestionnaireResponse.identifier at 0..*, and AdverseEvent.identifier is also 0..*. The AdverseEvent clone, FHIR-32802, was later Resolved - No Change by Patient Care because R4 was not open for this non-technical correction and emerging R5 already had AdverseEvent.identifier at 0..*. Confluence SDC minutes for 2021-08-12 record FHIR-32792 as Persuasive, and the relevant Zulip questionnaire discussions show the prior rationale and the request path for changing QuestionnaireResponse.identifier.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative build-source already preserves the accepted QuestionnaireResponse.identifier 0..* cardinality, and the related AdverseEvent concern is satisfied in the current source under its separate no-change clone.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No QuestionnaireResponse source follow-up is needed for FHIR-32792; any future change to other identifier elements such as Bundle.identifier should be handled through a separate final work-group decision rather than inferred from this QuestionnaireResponse issue.
