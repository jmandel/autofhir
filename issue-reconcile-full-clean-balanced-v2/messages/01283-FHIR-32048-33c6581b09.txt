Issue request:
FHIR-32048 is an Applied FHIR-core Patient Care change request asking to update Condition's FHIR Maturity Level to Normative. The final resolution is Persuasive: in alignment with the September 2025 Thursday Q1 Patient Care WGM vote, Condition is planned to go normative in R6.

Initial application:
No prior commit on the combined issue-reconcile branch carried Issue-Reconcile-Key: FHIR-32048, and git history did not show a FHIR-32048-tagged implementation commit. The current source was partially aligned by 1ade81c36b, which raised Condition's source/fhir.ini FMM from 3 to 5, b0795b968d, which removed trial-use status from Condition.stage and Condition.evidence after the September 2025 Patient Care discussion, and 5c20769cc7, which changed the Condition StructureDefinition standards-status from trial-use to normative. However, source/fhir.ini still said Condition FMM 5 and the Condition StructureDefinition fmm extension still said 3.

Additional context:
Condition is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini and has FMG approval. FHIR versions.html defines FMM 6 as synonymous with normative standards status. The cited September 2025 Patient Care WGM minutes record the Patient Care normative-resource discussion, including Condition concerns, agreement to remove trial-use status from Condition.stage and Condition.evidence, and a 31-0-1 motion to support the documented feedback to FMG. Zulip discussion also records that Patient Care could resolve old maturity ballot comments based on the intended normative direction, and a related implementers thread records the R6 target for clinical resources to become normative. Related Patient Care maturity commits for AllergyIntolerance, CareTeam, and Goal use FMM 6 for normative resources.
