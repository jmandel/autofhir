FHIR-48532: Audit DeviceAssociation status split

Issue request:
FHIR-48532 was Applied as Persuasive with Modification for R6. It reported that DeviceAssociation.status mixed association-state values such as implanted, explanted, and attached with resource-record status values such as entered-in-error, and that a required binding could not cover non-implantable devices such as beds or wheelchairs. The accepted resolution changed status to a code with active | inactive | entered-in-error | unknown, made statusReason extensible, and added DeviceAssociation.associationStatus as a preferred CodeableConcept for physical or operational association states.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation in commit 2b906e8ba0, which changed source/deviceassociation/structuredefinition-DeviceAssociation.xml, the DeviceAssociation status CodeSystem, examples, source/status-codes.xml, and added the associationStatus CodeSystem and ValueSet. The applying Jira comment also asked to update associated examples, and the commit updated the DeviceAssociation examples and the Observation DeviceMetric-focus bundle example to use status="active" plus associationStatus for implanted or attached states.

Additional context:
DeviceAssociation is active in source/fhir.ini, and DeviceAssociation.associationStatus is listed as a current element. Later same-change-family issue FHIR-48713 added abandoned to associationStatus in f09755d231 and is already audited on the combined branch. FHIR-51229 was also resolved by reference to FHIR-48532 and already audited on the combined branch. FHIR-46465 separately reports the same status-binding scope problem and is audited in a separate commit from this worker. Later Jira hits such as FHIR-54463, FHIR-50665, and FHIR-55254 are not final Applied/Published source-edit decisions for this reconciliation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative source already implements the accepted split: DeviceAssociation.status is a 1..1 code bound required to active, inactive, entered-in-error, and unknown; statusReason is extensible; associationStatus is 0..1 CodeableConcept with a preferred association-status value set; and current DeviceAssociation examples no longer use implanted, attached, or similar association-state codes as DeviceAssociation.status.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is for FHIR-48532. Handle any future refinements to the wording or broader relationship among status, statusReason, and associationStatus only under a final follow-up decision; do not reopen FHIR-48532 merely to add redundant explanatory prose.
