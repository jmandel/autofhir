FHIR-50160: Audit Device additive performer removal

Issue request:
FHIR-50160 was an Applied FHIR-core Device change request from OO, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R6, asking to remove Device.additive.performer and Device.additive.performed because dynamic procedure-focused details do not belong on Device. The final resolution also asked to add Boundary and Relationships guidance that Procedure may record inclusion of an additive and to add a Device.additive comment saying Procedure can document details of adding a substance to the device.

Initial application:
The issue was applied upstream before this reconciliation. Commit 7983eb8596 removed Device.additive.performer and Device.additive.performed from source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml, added the Device.additive comment, and updated source/device/device-introduction.xml. That commit was merged by PR #3703 as db5be60e5b. No prior AutoFHIR reconciliation commit for FHIR-50160 was present on the combined branch.

Additional context:
FHIR-49741 introduced Device.additive, including the performer and performed subelements that FHIR-50160 later reconsidered. The 2025-09-29 OO Specimen minutes record the rationale that performer/timing should be captured with Procedure on the referenced device, and the 2025-09-29 HCP minutes record agreement with the proposed disposition and the 7-0-1 Persuasive with Modification vote. Current source/fhir.ini lists Device as an active resource, so source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml and source/device/device-introduction.xml are in current build scope.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml contains Device.additive with only type and quantity children plus the requested Procedure comment, and a source search finds no remaining Device.additive.performer or Device.additive.performed definitions in source/device. Current source/device/device-introduction.xml also contains the requested Procedure boundary sentence.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current FHIR source already implements the final Jira disposition, so another source edit would be redundant and could risk disturbing later unrelated Device.additive refinements.
