FHIR-46789: Audit document signature guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-46789 was Applied with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It requested removing the problematic Document Structure sentence that tied document signatures to a listed attester, XML KeyInfo, and an attester Bundle.entry.fullUri, and replacing the local document page guidance with a shorter statement that a Document Bundle digital signature could follow the digital signatures page.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source/documents.html by 44d4699f20a0d3cc55144e2b22bd726e4d2b48fc, which removed the listed-attester/KeyInfo/fullUri sentence and inserted the Jira resolution's Bundle.signature wording. It was then adjusted by f06748cde92aa18ad0830b9f43fbc39639d38026, also explicitly tagged FHIR-46789, to remove the Bundle.signature mention while preserving the document page's pointer to the digital signatures page.

Additional context:
source/hierarchy.xml lists documents.html as the current Documents page, so source/documents.html is active build source for the Related URL. The current source no longer contains the problematic KeyInfo/fullUri/attester recommendation. Later R6 signature work made a direct Bundle.signature recommendation inappropriate: source/bundle/structuredefinition-Bundle.xml marks Bundle.signature deprecated and points to Provenance based Signatures, and source/signatures.html says Document Bundles are signed by Provenance resources in Bundle entries and that applications SHOULD use that method instead of Bundle.signature. FHIR-51099, already represented on the combined branch, confirms the applied decision to deprecate Bundle.signature rather than broaden its use. FHIR-53914 is a Triaged follow-up on Signing Bundles wording and does not reverse this state.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. The current document page already removes the confusing attester/KeyInfo/fullUri guidance and links readers to the active signatures page, while avoiding a stale recommendation to use deprecated Bundle.signature.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce Bundle.signature on the Documents page unless a later final work-group decision reverses the Bundle.signature deprecation; any future broad rewrite of Signing Bundles should proceed under separate follow-up issues such as FHIR-53914/FHIR-54082.
