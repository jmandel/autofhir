FHIR-26835: Audit Questionnaire group readOnly guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-26835 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for FHIR-core Questionnaire and asked whether Questionnaire.item.readOnly=true on an item of type group makes all sub-items read-only, and whether a descendant can override that with readOnly=false. The accepted clarification was that group-level visibility/editability controls such as readOnly, enableWhen, hidden, and similar controls apply to descendants and conflicting descendant declarations do not override the ancestor setting; systems may warn or error on such conflicts.

Initial application:
The Jira application comment cites commit 4b602199a770c77983f89646ed0ee358157e4efb. That commit added the Questionnaire notes "Group properties" section explaining that readOnly=true on a group causes all descendant items to be treated as readOnly even when a descendant says readOnly=false, that enableWhen disabling and hidden-style controls also affect descendants, and that systems may raise errors or warnings for conflicting declarations.

Additional context:
Questionnaire is an active core resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/questionnaire/questionnaire-notes.xml and source/questionnaire/structuredefinition-Questionnaire.xml are current build inputs. Later same-change-family issue FHIR-29311 clarified the narrower non-group case: readOnly on a question marks only that question's answer read-only and does not make descendant questions read-only. FHIR-29311 is already represented on the combined branch, so this audit does not duplicate it. Confluence SDC minutes for 2020-05-21 record the FHIR-26835 vote as agreement that enableWhen, hidden, and read-only apply to all subitems. A 2025 Zulip thread also points readers to the current Questionnaire group section and confirms that a readOnly group makes all descendants read-only even if a child says readOnly=false.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current active Questionnaire source already conveys the seed decision in the narrative group-properties section and is consistent with the StructureDefinition comments for enableWhen and readOnly after considering FHIR-29311's later clarification for readOnly on question items.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-26835 because the current build already states the accepted group-level inheritance rule and later related guidance only narrows behavior for non-group question items.
