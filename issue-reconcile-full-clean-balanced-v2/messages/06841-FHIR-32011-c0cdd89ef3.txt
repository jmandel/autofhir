FHIR-32011: Restore Bundle.issues STU markers

Issue request:
FHIR-32011 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for OperationOutcomes in history and other Bundles. The final decision added Bundle.issues as a 0..1 OperationOutcome for Bundle-level information/warnings, constrained Bundle.issues to warning/information severities, prohibited it for document Bundles, deprecated search.mode outcome in favor of Bundle.issues while preserving backward compatibility, and required the new element and invariants to be flagged STU.

Initial application:
The issue was mostly applied before this reconciliation. Commits 0550de5fca and 88c3289f77 added the search-entry-mode changes and Bundle.issues, 30c5d5f934 added bdl-16 and bdl-17, c1dda1d5fd moved the outcome deprecation marker into the CodeSystem, and later cleanup/refactor commits preserved the Bundle.issues type/profile and constraints. A broad 2025 status cleanup, 5c20769cc7, removed the element-level trial-use marker from Bundle.issues without a discovered Jira decision superseding FHIR-32011's STU requirement.

Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini actively includes Bundle in the build. FHIR-46484 later clarified that Bundle.issues should render as an OperationOutcome and is already represented on the combined branch, so this commit does not decide it again. FHIR-50491 and FHIR-54001 raise later search-page and bdl-16 wording questions, but both are Triaged/unresolved and are not safe to decide as part of this seed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Restored structuredefinition-standards-status trial-use extensions on Bundle.issues and on the bdl-16 and bdl-17 constraint components in the authoritative Bundle StructureDefinition. No search narrative wording was changed because the broader migration text is covered by unresolved FHIR-50491 rather than by a final later decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It preserves the applied FHIR-32011 conformance/status semantics while leaving unresolved search guidance cleanup for FHIR-I disposition of FHIR-50491.
