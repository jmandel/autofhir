FHIR-56301: Audit Specimen focus application

Issue request:
FHIR-56301 was an Applied OO change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R6, requiring Specimen to support a separate Specimen.focus Reference when the specimen is collected from an entity different from the subject of record. The final disposition also removed RelatedPerson from Specimen.subject and revised the subject short, definition, and comments to preserve a direct Patient subject when a specimen belongs in a patient's record.

Initial application:
PR #4075 merged as 301eb5a852578c5f4931316b9d5f7fcdc39b036f. Its source commit eced74d5feb4c29d6488e88409918b35366df504 updated source/specimen/structuredefinition-Specimen.xml by removing RelatedPerson from Specimen.subject, adding the final subject guidance, and adding Specimen.focus as the new Reference element.

Additional context:
FHIR-53384 had previously attempted to solve the same animal/other-entity specimen-source problem by allowing RelatedPerson on Specimen.subject, but Jira comments and OO minutes record the patient-compartment concern that led to this follow-up. FHIR-56547 is a related but unresolved AppointmentResponse/patient-compartment issue, so it is not a final Specimen source decision. The checked-in source/specimen/specimen.svg still has generated-looking stale details, but the authoritative StructureDefinition XML is current and in build scope.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Specimen source already has Specimen.subject as the subject of record without RelatedPerson, Specimen.focus as 0..1 Reference(Patient | Group | Device | BiologicallyDerivedProduct | Substance | Location | NutritionProduct | RelatedPerson), and the patient/Group/BiologicallyDerivedProduct comments requested by Jira. The existing Specimen-patient search parameter still resolves through Specimen.subject to a direct Patient reference.
