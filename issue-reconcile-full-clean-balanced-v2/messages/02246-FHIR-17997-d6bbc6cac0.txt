FHIR-17997: Audit PaymentReconciliation date successor

Issue request:
FHIR-17997 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Financial Management issue raised for STU3 that asked to add PaymentReconciliation.paymentDate as a 0..1 date for the date the payment is issued.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in the November 2018 financial-resource commit f369c813, whose message included GF#17997 and whose PaymentReconciliation source added a paymentDate example plus a spreadsheet row for PaymentReconciliation.paymentDate. Later source rework converted the old spreadsheet into StructureDefinition source, where the payment date was present in the published resource surface.

Additional context:
FHIR-26601 was a later Published/Persuasive with Modification R5 issue that explicitly renamed .paymentDate to .date while expanding PaymentReconciliation for patient payments. Current fhir.ini keeps a migration mapping from PaymentReconciliation.paymentDate to PaymentReconciliation.date, and the R4, R4B, and R5 published profiles all expose the payment date successor with the same issued-date meaning. Zulip implementer discussion also confirms that the element means the issued date, not the date funds were received.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. PaymentReconciliation is an active current resource, and its current build-scope StructureDefinition already contains PaymentReconciliation.date as a date element with short "When payment issued" and definition "The date of payment as indicated on the financial instrument." Restoring the obsolete paymentDate name would contradict FHIR-26601, and changing date requiredness based only on the old FHIR-17997 wording would be a new current-spec decision rather than a safe reconciliation of this applied tracker.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. If Financial Management wants PaymentReconciliation.date to become optional in the current build, handle that as a new or separately final cardinality tracker rather than reopening FHIR-17997's already-applied add-element decision.
