FHIR-30243: Audit Device subject reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-30243 was a Published OO change request, resolved Persuasive for R5, asking to rename Device.patient to Device.subject and allow references beyond Patient, specifically Patient, Practitioner, and Person for RTLS-style tracking of people or things.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git commit b8ca3beefba3e8de406db820d71bae446430910c, which renamed the Device.patient element to Device.subject, added Practitioner and Person target profiles, added a Device-subject search parameter, and retargeted the Device-patient search parameter to Device.subject.where(resolve() is Patient). Later R5 development did not leave that exact element shape in place: FHIR-34135 removed Person as a direct participant target, FHIR-32570 moved the subject into Device.association, and the later DeviceAssociation change family removed patient/subject association data from Device entirely.

Additional context:
FHIR-39009 explicitly describes the Device subject Jira conflict, including FHIR-30243, FHIR-34135, and FHIR-32570, and was resolved by linking to the FHIR-38904 disposition. FHIR-38904/FHIR-39226 directed the spec to add DeviceAssociation and remove Device.association from Device so reusable devices would not carry patient-identifying history in the Device instance. The current build scope confirms Device and DeviceAssociation are active resources, with DeviceAssociation approved. Current Device source has no active Device.patient or Device.subject element; only a commented-out Device.association block remains. Current DeviceAssociation.subject carries the association target, includes Patient, Practitioner, RelatedPerson, Device, Group, Location, Organization, and other concrete targets, and has subject and patient search parameters. Person is intentionally absent because FHIR-34135 established that Person is not used as a direct participant.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Re-adding Device.subject, Device.patient, or Person to Device would conflict with the later published DeviceAssociation resolution. The active current source already preserves the implementer-relevant outcome of FHIR-30243 through DeviceAssociation.subject, while incorporating later decisions that refined the allowed target types and moved the association out of Device.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-30243 unless the work group reopens the broader DeviceAssociation model; any future change should be made against DeviceAssociation rather than restoring Device.subject.
