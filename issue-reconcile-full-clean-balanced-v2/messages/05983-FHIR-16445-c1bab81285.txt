FHIR-16445: Audit ValueSet partial expansion guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-16445 was a Published Vocab change request for STU3, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking for a way to define when a ValueSet.expansion is partial. The accepted resolution was not to rely on the expansion count alone, but to clearly define use of expansion parameters for how an expansion, including a page of an expansion, was produced.

Initial application:
The Jira comments point to svn revision 14017. In git, b178b1be54533cde22ea827d6cb8ef2ab5ddef11 and follow-up a5f0637555b6b6f47439a73fce551b681355fe62 are the git-svn imports of revisions 14016 and 14017, both titled "GF#16841, GF#16484, GF#16445: document Valueset.expansion.parameters better". Those commits added the Expansion Parameters section to the ValueSet narrative, explicitly naming ValueSet.expansion.parameter and the offset/count parameters for pages of an expansion.

Additional context:
FHIR-14702 raised the earlier isLimited-element idea, and FHIR-16449 removed the limitedExpansion input parameter in favor of count-based guidance; both are the same 2018 partial-expansion change family, while FHIR-16449 and FHIR-14692 already have combined-branch audit commits. The 2018 Zulip thread "ValueSet partial expansion" records the underlying concern that expansion.total alone does not distinguish all incomplete/partial cases, and the 2018 Vocab minutes list FHIR-16445 as Persuasive with Modification. Later FHIR-40718 refined valueset-unclosed handling and is already audited on the combined branch. Later FHIR-42811 is only Resolved - change required, so it is context for future refinement rather than an Applied/Published source-edit decision for this seed.
