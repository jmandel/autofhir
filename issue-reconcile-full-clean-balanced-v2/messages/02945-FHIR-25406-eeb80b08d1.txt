FHIR-25406: Audit OrganizationAffiliation role clarity

Issue request:
FHIR-25406 was a Published PA change request with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked to clarify the difference between OrganizationAffiliation.organization and OrganizationAffiliation.participatingOrganization, with updated element descriptions/comments and examples showing a supplier-to-hospital relationship, a member-to-HIE relationship, and guidance for non-directional relationships.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by commit a37fa0b2e9 (FHIR-25406 Refine definition of organization and participatingOrganization in OrganizationAffiliation). That commit updated source/organizationaffiliation/structuredefinition-OrganizationAffiliation.xml to add the requested organization comment, participatingOrganization cross-reference comment, and association spelling cleanup, and updated source/organizationaffiliation/organizationaffiliation-introduction.xml with the supplier and member examples.

Additional context:
OrganizationAffiliation is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the authoritative current source still carries the FHIR-25406 clarification. The participatingOrganization short description is not a literal copy of the Jira proposed short text, but the paired definition says it provides/performs the role to the Primary Organization and the shared comments/examples identify the direction. Later source history for the same lines did not reverse that clarification. PA minutes record the 2021 need for better wording and the 2022 vote to make the updates, and the reviewed Zulip thread raises adjacent modeling questions without conflicting with the directional organization/participatingOrganization interpretation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already conveys the approved clarification through the element definitions/comments and Scope and Usage examples, so adding another wording tweak would be redundant and would risk drift rather than correcting a semantic problem.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR core follow-up is needed for FHIR-25406 unless PA later decides to reopen OrganizationAffiliation's directional modeling more broadly.
