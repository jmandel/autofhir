Initial application:

That application changed ArtifactAssessment.approvalDate and ArtifactAssessment.lastReviewDate from dateTime to date in source/artifactassessment/structuredefinition-ArtifactAssessment.xml.

Additional context:
ArtifactAssessment is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, MetadataResource is an active interface, and MetadataResource.approvalDate is defined as date. The current ArtifactAssessment StructureDefinition source defines ArtifactAssessment.approvalDate as date, and its sibling lastReviewDate is also date. A Zulip R5 implementation thread independently identified the original incompatibility with MetadataResource and confirms the intended generator-facing interpretation.
