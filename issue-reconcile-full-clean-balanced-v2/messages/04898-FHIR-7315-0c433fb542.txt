FHIR-7315 was a Published/Persuasive FM change request from the 2015May ballot asking for a claim-level service date period because line-level service dates are usually only included for outpatient services. The Jira resolution says this was already added in STU3, and the decisive comment records: "Persuasive, claim header level period added."

Zulip and Confluence searches found no conflicting community record.
