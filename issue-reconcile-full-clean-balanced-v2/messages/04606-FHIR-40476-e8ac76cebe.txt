OO minutes from 2023-02-16 record the motion for both FHIR-40475 and FHIR-40476, and the Zulip R5 changes thread shows the same concern that conformsTo.category should allow CodeableConcept translations/text. Later related issue FHIR-40289 is still Resolved - change required and concerns additional option/subspecification modeling; it preserves the CodeableConcept datatype and does not reverse this applied R5 datatype change.
