FHIR-47855: Audit MedicationRequest informationSource clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-47855 was an Applied/Persuasive Pharmacy technical correction for R6 asking that MedicationRequest.informationSource be clarified. The accepted resolution explains that informationSource is the reporter of a MedicationRequest when prescription details are being reported by someone other than the requester, normally alongside the reported/isRecordOfRequest indicator, and distinguishes this from MedicationStatement's role.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in HL7/fhir commit 25dc8f92e9c31ae0144d010dd7f1c342e445c752, which replaced the prior "often used when reported" sentence in source/medicationrequest/structuredefinition-MedicationRequest.xml with the clarified definition and added a comment preserving the MedicationStatement distinction. The active current source and the combined branch already contain that clarified text.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists medicationrequest as an active resource and MedicationRequest.informationSource as an active R6 element, so the StructureDefinition source is in build scope. Later commit 7ede8c1b57bc3c9238e2c3c8d530b7697ad3a39d for FHIR-49863 renamed MedicationRequest.reported to MedicationRequest.isRecordOfRequest and updated the FHIR-47855 wording accordingly.
