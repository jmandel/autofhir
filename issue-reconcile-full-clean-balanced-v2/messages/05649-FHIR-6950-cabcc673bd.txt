FHIR-6950: Audit Communication recipient receipt guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-6950 was a Published Patient Care change request for DSTU1 Communication.recipient 0..* asking how a sender can know whether a multi-recipient message was received by all recipients. The accepted resolution was Persuasive with Modification: add a usage note on recipient explaining that individual receipt tracking requires a separate Communication resource instance for each recipient, while multi-recipient communications are for untracked or aggregate receipt cases.
