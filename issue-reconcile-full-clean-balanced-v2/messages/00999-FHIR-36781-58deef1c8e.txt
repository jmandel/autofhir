FHIR-36781: Audit imaging boundary guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-36781 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification R5 Imaging Integration request to replace the ImagingSelection Boundaries and Relationships text and, through a Jira comment, update the ImagingStudy Boundaries and Relationships text. The requested change clarified when Observations, DiagnosticReports, DocumentReference, Binary, ImagingStudy, and ImagingSelection should be used around DICOM studies, selected images/frames/waveforms/documents, and rendered or non-DICOM content.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the June 2022 Imaging changes. Jira links merge commit c138fb1b9ed930251866ab1da3be7d524b3fb1d4 / PR #2000, whose first-parent patch updated source/imagingselection/imagingselection-introduction.xml with the requested Observation, multi-Series, DiagnosticReport, and DocumentReference guidance and updated source/imagingstudy/imagingstudy-introduction.xml with the requested ImagingStudy, DocumentReference, Binary, and ImagingSelection paragraphs.

Additional context:
The current source no longer matches the exact 2022 prose because later Applied/Published issues revisited the same boundaries. FHIR-35994 paired with FHIR-36781 to clarify ImagingStudy subset guidance and has already been audited on the combined branch. FHIR-47275 replaced the overlap-prone wording with the current distinction that ImagingStudy represents an entire DICOM Study while ImagingSelection references a subset. FHIR-52064 changed Observation guidance from apparent constraints to descriptive "typically relates" guidance. FHIR-54814 added explicit ImagingSelection.derivedFrom linkage to ImagingStudy. Those later decisions govern the current build.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. source/fhir.ini lists imagingselection and imagingstudy as active resources, and the active introduction files already preserve the implementer-facing substance after later decisions: ImagingStudy stores details of an entire DICOM Study and its relationships; ImagingSelection references a subset of a DICOM Study and can derive from ImagingStudy; Observation guidance distinguishes identified subsets from whole-study relationships; DocumentReference remains the guidance for non-DICOM or rendered image content. Restoring the older R5 paragraphs would duplicate or regress later Applied R6 decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. If the work group wants to reintroduce DiagnosticReport, Binary, or DocumentReference details into the mirrored Boundaries sections, that should be handled as a new Imaging Integration wording decision because later applied issues intentionally rewrote this section.
