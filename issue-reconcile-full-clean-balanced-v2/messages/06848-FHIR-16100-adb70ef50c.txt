FHIR-16100: Audit BDP collector target

Issue request:
FHIR-16100 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for BiologicallyDerivedProduct.collection.collector. The Jira text first recorded a motion to remove PractitionerRole and allow Patient/RelatedPerson, but the issue comments and later OO minutes document the accepted reversal: the collector is the person performing the BDP collection, not the donor/source, and Patient and RelatedPerson should be removed.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in the spreadsheet era. Commit f8ce0d5631 changed the collector target according to the first proposal, while commit b4bd0b114e pre-applied the reversed direction by changing BiologicallyDerivedProduct.collection.collector from Reference(Practitioner|Patient|RelatedPerson) to Reference(Practitioner) and restoring the definition to a healthcare professional performing the collection.

Additional context:
The BiologicallyDerivedProduct resource is active in source/fhir.ini. Commit 7475edc72c later added PractitionerRole back to the BDP collector as part of a broader OO pass for missing PractitionerRole references, and that later target is not the patient/related-person problem raised by this issue. OO HCP minutes from 2021-10-04 reopened FHIR-16100 and voted to remove Patient and RelatedPerson from the definition as currently applied to the build. The current StructureDefinition targets Practitioner and PractitionerRole only, and its definition says the collector is a healthcare professional performing the collection.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change because the active current source already excludes Patient and RelatedPerson from BiologicallyDerivedProduct.collection.collector. The remaining PractitionerRole target is supported by later source history and does not contradict the reopened FHIR-16100 decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Do not remove PractitionerRole under FHIR-16100 unless a separate work-group decision explicitly reverses the later PractitionerRole alignment; this issue is satisfied by the absence of Patient and RelatedPerson collector targets.
