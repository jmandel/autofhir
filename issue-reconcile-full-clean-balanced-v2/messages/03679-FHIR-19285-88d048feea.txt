FHIR-19285: Correct Account billing diagnosis guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-19285 is a Published/Persuasive with Modification Patient Administration issue, raised against STU3 and applied for R5, requesting that ranked billing diagnoses and procedures move from Encounter to Account. The final resolution added Account.diagnosis and Account.procedure backbones with sequence/rank support, CodeableReference diagnosis/procedure values, coded-date invariants, billing package fields, and related metadata.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by PR #2668, merged as 20ce73a818, with implementation commit 194c70c746 adding Account.diagnosis and Account.procedure to source/account/structuredefinition-Account.xml. Follow-up QA commits 809bb0dfbf, 51969a2ccf, and b4b2e7dda1 refined Encounter billing comments, removed an accidentally duplicated Account.procedure.onAdmission, clarified packageCode descriptions, and corrected the Account date invariants. The Account application left Account.diagnosis.dateOfDiagnosis with a copy/paste definition for ranking, and the pre-existing Condition notes still pointed implementers to removed Encounter.diagnosis.role and Encounter.diagnosis.rank elements.

Additional context:
FHIR-18829 opened the question about splitting procedures and ranked billing data out of Encounter. FHIR-16148 redesigned Encounter.reason and Encounter.diagnosis, removed Encounter.diagnosis.rank from active Encounter source, introduced Encounter.diagnosis.use, and documented that billing-related diagnosis ranking belongs on Account. Later no-change decisions FHIR-28188 and FHIR-22797 confirm that billing-specific diagnosis/procedure rank and present-on-admission information should not be reintroduced to Encounter. Confluence PA design notes and 2023-02-15 PA minutes record the Account direction and approval, and PA Zulip messages around the same implementation state that rank was being removed from Encounter and Account was updated with billing diagnosis/procedure information.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit fixes the remaining current-build source gaps. It updates the authoritative Account StructureDefinition so Account.diagnosis.dateOfDiagnosis describes a coded diagnosis date instead of another ranking field, and keeps the checked-in Account SVG tooltip consistent with that authoritative definition. It also updates the active Condition narrative to point to Encounter.diagnosis.use for diagnosis use and Account.diagnosis.sequence for billing-related numeric rank, removing links to retired Encounter.diagnosis.role and Encounter.diagnosis.rank. Account, Encounter, and Condition are active approved resources in source/fhir.ini; the SVG edit is included only to keep the diagram source aligned with the independently necessary Account XML fix.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It completes the FHIR-19285 reconciliation without reopening later Encounter design decisions: billing rank remains on Account, clinical Encounter diagnosis use remains on Encounter, and any broader diagnosis-use terminology work should follow the already-audited Encounter/THO issue family rather than this seed.
