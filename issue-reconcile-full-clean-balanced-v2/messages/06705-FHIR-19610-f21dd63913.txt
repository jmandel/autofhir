FHIR-19610: Audit ServiceRequest intro wording

Issue request:
FHIR-19610 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked OO to replace a misleading ServiceRequest introduction that described a request for a service "to be planned, proposed, or performed" with wording that treats ServiceRequest.intent as distinguishing an order, proposal, or plan for the service itself.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records that the change was added to the build in commit aaac1a905b596330d5f4e032e763afb1525e6272. That commit replaced the old ServiceRequest introduction sentence in source/servicerequest/servicerequest-introduction.xml with the order/proposal/plan framing requested by the resolution.

Additional context:
ServiceRequest is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. Later ServiceRequest scope rewrites reorganized and broadened the opening paragraph for non-human patients, groups, devices, and environmental locations, including the already-published FHIR-50610 reconciliation, but they preserved the key FHIR-19610 semantics that a ServiceRequest represents an order, proposal, or plan for a service. OO January 2019 minutes record the motion, discussion to put order/plan/proposal at the same level, final changed text, and the 19-0-5 vote. Zulip search found only a tracker-session listing and no conflicting rationale.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The combined branch's active ServiceRequest introduction already says "ServiceRequest represents an order, proposal, or plan" and the nearby intent paragraph repeats that intent identifies whether the resource represents an order, proposal, or plan to perform the indicated service. Searches of active source found no remaining copy of the old misleading "request ... to be planned, proposed, or performed" wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. The current source satisfies the published OO decision, and further editing would only restate wording already present in the active ServiceRequest page.
