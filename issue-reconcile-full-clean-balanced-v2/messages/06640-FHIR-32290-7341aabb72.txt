Its comments narrowed the remaining work to removal of DeviceDefinition.physicalCharacteristics and correcting Device.property.value[x] and DeviceDefinition.property.value[x] cardinality to 1..1.

Initial application:
Commit 01a6bc6ffa explicitly referenced FHIR-32290 and removed the remaining DeviceDefinition.physicalCharacteristics element.
