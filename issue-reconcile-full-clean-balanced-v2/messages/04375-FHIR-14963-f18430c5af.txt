FHIR-14963: Audit Account billing status application

Issue request:
FHIR-14963 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration change request for Account from STU3, applied for R5. It asked to add a separate account lifecycle/billing status because Account.status only records whether the Account resource is usable; the approved resolution added Account.billingStatus 0..1 CodeableConcept with an example binding and wording describing how allocated transactions are treated through the billing process.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source by d56dbee6b4dd99da6c7f15dcb5eddede7e338445, which added Account.billingStatus to source/account/structuredefinition-Account.xml and created source/account/codesystem-account-billing-status.xml plus source/account/valueset-account-billing-status.xml. The follow-up b21787b0158691d646d8be36f39be938ef8d8ccb corrected the example CodeSystem code casing. Account is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini.

Additional context:
FHIR-20496 is a duplicate/same-change-family request for an Account business status and does not require a separate reconciliation commit. The 2021 PA WGM minutes and a linked terminology Zulip thread confirm the WG/editor process question for applying FHIR-14963 and concluded that the new example binding could stay in FHIR space rather than go through UTG at that time. Later FHIR-55284 and TSMG discussion raise a broader, unresolved question about whether several Account terminology artifacts, including account-billing-status, should move to THO; that Triaged issue is not a final decision and does not reverse FHIR-14963.
