FHIR-3547: Audit maxLength definition

Issue request:
FHIR-3547 was a Published FHIR-core StructureDefinition change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking whether ElementDefinition.maxLength described the shortest or longest length. The approved motion revised the definition to describe the maximum length in characters permitted in conformant instances and expected to be supported by conformant consumers, with an implementation note that receivers are not required to reject longer instances and should handle truncation carefully.
