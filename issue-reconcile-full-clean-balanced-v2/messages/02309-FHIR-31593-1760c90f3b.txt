FHIR-31593: Audit Ingredient strength choices

Issue request:
FHIR-31593 was Published/Persuasive for BRR and asked to align Ingredient.substance.strength presentation and concentration with Medication.ingredient.strength[x] by allowing Ratio, CodeableConcept, and Quantity. It also asked that high-limit and reference-strength strength values allow Ratio and Quantity but not CodeableConcept.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in upstream commit c31cf6846019e402344efec66a1782bc32968829, which changed Ingredient.substance.strength.presentation and concentration into choice elements with Ratio, CodeableConcept, and Quantity, and changed presentationHighLimit, concentrationHighLimit, referenceStrength.strength, and referenceStrength.strengthHighLimit into choice elements with Ratio and Quantity. There was no separate prior AutoFHIR audit commit for FHIR-31593 on the combined branch.

Additional context:
The old MedicinalProductIngredient artifact now maps to the active Ingredient resource in source/fhir.ini. Later Published/Persuasive issue FHIR-31821, already represented on the combined branch, replaced the separate high-limit fields with Range/RatioRange choices and added Range to the remaining Ingredient strength choice elements. That later model preserves the FHIR-31593 semantic distinction: presentation and concentration include CodeableConcept and Quantity, while referenceStrength.strength includes quantity/range forms without CodeableConcept. Medication and MedicationKnowledge continue to expose the Ratio, CodeableConcept, Quantity choice set that FHIR-31593 cited.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Ingredient StructureDefinition already contains Ratio, RatioRange, CodeableConcept, Quantity, and Range for presentation[x] and concentration[x], and Ratio, RatioRange, Quantity, and Range for referenceStrength.strength[x]. The obsolete high-limit elements are absent because FHIR-31821 superseded them with explicit range datatypes.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source edit is needed for FHIR-31593 unless the work group wants a new search-parameter or narrative enhancement beyond the Jira's datatype request.
