FHIR-11047: Audit Consent policy cardinality

Issue request:
FHIR-11047 was a Published CBCC change request from the DSTU2 ballot asking that Consent.policy allow more than one policy reference rather than a 1..1 uri. Its Persuasive with Modification resolution says the requested change had already been made by CR 11058.

Initial application:
The application trail is GF#11058/FHIR-11058, not a separate FHIR-11047-named commit. Commit 612068c15886b6624c44de5d656417bcfa7d76d3 applied Consent STU3 updates including GF#11058 and changed the readable Consent spreadsheet from Consent.policy 1..1 uri to Consent.policy 0..* uri plus Consent.policyRule 0..1 uri.

Additional context:
The current build includes Consent as an active resource in source/fhir.ini. Later Published issue FHIR-32817 deliberately replaced the older .policy/.policyRule design with regulatoryBasis 0..* CodeableConcept, policyBasis 0..1 Reference/uri for the single computable policy an organization will enforce, and policyText 0..* DocumentReference for human-readable policy text. Current source matches that later final decision: Consent.regulatoryBasis and Consent.policyText are repeatable, while Consent.policyBasis remains singular by design. Triaged R6 questions about Consent policy terminology were found but are not final decisions that should change this audit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. Re-opening the DSTU2 wording by changing current Consent.policyBasis to repeat would conflict with the later FHIR-32817 resolution and with the current narrative explaining the single computable policy basis plus repeatable regulatory/text policy context.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. If the work group wants multiple computable policy bases in current R6, handle that through the current unresolved Consent policy clarification work rather than through this already-applied DSTU2 cardinality request.
