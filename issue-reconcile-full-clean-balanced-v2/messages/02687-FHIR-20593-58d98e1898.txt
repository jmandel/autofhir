FHIR-20593: Audit AdverseEvent description update

Issue request:
FHIR-20593 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for the STU3 AdverseEvent resource. It started from missing descriptions for subjectMedicalHistory, referenceDocument, and study, and the final Patient Care decision also merged subjectMedicalHistory and referenceDocument into supportingInfo, merged causality assessment/method into assessmentMethod, renamed productRelatedness to entityRelatedness, broadened causality.author to Patient and RelatedPerson, and supplied the requested element definitions.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by 0a593f76ae, which updated source/adverseevent/adverseevent-spreadsheet.xml with GF#20593 committee notes, the study definition, causality.assessmentMethod, entityRelatedness, author targets, and supportingInfo with item/contributingFactor children. The current source was later reworked into source/adverseevent/structuredefinition-AdverseEvent.xml, preserving GF#20593 notes on the relevant elements.

Additional context:
AdverseEvent is active in source/fhir.ini. The current R4-R6 diff mappings explicitly map the removed R4 names to their R5/R6 replacements. Later FHIR-46599, recorded in Patient Care minutes and applied by 017e9edbd0, intentionally removed the one-child BackboneElement pattern and changed supportingInfo, contributingFactor, preventiveAction, and mitigatingAction to repeating CodeableReference elements. FHIR-38911 independently recorded that the R4 deletions should be understood by reviewing the underlying Jira issues and identifies FHIR-20593 as the rationale for the relevant renamed/merged AdverseEvent elements.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The active StructureDefinition already defines AdverseEvent.study, causality.assessmentMethod, causality.entityRelatedness, causality.author with Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Patient, and RelatedPerson targets, and supportingInfo with the medical-history/document definition requested by FHIR-20593. The apparent difference from the 2019 BackboneElement shape is explained by the later applied FHIR-46599 CodeableReference change, not by an unapplied FHIR-20593 requirement.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce supportingInfo.item or supportingInfo.contributingFactor for FHIR-20593; doing so would conflict with later R6 Patient Care decisions. Any future change to the CodeableReference modeling should be handled under FHIR-46599 or a new Patient Care tracker, not as a FHIR-20593 reconciliation.
