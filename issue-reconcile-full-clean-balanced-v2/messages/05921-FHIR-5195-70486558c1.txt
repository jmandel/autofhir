FHIR-5195: Audit Practitioner birthDate date type

Issue request:
FHIR-5195 was a Published PA change request from the Jan 2015 ballot. It asked whether Practitioner.birthDate should match Patient.birthDate by changing from dateTime to date, with comments also asking that Person be aligned. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution says the Practitioner birthDate datatype was changed from datetime to Date to avoid timezone shifts and accidental duplicate records when birth dates are used for identification.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by git commit 8ed9982708ac31ba37ad6670f9be8ba628634329, whose message explicitly lists tracker 5195 among Practitioner ballot reconciliation issues. Its patch changed the Practitioner spreadsheet birthDate type cell from dateTime to date. Person alignment was handled by the later same-family issue FHIR-7311, applied by commit 0522d003c90c49a5774028204610056c3360d1c6 and already audited on the combined branch.

Additional context:
Practitioner, Person, Patient, and RelatedPerson are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini. The current StructureDefinition sources type Practitioner.birthDate, Person.birthDate, Patient.birthDate, and RelatedPerson.birthDate as date, so the person-resource birthDate family is date-only. FHIR-7311 is the directly related Published issue for Person consistency and is already represented on robo-spec-combined-issue-reconcile-full-clean-balanced-v2. Zulip and Confluence searches found no directly relevant FHIR-5195 discussion or minutes that would change the Jira/source/history conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change because the current build already implements the FHIR-5195 decision. Reintroducing dateTime or adding redundant prose would contradict or go beyond the accepted resolution.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up core source work is needed for FHIR-5195 unless PA reopens the date-only birthDate policy for practitioner/person resources.
