FHIR-36666: Align NutritionOrder nutrition product ModelInfo

Issue request:
FHIR-36666 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO change request for R5 asking how NutritionOrder should capture the amount of nutrition supplied by the order for any diet type. The accepted resolution changed supplement.type to CodeableReference(NutritionProduct), changed enteral formula product typing to CodeableReference(NutritionProduct), and added additive product/quantity structure for enteral feeding.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly tagged FHIR-36666 was found. Commit 1a5062f0a41dca024be7fb527908dcec638166f8, "Update Nutrition Order with JIRA dispositions," appears to have applied the NutritionOrder Jira disposition set: it changed NutritionOrder.supplement.type and enteral formula product/additive typing to CodeableReference with NutritionProduct target profiles and introduced an enteralFormula.additive backbone with type, productName, and quantity.

Additional context:
NutritionOrder is an active current-build resource, and the FHIR/QUICK ModelInfo Library content is linked from the Library examples and copied by publish.ini. Later NutritionOrder revisions, especially c83bf7756738440f1355122b581289005beb11f2 and FHIR-53504, renamed the enteral formula product fields to the current enteralFormula.type/productName shape and moved additive into the current root NutritionOrder.additive backbone. The authoritative NutritionOrder StructureDefinition already reflects that current target, but the published ModelInfo sidecars still exposed the old baseFormulaType/baseFormulaProductName and flat additiveType/additiveProductName fields.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the FHIR ModelInfo sidecar so NutritionOrder.supplement.type and NutritionOrder.enteralFormula.type are CodeableReference, NutritionOrder.enteralFormula uses current type/productName names, and NutritionOrder.additive is exposed as a repeating backbone with modularType, productName, quantity, and routeOfAdministration. Updated the QUICK ModelInfo sidecar with the parallel logical-model shape, and refreshed source/library/library-fhir-model-definition.xml so its embedded base64 payload matches the FHIR ModelInfo sidecar.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit. It completes the applied FHIR-36666 nutrition-product/additive data model across the active ModelInfo publication surfaces without taking over the broader FHIR-53504 NutritionOrder cleanup. Remaining NutritionOrder ModelInfo or example mismatches outside these nutrition product/additive fields should be handled by their own Jira reconciliation.
