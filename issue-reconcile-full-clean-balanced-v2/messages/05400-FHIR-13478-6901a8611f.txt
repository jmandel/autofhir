Current ConceptMap semantics use match.relationship and ConceptMapRelationship rather than the old match.equivalence/ConceptMapEquivalence naming, and the active operation definition links to valueset-concept-map-relationship.html. source/fhir.ini actively lists conceptmap=ConceptMap, and source/conceptmap/list-ConceptMap-operations.xml references OperationDefinition/ConceptMap-translate. Targeted Zulip searches found no conflicting discussion; Confluence searches found only the Vocabulary block-vote/minutes evidence.
