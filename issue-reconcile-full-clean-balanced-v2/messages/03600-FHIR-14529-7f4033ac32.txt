The final Jira comments narrowed the application because examples could not be added directly to the Dosage type at that time; the applied action was to update MedicationRequest examples to call out dosage-specific examples.

Initial application:

Current source/dosage.html links readers to dosage-examples.html, and the current dedicated Dosage Regime Examples page contains twelve structured MedicationRequest dosage examples.
