FHIR-42909: Audit logical id absence guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-42909 is Applied/Persuasive with Modification for FHIR-I and asked for the BaseResource/Resource logical ID guidance to name additional cases where a resource id may be absent. The approved resolution was to clarify that ids are typically absent for create submissions, search/history response Bundles, transient operation input/output resources including Parameters, and resources exchanged outside a RESTful context.
