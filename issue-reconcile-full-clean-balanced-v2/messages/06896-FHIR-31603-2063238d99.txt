FHIR-31603: Audit exists-slicing clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-31603 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for FHIR-core profiling/StructureDefinition. It asked that the exists discriminator documentation clearly state that exists slicing has at most two slices, differentiated by one slice with max 0 and the other with min 1 or more, and that declaration order does not matter.

Initial application:
The requested clarification was already applied by Jira-tagged commits b0ca47c034 and c7b89199bb on 2022-08-21. Commit b0ca47c034 updated the ElementDefinition discriminator-type code definition in source/datatypes/elementdefinition.xml, and commit c7b89199bb updated the profiling narrative in source/profiling.html. No prior AutoFHIR audit commit for FHIR-31603 existed on the combined branch.

Additional context:
The current combined-branch source still contains the requested wording in both the profiling page and the ElementDefinition spreadsheet source. source/fhir.ini lists ElementDefinition as an active datatype, and the profiling page is linked from active conformance/documentation pages. Confluence minutes for the 2021-07-12 FHIR-I call record the Persuasive with Modification motion for FHIR-31603. Jira related-link and targeted community searches found no later related decision that changes the interpretation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source changes. The current source already tells implementers that exists slices are based on presence or absence of the nominated element, that there SHALL be no more than two slices, that one slice has max 0 and the other min 1 or more, and that slice declaration order does not matter.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed in the FHIR core repository unless a future work-group decision changes the semantics of exists discriminators.
