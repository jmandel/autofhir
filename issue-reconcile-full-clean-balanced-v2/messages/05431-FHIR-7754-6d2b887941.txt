FHIR-7754: Correct not-done reason guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7754 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU1 by Patient Care. It asked for a way to capture reasons a requested action was not performed, citing Communication.reasonNotPerformed/reasonRejected in QUICK. The final resolution put the capability in the Event pattern by adding a notDone indicator, a separate reasonNotDone element with distinct binding, and usage guidance that not-done records are for explicit clinical/business statements that something was not done, not merely workflow tracking.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in 839bf586d6c1cf9a322e122852f0cc49bcfed263, the workflow-alignment commit that explicitly lists #7754 and added Event.notDone/Event.notDoneReason guidance. Later workflow commits, especially 5064224bd079b7f4ef4023d3ead9111b57bd8bff for pre-applied FHIR-13457 and FHIR-13907, collapsed Event.notDone into the not-done status and replaced notDoneReason with statusReason. FHIR-14063 then synchronized Communication to that model, and FHIR-12766 preserved the Communication not-done reason example binding on the current Communication.statusReason element.

Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini includes event in [logical] and Communication in [resources], so source/event/event-spreadsheet.xml and source/communication/structuredefinition-Communication.xml are active build inputs. Related Published issues FHIR-13306 and FHIR-13346 removed the redundant Communication reasonNotPerformed extension; both are already represented on the combined branch. FHIR-14063 and FHIR-12766 are also already represented on the combined branch. Clean Jira/Zulip/Confluence searches found no community evidence that changes the later status/statusReason interpretation.
