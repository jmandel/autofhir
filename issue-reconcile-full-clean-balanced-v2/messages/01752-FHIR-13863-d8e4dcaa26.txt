FHIR-13863: Audit MeasureReport population health example

Issue request:
FHIR-13863 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 CQI issue asking to expand MeasureReport description/examples beyond clinical data to include population health measures. The accepted resolution was to include a population health reporting example as described in the follow-up comment.

Initial application:
The request was applied before this reconciliation by GF#13863 commit 464fe3741d, which added the HIV Indicators Measure, Library, and MeasureReport public health reporting examples. A nearby historical cleanup commit, 1ce7bae0e8, also touched Measure/MeasureReport public health reporting use cases, though its old GForge GF#14908 reference does not correspond to the current unrelated Jira FHIR-14908 issue.

Additional context:
MeasureReport is active in the current build, and the active MeasureReport examples list includes the HIV Indicators report described as reporting public health data elements. The current Measure and Quality Reporting narrative also explicitly covers public health indicators and population analytics measures, while later MeasureReport work added direct support for group, location, summary, and related population-reporting use cases. No Zulip or Confluence discussion found during this reconciliation contradicted the Jira/source history.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already preserves the applied population/public health example and adjacent guidance, so adding another local mention of population health to MeasureReport Scope and Usage would be redundant rather than a needed semantic correction.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future public-health MeasureReport changes should be handled under their own CQI trackers, especially where they alter MeasureReport structure or example details rather than this already-applied STU3 example request.
