FHIR-7888: Generalize Encounter participant definition

Issue request:
FHIR-7888 was a Published FHIR-core Patient Administration change request, resolved Persuasive, for the DSTU1 Encounter.participant definition. It requested replacing "The main practitioner responsible for providing the service" because Encounter.participant.individual could be a RelatedPerson or Practitioner; the accepted resolution changed the definition to "The list of people responsible for providing the service" and the related short text was "List of participants involved in the encounter."

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied by commit 6a7d0cdd1a574368d73980b35cdfeb71b784de14, which changed the then-authoritative Encounter spreadsheet definition from the old main-practitioner wording to the accepted people-responsible wording. Later source refactors carried that wording into source/encounter/structuredefinition-Encounter.xml and the checked-in Encounter diagram/mapping exception metadata.

Additional context:
Encounter is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/encounter/structuredefinition-Encounter.xml is authoritative current source. Later Published PA decisions broadened the participant model beyond people: FHIR-20511 requested Device support and renaming individual to actor, and FHIR-22696 finalized Encounter.participant.actor targets including Patient, Group, Practitioner, PractitionerRole, RelatedPerson, Device, and HealthcareService. FHIR-35841 then aligned the actor short description to "The individual, device, or service participating in the encounter." Those related issues are already represented on the combined branch, but they make the inherited FHIR-7888 phrase "people responsible" too narrow for the current model.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the active Encounter StructureDefinition definition for Encounter.participant from "The list of people responsible for providing the service." to "List of participants involved in the encounter." This keeps the original FHIR-7888 correction away from a single practitioner while aligning the definition with the current participant.actor targets, the existing Encounter.participant short description, and Appointment.participant wording. The matching Event mapping exception resource text and the checked-in Encounter SVG tooltip were updated so parallel current source surfaces do not retain stale people-only wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It is a narrow editorial correction to current active source that preserves the FHIR-7888 intent and avoids conflicting with later PA decisions that allow non-person Encounter participant actors; no additional related Jira should be decided here because FHIR-20511, FHIR-22696, FHIR-23843, and FHIR-35841 already have separate combined-branch reconcile commits.
