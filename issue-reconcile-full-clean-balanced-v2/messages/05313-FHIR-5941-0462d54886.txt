FHIR-5941: Confirm medication administration boundary wording

Issue request:
FHIR-5941 was a Published/Persuasive Pharmacy change request from DSTU1 asking to clarify the boundary between MedicationAdministration and MedicationStatement, including whether a nurse-administered medication entered by someone else belongs as an administration record or a statement and how much verification separates the resources.

Initial application:
No git commit in the current repository history explicitly mentions FHIR-5941. The Jira resolution says the change was made as part of FHIR-7167 and FHIR-5916, and the relevant historical implementation is commit 7d50996061, which updated MedicationStatement scope/usage and the root definition with the statement-vs-administration distinction; commit e7200c208f immediately adjusted that definition text.

Additional context:
MedicationAdministration and MedicationStatement are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini. Later source history retained and refined the same distinction: TR#12356 expanded MedicationStatement boundaries in 2017, FHIR-37948 editorially updated the root description in 2025 while preserving the primary-difference paragraph, and the existing FHIR-38873 AutoFHIR commit normalized resource naming in the same wording. FHIR-53876 is a later Triaged typo issue against this area, but it is not a final change decision and does not alter the FHIR-5941 boundary conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current active source already explains that MedicationAdministration is for actual medication consumption/administration and that MedicationStatement is a reported or derived assertion, often less specific, independent of a specific administration event, and may be inferred from MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense, or MedicationAdministration.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is for FHIR-5941. Do not add redundant prose for this issue; any further cleanup should follow the later unresolved typo tracker or a new Pharmacy work-group decision rather than reopening the already-applied boundary clarification.
