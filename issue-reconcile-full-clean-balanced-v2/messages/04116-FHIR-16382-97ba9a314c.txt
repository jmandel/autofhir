FHIR-16382: Audit Invoice price component definitions

Issue request:
FHIR-16382 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration correction raised for STU3. It reported that the names/definitions of Invoice.lineItem.priceComponent.factor and Invoice.lineItem.priceComponent.amount were swapped, and the Jira resolution says to change as pre-applied with comments identifying revision 13742.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by git-svn commit c7f8830902, whose message is "GF#16382: BackboneElement priceComponent has factor/amount mixed up" and whose git-svn-id records SVN revision 13742. Before that commit, the factor row carried the amount short/definition and the amount row carried the factor short/definition; after it, factor carried factor text and amount carried amount text.

Additional context:
Invoice is still an active build resource in source/fhir.ini, but FHIR-16380 later refactored Invoice.lineItem.priceComponent and Invoice.totalPriceComponent from local backbone elements to the active MonetaryComponent metadata type. The current MonetaryComponent source defines factor as a decimal with factor-calculation text and amount as Money with explicit amount text, so the current authoritative source preserves the FHIR-16382 correction. The checked-in Invoice SVG is diagram-like generated material, and broader ModelInfo alignment for Invoice/MonetaryComponent is a separate FHIR-16380-family concern rather than a remaining factor/amount swap.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If future cleanup addresses stale Invoice ModelInfo references to the old Invoice.PriceComponent backbone, handle that under FHIR-16380 or a dedicated ModelInfo issue; it is not needed to resolve the factor/amount wording swap audited here.
