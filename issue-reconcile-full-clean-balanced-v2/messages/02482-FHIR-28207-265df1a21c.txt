Initial application:

Subsequent source refactoring moved the current authoritative definitions into structuredefinition-Evidence.xml and structuredefinition-EvidenceVariable.xml.

EvidenceReport is not active in source/fhir.ini and the current Clinical Reasoning evidence page says prior EvidenceReport Resource content has been replaced by an EvidenceReport Profile of Composition in the EBM IG. FHIR-28133 was a retracted earlier attempt at replacing Evidence.contributor, and FHIR-29320 is a separate published Clinical Reasoning content issue already represented on the combined branch.
