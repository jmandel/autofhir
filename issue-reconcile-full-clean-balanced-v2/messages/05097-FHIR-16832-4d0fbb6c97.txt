FHIR-16832: Audit supplement expansion warning

Issue request:
FHIR-16832 was Published with a Not Persuasive with Modification resolution. The ballot comment asked to mark CodeSystem supplements and supplement-related elements or operations as Trial Use because supplement use during profiled value set expansion needed clarification; the final disposition instead said to leave supplements normative and add a note in the ValueSet expansions section that supplement impact on expansion was subject to further experience, experimentation, and possible later rules.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by the historical SVN/Git commit 596446e2625e5d0b6cdf7dd11ffd36c9dd272e76, whose subject was "GF#16832 - warning about supplements effect on value set operation" and whose git-svn-id records SVN revision 14126. That commit added the warning text to both CodeSystem supplement guidance and ValueSet expansion guidance. Current source still contains the ValueSet expansion warning required by the resolution.

Additional context:
CodeSystem and ValueSet are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini, with Vocab ownership and normative/approved status. Later FHIR-14356 temporarily directed a TU marker for CodeSystem.supplements, but later FHIR-39260 directed removal of Trial Use references for CodeSystem supplements; commit 39f96b21ce8d65b5b086b3ee61a455e47a359a98 removed the CodeSystem supplement TU marker and the duplicate CodeSystem warning while leaving the ValueSet expansion warning family. Related supplement expansion mechanics are now represented by the useSupplement operation parameters and expansion-parameter reporting, and separate reconciliations already exist on the combined branch for FHIR-15817 and FHIR-37529.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current source already matches the FHIR-16832 final decision: CodeSystem supplements are not marked Trial Use, supplement-related ValueSet operations remain normative, and source/valueset/valueset-notes.xml contains the requested warning that the impact of CodeSystem supplements on expansion and validation remains subject to experimentation, testing, and possible future rules.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not re-add Trial Use labels for FHIR-16832; any future change to the remaining ValueSet expansion warning or supplement operation semantics should come from a later TI/Vocab work-group decision rather than this 2018 ballot item.
