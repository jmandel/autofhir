FHIR-31778: Audit formOf and producedFrom distinction

Issue request:
FHIR-31778 was a Published FHIR-core BRR change request for R5, resolved Persuasive. It asked how the then-named AdministrableProductDefinition.subject differed from producedFrom and requested clearer documentation; the accepted Jira comment supplied revised wording for formOf and producedFrom that distinguishes the overall medicinal product from the specific manufactured items used in a preparation.

Initial application:

Additional context:
AdministrableProductDefinition is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini, and the current StructureDefinition still carries the FHIR-31778 distinction: formOf references the product as a whole, while producedFrom references the specific manufactured items from that product that are used for the administrable form. Later FHIR-41432 added an accepted one-to-one producedFrom clarification, which is compatible with and should not be removed for FHIR-31778. FHIR-31785 is a separate producedFrom-versus-ingredient issue already represented on the combined branch.
