FHIR-13338: Audit CareTeam group scope wording

Issue request:
FHIR-13338 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 Patient Care change request asking the CareTeam introduction and scope text to make clear that CareTeam is not limited to a singular patient and can also apply to a group, with examples such as married-couple therapy or a support group; the resolution also noted subject-less organizational/event teams and dynamic team membership.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied in git commit bd3f8a7ba5aba2347a6542d772cdc96897766a9c, whose subject includes "#13338 Update CareTeam intro" and whose patch changed source/careteam/careteam-introduction.xml to mention a single patient, a group, subject-less organizational teams, and dynamic/transient team membership. Later CareTeam source history retained the group semantics while refactoring the prose.

Additional context:
CareTeam is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current authoritative source says the CareTeam is assigned to a single patient, a group, or an event before a subject is identified. The current StructureDefinition also allows CareTeam.subject to reference Patient or Group, says it identifies the patient or group whose care is handled, and includes the later FHIR-14760/FHIR-28457 clarification to use Group for care provision to all group members. FHIR-20909 later refined the introduction into the current bullet list without reversing FHIR-13338. No directly relevant Zulip or Confluence record was found that conflicts with this Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This is an empty audit commit. No source change was made because the current build-scope CareTeam introduction and subject definition already communicate the requested patient-or-group scope and the related subject-less event/team case.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future CareTeam edits should preserve the current distinction between CareTeam assigned to a group and the Group resource itself, but FHIR-13338 needs no further core-source work.
