FHIR-39484: Audit date search fractional seconds

Issue request:
FHIR-39484 was Published/Persuasive for FHIR-I after R4 and asked the core search page to clarify that date search parameters can include fractional seconds. The approved change was to update the format from yyyy-mm-ddThh:mm:ss[Z|(+|-)hh:mm] to yyyy-mm-ddThh:mm:ss.ssss[Z|(+|-)hh:mm] and to state that fractional seconds may be ignored by servers.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source/search.html by f551573a484c66c7153bbe9454fbd6f019cbfa87 on 2023-03-19, which changed the date format and added that fractional seconds MAY be ignored by servers. Commit 92f80e9f0e6f135227f474311cbbd67ff007bfd7 later removed the temporary TU marker from the sentence, and a3766ac36c61fa3448d53aca0519dcbd874cfc93 later wrapped the normative date text in fhir-conformance markup without changing the fractional-second semantics.

Additional context:
The linked Zulip implementer thread confirmed that multiple implementations already accepted fractional seconds or ignored them safely. FHIR-I minutes on 2023-02-06 record continuing the Zulip feedback collection, and the 2023-02-27 minutes record the Persuasive 13-0-0 vote. A Jira search for other fractional-seconds issues found only FHIR-39484, and source/search.html is listed in publish.ini as a built page.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Current source/search.html already has the requested fractional-second format and says fractional seconds MAY be ignored by servers. The wording is not identical to the proposal, but it preserves the approved meaning and is now tagged as conformance text.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed unless a future ballot asks FHIR-I to change how servers handle fractional precision in date search values.
