FHIR-19411: Audit RelatedPerson relationship directionality

Issue request:
FHIR-19411 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core RelatedPerson change request from PA asking whether RelatedPerson.relationship is related-person-to-patient or patient-to-related-person. Its approved resolution changed the short and definition to clarify that the related person has the relationship with the patient, using a mother-of-patient example.

Initial application:
The request was applied in git commit 1188130b6c17ef475c055f86a5eb12a1a77f3525, which changed RelatedPerson.relationship from the ambiguous patient/related-person wording to "The nature of the relationship to the patient" and "The nature of the relationship that the related person has with the patient." A later direct follow-up for FHIR-26994, commit 3b17466cd4311c8b261c00e1796fe76c1f303784 plus typo commit 47ecaaf91a5ec157f4b45c3e094e11a966b26ed1, replaced that with the current explicit directionality comment.

Additional context:
RelatedPerson is active in source/fhir.ini. Current source/source generation uses source/relatedperson/structuredefinition-RelatedPerson.xml, where the short, definition, and comment state the personal relationship of the related person to the patient and explicitly say the directionality is from RelatedPerson to Patient. FHIR-26994 is the same clarification family and was voted later with the PRN/MTH example. FHIR-15841 later split personal relationships from functional roles and preserved the directionality text while adding RelatedPerson.role. FHIR-29929 is related to that split and is already represented on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active StructureDefinition already satisfies FHIR-19411 once the later FHIR-26994 and FHIR-15841 edits are considered.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. Do not add duplicate wording elsewhere unless a future work group decision changes the relationship/role model; the authoritative element definition already carries the needed directionality.
