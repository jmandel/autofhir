FHIR-5871: Align primitive time seconds wording

Issue request:
FHIR-5871 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for FHIR-core datatypes. It reported that the time and dateTime datatype descriptions implied seconds were optional while the regexes required seconds, and the accepted resolution was to update the descriptions in SVN revision 4536.

Initial application:
The historical application commit af82109a5f changed source/datatypes.html for both dateTime and time to say seconds must be provided due to schema type constraints, may be zero-filled, and may be ignored. The current datatype narrative still carries that rule, and source/datatypes/primitives.xml already carried the matching dateTime primitive description.

Additional context:
The current primitive time StructureDefinition source in source/datatypes/primitives.xml still only said "A time during the day, with no date specified" even though its regex requires hh:mm:ss. That spreadsheet source feeds the generated primitive StructureDefinitions exposed from datatypes-profiles.html and the time.profile artifacts, so it is in current build scope. FHIR-16948 later clarified who may ignore seconds; that related wording is handled separately.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the active primitive time source description to state the hh:mm:ss format and the FHIR-5871 required-seconds rule, aligning generated primitive profile wording with the normative datatype table and the regex.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It fills the remaining build-scope primitive-profile gap for the published FHIR-5871 decision without changing the datatype regex or broadening the later receiver-discretion clarification.
