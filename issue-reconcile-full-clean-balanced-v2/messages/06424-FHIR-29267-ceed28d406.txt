FHIR-29267: Audit Substance instance backbone removal

Issue request:
FHIR-29267 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to remove the old Substance.instance backbone element, move instance.expiry to Substance.expiry, move instance.quantity to Substance.quantity, and rely on Substance.identifier for the former instance identifier.

Initial application:
The change was initially applied in ce7d94f0984ae009254c17c60df0d81252aafac0, which explicitly cited FHIR#29267 and removed the nested instance data from Substance examples while moving expiry and quantity out from the instance backbone. During the R4B correction work, FHIR-31526 temporarily reverted Substance toward the R4 shape for R4B compatibility, but beb040d0608727a093902f91d4e3d6675f0c33f6 restored the intended R5 source model with Substance.expiry and Substance.quantity as root elements.

Additional context:
The O&O 2020-10-26 HCP minutes record the same motion and vote as Jira: remove the Substance.instance backbone, add Substance.expiry and Substance.quantity, and pick up instance identifiers through Substance.identifier. Later FHIR-48405 changed the current R6 direction further by removing the R5 boolean Substance.instance flag and declaring Substance to be instance-only; that later change reinforces that no Substance.instance element should be reintroduced for this issue.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Substance is active in source/fhir.ini, current source/substance/structuredefinition-Substance.xml has root-level Substance.expiry and Substance.quantity and no Substance.instance element, and source/substance/bundle-Substance-search-params.xml points the expiry and quantity search parameters at the root paths. The remaining Substance.instance strings in source/fhir.ini are R5/R6 migration notes for old paths, not current element definitions.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. FHIR-29267 is already satisfied in current source; future work for FHIR-48405 or other Substance-definition policy changes should be handled under their own issue keys rather than changing this reconciliation.
