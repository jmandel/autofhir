FHIR-7996: Audit search comparator support

Issue request:
FHIR-7996 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 FHIR-core search issue asking whether supported search comparators, or a minimum expected set, should be listed. Its final resolution said to revive the work after DSTU2 by adding comparator to SearchParameter, moving target/modifier/chain to SearchParameter, and documenting that detailed support belongs in referenced SearchParameter resources.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git-svn commit de92e40608f0e8b102ff69f3c478f8d531e708b4, "#7996 - move things from CapabilityStatement to SearchParameter", with svn r10356 matching the Jira application comment. That commit added SearchParameter.comparator, moved target/modifier/chain out of CapabilityStatement searchParam and into SearchParameter, and updated CapabilityStatement.searchParam.definition guidance.

Additional context:
The current SearchParameter resource is active in source/fhir.ini and still includes SearchParameter.target, SearchParameter.comparator, SearchParameter.modifier, and SearchParameter.chain. Current CapabilityStatement source says systems should document modifier, comparator, target, and chained-parameter support using SearchParameter resources. Current search.html also lists the comparator/prefix codes and their matching semantics. Later comparator/search-qualifier work such as FHIR-37622 and FHIR-25041 refines this area and is already represented on the combined branch.
