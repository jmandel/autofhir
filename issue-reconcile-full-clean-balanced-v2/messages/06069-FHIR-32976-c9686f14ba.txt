FHIR-32976: Clarify DeviceDefinition market availability authorization

Issue request:
FHIR-32976 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5 by OO. It requested market distribution details for DeviceDefinition device-model and packaging UDI identifiers, and the accepted resolution named marketDistribution with marketPeriod and subJurisdiction under .udiDeviceIdentifier and .packaging.udiDeviceIdentifier, with comments clarifying that a device marked available on the market is fully authorized for distribution without EUA.

Initial application:
The change family was applied through 2021 DeviceDefinition commits. Commit bf5e974e550ffec4b855db6376c97df2e028cb0c introduced the market availability backbone as marketStatus, commit e37cc34b4ef08a28de16dd759213c2f8748e32aa renamed it to marketDistribution and added packaging coverage, and commit ec664b4076c960b13429fbf4f7b37ea522d898d7 corrected the parent marketDistribution cardinality to 0..*. Later same-family commits f8cefe81c5c2e493801523aa01875296c7e79243 and 2a3c31605e1f057f27252ba2e95f5846008f13bd applied FHIR-33950 cardinalities and FHIR-34840 definition text. The current source retained the marketDistribution structure but did not include the FHIR-32976 authorization/EUA clarification.

Additional context:
DeviceDefinition is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/devicedefinition/structuredefinition-DeviceDefinition.xml is in current core build scope. Current packaging.udiDeviceIdentifier contentReferences #DeviceDefinition.udiDeviceIdentifier, so the same marketDistribution backbone applies to packaging without a separate duplicated subtree. OO Confluence minutes from 2021-07-02 show the market status wording still needed refinement after the proposed resolution, and later 2021-09-10 and 2021-10-08 minutes confirm the parent cardinality should be 0..* and that FHIR-33950 only complemented FHIR-32976 by changing marketPeriod/subJurisdiction to 1..1. Zulip and Confluence searches found no later community evidence reversing the requested EUA clarification.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit adds an ElementDefinition comment to DeviceDefinition.udiDeviceIdentifier.marketDistribution stating that a device is marked available on the market only when it is fully authorized for distribution without an Emergency Use Authorization (EUA). This preserves the already-correct element name, cardinalities, definition, and packaging contentReference while applying the missing semantic clarification from FHIR-32976.

Recommendation:
Keep this source-changing commit. No additional core change is needed for FHIR-32976 unless OO later chooses different terminology for emergency-use authorization status or decides to model that status as a coded element instead of guidance on marketDistribution.
