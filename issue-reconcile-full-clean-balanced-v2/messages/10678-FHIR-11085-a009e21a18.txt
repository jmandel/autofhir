FHIR-11085: Audit corrected _type search example

Issue request:
FHIR-11085 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core technical correction for the Search page, raised against DSTU2 section 2.42.1.4.20. It asked to change the example `GET [base]/Observation?_type=Observation,Condition&other params...` to a root-level search such as `GET [base]?_type=Observation,Condition&other params...`, because requesting both Observation and Condition under the Observation resource endpoint does not make sense.
