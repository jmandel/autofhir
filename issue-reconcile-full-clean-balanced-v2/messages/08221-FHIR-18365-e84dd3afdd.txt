FHIR-18365: Audit extension profiling wording

Issue request:
FHIR-18365 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 clarification asking the Defining Extensions page to say that profiles specify where particular extensions are "required or expected," not where they are merely "used," because profiles guide extension expectations rather than enabling extension use.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git commit 6006f22c21 ("GF#18365 - clarify wording"), which changed source/defining-extensions.html from "where particular extensions are used" to the accepted "required or expected" wording, though it initially introduced a duplicate "to." The later QA commit e763f3562d corrected that typo without changing the accepted meaning.

Additional context:
