FHIR-25458: Mark IG grouping trial-use

Issue request:
FHIR-25458 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked for support for multi-level organization of ImplementationGuide artifacts; the final direction was to avoid adding nested grouping in core, explore organizer resources such as Composition/List in tooling, keep ImplementationGuide.definition.grouping and resource.groupingId, and mark that grouping mechanism STU/trial-use while its future was evaluated.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly mentions FHIR-25458. Current core source still has the flat grouping and groupingId elements. In the R5-era source the whole ImplementationGuide resource was trial-use, so the grouping mechanism effectively had trial-use status, but a later R6 normative sweep changed the resource as a whole to normative without preserving trial-use status on grouping.

Additional context:
The 2021-03-08 FHIR-I minutes record the formal vote to mark the grouping attribute STU. The Zulip discussion and HL7/fhir-ig-publisher#647 show the Composition/List organizer work belongs in tooling/Tooling IG evolution rather than a new core Composition extension/profile in this checkout. FHIR-51435 later deferred changing groupingId to 0..* because tools rely on artifacts appearing in one group, so this reconciliation does not change cardinality or add new organizer machinery.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the active build-scope ImplementationGuide StructureDefinition source to add structuredefinition-standards-status=trial-use to ImplementationGuide.definition.grouping and ImplementationGuide.definition.resource.groupingId, and added the trial-use/evaluation note to the corresponding comments. No generated SVG or rendered output was edited.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It restores the FHIR-25458 trial-use signal after the R6 normative transition while leaving the broader complex artifact-organization work to IG Publisher/Tooling IG follow-up.
