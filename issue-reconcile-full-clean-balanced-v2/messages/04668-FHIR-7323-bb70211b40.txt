FHIR-7323: Audit Claim filing indicator support

Issue request:
FHIR-7323 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Claim change request from DSTU1 asking for a filing indicator such as L&I or MVA. The recorded resolution was that this had already been added in STU3 as Claim.information.

Initial application:
No git commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-7323 was found in the Claim source history. The relevant application appears to be the 2016 Claim WGM source update that added Claim.information, its InformationCategory binding, and accident/employment categories, followed by later Claim maintenance. The R4-era Claim update then renamed Claim.information to Claim.supportingInfo under FHIR-19460 while preserving the same function.

Additional context:
The current build includes Claim as an active resource, and source/fhir.ini records the historical rename Claim.information=Claim.supportingInfo. The current Claim definition has Claim.supportingInfo for exceptions, special considerations, conditions, situations, and prior or concurrent issues; its category definition explicitly includes accident and employment; its value and timing elements carry the supporting data; and Claim.item.informationSequence links item lines to supporting information. Community searches found no direct FHIR-7323 Zulip or Confluence record and no conflicting work-group evidence. A later Zulip hit discussed X12 claim filing indicator codes in a Coverage/product-line context, but it did not change the Jira/source conclusion for this Claim issue.
