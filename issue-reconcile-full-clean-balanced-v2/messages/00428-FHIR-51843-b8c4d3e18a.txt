FHIR-51843: Audit Transport period application

Issue request:
FHIR-51843 was an Applied/Persuasive OO change request for R6 asking to replace Transport.completionTime, a 0..1 dateTime completion timestamp, with Transport.period, a 0..1 Period that can carry the start and end time of the transport event. The accepted resolution also required the short display, definition, summary flag, and comment explaining ongoing transports should omit the Period end.

Initial application:

Commit 3c3e1928ce9d6891448313963cd7c1c8c6471871 then updated the Transport example to use period. Earlier Transport history, including commit 9ca054b3f79cbb741e241664b76d3a55baf40439 for FHIR-36275, explains why completionTime existed for FHIR-51843 to replace.

OO Confluence minutes on 2025-08-18 recorded the need to use the Event occurrence pattern and update transport.completionTime to transport.period, and the 2025-08-25 OO Specimen minutes record the exact accepted replacement with a 4-0-0 vote.
