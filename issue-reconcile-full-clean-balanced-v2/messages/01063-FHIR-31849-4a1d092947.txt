FHIR-31849: Audit ClinicalUseDefinition indication undesirable effect

Issue request:
FHIR-31849 was a Published/Persuasive BRR clarification for the R4 ClinicalUseIssue/ClinicalUseDefinition artifact asking what indication.undesireableEffect meant and why it was relevant to an indication. The recorded resolution was to improve documentation, with the later Jira comment giving the target definition: "An unwanted side effect or negative outcome that may happen if you use the drug (or other subject of this resource) for this indication".

Initial application:
No source-history commit was found that explicitly mentions FHIR-31849, but commit d99459f74a7b955aff3fd87bae9662f127aa2c49, made on 2022-02-01 shortly after the Jira comment, changed ClinicalUseDefinition.indication.undesirableEffect from the vague "specific undesirable effects" wording to the requested unwanted side-effect/negative-outcome wording. Its subject appears to cite FHIR#31850 while changing undesirableEffect, so the Jira key in the historical commit looks likely mislabelled. BRR Confluence minutes from 2022-02-10 separately record FHIR-31849 as "Change has been applied".

Additional context:
The old ClinicalUseIssue artifact is now represented in the current build as ClinicalUseDefinition. source/fhir.ini actively includes ClinicalUseDefinition as a resource, marks it normative, assigns the cud code, and marks it approved. A later FHIR-48631 refactor in commit cd2490a5c55b816536b503b0cf85ffeed54c8e30 reorganized ClinicalUseDefinition backbone references and preserved the clarified short and definition on ClinicalUseDefinition.indication.undesirableEffect. Zulip searches for FHIR-31849 and the element wording found no direct conflicting discussion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

The current source/clinicalusedefinition/structuredefinition-ClinicalUseDefinition.xml entry for ClinicalUseDefinition.indication.undesirableEffect already says that it is an unwanted side effect or negative outcome of the subject when used for this indication, and its definition exactly matches the Jira-provided wording with only a terminal period.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-31849 unless BRR wants to revisit the broader ClinicalUseDefinition model or rename historical ClinicalUseIssue references, which would be a separate decision.
