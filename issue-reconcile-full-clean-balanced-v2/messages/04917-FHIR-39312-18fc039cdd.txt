FHIR-32896 later resolved no-change because the repeatability concern for Observation.instantiates was overcome by the FHIR-43456 removal path. The combined branch already contains cbedd54f4f188091be23b91500a561c43c895e18 auditing FHIR-43456, so this reconciliation should not duplicate that issue.
