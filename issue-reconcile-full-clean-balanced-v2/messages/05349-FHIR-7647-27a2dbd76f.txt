FHIR-7647: Audit MedicationStatement dosage site resolution

Issue request:
FHIR-7647 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy issue from the DSTU1 cycle asking to rename MedicationStatement.dosage.site to administrationSite. The final resolution did not direct a rename; it said to make the element point to a coded concept or a reference to a resource for consistency across medication resources.

Initial application:
The historical application commit adae3b44 changed MedicationStatement.dosage.site to MedicationStatement.dosage.site[x] and changed its type from CodeableConcept to CodeableConcept | Reference(BodySite), with updated requirements saying the site could be coded or referenced. No evidence was found that the work group adopted the proposed administrationSite name.

Additional context:
Later dosage refactoring moved MedicationStatement dosing details out of a MedicationStatement-local site element and into reusable dosage structures. Current MedicationStatement.dosage is an active build-scope element of type DosageDetails, and the current reusable Dosage.site row is also active build-scope source. A later Applied issue, FHIR-54848, already represented on the combined branch, further updated Dosage.site to CodeableReference(BodyStructure) and reconciled the related guidance. Focused Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later core decision requiring an administrationSite rename.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current core source no longer has a MedicationStatement.dosage.site element to rename, and the active shared Dosage.site source already provides the coded-or-referenced body-site capability that matches the modified FHIR-7647 resolution after later refactoring and FHIR-54848.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as the reconciliation for FHIR-7647. Do not rename current Dosage.site or MedicationAdministration.dosage.site based on this DSTU1 MedicationStatement issue; such a naming change would exceed the final resolution and conflict with the shared dosage model unless a new Pharmacy decision explicitly requires it.
