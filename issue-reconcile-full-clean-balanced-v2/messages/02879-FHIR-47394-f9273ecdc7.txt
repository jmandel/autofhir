The final resolution changed the Task.doNotPerform comment to retain the qualifier list, fix the double-negation wording, and add guidance for Request-focused Tasks and ambiguity from combining doNotPerform with Task.code negation.

Initial application:

Additional context:

The October 2025 Zulip workflow thread and O&O minutes show discussion about whether the negation should live in Task.doNotPerform or Task.code; the work group adopted the clarification text rather than a model change.

Recommendation:
