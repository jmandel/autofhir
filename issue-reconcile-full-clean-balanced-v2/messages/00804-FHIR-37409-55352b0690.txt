FHIR-37409: Audit ImagingSelection coordinate scope

Issue request:
FHIR-37409 was a Published FHIR-core ImagingSelection change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R5, asking II to clarify how ImageRegion coordinates relate to Frame of Reference and selected instances. The accepted resolution was to apply the grouped ImagingSelection coordinate updates documented on the IMIN Confluence page for FHIR-36648, FHIR-36649, and FHIR-37409.

Initial application:
The grouped source application is visible in commit 5c5a620527e102650cad53df57ff594d1706fe2a and merge commit 471fbc468b8fd5427fcebad076569e16590ac707. That change split 2D image-region coordinates under ImagingSelection.instance, kept 3D image regions at the resource level, removed the coordinateType element, updated related examples and terminology, and added explanatory notes.

Additional context:
ImagingSelection is active in source/fhir.ini. Later R6/R6-ballot work refined the same resource without reversing the seed decision: FHIR-48101 added base invariants around seriesUid, instance, frameOfReferenceUid, imageRegion3D, and mutual exclusion of 2D/3D regions, and FHIR-51645 later adjusted coordinate element minimums back to 1 while retaining explicit multiple-of-two and multiple-of-three constraints. Other related ImagingSelection issues from the 2022 source commit are separate audits or separate terminology/cardinality questions and are not decided here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change is needed. The current build-scoped source already distinguishes instance.imageRegion2D in image pixel space from top-level imageRegion3D in the Frame of Reference coordinate system, requires imageRegion3D to have frameOfReferenceUid, makes 2D and 3D image regions mutually exclusive, and constrains coordinate value counts to multiples of two or three.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce coordinateType or duplicate the Confluence wording; the current source carries forward the approved split and later clarifications in the authoritative StructureDefinition and notes.
