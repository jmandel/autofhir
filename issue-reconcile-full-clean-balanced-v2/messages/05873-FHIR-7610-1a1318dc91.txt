FHIR-7610: Audit Patient managing organization cardinality

Issue request:
FHIR-7610 was Published with resolution Not Persuasive with Modification. The ballot comment asked whether Patient.managingOrganization should become 0..* for continuum-of-care scenarios or be left to Provenance; the accepted resolution kept the cardinality 0..1 and required the property comments to clarify that it identifies the owner/custodian responsible for maintaining the patient record.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in git commit 58cf0bc95a3fd91d0abd717978b7898f53cfc5a3, "Tracker# 7558, 7609, 7610 Ballot recon," which added the Patient spreadsheet comment explaining that there is only one managing organization for a specific patient record and that other organizations should maintain their own Patient records, optionally linked together. The later source refactor preserved this content in source/patient/structuredefinition-Patient.xml.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively includes patient=Patient in the current resources list, so source/patient/structuredefinition-Patient.xml is in current build scope. The current element keeps min 0 and max 1, describes the managing organization as the custodian of the patient record, and carries the one-managing-organization comment requested by the resolution. Later FHIR-16155 aligned the Patient organization search parameter with this custodian wording rather than reversing the FHIR-7610 interpretation. Direct community searches found no FHIR-7610-specific conflicting minutes or Zulip discussion; a 2020 LHS minutes item discussed a different Patient Role DAM concern and did not create a core Patient change.
