Issue request:
FHIR-26595 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification R5 Pharmacy change request asking the MedicationRequest Scope and Usage and related boundaries guidance to clarify when MedicationRequest is appropriate for products involving medication. The approved text allowed medication-related devices such as prefilled PCA, insulin, or narcotic syringes, and clarified that medication-coated devices such as heparin-coated stents are not MedicationRequest orders.
