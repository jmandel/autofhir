FHIR-25089 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR Infrastructure change request for the FHIR-core license page. It asked the license page to better convey the open spirit of FHIR's CC0 license by adding a Promoting interoperability paragraph below the Disclaimer and Warning of Use section.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by PR HL7/fhir#2417. A same-day follow-up, 6521f07bd26637710cf04cc8753d5e5e7c0d7e7b, moved the R5 ballot/TODO warning back under the disclaimer while retaining the new license language.
