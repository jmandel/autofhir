FHIR-15780: Audit Patient Care identifier wording

Issue request:
FHIR-15780 was a Published / Persuasive STU3 Patient Care issue asking editors to replace Patient Care resource identifier definitions, requirements, and comments with the global business-identifier wording from the broader identifier review. The requested definition says identifiers are business identifiers assigned to the resource by the performer or other systems and remain constant as the resource is updated and propagates between servers; the requested requirements and comment distinguish business identifiers from resource logical ids.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in the STU3 spreadsheet era through a small sequence of QA commits. Git commit fd64266c287a9376f6eb224be14ba8d75430a6b3 added the preferred wording to FamilyMemberHistory, Goal, and Procedure; commit 531a04bbdd2c80677236b0fb087c573811ff9fa0 added or normalized it for CarePlan, CareTeam, ClinicalImpression, Communication, and Flag; and commit d2a0f6d8fbd7eda090adf1b40f3066fefbfeeb1f explicitly mentioned GF#15780 while applying the same definition/requirements/comment consistency to AdverseEvent, AllergyIntolerance, CommunicationRequest, and Condition. Later source refactors migrated these spreadsheet definitions into the current StructureDefinition XML files.

Additional context:
FHIR-15780 was a resource-specific follow-up to the broader GF/FHIR-15095 identifier harmonization, which is already represented on the combined branch by an empty no-change audit commit. The current build scope in source/fhir.ini includes the affected Patient Care resources, with AdverseEvent also listed in the active resources section despite draft-resource metadata. Current source has small domain or grammar variations, such as Flag using author rather than performer and Procedure saying "is propagated", but these do not change the business-identifier semantics requested by the issue. Linkage has no identifier element, matching the Jira description rather than requiring an edit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit intentionally makes no source change. Current StructureDefinition XML for AdverseEvent, AllergyIntolerance, CarePlan, CareTeam, ClinicalImpression, Communication, CommunicationRequest, Condition, FamilyMemberHistory, Flag, Goal, and Procedure already describes identifier as a business identifier, includes the global comment distinguishing it from resource identifiers, and states the cross-system consistency requirement. Editing only to force exact wording would be editorial churn rather than a necessary current-spec correction.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. If Patient Care wants exact byte-for-byte wording across all identifier elements, or wants to revisit whether every resource still needs identifier, that should be a new harmonization/design issue; FHIR-15780 itself is satisfied in the current build.
