FHIR-26424: Audit Device property consolidation

Issue request:
FHIR-26424 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO change request asking what distinguished DeviceDefinition.capability from specialization and, in the final disposition, requiring .specialization, .capability, and .physicalCharacteristics to be removed or deprecated on Device and DeviceDefinition while consolidating the represented information into property with a 1..1 type and 1..1 value[x] choices.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in explicit FHIR-26424 commits 188021453f and 777921733c, which removed Device.specialization and DeviceDefinition.specialization/capability/physicalCharacteristics and expanded property value choices. Follow-up FHIR-32290 work later completed gaps by removing the remaining DeviceDefinition.physicalCharacteristics and correcting Device.property.value[x] and DeviceDefinition.property.value[x] cardinality to 1..1.

Additional context:
Device and DeviceDefinition are active resources in source/fhir.ini. Current source has no Device or DeviceDefinition element path named specialization, capability, or physicalCharacteristics; both property.value[x] elements are 1..1 and include Quantity, CodeableConcept, string, boolean, integer, Range, and Attachment. Later FHIR-36154 and subsequent source history reintroduced standard-conformance modeling and then renamed it to conformsTo, which is a later standard/specification-conformance element rather than an unremoved FHIR-26424 specialization/capability leftover.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Device and DeviceDefinition StructureDefinition XML already reflect the FHIR-26424 consolidation after the explicit issue commits and tightly related follow-up fixes.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core-source follow-up is needed for FHIR-26424; any future review of Device.conformsTo or DeviceDefinition.conformsTo should be handled under the later conformance/specialization issue family, not as a missed application of this seed.
