FHIR-25926: Audit AdverseEvent status clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-25926 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked where to represent the status/outcome of the adverse event itself, resolved that AdverseEvent.status is the correct element, and required the status definition to say "The current state of the adverse event or potential adverse event" with a comment clarifying that this is not regulatory/quality reporting and not the outcome of the patient's condition.

Initial application:

The 2021 source rework carried that text into source/adverseevent/structuredefinition-AdverseEvent.xml and source/adverseevent/adverseevent-event-mapping-exceptions.xml.

Additional context:
AdverseEvent is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/adverseevent/structuredefinition-AdverseEvent.xml is in current build scope. The current source still includes the FHIR-25926 definition and both required comment sentences. A later Applied issue, FHIR-36009, appended a clinical-trial clarification to the same comment; that addition does not reverse the FHIR-25926 distinction between event status, reporting status, and patient-condition outcome. The seed Jira comment mentioned discussion of an averted/near-miss status code, but that text was not part of the final FHIR-25926 resolution, and current AdverseEvent models actual versus potential events separately with AdverseEvent.actuality.
