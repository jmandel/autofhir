FHIR-13130: Audit Communication failed-state guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-13130 was a Published Patient Care change request for FHIR-core Communication, raised against DSTU2 and applied for DSTU2. It asked to clarify whether Communication may be used for planned, in-progress, or failed communications. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution updated the Scope and Usage text to say the resource is a record of a communication even if planned or failed, clarified the nurse-notification recipient, and added an inability-to-deliver-lab-results use case.

Initial application:
Commit bac396f21d6dc845c746e29a9fb7c79e27ee6ff5 applied the issue on 2017-05-11 by changing source/communication/communication-introduction.xml from a generic record-of-communication sentence to "even if it is planned or has failed," adding the on-call physician recipient to the nurse notification example, and adding the unable-to-deliver-lab-results example. Later Communication source edits for FHIR-17902 and FHIR-13389 retained the failed-delivery example while refining surrounding use-case wording.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists communication=Communication as an active resource, so source/communication/communication-introduction.xml and source/communication/structuredefinition-Communication.xml are current build inputs. Later Applied Communication issues changed how the timing sentence should read now: FHIR-28235, as applied by 40aa5c88300a803dc5d821194e74821ab60e2340 and reconciled on the combined branch by 5bba6cd7bce30a882d0b330b45cb073265ac2e9d, replaced the literal planned/failed phrase with "has occurred or where the communication process has at least initiated." FHIR-40760 and its reconciliation preserved the same later wording in Boundaries and Relationships. The current StructureDefinition still supports failed communications through Communication.status = not-done and statusReason for reasons a communication did not happen.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Communication narrative already includes the clarified recipient wording and the inability-to-deliver-lab-results example, and the current status/statusReason definitions support failed communications. Restoring the older literal "planned" wording would conflict with the later FHIR-28235 Patient Care decision that narrowed the sentence away from future communications not yet initiated.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core follow-up is needed for FHIR-13130 unless Patient Care opens a new issue to revisit planned, not-yet-initiated Communication scope after FHIR-28235.
