FHIR-24856: Audit FHIRPath Quantity mapping

Issue request:
FHIR-24856 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core clarification for the FHIRPath page. It asked to add FHIR.Quantity to the implicit conversion table as System.Quantity, state that the conversion only applies when the FHIR Quantity has a UCUM system/code, and document the mapping from UCUM time-valued units to FHIRPath calendar duration units.

Initial application:
The requested text was applied by ea082c4074 (GF#24856 - Add note about FHIRPath Quantity usage), which added the FHIR.Quantity table row, the UCUM-code precondition, the a/mo/d/h/min/s calendar-duration mapping, and the 1 'a' to 1 'year' example to source/fhirpath.html. Later FHIRPath page rewrites retained that content on the active page referenced by source/hierarchy.xml.

Additional context:
The upstream FHIRPath issue FHIR-21606 established the calendar-vs-UCUM duration distinction that FHIR-24856 mirrors. Later FHIRPath-specification issues continue to refine calendar-duration equality and conversion semantics, but they do not show that this FHIR-core Jira was missed or that the original FHIR-I approved mapping needs a source change in this reconciliation. Zulip discussion after the change confirms that non-UCUM-coded FHIR.Quantity values are not implicitly converted to System.Quantity and that the FHIR-specific mapping is understood as a special conversion rule.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active source/fhirpath.html already conveys the final FHIR-24856 rule: FHIR.Quantity maps to FHIRPath System.Quantity only with a UCUM code, and time-valued UCUM units are mapped to FHIRPath calendar-duration units with the example requested by the Jira.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If the work group wants the FHIR-core copy of the mapping to track every later FHIRPath calendar-duration unit such as week or millisecond, that should be handled as a separate explicit FHIR-core follow-up rather than inferred from this already-published clarification.
