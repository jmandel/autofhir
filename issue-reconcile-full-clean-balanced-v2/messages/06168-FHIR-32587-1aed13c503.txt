FHIR-32587: Audit DocumentReference indexing guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-32587 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked for implementation guidance about using DocumentReference as an index and resolved to add (1) a note that a DocumentReference can be temporarily out of sync when its referenced resource changes and needs coordination, and (2) a Scope and Usage sentence saying DocumentReference is typically used in document indexing systems such as IHE XDS and IHE MHD.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by the FHIR-32587 PR merge on 2021-11-19. The original merge used a wiki XDS link and had a DocumentReference spelling typo in the notes.
