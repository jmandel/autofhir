FHIR-11188: Audit Observation DiagnosticReport guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-11188 was a Published OO change request from the DSTU2 cycle, resolved Persuasive with Modification. It asked for the Observation scope guidance to avoid implying that Observation is equivalent to DiagnosticReport and, as finally resolved, to add a sentence that DiagnosticReport provides clinical/workflow context and references Observation to represent lab, imaging, and other diagnostic data as a complete report.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source by commit 9ae54e657c546e9a9e41f38013b06ff811f1b807 ([#11188] update Observation intro), which added the requested sentence to source/observation/observation-introduction.xml. A later whitespace-only commit d14c45dbc18d0226f1d98255488da9beaa2af054 followed, and commit fe6f87024bb28fc7e9742e5d8b8d336423d96c26 later changed the wording from "lab" to "laboratory" while preserving the DiagnosticReport guidance.

Additional context:
Observation and DiagnosticReport are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini. Current Observation source still includes the requested DiagnosticReport context sentence, retains laboratory and imaging examples, and separately states that laboratory, pathology, and imaging reports should be represented with DiagnosticReport while Observation provides atomic results. The paired DiagnosticReport page has matching boundary text. Later related work, including already-reconciled FHIR-5273 and FHIR-29032, retained this relationship while clarifying examples and broadening Observation's scope; 2021 and 2026 OO Confluence minutes quote or rely on the same sentence without a discovered decision to remove it.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The authoritative current source already conveys the accepted FHIR-11188 resolution, and adding the proposer's broader point-of-care restriction would exceed the final Jira disposition and conflict with later broader Observation scope guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR core follow-up is needed for FHIR-11188; any future changes to laboratory panel grouping or DiagnosticReport-vs-Observation modeling should be handled through the newer dedicated grouping issues rather than reopening this applied clarification.
