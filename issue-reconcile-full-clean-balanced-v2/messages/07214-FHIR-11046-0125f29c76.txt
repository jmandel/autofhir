FHIR-11046: Audit BodyStructure standalone resource disposition

Issue request:
FHIR-11046 was Published with a Not Persuasive with Modification resolution for the DSTU2 BodySite request to deprecate or remove BodySite as a stand-alone resource with a mandatory patient reference. The final redisposition kept the resource concept and required removal of the DSTU#3/STU note asking whether BodySite should remain independent.

Initial application:
Commit 87a8e5f9c2ea21688115533d316c694f979a8ac1, "[#10586] and preapply [#11046]", removed the BodySite dstu-note block from source/bodysite/bodysite-introduction.xml and kept the narrative stating that BodySite supports recording and tracking an anatomic location or structure outside the context of another resource. That commit also carried the unrelated FHIR-10586 diagnostics roadmap typo fix.

Additional context:
FHIR-7577 is the earlier common-structure/no-change ballot item cited by the original FHIR-11046 disposition. FHIR-13396 later renamed BodySite to BodyStructure and moved the source into source/bodystructure; the combined branch already contains an audit commit for that rename. Current fhir.ini actively builds bodystructure=BodyStructure and lists BodySite under removed resources. Current BodyStructure.patient is optional (0..1), so the original "mandatory patient" objection is no longer present in the current StructureDefinition. Later R6 BodyStructure text work, including FHIR-54843, continues to treat BodyStructure instances as appropriate when anatomical structures need detail, persistence, reuse, or tracking, but that issue is not final Applied/Published scope for this seed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scope BodyStructure narrative and examples already preserve the work group rationale for retaining an independent resource: tracking an anatomic location/structure outside a single resource, including fetus and clinical-trial skin-patch examples. The obsolete DSTU#3 note is absent from the current BodyStructure source, and BodySite itself is a removed resource rather than an active source target.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Do not remove BodyStructure or add redundant explanatory prose for FHIR-11046; future edits should be handled through current BodyStructure issues such as the R6 bodyStructure/bodySite harmonization tickets rather than reopening this DSTU2 disposition.
