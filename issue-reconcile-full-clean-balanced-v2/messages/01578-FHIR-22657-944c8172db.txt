FHIR-22657: Restore MoneyQuantity as Quantity constraint

Issue request:
FHIR-22657 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification for the FHIR-core datatypes page. The issue asked whether Money should be treated as a Quantity constraint or datatype; the accepted resolution clarified that the constraint is MoneyQuantity, not Money, and directed the Quantity variations text to say there are two Quantity constraints and to add MoneyQuantity to the constraints table.

Initial application:
Commit ec83d5b5057f1402bdeb77453f5d214806de6b8c applied FHIR-22657 alongside other MnM non-substantive R5 changes by changing the Quantity constraints text to plural, adding MoneyQuantity to that table, and removing the duplicate MoneyQuantity table from the Money section.

Additional context:
The next-day RatioRange datatype commit b9fd58139d35c8aa90083ae99a279c8a6d3dd854 unintentionally reverted the FHIR-22657 datatype-page hunk, restoring the singular "one constraint" text and moving the MoneyQuantity formal-definition row back under Money. Current source/fhir.ini still lists Money as an active datatype and MoneyQuantity as a Quantity-based type/profile, so the open type list should continue to include Money while the Quantity variations section should list MoneyQuantity as the second constraint.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit restores the FHIR-22657 source placement in source/datatypes.html: the Quantity constraints text now says there are two constraints, the table immediately below includes MoneyQuantity, the explanatory note is pluralized, and the Money section now refers to the MoneyQuantity constraint without duplicating its formal-definition table.

Recommendation:
Keep this source-changing commit as-is. It restores the work-group-approved FHIR-22657 change in the active datatype source and avoids changing the open type semantics because the Jira comment and current configuration distinguish the Money datatype from the MoneyQuantity constraint.
