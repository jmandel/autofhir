FHIR-13566: Audit ActivityDefinition useContext searches

Issue request:
FHIR-13566 was a Published CDS change request for STU3 asking that ActivityDefinition support searching/filtering by useContext. Its Not Persuasive with Modification resolution requested multi-parameter useContext searching, with examples for gender and age range, and also called out Library and Measure.

Initial application:
The request was applied in SVN revision 12616, imported as Git commit bc0e408c80a3d8ff7d558e023496ecffd4d76e7b, together with FHIR-13565. That commit added the MetadataResource useContext search-parameter definitions in the legacy spreadsheet source; the Jira comment records context-quantity, context-type, context-type-value, and context-type-quantity as added there.

Additional context:
FHIR-17245 later noted that the MetadataResource parameters were initially not inherited consistently by resources such as ActivityDefinition and PlanDefinition, and its published resolution was to ensure the inherited parameters were applied consistently. The current build no longer relies on the legacy spreadsheet layout for this check: ActivityDefinition, PlanDefinition, Library, and Measure are active resources in source/fhir.ini and each has an active bundle-*-search-params.xml containing context, context-quantity, context-type, context-type-value, and context-type-quantity search parameters over useContext.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The active ActivityDefinition search-parameter bundle already includes the token, quantity, and composite useContext searches requested by FHIR-13566, and the companion resources named by the resolution have matching current search parameters.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-13566 in the core FHIR checkout unless a future issue changes the current useContext search-parameter model.
