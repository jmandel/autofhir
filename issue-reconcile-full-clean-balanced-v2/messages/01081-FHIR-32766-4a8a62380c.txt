FHIR-32766: Audit CodeableReference binding scope

Issue request:
FHIR-32766 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and reported that CodeableReference elements, specifically Ingredient.item, were missing example value-set bindings. The final resolution required an example binding for Ingredient.item to SNOMED CT Medication codes and also noted MedicationKnowledge IndicationGuideline.indication.

Initial application:
The Jira-tagged commit 3eb5717dff added the medication-codes example binding to Medication.ingredient.item, and that binding remains present in the active Medication source. MedicationKnowledge.indicationGuideline.indication never received a binding, but the current build no longer includes MedicationKnowledge as an active resource.

Additional context:
Pharmacy WGM minutes for May 2021 confirm the FHIR-32766 vote, the Ingredient.item binding rationale, and that MedicationKnowledge was the other resource to address. Later source history removed MedicationKnowledge from active resource build scope in commit 5a4307d78b, deleting its active [resources], abbreviation, approval, and mappings entries from source/fhir.ini. The stale source/medicationknowledge folder still exists, but source/fhir.ini is the build-scope authority for this reconciliation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
