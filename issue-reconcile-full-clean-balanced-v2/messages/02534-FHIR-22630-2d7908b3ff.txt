FHIR-22630: Audit Endpoint recipients for Communication

Issue request:
FHIR-22630 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Patient Care change request raised against STU3 and applied for R5. It asked that CommunicationRequest allow Endpoint as a recipient; the final resolution broadened the change so both Communication and CommunicationRequest allow Endpoint as recipient and as sender/informationProvider.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied by bacd75ef4fb5fe08e2b16d04e3fd5bfd278fc916, which added Endpoint to the then-authoritative Communication and CommunicationRequest spreadsheet target-type lists for Communication.recipient, Communication.sender, CommunicationRequest.recipient, and CommunicationRequest.informationProvider, and added GF#22630 committee notes. Later source refactoring removed those spreadsheet files and preserved the change in the current StructureDefinition XML sources.

Additional context:
Communication and CommunicationRequest are active, approved resources in source/fhir.ini, so their source folders are in current build scope. Current StructureDefinition source already includes Endpoint targetProfile entries and GF#22630 notes on all four affected elements. Current Communication and CommunicationRequest notes also list Endpoint in the sender/informationProvider and recipient target-type summaries after the already-published FHIR-22631 notes-alignment commit. Confluence minutes confirm Patient Care recorded GF#22630 as resolved in June 2019; later PA/CDEX minutes cite CommunicationRequest.recipient Endpoint use, while participant-pattern challenge notes did not lead to a discovered reversal of the published Jira decision or current source.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current source already conveys the final FHIR-22630 rule in the authoritative element definitions and the active notes pages, and adding more prose would be redundant rather than corrective.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. No core follow-up is recommended for FHIR-22630 unless a future work-group decision reopens the broader participant-pattern question of whether Endpoint should be a recipient target.
