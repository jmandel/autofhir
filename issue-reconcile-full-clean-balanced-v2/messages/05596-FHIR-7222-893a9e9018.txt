FHIR-7222: Audit patient-specific resource clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-7222 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Patient Care change request raised against DSTU1. It asked which of ClinicalImpression, Contraindication, QuestionnaireAnswers, CarePlan, Goal, RiskAssessment, and Alert were master files or knowledge bases versus patient-specific data, argued that ClinicalImpression and RiskAssessment were problematic, and requested clearer descriptions. The approved resolution said the listed resources include patient or subject data, are not master files or definitions of what can exist, and are specific records for a particular patient or subject; it also kept ClinicalImpression and RiskAssessment draft for DSTU2 review.

Initial application:
No current-source commit subject explicitly names FHIR-7222, but the Jira comment records SVN #6766. The corresponding git-svn commit d6c4981fa8fe7d58cc3e1cc393aa8cc0b6a83c54 added or preserved the key CarePlan, Goal, and QuestionnaireResponse introduction clarifications: CarePlan as a specific plan instance rather than a generic plan/protocol definition, Goal as a specific goal instance rather than potential goals in order sets or protocols, and QuestionnaireResponse as a response generated for a different subject or time. Neighboring source history also shows Contraindication renamed to DetectedIssue after its patient-specific scope text was added, and RiskAssessment/ClinicalImpression already had patient- or population-oriented scope text before the final SVN application.

Additional context:
The current fhir.ini [resources] list actively includes Flag, CarePlan, DetectedIssue, Goal, QuestionnaireResponse, and RiskAssessment; ClinicalImpression source files remain in the tree but ClinicalImpression is not an active current-build resource. Later applied decisions and refactors changed some of the old DSTU1 targets without reversing FHIR-7222's central distinction: Contraindication is now DetectedIssue, QuestionnaireAnswers is now QuestionnaireResponse, Alert is now Flag, CarePlan and Goal subject handling has been audited separately by related reconcile commits, and current Goal/CarePlan/DetectedIssue text explicitly directs generic protocols, order sets, and patient-independent knowledge to other artifacts such as PlanDefinition or ClinicalUseDefinition. Flag has been broadened to non-patient subjects by later work, but it still requires a concrete subject and is not a master-definition artifact.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active current source already tells implementers that the relevant resources are concrete subject- or context-specific records, not master files or reusable definitions, and adding another cross-resource restatement would be redundant. Editing the inactive ClinicalImpression folder would not change the current build.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. No FHIR core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-7222 unless Patient Care reopens the current boundaries of one of the successor resources; if that happens, make the change under the successor issue rather than retrofitting this DSTU1 clarification.
