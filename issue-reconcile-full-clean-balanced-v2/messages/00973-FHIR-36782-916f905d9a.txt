FHIR-36782: Audit ImagingSelection.subject wording

Issue request:
FHIR-36782 was a Published/Persuasive R5 Imaging Integration clarification asking to update ImagingSelection.subject. The Jira description requested short text "Subject of the selected instances" and a broader subject definition; the final resolution clarified the short display by removing the earlier "and / or frames" wording.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by the June 2022 Imaging changes merged as c138fb1b9ed930251866ab1da3be7d524b3fb1d4. The relevant PR commit e89adf0784a54c08ffd911a3263cf2c60ef430cb changed ImagingSelection.subject short from "Subject of the selected instances and / or frames" to "Subject of the selected instances" while preserving the broader subject definition.

Additional context:
ImagingSelection is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. A later Applied/Persuasive issue, FHIR-51645, deliberately cleaned up ImagingSelection detailed definitions to align with current ImagingStudy text. Its Confluence resolution page and implementation commit a5eef8f1cfc87af8a050ad1ae796cebd7abd78fb changed ImagingSelection.subject to the current short "Who or what is the subject of the imaging selection" and definition "The subject, typically a patient, of the imaging selection." This later wording supersedes the literal 2022 short text while retaining the key FHIR-36782 correction that the subject is not the selected frames.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already avoids the pre-FHIR-36782 "and / or frames" subject wording, and changing it back to the exact 2022 text would undo the later approved FHIR-51645 cleanup.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-36782 unless the Imaging Integration work group separately reopens the later FHIR-51645 wording.
