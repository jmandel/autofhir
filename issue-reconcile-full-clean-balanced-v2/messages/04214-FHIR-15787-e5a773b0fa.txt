Issue request:
FHIR-15787 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification PA change request, raised in STU3 and applied for R5, asking to add a qualification subclass at the Organization resource level like Practitioner.qualification. The final resolution also asked to broaden the Practitioner qualification wording and to add an Organization certification/qualification backbone covering official certifications, accreditations, training, designations, and licenses that authorize or endorse care provision by an organization.
