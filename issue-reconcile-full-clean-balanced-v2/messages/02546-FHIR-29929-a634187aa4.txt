FHIR-29929: Audit RelatedPerson relationship split

Issue request:
FHIR-29929 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It requested changing RelatedPerson.relationship into a BackboneElement with role, relation, and period fields because systems needed to distinguish functional roles such as emergency contact or guarantor from personal relations such as mother or spouse. The accepted modified resolution said FHIR-15841 added and standardized role and relationship in both Patient.contact and RelatedPerson, and said role-specific periods can be represented with multiple RelatedPerson instances.

Initial application:
No source commit whose subject or body uses FHIR-29929 was found beyond the adjacent FHIR-26994 directionality commit 3b17466cd4311c8b261c00e1796fe76c1f303784, whose body says it also covered FHIR-29929. The substantive relationship/role split referenced by the Jira resolution was later applied by commit 23f3b0abec5678767e3cc7e4b576fdbd3e3b9fb2 for FHIR-15841, adding Patient.contact.role and RelatedPerson.role and changing Patient.contact.relationship and RelatedPerson.relationship to personal-relationship semantics.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists patient=Patient and relatedperson=RelatedPerson, so the inspected StructureDefinition files are in current build scope. Current RelatedPerson source keeps relationship as a repeatable personal relationship CodeableConcept, adds RelatedPerson.role for functional roles, and keeps RelatedPerson.period as the period for the RelatedPerson instance. Current Patient.contact source mirrors the same relationship/role distinction. PA Confluence minutes for the 2021 September WGM record FHIR-29929 as handled through FHIR-15841, and existing combined-branch audits for FHIR-31670 and FHIR-32230 also treat FHIR-15841 as the later governing relationship/role split.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already conveys the final modified decision: do not embed role, relation, and period inside RelatedPerson.relationship; instead use separate personal relationship and functional role elements, and use separate RelatedPerson instances when different periods are needed.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. If PA later wants role-specific periods on individual role codings, that should be a new modeling decision rather than a reinterpretation of FHIR-29929, because the published resolution explicitly accepted multiple RelatedPerson instances for that case.
