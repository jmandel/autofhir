FHIR-26684: Audit Questionnaire required clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-26684 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Questionnaire clarification for R5 asking what Questionnaire.item.required means for omitted values, required groups, and required children under optional ancestors. The accepted resolution was to replace the vague "generally assumed" meaning-when-missing wording, note that required groups need at least one answered child/descendant while not making all children automatically required, and state that child required items matter only when the parent item is present in the QuestionnaireResponse.

Initial application:
The Jira application comment cites commit 3d82dea189a1065b0bba9be644260a7b4e2ae9cc. That commit changed the active Questionnaire StructureDefinition source by replacing the meaningWhenMissing text with "Items are assumed not to be required unless explicitly specified, though extensions may impose additional expectations" and adding parent-present/non-required-group guidance to Questionnaire.item.required.

Additional context:
Questionnaire is an active core resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/questionnaire/structuredefinition-Questionnaire.xml is in current build scope. Later Published/Persuasive same-element clarifications FHIR-29311 and FHIR-32151 refined the surrounding wording without reversing FHIR-26684: current source now explicitly says required questions need a direct answer, required groups need an answered descendant question, required only has meaning when the parent item is present, and a required container does not automatically make descendants required. The 2020 FHIR SDC minutes record FHIR-26684 as Persuasive, and relevant Questionnaire Zulip threads support the same contextual interpretation of required.
