FHIR-43508: Audit subscription topic registry guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-43508 was an Applied FHIR-core change request with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked the Subscriptions Framework to clarify registry.fhir.org SubscriptionTopic discovery because the page strongly suggested reusing existing topics while the registry had few canonical topics; the accepted resolution was to note that topic definition is still in early adoption and that more topics will appear as IGs are published. Jira comments also asked the editor to avoid implying only IG authors can contribute topics.

Later commit fc659be95edf49f9c1bcc4dfb2f9a631ea45e06a reformatted and broadened subscription terminology text while preserving the FHIR-43508 registry guidance.
