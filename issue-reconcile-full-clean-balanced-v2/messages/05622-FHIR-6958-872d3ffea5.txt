FHIR-6958: Audit FamilyMemberHistory family-history List guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-6958 was a Published Patient Care change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking how to represent maternal or paternal family history when the specific family member is unknown and the HL7 v3 FAMMEMB value set does not contain an exact relationship code. The accepted resolution was to add usage notes explaining that a full family history can be represented as a List containing both FamilyMemberHistory records and Observation records such as "maternal family history of breast cancer".

Later edits corrected and expanded the paragraph, but preserved the FHIR-6958 guidance.
