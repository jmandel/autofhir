FHIR-13782: Audit snapshot-only eld-10 application

Issue request:
FHIR-13782 was Published with resolution Persuasive. It said the old ElementDefinition.binding eld-10 invariant, "provide either a reference or a description (or both)", was intended only for snapshots but also affected differentials, preventing a differential from raising only binding.strength. The accepted resolution was to make the invariant apply only to snapshot.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records svn revision 12818. In the git-svn history, commit f57d0e635ba214885a60127ad55ad9b1f0b7f7f5 implements GF#13782 by removing invariant 10 from source/datatypes/elementdefinition.xml and removing its conditions from ElementDefinition.binding.description and ElementDefinition.binding.valueSet. Later source rework carries the binding-target rule as StructureDefinition-specific sdf-10 on StructureDefinition.snapshot.element rather than on StructureDefinition.differential.element.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini keeps ElementDefinition as an active normative datatype and StructureDefinition as an active resource, so these are current build-scope sources. Current source/structuredefinition/structuredefinition-StructureDefinition.xml has sdf-10 only under StructureDefinition.snapshot.element, while StructureDefinition.differential.element has no matching resource-level condition or constraint. Current source/datatypes/elementdefinition.xml does contain ElementDefinition.binding invariant 23, but that was added later by FHIR-26892 as a separate R5 normative binding-target decision; FHIR-26892, FHIR-22712, and FHIR-48325 are already represented on the combined branch and should not be duplicated here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The original FHIR-13782 defect is not present in current StructureDefinition source: the resource-level binding target rule is snapshot-scoped, not differential-scoped. Removing or narrowing the later ElementDefinition.binding invariant 23 would reverse FHIR-26892 rather than complete FHIR-13782.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If the work group wants the later eld-23 datatype invariant to stop applying to differential ElementDefinitions, raise or apply a new focused FHIR-I issue; do not use FHIR-13782 to undo the later published FHIR-26892 decision.
