Issue request:
FHIR-23935 was a Published/Persuasive PA change request for R5 asking the PractitionerRole narrative to be explicit that an organization can define a role without a pre-allocated practitioner, such as an unnamed surgeon at a hospital. It also asked Boundaries and Relationships to clarify that clinical or other content should reference PractitionerRole, rather than Practitioner directly, when organization and role are important to the reference context.
