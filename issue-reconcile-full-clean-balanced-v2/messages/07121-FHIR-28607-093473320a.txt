FHIR-28607: Audit MustSupport hierarchy guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-28607 is a Published FHIR-core R5 change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification. It asked the profiling page's Must Support guidance to clarify two hierarchy cases: when a child element is mustSupport but its parent is not, support for the child is required only if the system supports the parent; and when an IG marks a complex element mustSupport without marking any children, the IG should provide guidance, otherwise implementers are expected to support at least a subset of the must-support parent's child elements.

Initial application:
Upstream commit d1251ef4a0028fd12949632bf3c2aaa2c54a24b7, later merged by PR #1741 as c4432fdb674f4cc9bf651a8caba92b69b18b767b, added the two requested paragraphs to source/profiling.html. Subsequent profiling-page work changed "MS parent" to "must-support parent" and later same-section cleanup retained a corrected child-element sentence while removing the older duplicate with the "if it support the parent" typo.
