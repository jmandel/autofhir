FHIR-7129: Audit Procedure device clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-7129 was a Published Patient Care FHIR-core Procedure change request, resolved Persuasive for DSTU1. It said the old Procedure.device wording, "A device change during the procedure," was too vague because it did not distinguish implanted/manipulated devices from equipment set up for or used during surgery; the Jira resolution says this was addressed by FHIR-8058.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in historical commit 512b2462e3, which explicitly listed #7129 and #8058. That commit renamed Procedure.device to Procedure.focalDevice, replaced the vague definition with focal-device wording for devices that are implanted, removed, or otherwise manipulated, and added Procedure.used notes distinguishing incidental devices from devices that are the focus of the procedure.

Additional context:
Procedure is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. The current source uses Procedure.focalDevice rather than the old Procedure.device name, and source/procedure/structuredefinition-Procedure.xml now defines Procedure.focalDevice as the manipulated, implanted, or removed focal device while Procedure.used points implanted or removed devices to Procedure.focalDevice.manipulated. The active Procedure notes preserve the incidental-device versus focus-of-procedure distinction. FHIR-8058, FHIR-13976, and FHIR-34525 are already represented on the combined branch and confirm the same current expression of this issue family. Targeted Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-7129-specific discussion or conflicting later decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scope Procedure source already resolves the ambiguity raised by FHIR-7129 through the renamed Procedure.focalDevice element and associated Procedure.used guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up core source change is needed for FHIR-7129 unless the work group separately reopens the Procedure.used versus Procedure.focalDevice boundary.
