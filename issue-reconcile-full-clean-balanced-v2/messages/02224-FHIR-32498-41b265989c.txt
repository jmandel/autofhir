FHIR-32498: Audit allDay 24-hour wording

Issue request:
FHIR-32498 is Published/Persuasive for R5 and asks whether allDay can mean something other than 24-hour availability. Despite the confusing title/description wording, the accepted resolution says the short and long descriptions for allDay should use "i.e. 24 hour service" rather than "e.g. 24 hour service", and that HealthcareService and Location should be made consistent with PractitionerRole.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation in separate Jira-tagged commits: c1be0e746c updated PractitionerRole.availableTime.allDay, b2d93d1bec updated HealthcareService.availableTime.allDay, and d6f3dcdcee updated Location.hoursOfOperation.allDay. A later refactor, 447e89f4a4, moved these availability fields into the shared Availability datatype and preserved the "i.e. 24 hour service" wording there.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively includes the Availability datatype and the Location, PractitionerRole, and HealthcareService resources, and it records the R5 mappings from the old resource-specific allDay paths to the shared Availability paths. The current resource StructureDefinitions point to type Availability rather than carrying separate allDay definitions. PA Confluence minutes for the January 2022 WGM record the 7-0-0 vote, note the title/description confusion, and explicitly say the change should also apply to HealthcareService and Location. Zulip searches for FHIR-32498 and the allDay/resource terms found no relevant conflicting discussion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scope source has the authoritative allDay short and long text in source/datatypes/availability.xml as "Always available? i.e. 24 hour service" and "Is this always available? (hence times are irrelevant) i.e. 24 hour service.", and no current source definition under source/ still uses the rejected "e.g. 24 hour service" wording for allDay.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source edit is needed in core unless a future work group decision changes the meaning of allDay or reverses the shared Availability datatype wording.
