FHIR-14755: Audit Patient sex and gender lab guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-14755 was a Published/Not Persuasive with Modification STU3 Patient issue. It asked for laboratory-relevant biological sex/gender handling, including proposed LOINC mappings for 76689-9 Sex assigned at birth and 76691-5 Gender identity, when Administrative Gender is not clinically reliable. The final disposition was to clarify and extend the Patient Gender section, refer to FHIR-14756 for the sex-assigned-at-birth LOINC request, and note that relevant sex/gender observations could be included on the lab order through ServiceRequest.supportingInfo.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly naming FHIR-14755 was found. The contemporaneous Patient sex/gender issue family was applied through nearby commits: #14482 rewrote the Patient Gender and Sex section, b7d0f473c3 applied #14754/#14719 by improving clinical gender Observation guidance with LOINC 76691-5, and 42c4df50f2 applied FHIR-14756 by adding LOINC 76689-9 Observation guidance for sex assigned at birth. The Patient resource is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/patient/patient-notes.xml and source/patient/structuredefinition-Patient.xml are in current build scope.

Additional context:
Later Patient Administration/Gender Harmony work changed the representation model. FHIR-29673 introduced Sex for Clinical Use/Sex Parameter for Clinical Use and Recorded Sex or Gender guidance, while FHIR-39203 moved exchange recommendations out of the FHIR base Patient narrative and into the Gender Harmony IG. The current Patient Gender and Sex section says Patient.gender is administrative, directs clinical sex-characteristic observations to Observation with clinical codes, points Gender Identity/SPCU/RSG/SAAB concepts to the standard extensions or Gender Harmony IG, and warns that sex assigned at birth can be a poor proxy for current clinical sex. ServiceRequest.supportingInfo is still defined for additional clinical information and observations needed for laboratory orders. FHIR-14756 is already represented on the combined branch by audit commit a202416e13.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Restoring the old direct LOINC mapping text or adding new lab-specific Patient prose would duplicate or partially reverse later Gender Harmony decisions. The current build already conveys that Patient.gender is not clinically authoritative, that sex/gender clinical observations belong in Observation or related extensions/IG guidance, and that lab orders can carry relevant observations as ServiceRequest.supportingInfo.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If PA or Gender Harmony wants direct base-spec LOINC mapping language for laboratory biological sex again, that should be a new work-group decision coordinated with the Gender Harmony IG and extension-pack content rather than a replay of this STU3 tracker.
