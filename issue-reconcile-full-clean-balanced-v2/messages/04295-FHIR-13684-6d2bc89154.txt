FHIR-13684: Audit date regex year-zero correction

Issue request:
FHIR-13684 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 FHIR-core datatypes issue reporting that the informative regular expressions for date and dateTime allowed year 0000 and day 00 examples such as 0000-01-00. The accepted resolution was to replace the four-digit year component with a non-zero-year expression in date and in the leading date portion of dateTime, and to align validator behavior.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git-svn commit f2b3f28b2ff336fda895e176b2bdb2690ade688b, whose subject explicitly says "#13685 and #13684: validation improvements to date/time regex (leap seconds, year 0)" and whose patch replaced `-?[0-9]{4}` with the current non-zero-year expression for both date and dateTime. The Jira comment records svn revision 12460, matching that commit.

Additional context:
The current build-scope primitive datatype source is source/datatypes/primitives.xml. source/fhir.ini routes primitive datatype pages to datatypes, and source/datatypes.html renders the date and dateTime regexes with [%regex date%] and [%regex dateTime%]. The historical release files show the old STU3-style `-?[0-9]{4}` regex in release3 and the corrected non-zero-year expression in release4 and later. Later related issue FHIR-52866 asked to simplify this exact year expression with a negative look-ahead, but was resolved No Change because FHIR limits these regexes to widely supported constructs.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current date and dateTime primitive regexes already use the non-zero-year expression requested by FHIR-13684 and reject 0000, 0000-01, 0000-01-00, and 9999-12-00 while accepting ordinary partial and full dates. The current wording that dates SHALL be valid dates remains the normative guardrail for calendar validity beyond the regex.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core source edit is needed for FHIR-13684; future changes to readability or cross-language regex style should proceed under a new current-version tracker or the already resolved FHIR-52866 discussion rather than reopening the STU3 year-zero correction.
