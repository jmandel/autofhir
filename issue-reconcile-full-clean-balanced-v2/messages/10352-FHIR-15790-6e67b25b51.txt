Issue request:
FHIR-15790 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core STU3 change request reporting that http://hl7.org/fhir/STU3/igpack.zip was missing hierarchy-table PNG images such as tbl_vjoin_end_slice.png, tbl_vjoin_slice.png, and tbl_vline_slice.png when used by the 3.2 publisher. The Jira resolution was auto-approved, and the reporter later commented that the issue could no longer be reproduced.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly mentions FHIR-15790. Source history shows the relevant hierarchy rendering work immediately before the report: a17c22c704 changed the table generator and publisher plumbing to track hierarchy image outputs, and 8741324447 added the missing slice/slicer hierarchy PNG files under images/. The missing piece described by the issue was publication/deployment of the STU3 IGPack, not a remaining current source definition change.

Additional context:
The Zulip thread linked from Jira confirms the diagnosis: the build.fhir.org IGPack already had the images, but the STU3 IGPack at hl7.org/fhir/STU3/igpack.zip had not yet been updated. Current source still tracks the reported images, and a direct check of the current STU3 igpack.zip finds each reported PNG. Confluence refs and related Jira search found no conflicting or broader source decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
