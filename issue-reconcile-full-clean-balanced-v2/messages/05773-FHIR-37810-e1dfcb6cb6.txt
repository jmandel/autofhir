FHIR-37810 is a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core clarification for FHIR-core-BaseResource, applied for R5, asking that Resource and likely the other abstract resources have the same hyperlinks to StructureDefinition files as concrete resources such as Patient.

Initial application:
