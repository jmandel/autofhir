It asked to replace the flat SpecimenDefinition container* elements with a single 0..1 container BackboneElement containing material, type, cap, description, capacity, minimumVolume, and additive; the Jira comment confirmed that material belongs under the same container backbone.

FHIR-14746 is the same January 2018 SpecimenDefinition family and explains why the final path is typeTested.container rather than specimenToLab.container. Later Published issues under the same container area, including FHIR-19422, FHIR-19425, FHIR-19423, and FHIR-39590, adjusted child bindings or types without reversing the backbone grouping; those issues are already represented separately on the combined branch where applicable. Later Zulip and Confluence discussions considered whether SpecimenDefinition.container might eventually be replaced by DeviceDefinition, but no final current-core Jira decision requiring that replacement was found.
