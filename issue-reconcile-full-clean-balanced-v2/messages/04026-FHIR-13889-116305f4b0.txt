The current source preserves the seed's cardinality and type changes while reflecting the later binding-strength decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not change AdverseEvent.category back to extensible unless Patient Care reopens or supersedes FHIR-25396 with a new explicit binding-strength decision.
