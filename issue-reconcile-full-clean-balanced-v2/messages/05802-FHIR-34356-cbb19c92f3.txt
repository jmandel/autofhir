FHIR-34356: Audit DeviceDefinition regulatory identifiers

Issue request:
FHIR-34356 was an Applied/Persuasive R5 Orders and Observations change request to add DeviceDefinition.regulatoryIdentifier as a 0..* BackboneElement for regulatory documentation identifiers such as EUDAMED Basic UDI-DI, master UDI-DI, and license identifiers, with type, identifier, issuer, and jurisdiction components.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by source commits 3148d59e1d64 and 92554c131c43. Those commits added DeviceDefinition.regulatoryIdentifier to source/devicedefinition/structuredefinition-DeviceDefinition.xml and added the devicedefinition-regulatory-identifier-type CodeSystem and ValueSet for basic, master, and license.

Additional context:
DeviceDefinition is active in source/fhir.ini. The current source still contains the regulatoryIdentifier backbone with 0..* cardinality; required type, identifier, issuer, and jurisdiction children; and the regulatory identifier type terminology. Later DeviceDefinition work changed the originally requested deviceIdentifier child to identifier, added identifierSystem, and relaxed type to CodeableConcept/extensible; those changes are compatible later refinements rather than omissions from FHIR-34356.
