FHIR-20973: Audit DocumentReference scope clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-20973 was a Published FHIR-core OO change request asking to rename DocumentReference and redefine its scope because implementers wanted metadata for raw binary patient-associated content such as certificates. The final Not Persuasive with Modification decision rejected a rename as too substantive for the established resource name, but asked the editors to review and clarify that the resource is not limited to traditional documents.

Initial application:
No current-branch commit explicitly mentions FHIR-20973. The relevant scope clarification appears to have been applied through DocumentReference source evolution: b7f4a901e1 updated the introduction shortly after the originating Zulip discussion to say DocumentReference indexes documents, clinical notes, and other binary objects such as photo, video, or audio recordings; the generated R5 StructureDefinition source later carried the stronger definition that the term document encompasses any serialized object with a mime-type.

Additional context:
The originating Zulip thread confirms the concrete use case was patient-related raw binary content and records that DocumentReference was the intended metadata carrier, while the 2022-02-24 OO minutes record the formal decision not to rename DocumentReference and to ensure the introduction made the broad scope clear. Later DocumentReference boundary work, especially FHIR-38950 and FHIR-19250, further clarified that DocumentReference captures data that might not be in FHIR format and is not limited to formal FHIR Documents.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. DocumentReference is an active build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current authoritative source already has the requested clarification in source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml and source/documentreference/documentreference-introduction.xml: the StructureDefinition states that DocumentReference encompasses any serialized object with a mime-type and includes photos, videos, audio, policy text, clinical notes, CDA, and scanned paper; the narrative states that the document can be any object and is not limited to formal HL7 FHIR Documents.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Reopening the resource name would contradict the OO decision and would be a substantive compatibility change, while adding more local prose would duplicate existing build-scope wording and later boundary clarifications without changing implementer understanding.
