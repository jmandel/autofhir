FHIR-48924: Correct Device maintenance extension names

Issue request:
FHIR-48924 was an Applied/Persuasive FHIR-core FMG approval for creating Device maintenance extensions proposed by FHIR-47323, covering Device elements moved out of core by FHIR-32565.

Initial application:
Core commit 9d3783acc0 commented out Device.mode, Device.cycle, and Device.duration in source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml. Extension-pack commit 8b6b7b0 created the corresponding DeviceOperationMode, DeviceOperationCycle, and DeviceOperationDuration extension definitions under canonical ids device-operation-mode, device-operation-cycle, and device-operation-duration.

Additional context:
FHIR-32565 approved moving Device.mode, Device.cycle, Device.duration, Device.gateway, and Device.endpoint out of Device. FHIR-47323 implemented the maintenance operation extensions in the FHIR Extension Pack. Later Device introduction text remains in build scope because Device is active in source/fhir.ini, but its ballot note referred to non-existent device-mode, device-cycle, and device-duration extension ids.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated source/device/device-introduction.xml so the Device ballot note points readers to the actual extension-pack maintenance ids: device-operation-mode, device-operation-cycle, and device-operation-duration. The gateway and endpoint extension references were already aligned and were left unchanged.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It preserves the approved removal-and-extension model while avoiding misleading implementers toward extension canonicals that do not exist in the Extension Pack.
