FHIR-13389: Audit Communication scope guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-13389 was a Published Patient Care change request for FHIR-core Communication, raised against STU3 and applied for R5. It asked to clarify whether Communication is an event or workflow resource, when Communication can exist outside CommunicationRequest, when the two resources are duplicative, and when Communication may wrap or accompany exchanged data. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution approved definition, scope/use-case, data-sharing, and CommunicationRequest relationship guidance.

Initial application:
Commit f55ce1246f6f94cee3c82bde7721a1e15d8def0a applied the issue in PR #836 by adding the Communication purpose paragraph, use cases, the wrapping/accompanying-data note, and the CommunicationRequest relationship note. The Jira application comment explicitly omitted the two TBD guidance links until targets existed. Commit 7c753e02f31f8e55c40f8d5fa5338f31bf357c14 later completed that gap by adding the wrapping anchor from Scope and Usage and linking the note to the Approaches to Exchanging FHIR Data decision tree. The current StructureDefinition source also carries J#13389 on the root element and the approved clinical/business-level definition.
