FHIR-39009: Audit Device subject reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-39009 was a Published, Not Persuasive with Modification R5 Device change request noting that prior Device subject trackers had overwritten each other. It proposed adding Practitioner and RelatedPerson to Device.association.humanSubject and either broadening/renaming it to a generic subject that can reference Device or adding a non-human sibling. The final Jira resolution was to link to the FHIR-38904 disposition rather than make those edits directly on Device.

Initial application:
No source commit was found that directly mentioned FHIR-39009. The relevant application appears through the linked FHIR-38904 work: DeviceAssociation was introduced in commit 620b8fa10c746066887014813e72cf08475a42c0 with DeviceAssociation.subject referencing Patient, Group, Practitioner, RelatedPerson, and Device, and commit 7a135eb3489ef36b8935e36d80d88b1729a2e8e8 recorded FHIR-38904 QA follow-up. Earlier FHIR-32570 commits removed Device.subject and moved the prior patient association into Device.association, but the later FHIR-38904 disposition moved that association model out of Device.

Additional context:
FHIR-38904 was Published/Persuasive with Modification and explicitly directed creation of a DeviceAssociation resource, removal of the Device.association backbone from Device, and a DeviceAssociation.subject reference set including Patient, Group, Practitioner, RelatedPerson, and Device. OO WGM minutes record acceptance of the proposed DeviceAssociation disposition with an 8-0-2 vote. Current source has DeviceAssociation active in fhir.ini, describes Practitioner, Device, and RelatedPerson subject examples in the DeviceAssociation narrative, and includes active subject/patient search parameters on DeviceAssociation.subject. The old Device.association.humanSubject and Device subject/patient search parameters remain only inside XML comments, so editing them would not change the built specification.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build already implements the FHIR-39009 resolution through active DeviceAssociation source, including the subject types and narrative examples needed for the RTLS/caregiver/device-association use cases. Restoring or editing Device.association.humanSubject would conflict with the linked FHIR-38904 decision to keep patient-identifying association data out of Device.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future work on DeviceAssociation subject scope or relationship semantics should be handled by separate DeviceAssociation trackers, not by reopening FHIR-39009's superseded Device.association proposal.
