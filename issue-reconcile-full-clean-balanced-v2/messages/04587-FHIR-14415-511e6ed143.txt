FHIR-14415: Audit provider contact-purpose support

Issue request:
FHIR-14415 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked for purpose-specific contact details originally described for HealthcareService and Practitioner, then narrowed in Jira comments to HealthcareService.contact and PractitionerRole.contact, with the final resolution also naming OrganizationAffiliation. The approved shape was a repeating contact element with purpose, name, telecom, and address, plus guidance that telecoms with a specific purpose belong under that contact structure.

Initial application:
The structural change was applied before this reconciliation. Commit 6c6927cc55 added HealthcareService.contact and PractitionerRole.contact as ExtendedContactDetail and deprecated their old telecom elements. Commit e1320848bd removed the deprecated HealthcareService.telecom and PractitionerRole.telecom elements and converted OrganizationAffiliation.telecom to OrganizationAffiliation.contact as ExtendedContactDetail. Merge commits 522404cd22 and 2f70a91b0d tied that work to the R5 PA ExtendedContactDetail/contact-details change family.

Additional context:
FHIR-29035 created the reusable ExtendedContactDetail datatype with purpose, name, telecom, address, organization, and period, matching Brian Postlethwaite's 2022 Jira comment that FHIR-14415 was applied through the datatype across provider-directory resources. The current build actively includes HealthcareService, PractitionerRole, and OrganizationAffiliation in source/fhir.ini. The original Practitioner wording does not require a Practitioner.contact change because the issue's own comments redirected that part of the use case to PractitionerRole.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source/healthcareservice/structuredefinition-HealthcareService.xml, source/practitionerrole/structuredefinition-PractitionerRole.xml, and source/organizationaffiliation/structuredefinition-OrganizationAffiliation.xml already define contact 0..* ExtendedContactDetail, and ExtendedContactDetail already carries the requested purpose, name, telecom, and address fields. The old top-level telecom elements for those three resources are absent, so there is no remaining telecom definition that needs the requested guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No additional core source change is needed for FHIR-14415 unless the work group later decides to reopen the broader question of purpose-specific contact details on Practitioner itself.
