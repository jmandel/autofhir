FHIR-17457: Audit Group characteristic/member semantics

Issue request:
FHIR-17457 was Published with a Persuasive resolution for STU3. It asked what it means when Group.characteristic and Group.member are both present, and the accepted answer was that listed members are individuals found to meet the characteristic, that other qualifying candidates may exist outside the listed members, and that listed members cannot fail the listed characteristics.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git-svn commit 348fdf15b1c1d81837afaf04707cc4a0d3c8536b (SVN revision 14450), which added the "Mixing Characteristics and Members" Group narrative and populated the Group root comment in the spreadsheet source. Later source refactors carried that comment into the current StructureDefinition source.

Additional context:
Group is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/group/structuredefinition-Group.xml and source/group/group-notes.xml are current build-scope inputs. Later FHIR-23724 replaced Group.actual with Group.membership and incorporated compatible membership-basis wording: definitional/conceptual Groups treat characteristics as sufficient, while enumerated Groups still require listed members to satisfy the characteristics. A later FHIR-56092 proposal questions some membership-basis wording, but it is Triaged/Unresolved and is not a final decision superseding FHIR-17457. The referenced Zulip patient roster thread supports the same interpretation, and no Confluence reference or additional conflicting community record was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Group root comment and Group notes already state the approved FHIR-17457 rule, and the current Group.membership definitions refine rather than contradict that rule.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. If FHIR-I later resolves FHIR-56092 or another issue to revise Group.membership definitions, handle that as a separate future change rather than rewriting the already-applied FHIR-17457 clarification.
