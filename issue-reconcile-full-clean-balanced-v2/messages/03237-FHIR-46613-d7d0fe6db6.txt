FHIR-46613: Audit NutritionProduct boundary clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-46613 was Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6 by OO. It asked to clarify NutritionProduct scope and boundaries with BiologicallyDerivedProduct, especially donated or pooled human milk and when a dietary product should remain a NutritionProduct.

Initial application:
Commit ccb652de62 added the requested NutritionProduct boundary text as standalone headings, and PR/merge c42ec82803 carried that work into the build. That application introduced the intended human-milk boundary but also duplicated boundary material in a form that later OO minutes identified as needing cleanup.

Additional context:
The paired Applied issue FHIR-46604 made the analogous BiologicallyDerivedProduct-side change. Later Applied issue FHIR-51842, created from the FHIR-50314/module cleanup trail, explicitly replaced the duplicated NutritionProduct subsections and added the current BiologicallyDerivedProduct/NutritionProduct list entries. Current source/fhir.ini keeps NutritionProduct and BiologicallyDerivedProduct active in the build, and current source contains the FHIR-51842 wording. FHIR-46702 and FHIR-48640 are already represented on the combined branch and confirm that FHIR-46613/FHIR-46604 plus traceability work are the intended handling for human milk.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current NutritionProduct boundary already says that transplant, infusion, therapeutic administration, or injection is BiologicallyDerivedProduct, dietary administration is NutritionProduct, donated human milk can be ambiguous, and local implementation guidance may provide direction. Re-adding FHIR-46613's older standalone paragraph would reintroduce the duplication that FHIR-51842 removed.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Future wording changes should be handled through the later BDP/NutritionProduct boundary family rather than by restoring the original duplicated NutritionProduct text.
