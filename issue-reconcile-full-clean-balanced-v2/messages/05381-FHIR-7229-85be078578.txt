FHIR-7229: Audit superseded MedicationOrder rename

Issue request:
FHIR-7229 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request from the DSTU1 ballot asking that MedicationPrescription be generalized to MedicationOrder rather than remain prescription-focused. Its resolution says the change had already been applied through a separate request and that the resource was called MedicationOrder instead of MedicationPrescription.

Initial application:
The direct seed key is not named in source history, but the same rename family was applied by commit 9d6493a912 for FHIR-7224 and FHIR-5896. That commit mechanically changed MedicationPrescription to MedicationOrder in fhir.ini, history, Composition sample content, and ElementDefinition examples, matching FHIR-7229's resolved statement that a separate request had applied the rename.

Additional context:
Later Published issue FHIR-10456 intentionally changed MedicationOrder to MedicationRequest for workflow alignment and scope clarity. Current source follows that later decision: source/fhir.ini has MedicationRequest as the active resource, keeps MedicationOrder as an old-name conversion to MedicationRequest, and source/medicationrequest/structuredefinition-MedicationRequest.xml explains that the resource is called MedicationRequest rather than MedicationPrescription or MedicationOrder. A contemporaneous Zulip discussion likewise records the rationale that MedicationOrder would be misleading for plans, recommendations, and proposals.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. Reintroducing MedicationOrder for FHIR-7229 would undo the later published FHIR-10456 decision, while the original FHIR-7229 concern about MedicationPrescription being too narrow is already satisfied by the current MedicationRequest resource name and aliases.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Treat FHIR-7229 as historically applied and now superseded by the MedicationRequest workflow rename; do not change current core source back to MedicationOrder.
