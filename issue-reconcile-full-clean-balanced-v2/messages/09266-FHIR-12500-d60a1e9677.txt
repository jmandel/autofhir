Issue request:
FHIR-12500 was a Published FHIR-I change request resolved Persuasive for DSTU2. It asked to clarify the Contained Resources guidance by saying that contained resources can be placed in resources inheriting from DomainResource and identifying where the contained element is located.

Initial application:

That original text said the contained resource can be put in any DomainResource and that the contained element was at the beginning of the resource after narrative and before extensions.
