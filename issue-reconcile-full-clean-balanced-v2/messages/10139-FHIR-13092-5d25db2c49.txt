FHIR-13092: Audit QuestionnaireResponse display linkId guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-13092 was Published/Not Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2. It asked whether QuestionnaireResponse.item.linkId should stop being mandatory for display items, because instructions need not be echoed in QuestionnaireResponse. The accepted disposition kept linkId mandatory for any included QuestionnaireResponse item, while documenting that display items can be omitted, display items should be included when relevant to interpreting answers, and disabled/not-enabled item handling needed explicit guidance.

Initial application:
The SVN-mirrored commit e3b09d6fbb06b0791fb8d6c891df650930f62201 applied the issue as revision 13076 by adding the QuestionnaireResponse "Links to Questionnaire" notes: item linkage uses linkId, relevant display items may be included, and not-enabled items were discussed. No cardinality change was made to QuestionnaireResponse.item.linkId because the Jira resolution rejected making linkId optional for included display items.

Additional context:
Later final SDC/FHIR-I decisions refined the same guidance without reversing FHIR-13092. FHIR-25949 clarified that completed QuestionnaireResponses do not carry ordinary unanswered question items, while display items may be retained when needed. FHIR-31077 then replaced the original "not enabled ... will not have answers" wording with a status-sensitive rule: non-completed QuestionnaireResponses may contain currently not-enabled items, but completed/amended QuestionnaireResponses SHALL only contain enabled items. QuestionnaireResponse remains an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current source also includes the later FHIR-37550 clarification that linkId semantics depend on a resolvable Questionnaire.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current authoritative source keeps QuestionnaireResponse.item.linkId at 1..1 for all included items, and the active QuestionnaireResponse notes already say that display items should be included when relevant to interpretation, may be retained in completed responses, and that disabled/not-enabled handling follows the later completed-vs-incomplete rules. Adding the older wording back verbatim would conflict with FHIR-31077 and would not improve implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. The current source correctly reflects FHIR-13092 as modified by later published decisions; no core follow-up is needed unless a future work-group decision changes completed QuestionnaireResponse handling for display, unanswered, or disabled items.
