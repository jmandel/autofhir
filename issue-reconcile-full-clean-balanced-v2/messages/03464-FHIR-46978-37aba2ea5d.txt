Issue request:
FHIR-46978 was an Applied/Persuasive R6 technical correction for the Device Boundaries and Relationships section. It requested changing the DeviceAssociation bullet from recording association of a device with a subject to recording association with a subject and/or operator, and the Jira resolution says to fix as suggested.

Initial application:
No prior AutoFHIR reconciliation commit existed for FHIR-46978 on the combined branch. The source change appears to have been applied in commit 3cf635fa2b, which explicitly listed FHIR-46978 and changed source/device/device-introduction.xml from the subject-only DeviceAssociation bullet to the subject/operator wording. A later typo-cleanup commit, d8af80193d, corrected the added ownership/custodianship sentence without changing the requested subject/operator scope.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists Device as an active OO resource, so source/device/device-introduction.xml is in current build scope for the Device page. OO HCP minutes for 2024-10-28 record the same exact DeviceAssociation BnR replacement and mark it Will Fix. Related FHIR-46976 aligned the DeviceAssociation resource/StructureDefinition purpose wording to the same subject/operator scope and is already represented on the combined branch, so it was not re-decided here. Quoted Zulip searches found no conflicting discussion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Device BnR source already says, "Records the association of a device with a subject and/or operator (e.g. a Patient by ownership, or a Practitioner by usage during a procedure)," and the stale subject-only sentence requested in the Jira is absent.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No further core source work is needed for FHIR-46978 unless future Device BnR edits accidentally remove the operator scope.
