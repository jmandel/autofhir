FHIR-5828: Audit Procedure tense definition

Issue request:
FHIR-5828 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification DSTU1 Patient Care request to correct the Procedure definition from present tense, "An action that is performed on a patient", to the past-tense summary "A summary description of an action performed on a patient" because Procedure was understood as a summary of performed procedures rather than real-time execution tracking.

Initial application:
No Jira-keyed source commit for FHIR-5828 was found. The likely untagged SVN-import application is 07aa93633f, shortly after the recorded vote, which changed the Procedure spreadsheet root short/definition from present-only wording to "was or is currently being" and "is or was" wording and added narrative that Procedure provides summary information rather than real-time snapshots.

Additional context:
Later Published Patient Care decisions intentionally broadened Procedure beyond the exact DSTU1 request. FHIR-15127 updated Procedure scope for services performed on or for a patient while retaining "is or was" wording, and FHIR-26859 expanded Procedure.subject and the Procedure definition to patient, practitioner, device, organization, or location while keeping current-and-historical Procedure scope.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The active current source already says Procedure is an action that is or was performed and the narrative states it records current and historical procedures while not providing real-time procedure execution snapshots; changing it back to the 2015 past-only patient wording would conflict with later published decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Future edits to the Procedure root definition should be driven by new Patient Care decisions, not by reapplying the narrower DSTU1 wording from FHIR-5828.
