FHIR-42959: Audit Identifier registry guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-42959 was an Applied/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-I correction for R5 Identifier.system text. It asked to remove the claim that UUIDs may be registered in the HL7 OID registry and to consider guidance about NamingSystem construction and cross-institution UUID use. The approved resolution replaced the registry sentence with shared-registry guidance: OIDs and UUIDs SHOULD be available to participants when exchanged across institutional boundaries, the HL7 OID registry applies to OIDs, and terminology.hl7.org can host NamingSystems for identifiers of broad international or national interest.

Initial application:
