FHIR-13151: Audit Ratio datatype guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-13151 was a Published FHIR-core MnM change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for STU3, asking that Ratio clarify when a relationship should be represented as Quantity instead. The accepted language said Ratio should be used only when the relationship cannot be suitably expressed with Quantity and a common unit, that fixed-one denominators should use Quantity, and that rates, densities, and concentrations are typically Quantity while titers, significant-denominator concentrations, and observed frequencies are Ratio examples.
