FHIR-7765: Audit harmonized reaction guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7765 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for the DSTU1 Patient Care/Public Health request to harmonize AllergyIntolerance.event, Contraindication, and Immunization.reaction concepts. The approved resolution required a usage note on Immunization.reaction explaining that reactions may indicate allergy or intolerance and should become a separate AllergyIntolerance record when appropriate, plus relationship guidance for Contraindication and AllergyIntolerance.

Initial application:
The request was applied in 2015. Commit 67f5883885 added the Immunization.reaction element comment, and commit b6b2159d66 added matching Boundaries and Relationships narrative to Immunization and AllergyIntolerance. The contemporaneous Contraindication rename to DetectedIssue was handled by FHIR-8043 in commit 1408563d74 and follow-up commit cc54b56e72 adjusted AllergyIntolerance wording to reference DetectedIssue.

Additional context:
The current build includes AllergyIntolerance, DetectedIssue, and Immunization as active resources in source/fhir.ini, while the deleted-resource notes record that Contraindication became DetectedIssue. Later FHIR-15124 discussion rejected replacing Immunization.reaction guidance with AdverseEvent guidance and commit 0edb306a7 restored the AllergyIntolerance-focused text. The combined branch already contains a separate FHIR-8043 audit commit, so this commit does not duplicate that issue.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Current source/structuredefinition-Immunization.xml keeps the FHIR-7765 element comment, source/immunization/immunization-introduction.xml and source/allergyintolerance/allergyintolerance-introduction.xml keep the reciprocal narrative, and source/detectedissue/detectedissue-introduction.xml describes the DetectedIssue/AllergyIntolerance/Flag boundaries for clinical issues and general propensity to risk.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty no-change audit commit. The current specification preserves the approved FHIR-7765 guidance in active build-scope source, and the small wording differences from the 2015 resolution do not change implementer understanding.
