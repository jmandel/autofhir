Initial application:

Additional context:
The active Requirements resource is in build scope through source/fhir.ini. FHIR-I minutes from 2024-10-28 confirm the approved distinction: derivedFrom refines an existing requirement in the same scope, while partOf is a narrower sub-requirement necessary to satisfy a higher-level requirement.
