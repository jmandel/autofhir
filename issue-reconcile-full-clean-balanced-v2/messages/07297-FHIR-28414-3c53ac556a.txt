FHIR-28414: Audit Questionnaire profiling guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-28414 was Published/Persuasive for R5. It asked the core Questionnaire page to explain the design distinction between defining a specific form as a Questionnaire instance versus profiling Questionnaire, and between defining a Questionnaire versus profiling QuestionnaireResponse for broader capability restrictions.

Initial application:
The Jira comment identifies commit 5a5e85119d. That commit added active narrative sections to source/questionnaire/questionnaire-notes.xml and source/questionnaireresponse/questionnaireresponse-notes.xml explaining that profiles are for constraints across sets of Questionnaires or QuestionnaireResponses, while a specific form should be represented by a Questionnaire instance and a specific answer set by a QuestionnaireResponse tied to a Questionnaire.

Additional context:
Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse are both active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini. The 2020 SDC minutes record the accepted 10-0-0 decision to add clarification. Later Questionnaire/QuestionnaireResponse reconciliations already on the combined branch touched adjacent guidance, including FHIR-28305 and FHIR-37550, but they do not reverse or weaken the FHIR-28414 profiling-vs-instance design rule.
