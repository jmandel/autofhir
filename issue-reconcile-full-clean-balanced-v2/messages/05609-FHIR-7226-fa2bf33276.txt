FHIR-7226: Audit MedicationStatement definition

Issue request:
FHIR-7226 was a Published/Persuasive Pharmacy change request for DSTU1. It objected that the original MedicationStatement definition distinguished the resource by how the information arrived and noted that the information would often be data about dispensing; the accepted resolution was to update the definition to "A record of medication use as reported by a patient, a clinician or another party."

Initial application:
The issue was applied in historical commit e5b1777610 (#7226, 7168), which changed the MedicationStatement spreadsheet definition from the old patient-or-clinician report wording to the accepted "medication use as reported by a patient, a clinician or another party" wording. The next related historical commit, 7d50996061 (#7167), expanded the same root definition and scope/usage text with the more durable MedicationStatement purpose wording that remains in current source.

Additional context:
FHIR-7168 was applied in the same e5b1777610 commit and addressed the narrower "who can report/capture" part of the same wording. FHIR-7167, FHIR-5916, and FHIR-5941 are the same 2015 MedicationStatement clarification family; the combined branch already contains separate audit commits for FHIR-5916 and FHIR-5941. Later MedicationStatement source history retained the key semantics: MedicationStatement records reported medication use, can be derived from other records, and can link to request/dispense/administration information through derivedFrom.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. In the current build, MedicationStatement is active in source/fhir.ini, and the active StructureDefinition and introduction already avoid the obsolete "how it arrived" distinction by describing MedicationStatement as medication consumed or used by a patient, sourced from the patient, patient representative, clinician, lists, prescription bottles, or other maintained information, and derived from other records such as MedicationRequest, MedicationDispense, Claim, Observation, or QuestionnaireResponse.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Replacing the current richer definition with the short 2015 proposed sentence would lose later accepted clarification, and adding more prose for FHIR-7226 would be redundant unless Pharmacy opens a new work-group decision to revise the broader MedicationStatement boundaries.
