FHIR-31866: Audit datatype removal

Issue request:
FHIR-31866 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and requested reincorporating the Statistic datatype elements into the Evidence resource, removing the Statistic and OrderedDistribution datatypes, and removing Evidence.distribution because the structures were only used by Evidence and OrderedDistribution lacked active community use.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in stages: 4b01cc1ed746c7597da272dc8cb0a9bbfcebc9e9 moved Statistic content into Evidence, 8454f5094b892669faa0b194a04a9dc695aebbb5 removed OrderedDistribution, 7a66857e48c8dac76aaff07350a54f4d8133637e removed Statistic, and 932c9b7cbfe416c3e8410405debc478fe2fe40ca migrated Statistic-related terminology under Evidence. A later same-family commit, 9545244b7167d105bd862aa06a62fea63101dec7 for FHIR-31564, removed the datatypes again from R4B/R5 source after branch divergence.

Additional context:
FHIR-31564 is the same change family and records the earlier CDS decision to remove OrderedDistribution rather than add an example. CDS agenda/minutes on 2021-06-23 approved the FHIR-31866 resolution, and OO minutes on 2021-06-03 recorded that OO did not need to keep Statistic as a separate datatype at that time. Current source/fhir.ini has Evidence as an active resource and has no Statistic or OrderedDistribution entries in the active datatype list; source/evidence/structuredefinition-Evidence.xml now defines Evidence.statistic as backbone content and no Evidence.distribution element remains.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source files were changed. The current build inputs already reflect the published decision: Statistic and OrderedDistribution are not current datatypes, OrderedDistribution source pages/files are gone, and the statistics model is incorporated directly into Evidence.statistic.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core FHIR source follow-up is needed for FHIR-31866 unless a future work group decision reintroduces either structure as a datatype.
