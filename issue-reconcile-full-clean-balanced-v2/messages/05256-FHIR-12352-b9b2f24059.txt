FHIR-12352: Audit Timing timeOfDay application

Issue request:
FHIR-12352 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2 and asked to support medication orders at specific times of day by adding a repeating list of times to Timing.repeat. The accepted resolution added Timing.repeat.timeOfDay 0..* time with definition "Specified time of day for action to take place" and an explanatory comment that originally made timeOfDay mutually exclusive with when, frequency, and period.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in SVN r10350, represented in git by 91bf3e7b836dc9486da4c4d4b7829d9df7167900, which added dayOfWeek/timeOfDay support to the Timing datatype for FHIR-10478, FHIR-12201, and FHIR-12352. Current source/datatypes/timing.xml contains Timing.repeat.timeOfDay as 0..* time with the requested definition, and source/fhir.ini keeps Timing in the active datatype build and normative list. The current medication-order successor path also still reaches Timing through MedicationRequest.dosageInstruction -> DosageDetails -> Dosage.timing.

Additional context:
Later Published issues changed the part of the original resolution that prohibited combining timeOfDay with frequency and period. FHIR-12460 loosened the Timing invariants so timeOfDay/dayOfWeek may be used with frequency and period, FHIR-23710 approved timeOfDay being exclusive only with when, and FHIR-25289 was already published on the combined branch as 542e45405a17e048b338101140592af45aae9dcd to clarify that timeOfDay occurs at the specified time(s) as otherwise constrained by repeat timing. FHIR-12709 fixed the original typo in the timeOfDay comment, and FHIR-36940 was retracted/no-change. Zulip discussion around the medication scheduling use case and the r10350 commit supports the original need, while Confluence has no seed-specific reference and only confirms the later FHIR-25289 MnM vote.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative source already satisfies the durable part of FHIR-12352 by defining Timing.repeat.timeOfDay, and restoring the original frequency/period prohibition would contradict later final Jira decisions and current examples.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Future ambiguity about complex Timing schedules should be handled through a new Timing work-group decision rather than reopening FHIR-12352.
