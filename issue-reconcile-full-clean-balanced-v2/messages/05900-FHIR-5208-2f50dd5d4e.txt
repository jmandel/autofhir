FHIR-5208: Audit EpisodeOfCare period clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-5208 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration DSTU1 clarification for EpisodeOfCare.period. It asked the specification to make clear whether the period is a single organization's tracking interval rather than a multi-organization shared-care design, and its final resolution said to apply FHIR-5201's further explanation: yes, this is the period for a single organization, and other organizations would have their own episode of care as required.

Initial application:
No explicit FHIR-5208 source commit was found. The likely initial application is the same March 2015 EpisodeOfCare ballot reconciliation trail as FHIR-5201: git commit f659c3fbc16870414aac80f798d75afcb5480bf8 added the active "Multiple Organizations and Transfer of Care" narrative and status/history period notes, with later refactors preserving that guidance. The period element definition itself remained the interval during which the managing organization assumes responsibility.

Additional context:
FHIR-5201 is the controlling companion issue named in the FHIR-5208 resolution. Later FHIR-20612 refined the original "each organization will have its own EpisodeOfCare" wording into the current primary-managing-organization model: multiple organizations can be involved, care may be provided by care teams from different organizations, and other organizations may have their own EpisodeOfCare resource instances for tracking their activities. That later decision means the current source should not be changed back to an absolute single-resource-per-organization rule.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current active source already conveys the approved clarification: EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization is the primary organization assuming responsibility, EpisodeOfCare.period is the interval for that responsibility, and the Scope and Usage narrative explains multiple organizations, transfer of care, and separate organization tracking instances. This is sufficient to answer the original period ambiguity without adding redundant wording to the period definition.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. The current specification is correct after applying FHIR-5201 and later FHIR-20612; do not add a new period note unless a future PA decision asks for different cross-organization semantics.
