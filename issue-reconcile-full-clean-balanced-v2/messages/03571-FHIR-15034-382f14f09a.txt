FHIR-15034: Audit Procedure clarifications

Issue request:
FHIR-15034 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Care STU3 change request to clarify Procedure.note, Procedure.report, and Procedure.partOf. The resolved change was to define Procedure.note as "Any other notes and comments about the procedure.", to allow Procedure.report to reference DiagnosticReport, DocumentReference, or Composition, and to clarify in Procedure.partOf comments that Procedure and MedicationAdministration can point to each other in different workflow contexts without a circular-reference problem.

That commit changed source/procedure/procedure-spreadsheet.xml accordingly. The later source rework and R4B conversion carried the same semantics into source/procedure/structuredefinition-Procedure.xml.
