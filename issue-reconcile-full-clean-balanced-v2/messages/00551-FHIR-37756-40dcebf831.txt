The leftover source/genomicstudy files still contain the requested example binding and 0..* cardinality, but those files are not authoritative current-build core source. The same terminology-binding family issues FHIR-37757, FHIR-37758, and FHIR-37759, and the later Clinical Genomics QA issue FHIR-40585, are already represented on the combined branch and do not need duplicate commits here.
