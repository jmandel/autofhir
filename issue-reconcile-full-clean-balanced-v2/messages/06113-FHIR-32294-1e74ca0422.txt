Issue request:
FHIR-32294 was a Published/Persuasive R5 ballot issue asking whether NutritionIntake existed because the 2021 May NutritionProduct page referred to NutritionIntake while the resource was not visible in the ballot resource list and the NutritionProduct page did not link to it.

Initial application:
The resource source was added earlier by bc846f6893 ("Added new NutritionIntake branch"), which registered nutritionintake in source/fhir.ini. The resource-list omission identified by the ballot issue was later corrected by f246497111 ("Update the nutrition resources"), which added NutritionIntake entries to source/resourcelist.html before the R5 reconciliation vote. No Jira-keyed source commit for FHIR-32294 itself was found.

Additional context:
OO minutes on 2023-01-09 record that the resource existed, was in the listing/CI build, and the remaining concern was why Jira still appeared not to expose it. OO minutes on 2023-02-10 record the motion to find the issue persuasive and pre-applied for R5 because the resource exists now. Current R6 source keeps NutritionIntake as an active resource and includes a Nutrition module page with NutritionIntake links.
