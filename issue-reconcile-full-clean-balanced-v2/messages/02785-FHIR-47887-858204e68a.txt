Issue request:

It asked editors to replace the unclear statement "These are the device related resources" with text explaining what a device-related resource is and why resources are listed there.

FHIR-47886 is the closest same-change-family issue for the separated workflow-resource bullets, but this commit audits only FHIR-47887.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Adding another local criterion sentence would duplicate the Device module and risk drift without changing implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
