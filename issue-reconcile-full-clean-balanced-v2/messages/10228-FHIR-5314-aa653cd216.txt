FHIR-5314: Audit Bundle collection wording

Issue request:
FHIR-5314 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 ballot comment for FHIR-core BaseResource/resource.html. It asked to replace the sentence "There is a special type of resource called Bundle for groups/lists of resources" with collection-oriented wording because saying groups/lists could be confused with the Group and List resources.

Initial application:
The old sentence was introduced in source/resource/resource-introduction.xml by commit 39fb3f866be9936d3c1b27f3f925ff58061d18e0. Commit 368c9c3074eeb60fb30efdb8d319e75345501ebc, dated before the Jira resolution and continuous-build approval, changed that sentence to say Bundle is for collections of resources. Later source restructuring removed the old resource-introduction file, and current authoritative Bundle source now describes Bundle as a container for a collection of resources.

Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini keeps Bundle as an active resource and maps resource type pages through the current resource/resourcelist machinery. The active Bundle introduction still compares Bundle with List, Group, and Composition where that distinction is the subject of the section, and related reconciliations for FHIR-16894 and FHIR-7383 are already present on the combined branch. Those later decisions explain why boundary text may intentionally mention List and Group without recreating the obsolete "groups/lists" BaseResource sentence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit makes no source change. The current active source no longer contains the obsolete "groups/lists" sentence, and the Bundle StructureDefinition already uses the accepted collection/container wording. Additional edits to remove every generic use of "group" or every comparison with List/Group would go beyond the accepted ballot comment and would make the boundaries text less clear.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-5314 unless the work group opens a separate issue to change the broader Bundle/List/Group boundary narrative.
