FHIR-34301: Audit Immunization.recorded removal

Issue request:
FHIR-34301 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification R5 PHER change request saying Immunization.recorded was ambiguous, especially around the first capture time and which system's or subject's record should be used when immunization data is shared. The accepted resolution was to remove Immunization.recorded from the Immunization resource because usage was not known and the element could not be clearly defined across systems.

Initial application:
The issue was previously applied by git commit 3a84ba11931614dd0d5c39b76b26fc0b261e33c4, which removed the Immunization.recorded element from source/immunization/structuredefinition-Immunization.xml. Current source/fhir.ini keeps Immunization active in the build and retains only an empty deleted-element/version-conversion marker for Immunization.recorded.

Additional context:
FHIR-39016 later asked to keep Immunization.recorded, but PHER resolved it Not Persuasive/Resolved - No Change and explicitly decided to stick with the R5 removal even after FHIR-I clarified Event.recorded in FHIR-39485. PHWG minutes from the May 2022 WGM record the ambiguity and low-use/extension discussion, and January 2023 PHWG minutes record the later decision to continue omitting the Immunization element.
