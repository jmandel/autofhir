FHIR-7135: Audit supportingInfo clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-7135 was a Published, Persuasive with Modification OO change request against DSTU1 DiagnosticOrder.supportingInformation. It asked to correct the supporting-information text, including the inspired carbon dioxide error, and to clarify that laboratory ask-at-order-entry answers are discrete referenced resources such as Observations, not free-form narrative. The final resolution required adding that supportingInformation includes observations explicitly requested by the producer/filler to provide context or supporting information needed to complete the order.

Initial application:
Git history shows the issue was applied in 2015 before this reconciliation. Commit 328fda3133 added AOE aliases and text to DiagnosticOrder.supportingInformation and changed the example to inspired oxygen. Commit 0f465cf0ca expanded the DiagnosticOrder example/notes and continued to show the AOE as an Observation referenced by supportingInformation. Later source history renamed DiagnosticOrder to DiagnosticRequest and ultimately subsumed it into the active ServiceRequest resource.

Additional context:
The current build scope is ServiceRequest: source/fhir.ini has an active servicerequest resource and comments that DiagnosticOrder was subsumed into ServiceRequest. Current ServiceRequest.supportingInfo retains the FHIR-7135 substance: AOEs are named, the producer/filler observation wording is present, and the example is inspired oxygen for blood gas measurements. Later FHIR-31589 clarified the boundary with orderDetail without removing this AOE-as-existing-information guidance; the combined branch already contains a FHIR-31589 reconciliation commit. FHIR-37964 is a duplicate/background AOE question and the 2025 OO Lab minutes found during this reconciliation also treat required AOEs for testing as ServiceRequest.supportingInfo, distinct from OrderDetail. Searches for FHIR-7135-specific Zulip and Confluence evidence found no conflicting community record.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The active build source already contains the corrected AOE/filler guidance and oxygen example, and later ServiceRequest guidance addresses the possible confusion about questions to be gathered versus existing supporting information.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-7135 unless OO wants a broader future redesign of AOE representation, which would be separate from this already-applied DSTU1 clarification.
