FHIR-13894: Audit AdverseEvent participant history

Issue request:
FHIR-13894 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for STU3. It asked to rename AdverseEvent.eventParticipant to AdverseEvent.contributor, change the cardinality to 0..*, and replace copied v3/BRIDG-style wording with a definition about parties contributing information to the adverse event and its activities.

Initial application:
The change was applied in commit 1051f7d9e92921d517519953657b5f739b0ed15a, which renamed AdverseEvent.eventParticipant to AdverseEvent.contributor in the AdverseEvent spreadsheet source, changed the cardinality from 0..1 to 0..*, and inserted the requested adverse-event-specific definition with GF#13894 committee notes.

Additional context:
FHIR-18853 later revisited the same AdverseEvent.contributor wording and was Published for R5 with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. That later issue explicitly cited FHIR-13894 as historical context and replaced contributor with a participant backbone containing participant.actor and participant.function so actor can identify the involved party and function can distinguish contributor, informant, witness, and related roles. Current source/fhir.ini lists AdverseEvent as an active resource and maps the old R4/R6 AdverseEvent.contributor element to AdverseEvent.participant.actor. Later FHIR-38823 and FHIR-41402 decisions further clarified participant use without undoing the FHIR-18853 refactor.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source is already consistent with FHIR-13894 as superseded/refined by later AdverseEvent participant decisions: the original 0..* multiplicity remains on AdverseEvent.participant, the former contributor actor is represented by participant.actor, and participant.function carries the role distinction that resolved the ambiguity raised after FHIR-13894.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Reintroducing AdverseEvent.contributor or the original FHIR-13894 definition would conflict with the later Published FHIR-18853 model; no core source follow-up is recommended for FHIR-13894.
