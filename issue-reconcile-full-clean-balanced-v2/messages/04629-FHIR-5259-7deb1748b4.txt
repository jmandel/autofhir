Initial application:

Later commit f0f6a05d8a7599d9a4be1f64d962e8f24ee884c3 copied the same relationship into the OperationOutcome introduction.

The current DetectedIssue and OperationOutcome introductions both say that OperationOutcome may indicate a requested action was rejected due to a clinical issue while DetectedIssue provides the details, and no current source hit retains the ambiguous first/latter text or the reversed Contraindication/OperationOutcome roles.
