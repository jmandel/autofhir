FHIR-37467: Audit DocumentReference CDA relatedDocument mapping

Issue request:
FHIR-37467 was a Published/Persuasive Orders and Observations change request for R5 asking to correct the CDA mapping for DocumentReference. The issue said the older mapping from DocumentReference.context.related to ClinicalDocument/relatedDocument was incorrect and requested mapping DocumentReference.relatesTo.code to ClinicalDocument/relatedDocument/typeCode and DocumentReference.relatesTo.target to ClinicalDocument/relatedDocument/parentDocument.

Initial application:

That change added the two requested CDA mappings to source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml. No prior Issue-Reconcile-Key commit for FHIR-37467 was present on the combined branch before this reconciliation.

Additional context:
DocumentReference is active and approved in source/fhir.ini, so source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml is in current build scope and is the authoritative source for the generated mapping table. The current source also still maps Composition.relatesTo to DocumentReference.relatesTo and CDA .relatedDocument, which supports the Jira rationale that CDA relatedDocument belongs with relatesTo semantics. The only remaining DocumentReference.context.related hit is an R4/R5 conversion note mapping it to basedOn, not a CDA mapping. SDWG and OO minutes confirm SDWG agreed with the requested resolution and OO voted FHIR-37467 persuasive.
