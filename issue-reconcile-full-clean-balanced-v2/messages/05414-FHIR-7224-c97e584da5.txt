FHIR-7224: Audit superseded MedicationOrder rename

Issue request:
FHIR-7224 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request from the DSTU1 ballot asking that MedicationPrescription be renamed to MedicationOrder. Its resolution required changing the resource name to medicationOrder and adding wording that the resource was called MedicationOrder rather than MedicationPrescription to generalize use across inpatient, outpatient, and care-plan settings.

Initial application:
The request was applied historically by commit 9d6493a912, whose message explicitly cites #7224 and #5896 and mechanically renamed MedicationPrescription to MedicationOrder in source/fhir.ini and related medication source files. That application matched the 2015 resolution for the then-current DSTU source.

Additional context:
Later Published issue FHIR-10456 intentionally changed MedicationOrder to MedicationRequest for workflow alignment and broader request semantics. Current source follows that later decision: source/fhir.ini has MedicationRequest as the active resource, legacy mappings record MedicationOrder to MedicationRequest, and source/medicationrequest/structuredefinition-MedicationRequest.xml explains that MedicationRequest is used rather than MedicationPrescription or MedicationOrder to generalize the resource and harmonize with workflow patterns. The related FHIR-7229 no-change audit is already present on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. Reintroducing MedicationOrder or adding MedicationOrder-specific naming prose would undo the later final FHIR-10456 decision, while the current MedicationRequest description already preserves the generalized-use rationale from FHIR-7224.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Treat FHIR-7224 as historically applied and now superseded by the MedicationRequest workflow rename; do not change current core source back to MedicationOrder.
