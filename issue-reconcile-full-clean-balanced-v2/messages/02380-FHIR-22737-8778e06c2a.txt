FHIR-22737: Audit AdverseEvent element order

Issue request:
FHIR-22737 was a Published FHIR-core Patient Care change request for the AdverseEvent resource. The QA warning said the AdverseEvent elements were out of W5 order and the persuasive-with-modification resolution specifically called for AdverseEvent.subject to sit after AdverseEvent.event/code and before AdverseEvent.encounter, with the subject W5 mapping updated from who to who.focus.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in 19edc88a0ded155ed3e803b73666a87dc384f4bb, a multi-issue AdverseEvent QA commit whose subject explicitly included GF#22737. That commit changed the old AdverseEvent spreadsheet's subject W5 mapping from who to who.focus. The later 33b409a4b6d544598262f2f074ebb4b6dc466157 source rework replaced the spreadsheet with structuredefinition-AdverseEvent.xml while keeping the subject before encounter and carrying the W5 subject mapping into the new representation.

Additional context:
AdverseEvent is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. Since the original STU3/R5 issue, AdverseEvent.event has become AdverseEvent.code, AdverseEvent.date has become AdverseEvent.effect[x], resultingCondition has become resultingEffect, severity was removed, and participant-related elements were refactored by later Jira decisions. Those later changes do not reverse FHIR-22737's ordering intent. The current FiveWs source defines FiveWs.subject as who.focus and FiveWs.author as who.author; later W5 cleanup commits ab7d0088743cb638033cd9a214779cd3c9a0bdcc and cd774234a5b73a2f28165e4393cb681715233c43 reduced the AdverseEvent mapping to the current FiveWs.subject form rather than the older generated FiveWs.subject[x] spelling.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current active AdverseEvent StructureDefinition already has code, subject, encounter, effect[x], recordedDate, and recorder in the expected relative order, and AdverseEvent.subject maps to FiveWs.subject, whose FiveWs definition is the current source equivalent of who.focus.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Future changes should only revisit this if the FiveWs model is renamed again or a later Patient Care decision intentionally changes AdverseEvent ordering semantics.
