FHIR-5533: Audit HumanName name-part clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-5533 was a Published FHIR-core/MnM change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking whether HumanName.given and HumanName.family should be simple strings rather than repeatable strings to avoid ambiguous combinations. The accepted disposition was to keep the datatype model but clarify that repeated given/family parts belong to one name, make the relationship between text and parts explicit, and emphasize HumanName.text for display.

Initial application:
The Jira records "updated humanName narrative. svn revision 4613". The corresponding git-svn commit 998d6fc99991c1662bcf15c1d675a92a99683741, "#5533 - human name clarifications", changed source/datatypes.html to add the requested HumanName narrative guidance about name parts, alternate names, culture-dependent assembly, presentation via text, and search/index use of parts.

Additional context:
The current build-scope datatype page still contains the substance of that resolution: multiple given parts and the family name combine to form a single HumanName, alternate names are separate HumanName instances, HumanName.text is the full display form, assembly order is culture-dependent, systems should use text for presentation and parts for search/indexing, and applications should populate text for robustness. source/fhir.ini marks HumanName as normative and gives it an active datatype code, so source/datatypes.html and source/datatypes/humanname.xml are current build inputs. A later Published core issue, FHIR-12351, intentionally changed HumanName.family to 0..1 and added family-name decomposition guidance/extensions; that later change narrows and refines the family-name side without contradicting FHIR-5533. Searches found no FHIR-5533-specific Zulip or Confluence discussion and no conflicting community evidence.
