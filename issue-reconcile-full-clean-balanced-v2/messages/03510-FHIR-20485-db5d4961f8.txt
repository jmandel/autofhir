FHIR-20485: Audit Person PMI network clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-20485 was a Published Patient Administration change request asking to rename RelatedPerson.patient to a Person reference, or otherwise allow references to both Patient and Person, for Person Master Index person-to-person relationship use cases. The final disposition was Not Persuasive with Modification: RelatedPerson remains tied to a Patient, and the approved change was to clarify the Person narrative so that "networks" means healthcare networks rather than arbitrary personal relationship graphs.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by git commit 70783b76e563431f31927ee0126353e67812ecab (#FHIR-20485 Clarify Person PMI networks described), which changed source/person/person-notes.xml in the Person Master Index section from "associations or networks" to "associations or healthcare networks." The current source still contains that applied wording.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini has active Person and RelatedPerson resource entries and assigns both to PA, so source/person/person-notes.xml and source/relatedperson/structuredefinition-RelatedPerson.xml are in current build scope. RelatedPerson.patient still targets Patient only, which is consistent with the Not Persuasive with Modification decision and Lloyd McKenzie's Jira/Zulip rationale that RelatedPerson is owned by a patient record rather than a general Person-to-Person graph. PA minutes on 2021-05-12 record the 4-0-0 vote and the specific clarification that "network" is not a network of people but the healthcare network. Later FHIR-41559 clarified that a RelatedPerson can also be represented as a Patient in their own right and use Patient.link, but it did not reopen the FHIR-20485 request to allow RelatedPerson.patient to target Person.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Person narrative contains the requested healthcare-network clarification, and the RelatedPerson Patient-only reference remains the correct result of the final Jira disposition.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-20485 unless PA later decides to add a distinct Person relationship extension or resource for general person-to-person graphs.
