FHIR-33395: Audit BDP property value type expansion

Issue request:
FHIR-33395 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked how to store blood group, Rh factor, and bag volume for BiologicallyDerivedProduct instances, and the accepted resolution was to expand the data types agreed in FHIR-33068 for BiologicallyDerivedProduct.property.value[x] to cover Quantity, Ratio, Range, CodeableConcept, string, and Period.

Initial application:

That commit added Period and Ratio to property.value[x] and updated the explanatory comment while preserving the FHIR-33068 property model.

Additional context:
FHIR-33068 is the foundation for the BDP.property backbone and value[x] element. The current source/fhir.ini actively includes biologicallyderivedproduct in the resource list, so the StructureDefinition source is in current build scope. The current source also keeps an example binding for property.type and BDP examples with ABORhD CodeableConcept values, while property.value[x] supports Quantity for bag volume. Confluence OO HCP minutes from 2021-11-01 list FHIR-33395 as done; no directly relevant Zulip discussion or conflicting Confluence full-text hit was found.
