Additional context:
The apparent missing value binding is intentional rather than stale source. Later Published issue FHIR-8479 explicitly requested removal of the Observation.value[x] example binding because choice-element bindings caused profiling problems, while retaining explanatory narrative intent. Commit 861e258d1a204905f5c0a41386f0d95cb5495ce9 removed the Observation.value[x] and Observation.component.value[x] bindings and added narrative guidance. AutoFHIR reconciliation:
