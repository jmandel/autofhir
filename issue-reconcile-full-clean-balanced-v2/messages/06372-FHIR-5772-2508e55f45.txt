FHIR-5772: Confirm patient-animal species wording

Issue request:
FHIR-5772 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration change request for DSTU1 Patient.animal. It asked for the species definition to say high-level taxonomic categorization and for comments explaining that non-human patients need a recognized taxonomic species classification, finer-than-species codes must imply species, and breed may carry further taxonomic or non-taxonomic classifications such as strain or production type.

That commit also carried unrelated Patient deceased search-parameter work for Tracker#5531.
