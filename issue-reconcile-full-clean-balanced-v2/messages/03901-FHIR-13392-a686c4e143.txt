FHIR-13392: Audit Medication IDMP PSS supersession

Issue request:
FHIR-13392 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request asking to enhance the Medication resource package and ingredient areas to align with the IDMP Authorized Medicinal Product model. The recorded resolution did not direct a concrete Medication element change; it closed the tracker because an approved Project Scope Statement would develop MedicationKnowledge and a medication logical model, superseding the tracker.

Initial application:
No current source commit was found that directly applies FHIR-13392 by key. The relevant history shows the PSS path was pursued through a draft MedicationKnowledge resource added in 2018, while a same-family Medication package/product tracker later recorded that similar requirements no longer related to Medication and were incorporated into MedicationKnowledge.

Additional context:
The current build still includes Medication as an active Pharmacy resource, but MedicationKnowledge was removed from the active resource list in 2025. The current build also includes active medication-definition resources such as MedicinalProductDefinition, PackagedProductDefinition, AdministrableProductDefinition, ManufacturedItemDefinition, RegulatedAuthorization, Ingredient, and SubstanceDefinition, and the Medication Definition module describes these as the detailed product, packaging, administrable/pharmaceutical product, and ingredient model for regulatory/manufacturing contexts. Medication remains scoped to direct-care identification/definition with limited package-size and ingredient detail rather than a full IDMP authorized medicinal product model.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Adding IDMP-specific Medication enhancements now would contradict the final Jira disposition, duplicate the separate medication-definition model, and potentially reopen a Pharmacy/BR&R boundary decision that later source history has already evolved.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If Pharmacy wants a new MedicationKnowledge, FormularyItem, or IDMP alignment change after the 2025 MedicationKnowledge removal, it should be handled as a new work-group decision rather than as a late source edit for FHIR-13392.
