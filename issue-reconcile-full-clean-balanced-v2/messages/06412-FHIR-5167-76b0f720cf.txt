FHIR-5167: Audit body site modeling

Issue request:
FHIR-5167 is a Published Patient Care change request from the Jan 2015 ballot. It asked for Condition and Procedure to model body site consistently, with the final persuasive-with-modification resolution calling for a repeating bodySite element whose nested site[x] could be either CodeableConcept or Reference(BodySite).

Initial application:
No git commit explicitly tagged FHIR-5167 was found. The legacy source history shows the BodySite resource and choice-style body-site work being added around the 2015 ballot cycle, Procedure/ProcedureRequest bodySite being made repeatable in March 2015, and Condition.location being renamed to Condition.bodySite in August 2015. BodySite was later renamed to BodyStructure in the core source in 2017, and the resource remains active in the current build.

Additional context:
Later applied Patient Care decisions supersede the exact 2015 nested site[x] shape. FHIR-39630 added Condition.bodyStructure 0..1 Reference(BodyStructure) with an invariant preventing simultaneous Condition.bodySite, and FHIR-39288 did the same for Procedure. FHIR-51792 then restored Procedure.bodyStructure to 0..1 after the FHIR-41690 cardinality discussion. Current unresolved/triaged requests, including FHIR-54987 and FHIR-54992, discuss possible future CodeableReference harmonization but are not final applied decisions.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current in-build source has Condition and Procedure active in source/fhir.ini, keeps bodySite as repeating CodeableConcept, adds bodyStructure as Reference(BodyStructure), and constrains the two representations so implementers choose coded body site or referenced BodyStructure. That current pattern preserves the substance of FHIR-5167's consistency goal while reflecting later final decisions; restoring the obsolete nested BodySite choice would contradict those later applied issues.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Do not alter Condition or Procedure for FHIR-5167 unless a later work-group decision, such as the open CodeableReference harmonization proposals, is finalized and applied.
