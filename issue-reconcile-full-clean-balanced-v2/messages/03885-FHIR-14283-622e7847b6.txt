FHIR-14283: Audit PlanDefinition Questionnaire actions

Issue request:
FHIR-14283 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for STU3 and asked for PlanDefinition/CarePlan realization guidance to make clear how PlanDefinition/$apply results relate to CarePlan/RequestGroup, and to expand the action definition element so a Questionnaire can represent a data-collection step in the plan.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by GF#14283 commits 745e5821c8 and d2ee3b22ca, matching the Jira comments that cite SVN 13263 and SVN 13327. The first commit added Questionnaire as an allowed PlanDefinition.action.definition target, and the second updated the Applying a PlanDefinition narrative to describe handling ActivityDefinition, PlanDefinition, and Questionnaire actions.

Additional context:
RequestGroup was later renamed to RequestOrchestration by FHIR-24619. Later PlanDefinition/$apply work changed the result model: FHIR-33169 moved away from the older CarePlan/useCarePlan framing toward Bundle output, FHIR-46313 relaxed the strict RequestOrchestration-wrapper wording so results may include CarePlan, RequestOrchestration, and individual request resources, and FHIR-49988 preserved the non-ActivityDefinition/PlanDefinition definitional resource types while documenting Task-with-focus realization for resources such as Questionnaire. No direct Zulip or Confluence record for FHIR-14283 was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. In the current active build, PlanDefinition.action.definition[x] and RequestOrchestration.action.definition[x] both allow Questionnaire and say it should be filled out, while the PlanDefinition realization narrative and operation definition reflect the later approved Bundle/request-resource semantics rather than the older CarePlan/RequestGroup wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. The current source already preserves the FHIR-14283 data-collection capability and has been intentionally refined by later CDS decisions, so restoring the older CarePlan/RequestGroup wording or adding duplicate Questionnaire prose would be misleading.
