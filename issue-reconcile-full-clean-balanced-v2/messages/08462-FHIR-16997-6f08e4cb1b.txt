FHIR-16997: Audit bulk format status history

Issue request:
FHIR-16997 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core FHIR-I correction for the STU3-era formats page. It asked that the Bulk Data Formats section on FHIR-core-formats section 2.6.0.1.4.1 be declared non-normative.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records svn revision 14248. The corresponding git-svn commit c186eb68d4e1ad666db113ba8611651919b0ce38 explicitly mentioned GF#16997 and GF#16425 and wrapped the Bulk Data Formats subsection in draft-content with a warning that the bulk use formats were draft until further experience was gained. The later FHIR-18448 page split moved that applied text from source/formats.html into source/resource-formats.html while preserving the draft warning at that time.

Additional context:
FHIR-16425 is the same 2018 change family and requested the same section be flagged Informative; FHIR-16500 separately corrected the NDJSON MIME type and said the NDJSON page should not have been normative then. Those 2018 decisions established the historical STU/draft posture, not a permanent prohibition on later normative advancement. Current source/hierarchy.xml links source/resource-formats.html and source/nd-json.html into the Resource Definitions section. Later R6 work changed the status deliberately: commit 270e0ceb7b029dad4ca0fc5ccf5296ff370888cd removed the draft-content warning from source/resource-formats.html and changed source/nd-json.html from Draft to Normative, and 2025-09 FHIR-I WGM minutes record the R6 review noting "JSON, NDJSON (already normative)".

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Re-adding a non-normative/draft declaration to the current FHIR source would undo a later R6 normative-status decision rather than complete FHIR-16997. The current source is therefore correct for this issue family after considering the original application and later status history.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce the 2018 draft warning for FHIR-16997 unless FHIR-I or FMG makes a new decision to reverse NDJSON/bulk-format normative status.
