FHIR-5187: Audit Person individual wording

Issue request:
FHIR-5187 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 Patient Administration change request for the Person page. It objected to the wording "A natural person has identity outside of a healthcare setting" and the final resolution changed that line to "An individual has identity outside of a healthcare setting."

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in git commit dc2afae9f6e31b220e90a9d948c46e3743363e68, a grouped Person ballot reconciliation commit whose message explicitly included tracker 5187. That commit changed source/person/person-introduction.xml from "A natural person has identity outside of a healthcare setting" to "An individual has identity outside of a healthcare setting."

Additional context:
The Person resource is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini, and current source/person/person-introduction.xml no longer contains the old sentence because c385e310a0370638c7178832f5c6032cdbb26128 later rewrote the Person scope narrative. The replacement text still uses the resolved terminology by describing Person as a linkage resource for "an individual (human or animal) across multiple roles," and source/person/structuredefinition-Person.xml describes Person as a linkage resource for "an individual across roles." Focused source searches found no remaining "natural person" wording in the Person or RelatedPerson source. Quoted Zulip and Confluence searches found no relevant conflicting discussion or minutes for FHIR-5187 or the challenged wording.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current authoritative Person source already avoids the rejected "natural person" wording and preserves the resolved concept through later-maintained "individual" wording, so reintroducing or duplicating the old sentence would not improve the current specification.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-5187 unless the Patient Administration work group separately chooses to revisit the broader Person scope wording introduced after DSTU1.
