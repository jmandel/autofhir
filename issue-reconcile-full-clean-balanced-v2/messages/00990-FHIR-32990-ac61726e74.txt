FHIR-32990: Audit AdverseEvent.actuality clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-32990 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core AdverseEvent clarification for R5. It asked to make AdverseEvent.actuality clearly distinguish actual adverse events from near misses, and to add guidance that AllergyIntolerance is the proper place to capture an individual's propensity to an adverse reaction.

Initial application:

Additional context:
AdverseEvent is in current build scope through source/fhir.ini, and the current structure definition still contains the requested FHIR-32990 definition and comment. The AdverseEvent introduction also describes potential adverse events as near misses and points propensity/risk concepts to more specific resources.
