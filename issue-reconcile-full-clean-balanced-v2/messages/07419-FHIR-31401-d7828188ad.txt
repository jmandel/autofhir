FHIR-31401: Audit _count page-size clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-31401 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core search clarification for R5. It asked to clarify that _count limits the number of resources returned in a single page, not the total search set, and to add that servers supporting search or history SHALL support checking the _count parameter.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation through PR #1282.
