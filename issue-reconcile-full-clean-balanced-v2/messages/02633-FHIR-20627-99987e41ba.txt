FHIR-20627: Audit Procedure occurrence Timing support

Issue request:
FHIR-20627 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification for the STU3 ballot request that Procedure.occurrence support Timing for recurring historical or ongoing procedures such as physical therapy or counseling twice weekly. The accepted resolution was to rename Procedure.performed to Procedure.occurrence, add Procedure.occurrenceTiming, and remove the procedure-schedule extension.

Initial application:

The current refactored source preserves that application in the active Procedure StructureDefinition.

Additional context:
Procedure is an active resource in source/fhir.ini. The current source/procedure/structuredefinition-Procedure.xml has Procedure.occurrence[x] with committee note GF#13246, GF#20627 and a Timing type. The R4-to-current alias table maps Procedure.performed[x] to Procedure.occurrence[x], and the active Procedure-date SearchParameter expression includes Procedure.occurrence.ofType(Timing). The old procedure-schedule definition is absent from core source and now appears in the FHIR Extension Pack as version-specific historical support ending at FHIR 4.3 with guidance to use Procedure.occurrenceTiming in R5+.
