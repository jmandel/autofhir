Issue request:
FHIR-43516 was an Applied/Persuasive FHIR-I R6 change request noting that the Subscriptions Framework page repeated the notification delivery limitation warning in both the Subscription Notifications overview and Scoping Responsibilities sections. The approved resolution was to align the conformance-strength wording and add the Detecting Delivery Errors link so the duplicate text would be consistent or removed.
