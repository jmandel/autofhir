FHIR-15812: Audit Device component hierarchy

Issue request:
FHIR-15812 was a Published, Persuasive with Modification STU3 Device issue asking whether merging Device and DeviceComponent would solve modeling for multi-part devices. The request described sub-component devices that can also be standalone devices with their own UDI, type, version, production specification, and other data, and proposed using only Device resources linked by a parent relationship so the top device is the one without a parent. The final resolution said this had already been done and includes UDI and other information whether represented as a parent or a sub-component.

Initial application:
No source commit in the local history explicitly mentions FHIR-15812. The issue appears to have been applied through the broader R4 Device rework: commit 10fb28b796d9747f22d741bdee57bad72337b880 changed Device and added DeviceDefinition, commit 1834c25435b71a9069172abf5cc96b0b6d6f79de removed DeviceComponent from active build/navigation listings, and commit a7f2575daa updated Device narrative to say Device can represent a whole device or a component that points to its parent. OO minutes from 2018-07-17 record the same implementation path: DeviceDefinition, UDI carrier naming, and a core Device parent 0..1 relationship for the DeviceComponent merge.

Additional context:
Device and DeviceDefinition are active OO-owned resources in source/fhir.ini, while DeviceComponent is commented out. Current Device narrative says Device is for actual instances, DeviceDefinition is for device kind/model definitions, and Device can represent either the device in total or a component of an encapsulating device that points to its parent. The authoritative Device StructureDefinition includes instance identifiers, repeatable UDI carriers, type, version/property data, and Device.parent as a 0..1 Reference(Device) with a hierarchy extension and vital-signs-monitor-module example. DeviceDefinition now covers the kind/model side and explains that complex point-of-care devices can be represented by a hierarchy of Device resources. Related Published issues FHIR-13965 and FHIR-16960 are the same change family; FHIR-16960 is already represented on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scoped source already implements the seed resolution: DeviceComponent's component-hierarchy role was refactored into Device with a parent reference, while kind/model information is separated into DeviceDefinition and instance/UDI details remain on Device.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Future work should address only specific later Device, DeviceDefinition, or UDI trackers; FHIR-15812 itself does not identify a remaining current-spec source defect.
