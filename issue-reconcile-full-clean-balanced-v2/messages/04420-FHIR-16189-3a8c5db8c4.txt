FHIR-16189: Audit AdministrativeGender concept maps

Issue request:
FHIR-16189 was a Published/Not Persuasive with Modification PA change request for the STU3 Patient-related AdministrativeGender value set. It asked to add an authoritative SNOMED CT ConceptMap and remove duplicated v2/v3 AdministrativeGender ConceptMap links. The final resolution rejected the proposed SNOMED CT map as clinical-sex/clinical-gender finding content rather than administrative gender content, and accepted only removing the duplicate v2/v3 map rendering caused by tooling.

Initial application:

By the thread's follow-up, the May ballot duplicate rendering was gone.

Additional context:
Current core source keeps Patient active and normative, with AdministrativeGender defined by the Patient code system and the terminology bindings worksheet. The worksheet has v3 and v2 mapping columns for AdministrativeGender and no SNOMED CT mapping column or SNOMED CT target values. Later related ConceptMap/example issues such as FHIR-17670, FHIR-24900, and FHIR-29947 concern v2 target exposure, ConceptMap rendering, or example comments, and do not revive the rejected FHIR-16189 SNOMED CT map request.
