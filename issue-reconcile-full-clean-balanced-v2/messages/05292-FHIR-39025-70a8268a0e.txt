Issue request:
FHIR-39025 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5 by FHIR-I. It asked whether SubscriptionTopic.resourceTrigger.queryCriteria.resultForCreate, resultForDelete, and requireBoth are server/topic-definition controls or client-overridable Subscription request values, and requested clearer narrative; the resolution required further documentation explaining resultForCreate/resultForDelete as topic-definition semantics that cannot be changed or overridden by a Subscription, while declining to rename the elements.

Initial application:
Commit ba83bbc88184d7b8c6140c49f65b0e693eb35ea8 applied FHIR-39025 by changing SubscriptionTopic StructureDefinition comments/definitions for supportedInteraction, resultForCreate, resultForDelete, and requireBoth, and by adding SubscriptionTopic narrative explaining queryCriteria behavior during create, delete, and update interactions plus trigger/filter separation. The original R5 resourceTrigger path has since been refactored to the current SubscriptionTopic.trigger path, with fhir.ini aliases preserving the R5 element names.

Additional context:
SubscriptionTopic is an active current-build resource. The current SubscriptionTopic narrative separates trigger criteria from client filters, states that triggers are topic-level tests used to generate notifications for any Subscription linked to the topic, and states that actual Subscription filter values live on Subscription. The current queryCriteria section explains resultForCreate/resultForDelete/requireBoth behavior and includes a behavior table. The Subscription resource exposes filterBy fields only, not resultForCreate/resultForDelete/requireBoth. FHIR-39028 is the same change family for SubscriptionTopic trigger/filter timing and is already represented on the combined branch. FHIR-50619 is a later R6 clarification, also already represented on the combined branch, that corrected the create/delete examples to use test-passes/test-fails and confirms rather than reverses the FHIR-39025 interpretation.
