The final resolution says CBCC was actively working toward a DSTU 2.1 ballot with an implementation guide.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly citing FHIR-7531 was found. Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini lists Consent and Contract as active resources, has no active profiles entry for consentdirective, and records that ConsentDirective became Consent. The old Privacy Consent Directive IG entry is commented in source/igs.xml, and source/guides/pcd is no longer present. Current build-scope Consent source contains Scope and Usage text, Privacy Consent Directive background, examples, and a Consent StructureDefinition; adding a 2015 DSTU 2.1 draft warning to the current Consent page would be stale rather than corrective. Companion ConsentDirective/Contract issues FHIR-7532, FHIR-7533, FHIR-7253, and FHIR-7871 are already represented on the combined branch with no-change audits, and FHIR-6284 is being audited separately because it is the same direct resource-vs-profile decision family.
