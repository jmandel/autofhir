Initial application:

Additional context:

Current definitions for paymentIssuer, outcome, disposition, allocation.target, allocation.response, allocation.submitter, and allocation.payee convey the same implementer semantics as the approved modified FHIR-18004 family after those later changes.

Recommendation:
