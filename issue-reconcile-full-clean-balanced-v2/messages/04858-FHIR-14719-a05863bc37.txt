FHIR-14719: Audit Patient gender guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-14719 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification STU3 Patient issue. It asked for better guidance around patient gender representation: remove the old Meaningful Use-specific birth-sex wording, refer to a US-specific birth-sex extension without linking to US Core, add and reference a Clinical Gender Observation example, and include the Gender Identity standard extension from FHIR-13843.

Initial application:
The issue family was applied in 2018. Commit 7fa7a34f97 (#14482) rewrote the Patient Gender and Sex section, removed the old "for Meaningful Use" birth-sex sentence, and added Gender Identity and other sex/gender bullets. Commit f71751e893, explicitly marked part of GF#14719, created the clinical-gender Observation example and listed it under Observation examples. Commit b7d0f473c3 explicitly named 14719 and 14754, linked the Patient page to that Observation example, and corrected the US-specific birth-sex sentence. The Patient and Observation resources are active in source/fhir.ini, so the checked source locations are in current build scope.

Additional context:
Later Patient Administration and Gender Harmony decisions intentionally changed the current model. FHIR-29673 refreshed the Patient Gender and Sex section around Gender Identity, Sex Parameter for Clinical Use, clinical observations, Recorded Sex or Gender, Legal Sex, and Sex assigned at Birth. FHIR-39203 then moved sex/gender exchange recommendations out of the FHIR base Patient narrative and into the Gender Harmony Implementation Guide. Current core source links to extension-pack definitions such as individual-genderIdentity, and existing combined-branch audits for FHIR-14754, FHIR-14755, FHIR-14756, FHIR-29673, and FHIR-38861 independently reached no-change conclusions for neighboring Patient sex/gender requests.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source no longer contains the old gender-section Meaningful Use birth-sex sentence; it has current Patient Gender and Sex guidance, the Clinical Gender Observation example still exists and is registered in Observation examples, and Gender Identity is represented through current extension-pack links. Reintroducing the old Clinical Gender or direct birth-sex exchange wording on the Patient page would partially reverse later Gender Harmony decisions. The remaining generic "US Meaningful Use Program" example in patient-introduction.xml is outside the Patient Gender paragraph addressed by this tracker and is not a FHIR-14719 source defect.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-14719 unless PA/Gender Harmony makes a new decision to restore direct base-spec guidance for clinical gender or birth-sex exchange instead of relying on the current Patient section, extension-pack content, and Gender Harmony IG.
