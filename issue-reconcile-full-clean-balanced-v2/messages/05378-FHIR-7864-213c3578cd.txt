FHIR-7864: Audit Condition status split

Issue request:
FHIR-7864 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU1 Condition. It asked that Condition.clinicalStatus not combine record status such as entered-in-error with confirmation status such as working, confirmed, or refuted. Its final resolution treated it as a duplicate of FHIR-7581.

Initial application:
The duplicate target FHIR-7581 resolved to split Condition.clinicalStatus into verificationStatus and clinicalStatus and to add a usage note about Condition.stage and Condition.clinicalStatus interdependencies. Git commit 0a6643ad139240e0f578cbe596e007d6c516df3e, "Split Condition.status {#7581} {#7617} {#7864}", applied that family by adding Condition.verificationStatus and a separate Condition Clinical Status value set.

Additional context:
The current build-scope Condition resource keeps clinicalStatus and verificationStatus separate: clinicalStatus is required and bound to condition-clinical, while verificationStatus is optional and bound to condition-ver-status with refuted and entered-in-error in that status family. Later published Patient Care decisions refined the exact shape: FHIR-14821 added entered-in-error/clinicalStatus interaction guidance, FHIR-22973 was largely superseded, and FHIR-29409 made clinicalStatus 1..1 with an unknown code/comment. During this reconciliation, the tightly related FHIR-19730 List narrative hyperlink defect was fixed in a separate source commit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no FHIR-7864 source change. The authoritative current Condition source already reflects the approved split and later decisions, and changing it to introduce a separate record-status element would go beyond the published duplicate resolution and subsequent Patient Care decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-7864 in core unless Patient Care opens a new issue to revisit whether entered-in-error should move out of Condition.verificationStatus entirely.
