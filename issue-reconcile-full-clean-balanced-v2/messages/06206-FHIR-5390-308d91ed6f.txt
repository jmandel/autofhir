FHIR-5390: Audit binding strength replacement

Issue request:
FHIR-5390 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU1. The ballot comment asked why the old binding-definition properties isExtensible and Conformance were distinct when conformance was not Required; the recorded resolution was to use svn 4300 to remove isExtensible and Conformance and replace them with a single strength element, referring to FHIR-3187.

Initial application:
The Jira resolution appears to have been applied by git-svn build@4300, commit f45d3ec3d1, which added ElementDefinition.binding.strength, inactivated the former ElementDefinition.binding.isExtensible and ElementDefinition.binding.conformance rows, and added binding-strength terminology prose.

Additional context:
FHIR-3187 records the detailed work-group motion for the same binding model change, and an existing combined-branch audit commit already concludes that the current source implements that decision. FHIR-5781 is the same ElementDefinition.binding rationalization family, and FHIR-7472 later cleaned up vestigial profiling/terminology references after the binding-strength replacement. Those related issues are already represented on the combined branch and do not require another commit here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. FHIR-5390 is satisfied by the existing binding-strength model; follow-up should occur only through a new terminology/profiling issue if the work group wants to change current binding-strength semantics.
