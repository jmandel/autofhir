FHIR-5755: Audit QuestionnaireResponse example scope

Issue request:
FHIR-5755 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 Patient Care ballot comment for FHIR-core-QuestionnaireAnswers. It asked that the QuestionnaireAnswers element/examples be general and usable for multiple purposes, not limited to medications and immunizations; the recorded disposition was to make clear there were no such limitations and to ensure Questionnaire and QuestionnaireAnswers examples were consistent.

Initial application:
The issue was pre-applied in migrated SVN revision 4174, now git commit da69de4c27ee463d8f8b538d46833c7f48daff79, which changed source/questionnaireanswers/questionnaireanswers-introduction.xml to replace the narrower example list with the same broader examples used for Questionnaire, including research/CRFs, quality/evaluation forms, patient intake forms, and insurance claim forms. Later history renamed QuestionnaireAnswers to QuestionnaireResponse in c51b8682420300c0f51dd0ad260d0be7f5ae20ab.

Additional context:
QuestionnaireResponse is the active current resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current source no longer has QuestionnaireAnswers as the build artifact. The current QuestionnaireResponse examples list is aligned with the Questionnaire examples at a broader level: both include general, lifelines, BlueBook, Glasgow Coma Score, and family-history examples. Current narrative also explicitly says QuestionnaireResponse can capture any type of data and Questionnaire can capture any information at all. FHIR-3015 is related background because it records the broader subject-scope concern for Questionnaire/QuestionnaireAnswers, but it does not require a separate decision here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source changes. The active source already conveys the requested non-limitation through broad current examples and explicit narrative, and adding another statement would be redundant rather than corrective.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-5755 unless the work group wants a new, separate editorial rewrite of Questionnaire/QuestionnaireResponse examples.
