The accepted resolution said the request was already covered by the yet-to-be-applied FHIR-24848 resolution.

Additional context:
FHIR-24848 was a broader Published/Not Persuasive with Modification decision to keep Group focused on passive participant-type members rather than expand it to Evidence/information collections.
