Issue request:
FHIR-5510 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Care DSTU1 clarification asking for the Boundaries section to differentiate ProcedureRequest, DiagnosticOrder/DiagnosticRequest, and ReferralRequest because implementers saw multiple ways to model the same requested service, such as an imaging test.

Initial application:
No git commit explicitly names FHIR-5510. Additional context:
FHIR-6196 is the concrete later Published/Persuasive issue in the same boundary family and is already represented on the combined branch by commit 8393152c9d4f075fab4cf4410b82576a683af2f8. Later source history consolidated the old resources: DiagnosticOrder became DiagnosticRequest, DiagnosticRequest was merged into ProcedureRequest, ProcedureRequest was renamed to ServiceRequest, and ReferralRequest was merged into that same lineage. source/fhir.ini now lists ServiceRequest as active and records DiagnosticOrder as subsumed into ServiceRequest, ProcedureRequest as merged into ServiceRequest, and ReferralRequest as becoming ServiceRequest. Current ServiceRequest source covers diagnostic tests, procedures, specialist consultation/referral-style use, resulting resources, request workflow, and boundaries with Task and CommunicationRequest.
