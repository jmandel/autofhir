FHIR-29554: Audit Substance instance-definition clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-29554 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO change request for R5. It asked to clarify whether a Substance represented an actual instance or a definition/kind by removing the old Substance.instance backbone, moving expiry and quantity to the resource root, and adding a required Substance.instance boolean where true meant instance and false meant definition.

Initial application:
The issue family appears to have been applied in R5 after the R4B revert. Commit beb040d0608727a093902f91d4e3d6675f0c33f6 restored the intended R5 Substance shape by adding top-level Substance.expiry and Substance.quantity, changing Substance.code to CodeableReference(SubstanceDefinition), and adding the boolean Substance.instance element. The Jira comments also record that the changes were present in a May 2021 R5 snapshot, accidentally reverted with R4B work, and then fixed again.

Additional context:
Later BR&R issue FHIR-48405 superseded the boolean-flag approach by deciding that Substance is only for instances and definitions must use SubstanceDefinition. Its source commits, including c7b1723155a5f4c24466742a1fd1b8beedad5ac2 and the existing combined-branch reconciliation commit 1266a70e30373662bcaf36d0e1d4daf8a59f7c1f, removed Substance.instance and stale remnants while preserving top-level expiry, quantity, and the SubstanceDefinition reference. FHIR-43166 was later marked duplicate of FHIR-48405 and supports the same direction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Substance and SubstanceDefinition source already gives an unambiguous answer: Substance is for instances, SubstanceDefinition is for definitions, Substance.code can reference SubstanceDefinition, and the old Substance.instance flag is absent. Reintroducing the FHIR-29554 boolean would contradict the later applied FHIR-48405 decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Future work should not restore Substance.instance unless BR&R/OO explicitly reverses the later instance-only Substance decision; any further cleanup should be handled under the later FHIR-48405/duplicate trail rather than under this R5 clarification.
