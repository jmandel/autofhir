FHIR-13607: Audit Location operating hours support

Issue request:
FHIR-13607 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 Patient Administration change request asking for a way to represent clinic operating hours on Location or Organization. The approved resolution added a Location hoursOfOperation structure with daysOfWeek, allDay, openingTime, closingTime, plus an availabilityExceptions property, modeled after HealthcareService but named for Location.

Initial application:
No explicit git commit mentioning FHIR-13607 was found in the available history. The current source history shows Location.hoursOfOperation present with the requested children and availabilityExceptions before later refactoring. Commit 447e89f4a4 replaced the local backbone children and string exception with the reusable Availability datatype while preserving the Location.hoursOfOperation element and adding comments that availableStartTime and availableEndTime are the opening and closing times.

Additional context:
FHIR-17622 later confirmed that physical Location hours should retain their Location-specific name and remain distinct from service or role availability, while adding unavailable-time support. FHIR-25381 introduced the shared Availability datatype and the Organization/ExtendedContactDetail contact availability extension, and FHIR-36017 later made Location.hoursOfOperation 0..1 because repeated availability blocks live inside Availability.availableTime. Zulip discussion likewise distinguishes Location building hours from HealthcareService and PractitionerRole/service availability and notes that Organization does not have a core hours element.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. On the combined branch, source/fhir.ini actively includes Location and Organization; Location.hoursOfOperation is present as 0..1 Availability, and Availability contains repeated availableTime entries with daysOfWeek, allDay, availableStartTime, availableEndTime, plus notAvailableTime description/during for exceptions. That current representation preserves the implementer-facing capability requested by FHIR-13607 and its later refinements.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Do not reintroduce the obsolete inline Location.hoursOfOperation child backbone or Location.availabilityExceptions string; the current Availability datatype is the maintained representation. Organization-wide or contact-specific availability should continue to be handled through the later HealthcareService/Location/PractitionerRole guidance and the ExtendedContactDetail availability extension rather than by adding a new core Organization.hoursOfOperation element under this old issue.
