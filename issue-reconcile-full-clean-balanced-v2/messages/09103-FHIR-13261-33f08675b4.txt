Issue request:
FHIR-13261 was a Published FHIR-core Conformance change request for STU3, resolved Persuasive. It asked to document that a CapabilityStatement retrieved with a specific user's credentials can reflect the capabilities granted to that user, while still noting the capabilities endpoint should be available before authentication when authentication is required.
