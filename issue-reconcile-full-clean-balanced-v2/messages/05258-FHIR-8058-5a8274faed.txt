FHIR-8058: Audit Procedure focal-device guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-8058 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for Procedure. It asked that Procedure.device clearly include implanted devices, with the definition changed from "A device change during the procedure." to wording covering implanted, removed, or otherwise manipulated devices as a focal portion of the Procedure, and asked for Procedure.used usage notes distinguishing incidental devices from devices that are the focus of the procedure.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in commit 512b2462e3, which explicitly listed #8058, renamed Procedure.device to Procedure.focalDevice, changed the old Procedure.device definition to the implanted/removed/otherwise-manipulated focal-device wording, and added Procedure.used notes for incidental devices versus devices that are the focus of the procedure. Later source refactors preserved that content in the current StructureDefinition source.

Additional context:
Procedure is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. The current source uses Procedure.focalDevice rather than the old Procedure.device name. Procedure.focalDevice currently says it is a manipulated, implanted, or removed device and defines it as a device that is implanted, removed, or otherwise manipulated as a focal portion of the Procedure. Procedure.used currently points implanted or removed devices to Procedure.focalDevice.manipulated, and the Procedure notes retain the broader incidental-device versus focus-of-procedure guidance. FHIR-34525 later corrected a stale Procedure.used reference from Procedure.device to Procedure.focalDevice.manipulated, confirming that the renamed element is the current expression of this issue family. Targeted Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-8058-specific discussion or conflicting later decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scope Procedure source already conveys the FHIR-8058 rule under the renamed Procedure.focalDevice element and associated Procedure.used notes.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up core source change is needed for FHIR-8058 unless the work group separately decides to revisit the broader Procedure.used versus Procedure.focalDevice boundary.
