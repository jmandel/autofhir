FHIR-11089: Audit AllergyIntolerance and Condition alignment

Issue request:
FHIR-11089 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Patient Care request from the DSTU2 era to address naming inconsistencies between AllergyIntolerance and Condition: keep AllergyIntolerance.patient and Condition.subject, resolve status naming through the same block vote, change AllergyIntolerance.reporter to asserter with RelatedPerson support, rename AllergyIntolerance.attestedDate and Condition.dateRecorded to assertedDate, and make AllergyIntolerance.onset polymorphic.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by ed170ff0754268f2a677a011f9d432d1328415f0, which explicitly cites #11089 and synchronized AllergyIntolerance and Condition for asserter, assertedDate, and onset while the same commit also applied FHIR-11151 by splitting AllergyIntolerance.status into clinicalStatus and verificationStatus.

Additional context:
Both AllergyIntolerance and Condition are active resources in source/fhir.ini. Later Patient Care decisions changed the date model: FHIR-17285 moved assertedDate out of the core resources, added recordedDate to both core resources, and the assertedDate definitions now live in the Extension Pack. Later FHIR-34395 temporarily introduced participant, but FHIR-39513, FHIR-39602, and FHIR-44517 reverted the current resources back to explicit recorder/asserter elements.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active source has AllergyIntolerance.clinicalStatus and verificationStatus, keeps AllergyIntolerance.patient while Condition.subject supports Patient or Group, has AllergyIntolerance.onset[x], has recordedDate on both resources after the later assertedDate decision, and has explicit recorder/asserter elements with RelatedPerson support and no active reporter/dateRecorded/attestedDate XML elements in these resources.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Further source edits for FHIR-11089 would either reverse later Patient Care decisions or make redundant editorial changes; any future assertedDate work belongs with the Extension Pack or a new Patient Care decision.
