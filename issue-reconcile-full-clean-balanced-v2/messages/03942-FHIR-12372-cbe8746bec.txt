FHIR-12372: Audit Claim and EOB careTeam placement

Issue request:
FHIR-12372 was Published/Persuasive for the FM work group and requested moving Claim.item.careTeam, and the equivalent ExplanationOfBenefit item careTeam, to a root-level careTeam backbone with a required positiveInt sequence and item-level positiveInt links replacing the nested item careTeam.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git commit d7b9445f7d0910f6dd2552bd623d21f3c365447b, "Tracker #12372", on 2016-11-19. That commit changed the then-authoritative Claim and ExplanationOfBenefit spreadsheet sources and examples by adding root Claim.careTeam and ExplanationOfBenefit.careTeam backbones, adding careTeam.sequence, disabling the old item-level careTeam backbones, and adding item-level careTeamLinkId references. It did not change ClaimResponse, and the checked pre-change ClaimResponse/EOB addItem definitions had no addItem careTeam element to move or replace.

Additional context:
Claim and ExplanationOfBenefit are active resources in source/fhir.ini, and the current source has since moved from spreadsheets to StructureDefinition XML. The apparent name mismatch with the Jira text is explained by the later Published/Persuasive FHIR-14254 sequence-link naming decision: fhir.ini maps Claim.item.careTeamLinkId to Claim.item.careTeamSequence and ExplanationOfBenefit.item.careTeamLinkId to ExplanationOfBenefit.item.careTeamSequence, and FHIR-14254 already has its own combined-branch reconciliation commit. A later FHIR-46624 decision removed Claim.careTeam.responsible and ExplanationOfBenefit.careTeam.responsible, so that current difference from the original 2016 backbone is a separate later change rather than an FHIR-12372 defect.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. Current Claim and ExplanationOfBenefit definitions already carry the careTeam backbone at the resource root, make careTeam.sequence 1..1 positiveInt, and provide item-level 0..* positiveInt careTeamSequence links. Current ClaimResponse and ExplanationOfBenefit addItem definitions were checked; they do not contain a stale nested careTeam and instead use their own item/detail/subDetail sequence links and provider elements.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. The current build already reflects the substantive FHIR-12372 model change after later approved naming and careTeam refinements, and adding new careTeam links to addItem would be unsupported by the original implementation history and later current-source shape.
