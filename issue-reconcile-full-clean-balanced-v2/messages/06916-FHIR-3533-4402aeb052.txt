FHIR-3533: Audit ContactPoint datatype rename

Issue request:
FHIR-3533 was a Published FHIR-core datatypes change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, after Patient Administration proposed renaming the Contact datatype because it conflicted with resource contact components and also asked about a preferred contact flag. The final recorded resolution was to rename the Contact data type to ContactPoint, with the Jira comments saying it was applied in SVN revision 2919.

Initial application:
The rename was applied in historical source by git commit 05f794cd7a297f22268684c1f9191ab7d1df51f1, whose subject is "Change Contact to ContactPoint" and whose git-svn-id points to trunk/build@2919. That commit renamed source/datatypes/contact.xml to source/datatypes/contactpoint.xml and updated datatype pages, examples, mappings, diagrams, and source/fhir.ini references from Contact to ContactPoint.

Additional context:
ContactPoint is now an active datatype in source/fhir.ini and is marked normative. Current datatype source and narrative pages expose ContactPoint, not a standalone Contact datatype. The preferred-flag part of the original description was not in the FHIR-3533 final resolution; later related decisions FHIR-7625 and FHIR-7626 handled that preference concern by rejecting a redundant preferred=true/false flag and adding ContactPoint.rank, and the FHIR-7626 audit is already present on the combined branch. Later FHIR-16984 and FHIR-38824 only clarified rank semantics.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scoped datatype source already reflects the approved ContactPoint rename, and the current ContactPoint.rank mechanism covers the preference-ordering concern without reintroducing a boolean preferred flag.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up core source edit is needed for FHIR-3533 unless a future MnM decision reopens the ContactPoint naming or preferred-contact model.
