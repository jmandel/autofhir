FHIR-14446: Audit Patient Care workflow alignment

Issue request:
FHIR-14446 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Patient Care change request asking the work group to clear workflow-report warnings for CarePlan, ClinicalImpression, Communication, CommunicationRequest, Condition, FamilyMemberHistory, and Procedure. The approved actions included workflow mapping changes and suppressions, CarePlan activity doNotPerform/statusReason updates, CommunicationRequest doNotPerform/statusReason and performer mapping updates, ClinicalImpression status/effective alignment, CarePlan status/intent/author-contributor changes, instantiates target updates, and Procedure.performer.role to function.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in several 2018 source commits. GF#14446 commits on 2018-03-02 and 2018-03-04 renamed CarePlan.activity.detail.prohibited to doNotPerform and added workflow warning suppressions; the 2018-06-20 GF#14446 commit applied the main mapping, element, binding, and exception changes; and the 2018-07-03 GF#14446 commit applied the remaining June vote, including CommunicationRequest.recipient mapping, CarePlan contributor/author changes, instantiates target changes, and Procedure.performer.function.

Additional context:
The affected resource folders are active in source/fhir.ini. Current source keeps the durable FHIR-14446 semantics, but not every 2018 name remains literal: CarePlan.author was later renamed to CarePlan.custodian and paired with CarePlan.contributor, and applied FHIR-40500 later removed top-level instantiates[x] elements from CarePlan, Communication, FamilyMemberHistory, and Procedure because workflow patterns moved to more precise extensions. Confluence minutes and the linked Zulip workflow thread match the Jira votes and do not show a conflicting unresolved requirement.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already expresses the approved workflow decisions through active resource definitions and mapping-exception files: CarePlan has Request.status/intent mapping, custodian/contributor, activity statusReason as CodeableConcept, and activity doNotPerform; CommunicationRequest has statusReason, doNotPerform, recipient mapped to Request.performer, and category not mapped as Request.code; ClinicalImpression has status/statusReason/effective workflow alignment; Condition.asserter no longer maps to Event.performer.actor; FamilyMemberHistory records the Event.occurrence exemption; Communication.recipient maps to the event actor role; and Procedure uses performer.function.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Future edits should be handled under the later, more specific workflow issues that changed these surfaces, especially FHIR-40500 for instantiates removal and FHIR-48444 for CarePlan contributor/custodian FiveWs mapping.
