It asked what the gray-background grouping in the table meant and whether a table should be rendered; the approved resolution said the grouping was resources and to call it a list unless the section was deleted.

Initial application:
No git commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-16074 was found. Additional context:
FHIR-17015 and FHIR-17016 are the tightly related BackboneElement Specializations table issues from the same page and ballot cycle. AutoFHIR reconciliation:

Recommendation:
