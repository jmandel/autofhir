Issue request:
FHIR-13159 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for STU3 and asked to allow PractitionerRole for the ProcedureRequest performer use case, where naming only a Practitioner could not express a practitioner acting in a role such as surgeon for a department. Its resolution moved the change to the workflow patterns, adding PractitionerRole to Request.requester, Event.performer, Request.performer, and Definition.publisher.

Initial application:
The Jira comment identifies SVN#12374. In the git mirror, commit 5064224bd079b7f4ef4023d3ead9111b57bd8bff is the SVN#12374 workflow-pattern application and shows the requested PractitionerRole additions to Request, Event, and Definition pattern rows.

Additional context:
ProcedureRequest was later replaced by ServiceRequest. The current active Request logical model includes PractitionerRole on Request.requester and Request.performer, and the current ServiceRequest requester and performer reference targets include PractitionerRole. The Definition.publisher part of the historical resolution was later superseded by Published FHIR-40406, which aligned Definition with CanonicalResource by making publisher a string.
