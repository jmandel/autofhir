Issue request:
FHIR-16084 was Published/Persuasive for the STU3 conformance-rules page. It asked to replace the negative statement that system capabilities not described by a CapabilityStatement cannot be considered FHIR conformant with the affirmative rule "Systems can only claim FHIR Conformance for functionality described in the applicable CapabilityStatement." The Jira also asked to consider removing the adjacent "limited utility" assertion sentence, but the final resolution was the affirmative wording update.
