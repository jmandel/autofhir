Initial application:

Later search-page refactors added a dedicated `_source` anchor and description, removed the older top search-parameter Summary Table that existed when FHIR-20638 was raised, and retained a consolidated Summary Tables row for `_source`. FHIR-28313 and FHIR-31711 are same-family/later `_source` summary-table issues and are already represented on the combined branch, so they are cited as context rather than republished.
