FHIR-31827: Audit SubstanceDefinition supplier definitions

Issue request:
FHIR-31827 was a Published/Persuasive BR&R change request asking that the tautological SubstanceSpecification supplier definition be clarified and that manufacturer and supplier be differentiated, ideally with examples. The accepted discussion settled on manufacturer as the entity that creates, makes, produces, or fabricates the substance, and supplier as the source/distributor that may differ from the manufacturer.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the SubstanceSpecification-to-SubstanceDefinition R5 source. Commit 2ac0ae1b79cf51fd3a9cf869dea843ac91929bf8 first replaced the old supplier tautology with supply-chain wording while also carrying related manufacturer wording from FHIR-31826. Commit b779dcf8e3da5a0c0514c75b5435be2b09c8a691 made an editorial modal-word refinement. Commit 9be2c2e67c4213e88ec07cbc8f55595e065bd428 then applied the final FHIR-31827 wording for both SubstanceDefinition.manufacturer and SubstanceDefinition.supplier, matching the Jira comment consensus.

Additional context:
The current build no longer has a SubstanceSpecification resource entry; source/fhir.ini actively lists substancedefinition=SubstanceDefinition and assigns it to BR&R, so the current in-scope artifact is source/substancedefinition/structuredefinition-SubstanceDefinition.xml. FHIR-31826 is a same-area supporting decision for the manufacturer cardinality explanation and is preserved as the second sentence of the manufacturer definition. FHIR-25054 is historical background for adding the manufacturer and supplier elements. A BR&R Confluence minute records FHIR-31827 as change applied and persuasive with a 10-0-0 vote, and no Zulip or Confluence search found conflicting implementation guidance.
