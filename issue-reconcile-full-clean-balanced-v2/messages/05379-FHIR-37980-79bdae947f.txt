FHIR-37980: Audit SupplyDelivery boundary update

Issue request:
FHIR-37980 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO clarification for R5 SupplyDelivery. It requested replacement Scope and Usage plus Boundaries and Relationships text to distinguish SupplyDelivery's movement/delivery context from MedicationDispense and DeviceDispense association of an item with a specific patient for use, while preserving the implementation-guide-dependent purchase-date guidance.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied in c3e16ec64ef2620d5708d9e53c0859bf58c7f415, which changed source/supplydelivery/supplydelivery-introduction.xml to add the requested delivery-vs-dispense wording and replace the boundary paragraph. That commit is an ancestor of the current worker branch. The related FHIR-39552 audit on the combined branch also found that its patient-wording refinement was pre-applied by the same FHIR-37980 source change.

Additional context:
OO/HCP minutes record iterative review of the SupplyDelivery/dispense boundary, followed by the 2022-11-14 Persuasive with Modification motion and 10-0-0 vote to accept the changes. Later SupplyDelivery issues, including FHIR-38818, FHIR-42947, and FHIR-51689, reworked dispatch/reception staging, Transport differentiation, and formatting/links while preserving the FHIR-37980 semantic distinction. Current core history then removed SupplyDelivery from active source/fhir.ini resource/workgroup/security/FMM/code entries in 3b38b80cf23f9cbb306fab8fcedb293315478c32, with current core narrative pages linking SupplyDelivery to the OO incubator instead.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The remaining source/supplydelivery narrative already conveys the requested rule: SupplyDelivery documents dispatch/receipt and movement, does not imply the supply has been dispensed to the patient or person, and leaves association-for-use to MedicationDispense and DeviceDispense. Because SupplyDelivery is no longer an active current-core resource entry, editing the retained inactive core source copy would not improve the current build.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. No FHIR core follow-up is needed for FHIR-37980; if the successor OO incubator artifact is maintained separately, preserve this delivery-vs-dispense distinction there together with the later R6 dispatch/reception and Transport refinements.
