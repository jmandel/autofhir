FHIR-5674: Audit Timing duration non-negative constraint

Issue request:
FHIR-5674 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core datatypes correction asking that the Timing duration invariant say "duration SHALL be a non-negative value" rather than "duration SHALL be a positive value", and that the technical constraint allow zero.

Initial application:
The Jira records the issue as pre-applied. Git history shows commit 91ec40b9322da6cf3528962f82eecaa2d6628ba5 (#5674: fix positive to non-negative) changed source/datatypes/timing.xml from the positive wording and @value > 0 expression to non-negative wording and @value >= 0.

Additional context:
Timing remains an active current datatype: source/fhir.ini routes datatypes through the datatypes page, marks Timing normative, and defines the Timing abbreviation. A later Published Timing cleanup issue, FHIR-8697, concerns timing.code, offset, examples, and notes, but does not reverse or narrow the non-negative duration constraint. Corrected historical generated type profiles also carry tim-4 as non-negative. Zulip and Confluence searches found no direct FHIR-5674 discussion or conflicting community decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source files were changed. The authoritative current spreadsheet source already says "duration SHALL be a non-negative value", has the FHIRPath expression duration.exists() implies duration >= 0, and the XPath/technical expression allows f:duration/@value >= 0 or an absent value. A source-wide search found no remaining old "duration SHALL be a positive value" Timing constraint to correct.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-5674 unless a future work group decision changes the intended lower bound for Timing.repeat.duration.
