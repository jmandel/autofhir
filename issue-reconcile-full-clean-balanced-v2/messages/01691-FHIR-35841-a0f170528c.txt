FHIR-35841: Audit participant.actor descriptions

Issue request:
FHIR-35841 was Published/Persuasive for R5 by Patient Administration. It asked to align Appointment.participant.actor and Encounter.participant.actor wording so Appointment no longer suggested a single person while Encounter suggested multiple persons, especially around CareTeam, Group, and Location planning semantics.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source by b7582c9d70, which updated the Appointment and Encounter participant.actor short/definition text and added the Encounter comment explaining why CareTeam and Location planning participants do not become Encounter.participant.actor targets. The follow-up commit 0d29ecdb21 made the approved wording less absolute by changing "may not" to "might not". Current source still contains those FHIR-35841 changes.

Additional context:
The predecessor FHIR-34141 was Resolved - No Change and explains that Appointment CareTeam participation remains singular in nature: a specific member of the team is selected, and multiple needed members should be represented by repeated CareTeam participants. PA WGM minutes for January 2022 record FHIR-35841 discussion and persuasive disposition. Later FHIR-32347 appended additional Encounter participant.actor comments about optional actors without reversing the FHIR-35841 CareTeam/Location distinction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This is an empty no-change audit commit. Appointment and Encounter are active build resources in source/fhir.ini; the authoritative StructureDefinition XML already has the requested Appointment wording, Encounter wording, Appointment CareTeam/Location targets, and Encounter participant.actor target list excluding CareTeam and Location.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No source edit is recommended unless PA later changes the underlying model for team or location participation; redundant prose would add maintenance risk without changing implementer understanding.
