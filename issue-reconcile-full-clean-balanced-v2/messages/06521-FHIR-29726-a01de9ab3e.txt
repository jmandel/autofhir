FHIR-29726: Audit DeviceDefinition classification

Issue request:
FHIR-29726 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. The accepted change replaced the former DeviceDefinition.type CodeableConcept with a repeatable DeviceDefinition.classification backbone containing classification.type 1..1 CodeableConcept and classification.justification 0..* RelatedArtifact, and asked that the comments describe use of classifications such as GMDN, EMDN, SNOMED CT, risk classes, and national product codes.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation. Historical commits ed34dd7 and b04b42f replaced DeviceDefinition.type with DeviceDefinition.classification and the requested child elements; b04b42f also moved the DeviceDefinition type search parameter expression to DeviceDefinition.classification.type. Commit ac31097 added the requested classification examples comment, and 8b3e126 updated examples that depended on the DeviceDefinition shape.

Additional context:
DeviceDefinition is in active build scope through source/fhir.ini. Current source/devicedefinition/structuredefinition-DeviceDefinition.xml contains DeviceDefinition.classification 0..*, classification.type 1..1 CodeableConcept, classification.justification 0..* RelatedArtifact, and the requested classification examples comment. Current source/devicedefinition/bundle-DeviceDefinition-search-params.xml searches DeviceDefinition.classification.type for the type search parameter. A later related issue, FHIR-46562, addressed the type/conformsTo.category search-parameter split and is already represented on the combined branch, so it was not republished here.
