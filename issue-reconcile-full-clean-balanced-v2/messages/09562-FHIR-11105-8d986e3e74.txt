FHIR-11105: Audit Device UDI multi-barcode guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-11105 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Device change request from the DSTU2 ballot asking whether a UDI carrier could be split across multiple barcodes. The accepted resolution said that, when separate DI and PI barcodes are present, the UDI carrier string may be a concatenation with DI first and then the order of human-readable expression on the label.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in the February 2017 Device UDI cleanup commits, especially 05a8652714 and d9163a93a7, which added the DI-before-PI concatenation comment to the UDI carrier rows. Later source refactors preserved the wording into the current StructureDefinition source.

Additional context:
Device is an active current build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the authoritative current source has Device.udiCarrier cardinality 0..* plus explicit comments on both Device.udiCarrier.carrierAIDC and Device.udiCarrier.carrierHRF covering the separate-barcode concatenation rule. Searches of indexed Zulip and Confluence content found no directly relevant discussion or later contradictory decision, and a related Jira full-text search only found FHIR-11105.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Device source already conveys the published resolution in the relevant UDI carrier elements, so editing would only restate existing guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is recommended for FHIR-11105 unless the OO work group later chooses to revise UDI carrier modeling more broadly.
