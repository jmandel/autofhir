FHIR-28425: Audit Device statusReason resolution

Issue request:
FHIR-28425 was a Published OO change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R5. It reported that Device.statusReason was ambiguous because some reason values described the Device record while others described the physical or operational device, and it suggested restricting record-status reasons while adding separate operational and association reasons as needed. The Jira resolution said the concern had been resolved by FHIR-26300.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-28425 was found. The Jira-cited FHIR-26300 issue was itself resolved as Retracted/No Change, so it is not a current source-edit authority on its own. The underlying Device status concern was later addressed through the Device status issue family: historical commits and later reconciliations made Device.status a record status, removed Device.statusReason from the active Device StructureDefinition, introduced Device.availabilityStatus for the physical device availability, and corrected the remaining status-related terminology and narrative.

Additional context:
Device is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. Current source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml has Device.status as "The status of the Device record." and Device.availabilityStatus as "The availability of the device." XML parsing finds no active Device.statusReason element. The only statusReason strings left in Device.xml are inside commented-out historical Device.operation and Device.association blocks, and related current-build decisions such as FHIR-38904/FHIR-37545 mean those blocks should not be restored for this seed. The current Device notes section also says Device.status is about the record while availabilityStatus is about the device itself. Related reconciliation commits for FHIR-32557, FHIR-34542, FHIR-36213, FHIR-37545, FHIR-38904, and FHIR-46962 are already represented on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The ambiguity raised by FHIR-28425 is absent from the current active Device conformance surface because Device.statusReason no longer exists, Device.status is explicitly record-oriented, and device availability has its own element. Reintroducing operational or association reason elements would conflict with later applied modeling decisions rather than completing FHIR-28425.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. No further core-source change is recommended for FHIR-28425 unless the work group opens a new issue to reintroduce or redesign Device operational/association reason modeling after the later DeviceAssociation and extension-pack changes.
