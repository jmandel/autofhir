Issue request:
FHIR-6284 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 FHIR-core Contract issue from CBCC arguing that ConsentDirective should be promoted to a resource rather than hidden as a Contract profile because implementers expected and understood a ConsentDirective resource. The final resolution was to continue work on the Consent resource.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly citing FHIR-6284 was found. The history shows the old Contract ConsentDirective profile path in earlier Contract source, then commit 5a390b2596 added a draft Privacy Consent Directive IG entry, commit acc3f3137b introduced Consent as a resource rather than a Contract profile, and later Consent commits documented and refined that resource. Commit 94445ca431 records in fhir.ini that ConsentDirective became Consent.

Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini lists Consent as an active resource, has no active profiles entry for consentdirective, and records ConsentDirective became Consent. The old PCD IG entry is commented in source/igs.xml, and current Consent source has its own StructureDefinition, narrative, examples, and privacy consent directive background. Zulip discussions in May 2016 directly mention GF#6284 among the unresolved comments asking for a resource instead of a profile/IG and show CBCC agreement to pursue Privacy Consent Directive as a resource.

Editing the dormant profile or reactivating the old PCD IG would not be a current-spec correction for FHIR-6284.

Keep this no-change audit. Future cleanup of dormant ConsentDirective profile files, historical OID entries, or broader Contract/Consent guidance should proceed under a specific current issue rather than this already-satisfied resource-migration request.
