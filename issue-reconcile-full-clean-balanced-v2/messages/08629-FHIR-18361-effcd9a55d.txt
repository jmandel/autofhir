FHIR-18361: Confirm modifier-extension update scope

Issue request:
FHIR-18361 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked to clarify that systems are not barred from updating unrelated parts of a resource merely because another part contains an unknown modifier extension; the final wording bars modification when the resource as a whole or the element being modified has child modifier extensions it does not understand, or when the modified element is a descendant of an element that contains such modifier extensions.
