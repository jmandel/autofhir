FHIR-11060: Audit Consent policy authority handling

Issue request:
FHIR-11060 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2 Consent. It requested replacing the then-current Consent.policy URI with a structure containing policy.authority 0..1 uri for the entity or organization with regulatory jurisdiction or accountability for enforcing Consent Directive policies, and policy.uri 0..1 uri for the specific policy.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in git commit 687d10304f, which explicitly listed GF#11060 and changed the Consent spreadsheet to add Consent.policy as a backbone with authority and uri child elements. Later source-generation and cleanup commits carried that structure forward until the R5 Consent policy model was deliberately refactored.

Additional context:
The current build includes Consent as an active resource in source/fhir.ini. FHIR-32817, a later Published/Persuasive with Modification Consent issue, explicitly changed Consent.policy and Consent.policyRule into regulatoryBasis, policyBasis, and policyText. Current source reflects that later structure: Consent.regulatoryBasis records the regulatory basis, while Consent.policyBasis.reference and Consent.policyBasis.uri identify the computable policy the organization will enforce. FHIR-43331 and FHIR-43326 are later no-change decisions that clarify/retain this model rather than restoring the old Consent.policy.authority shape, and FHIR-45450 only adjusted policyBasis.url back to policyBasis.uri.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The old DSTU2 authority child was historically applied, and the current absence of Consent.policy.authority is explained by a later final R5 refactor that superseded the old element shape while preserving the implementer-relevant separation between regulatory context and enforceable policy identity.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce Consent.policy.authority for FHIR-11060; any remaining R6 concerns about Consent policy clarity are represented by later non-final issues and should be handled through those work-group decisions, not by reviving the DSTU2 structure.
