FHIR-24828: Audit breaking-change policy reference

Issue request:
FHIR-24828 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core change request asking to add the FMG breaking-change policy to the change/version management page, specifically under "Rules for Inter-version change." The Jira resolution directed that the policy be added there while Bas would start a broader Zulip conversation about whether these rules should live in the specification or elsewhere.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by git commit fe5687b085003c80ef93bc7e6e980d666747fa86, "FHIR-24828 - Add reference to FMG breaking change policy," matching Lloyd McKenzie's Jira comment "Git master fe5687b085." That commit edited source/versions.html in the Rules for Inter-version change section to add the current note that the FHIR Management Group has a process allowing carefully vetted violations of the compatibility rules, with a link to the Confluence Policy for Breaking Changes.

Additional context:
Current source/versions.html is still in build scope as a published page listed in publish.ini. FHIR-I minutes for 2021-12-13 record the work-group discussion for FHIR-24828: the page applies to IGs, a separate policy document was considered, and the group decided to add the material to the current spec while continuing discussion of whether it belongs in a separate specification or community policy document. Earlier FMG minutes and the FMG Zulip thread show the breaking-change policy discussion and the same Google document that the Jira description referenced. Later compatibility-section work, including FHIR-13089 and FHIR-38876, refined the surrounding guidance without removing the FHIR-24828 policy link.

Recommendation:
