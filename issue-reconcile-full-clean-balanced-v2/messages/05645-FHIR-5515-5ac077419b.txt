The then-current effectiveTime[x] row already used dateTime|Period.

Later dosage wording issue FHIR-12839 also said to leave MedicationAdministration dosage as-is while changing other Pharmacy resources, so there is no evidence-backed need for a new editorial dosage rewrite under this seed.
