FHIR-12067: Audit Coverage policyholder and subscriber terms

Issue request:
FHIR-12067 was Published with resolution Not Persuasive with Modification. The request asked to rename the Coverage PlanBenificary/beneficiary concept to Patient; the accepted resolution retained beneficiary, renamed PlanHolder to Subscriber, and added PolicyHolder 0..1 Reference(Patient|RelatedPerson|Organization) for the party that owns the policy.

Initial application:
The historical application is commit 43806174288e821cb06173a72cde96317186412b, whose message includes tracker 12067. It changed Coverage examples from planholder to subscriber, changed the old Coverage.planholder spreadsheet row to Coverage.policyHolder, added Coverage.subscriber and Coverage.subscriberId rows, and left Coverage.beneficiary as the beneficiary/patient element.

Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches did not find a later conflicting decision for FHIR-12067.
