FHIR-32151: Audit Questionnaire required-answer clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-32151 was a Published/Persuasive R5 FHIR-core Questionnaire clarification asking that Questionnaire.item.required=true be clear for question items: a required question must have its own direct answer, not merely answered child questions, and a required group must have at least one descendant question with an answer.

Initial application:

That commit added the requested direct-answer and required-group wording to source/questionnaire/structuredefinition-Questionnaire.xml. The current build-scoped Questionnaire resource still contains that clarification.
