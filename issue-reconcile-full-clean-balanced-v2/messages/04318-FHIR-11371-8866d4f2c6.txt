FHIR-11371: Audit Claim diagnosis DRG rename

Issue request:
FHIR-11371 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU2 Financial Management change request for Claim. It asked to rename the drg attribute in Claim.diagnosis to packageCode, following the package-code terminology being chosen for the same Claim DRG/package concept.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-11371 was found. Git history shows d06f8787e65d9eb23bd903d44c86c650b2fac541, committed 2016-11-19, changed the historical spreadsheet source from Claim.diagnosis.drg to Claim.diagnosis.packageCode and updated the short, definition, and requirements text from diagnosis-specific DRG wording to package billing code wording. That is the apparent original application of this issue family.

Additional context:
FHIR-11370 is the paired Published tracker that asked for a consistent term for DRG/bundle/package codes and resolved with the same packageCode rename. Later Published issue FHIR-29688 explicitly superseded the diagnosis-level packageCode location by requiring diagnosisRelatedGroup 0..1 CodeableConcept at the Claim, ClaimResponse, and ExplanationOfBenefit roots and removal of resource.diagnosis.packageCode. The current build-scope source lists Claim as an active resource in source/fhir.ini and contains Claim.diagnosisRelatedGroup at the Claim root, while Claim.diagnosis has no drg or packageCode child. FHIR-26712 remains Triaged/Unresolved and is background only.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The obsolete drg child requested by FHIR-11371 is absent, the intermediate packageCode child was removed by the later FHIR-29688 decision, and the current Claim source carries the DRG/package concept as Claim.diagnosisRelatedGroup.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. Do not reintroduce Claim.diagnosis.packageCode for FHIR-11371; any future change to make DRG a different structure belongs to the unresolved FHIR-26712 or another final work-group decision, not this DSTU2 rename.
