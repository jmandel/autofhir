The final Persuasive resolution says this was already in STU3.

Initial application:

Current workflow and clinical-summary pages list VisionPrescription with clinical orders/resources. Later FHIR-39279 did not remove VisionPrescription; it retained the resource and moved custodianship to Orders and Observations, and that issue is already represented on the combined branch by commit 389cab6493dfada990d24e42675e8c1b1da10b20.
