FHIR-49190: Audit DiagnosticReport patient performer support

Issue request:
FHIR-49190 is an Applied/Persuasive with Modification OO issue asking DiagnosticReport.performer and DiagnosticReport.resultsInterpreter to allow Patient and RelatedPerson for self-performed and at-home testing, and to add a DiagnosticReport section clarifying how performer/resultsInterpreter relate to subject when those roles are filled by the patient or a related person.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation. Jira comments linked a 2025-10-31 build addition and a 2025-11-04 follow-up after Lorraine Constable noted that the reference target lists still needed Patient and RelatedPerson. Git history shows the narrative clarification was added in the FHIR-49190 branch and is now blamed to fd9b30c, while d00fd84 added Patient and RelatedPerson target profiles to both DiagnosticReport.performer and DiagnosticReport.resultsInterpreter and updated the Event mapping exceptions.

Additional context:
DiagnosticReport is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/diagnosticreport/structuredefinition-DiagnosticReport.xml and source/diagnosticreport/diagnosticreport-introduction.xml are current build inputs. The related FHIR-41691 self-testing issue was an earlier R5 Applied clarification that patient-generated self-test results are usually Observations, but it did not decide the later R6 target-profile question and was superseded in that narrow respect by FHIR-49190. Confluence OO minutes confirm the January 2025 motion to add Patient and RelatedPerson plus relationship clarification, the March 2025 reopen concern, and the October 2025 listing of FHIR-49190 for performer/resultInterpreter Patient and RelatedPerson application. No direct Zulip discussion for FHIR-49190 was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current DiagnosticReport StructureDefinition already permits Patient and RelatedPerson on both performer and resultsInterpreter, and the introduction already explains self-performed/at-home testing, the same-Patient-as-subject case, RelatedPerson acting on behalf of the patient, and the distinction between performer and resultsInterpreter roles.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Future work should only revisit this if OO wants stronger conformance language or an invariant for subject alignment; the applied Jira request itself is represented in active current source.
