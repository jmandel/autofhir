FHIR-7871: Audit Consent resource replacement for Contract

Issue request:
FHIR-7871 was a Published FHIR-core change request from the 2015May ballot, resolved Persuasive, asking where consent status, granted/refused decisions, and advance directive details should be communicated because the then-current Contract-based approach was too generic, too heavy, and lacked meaningful consent examples. The final Jira resolution was that FHIR now has Consent as a Resource, with comments narrowing the active CBCC work to Privacy Consent Directive rather than all advance directives.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly citing FHIR-7871 was found. The historical application path is the untagged Consent resource work: after older Contract-based ConsentDirective commits, commit acc3f3137b prototyped Consent as a resource rather than a profile on Contract, followed by ba3db96597 documenting it and 1264e32b13 simplifying the resource and examples. Current source/fhir.ini lists Consent as an active resource and records that ConsentDirective became Consent.

Additional context:
The current active Consent source addresses the implementer-facing gap: the introduction covers privacy, treatment or refusal to consent, and research consent uses; the structure defines Consent.status, sourceAttachment/sourceReference, Consent.decision permit/deny, and provision-based rules; and the current examples include privacy withholding and treatment-service refusal. Later final decisions refine the interpretation: FHIR-19529 intentionally keeps Contract guidance saying Consent should be used where it applies and may reference Contract; FHIR-23719 removed Advance Directives from current Consent scope; FHIR-22684 and FHIR-29774, already represented on the combined branch, further clarify Consent category/general model and treatment-refusal examples.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current specification already has the Consent resource requested by the Jira resolution, and adding new advance-directive detail or removing the current Contract/Consent relationship would conflict with later final decisions rather than complete FHIR-7871.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Future work on broader advance-directive modeling, current Contract removal, or cleanup of historical Contract consent examples should proceed under specific later issues or a new work-group decision, not under this 2015 Consent-resource replacement issue.
