FHIR-39176: Audit CodeableReference SHALL alignment

Issue request:
FHIR-39176 was a Published FHIR-core R5 change request resolved Persuasive with Modification. It identified that the CodeableReference narrative used SHOULD NOT for constraints on CodeableReference.reference and CodeableReference.concept even though StructureDefinition constraints sdf-24 and sdf-25 are error-severity rules. The approved resolution was to change that narrative rule to SHALL NOT.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied in git commit b2f9914244a825465609e9fe2bf6772f68d402d8 on 2023-02-14, which changed source/references.html from SHOULD NOT to SHALL NOT for the affected CodeableReference child-element constraints. The current reconciliation branch contains that commit.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists CodeableReference as a datatype and maps codeablereference to the references page, so source/references.html is build-scoped source for this narrative. The StructureDefinition source still contains sdf-24 and sdf-25 as error constraints, matching the SHALL NOT interpretation. Later CodeableReference work, especially the FHIR-38957 reconciliation already on the combined branch, clarified targetProfile and binding mapping while preserving the SHALL NOT rule. FHIR-41852 and FHIR-40955 removed the stale follow-up sentence about a possible future SHOULD NOT-to-SHALL NOT change. FHIR-I 2022-11-14 minutes confirm the FHIR-39176 vote, and no conflicting Zulip or Confluence evidence was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active CodeableReference paragraph says targetProfile and binding constraints declared on CodeableReference apply to CodeableReference.reference and CodeableReference.concept respectively, and that these constraints SHALL NOT be specified directly on the .reference or .concept elements.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-39176 unless a future work group decision changes the CodeableReference profiling policy.
