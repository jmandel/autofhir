Issue request:
FHIR-31784 was a Published/Persuasive BR&R change request asking how an AdministrableProductDefinition could have multiple integral devices for one substance and form. The accepted resolution changed AdministrableProductDefinition.device cardinality to 0..1 because no real examples supporting multiple devices were found.
