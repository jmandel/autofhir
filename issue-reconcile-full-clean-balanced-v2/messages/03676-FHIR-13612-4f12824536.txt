FHIR-13612: Audit Claim submit operation

Issue request:
FHIR-13612 was Published/Persuasive for STU3 and requested a Claim $submit operation to support request-response exchange of Claim requests. The accepted change was to add the operation without requiring a Parameters wrapper because the input and output are single resources, with the input being a Claim request resource or a Bundle of request resources.
