Issue request:
FHIR-11092 is a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO issue asking to reopen the FHIR-10295 decision that had renamed NutritionOrder to NutritionRequest. The accepted disposition was to keep NutritionOrder, reversing the prior NutritionRequest rename because "Request" would be misleading for clinical nutrition.

Initial application:
The earlier FHIR-10295 rename was applied in SVN-era git history by commits e6290bb57f and 16997a29ca, which moved the NutritionOrder source to NutritionRequest. A current source search finds NutritionRequest only in fhir.ini's [removed-resources] list, which is appropriate historical/deleted-resource bookkeeping. Related Jira FHIR-10295 remains the prior rename decision that this issue partially reversed; it is not separately audited here because it also covered DiagnosticRequest and is broader than the seed.
