FHIR-7994: Audit numeric search significant figures

Issue request:
FHIR-7994 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Search change request from the DSTU1 era. It asked why the number-search examples described [parameter]=100 as two significant figures and [parameter]=100.00 as four significant figures even though the displayed ranges correspond to three and five significant figures. The accepted resolution was to change the examples to 3 digits and 5 digits.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in SVN revision 6473, represented in git as c6f415f1fa39ad81b28512965f7629e9b929b23a, which changed source/search.html from "to 2 significant figures" to "to 3 significant figures" for [parameter]=100 and from "to 4 significant figures" to "to 5 significant figures" for [parameter]=100.00.

Additional context:
source/search.html is the current active Search page and is referenced from the build hierarchy at source/hierarchy.xml. Later number-search work, including FHIR-17668, FHIR-30096, FHIR-26311, and FHIR-51080, clarified adjacent implicit-range and exponential-form behavior but did not reverse the 3- and 5-significant-figure correction requested by FHIR-7994.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current source/search.html number-search table already says [parameter]=100 uses 3 significant figures and [parameter]=100.00 uses 5 significant figures, matching the FHIR-7994 resolution.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core follow-up is needed for FHIR-7994 unless a future issue reopens the broader numeric-search precision model.
