FHIR-53384: Audit Specimen focus supersession

Issue request:
FHIR-53384 was an Applied OO change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R6, asking Specimen to handle specimens whose testing focus/source is not the record subject. Its recorded disposition first allowed RelatedPerson on Specimen.subject with comments explaining use for other entities, while the description also proposed a Specimen.focus CodeableReference pattern.

Initial application:
The issue was initially tried in commit 7b25639a03303a2b75b2c7117d2fc9f24be94335 as a 0..* CodeableReference Specimen.focus, then commit 1764e013bfe972d03cbc1aac2bbdf5d43e4d6a92 removed that draft focus and added RelatedPerson plus the approved comment to Specimen.subject. PR #4027 merged as 3e23e3cc5a49ded0a18ce95738e48cbfbff44bbb with the RelatedPerson-on-subject application.

Additional context:
The Jira comments explicitly record Lloyd McKenzie's later concern that patient-specific resources need a direct Patient reference and point to FHIR-56301 for the follow-up. FHIR-56301 was subsequently Applied and replaced the RelatedPerson-on-subject approach with a separate Specimen.focus Reference while restoring Specimen.subject as the subject of record.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Specimen StructureDefinition already implements the later accepted family outcome: Specimen.subject no longer targets RelatedPerson, Specimen.focus is 0..1 Reference(Patient | Group | Device | BiologicallyDerivedProduct | Substance | Location | NutritionProduct | RelatedPerson), and the subject comments carry the patient-compartment guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not re-add RelatedPerson to Specimen.subject for FHIR-53384; its original application was superseded by the later, more specific FHIR-56301 decision. If diagrams are regenerated, the stale checked-in specimen.svg may update, but the authoritative XML source is already semantically correct.
