Issue request:
FHIR-36797 was Published/Persuasive for R5 by OO. It requested replacing the BiologicallyDerivedProduct Boundaries and Relationships Medication sentence so that products derived from an individual or small pool, or that are blood group specific, are BiologicallyDerivedProduct; the old wording said "within the same blood group" instead.

Initial application:
The Jira comment points to HL7/fhir commit 6d071e58b52cc9c0c9252d51724e5b341eccdf7e in PR #1887.
