FHIR-36693: Audit Device definition updates

Issue request:
FHIR-36693 was a Published/Persuasive R5 OO change request to update Device.property so it distinguished device-instance properties from DeviceDefinition capabilities, and to update Device.specialization so it described standards the device adheres to and may be certified to for communication, performance, process, or measurement capabilities.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation by f357d3bbda13eb7ad310dccfc812715b3d1afe02, merged by d8612d0db8b4bdbfb09bae8f0a71bc8e660a705c, which changed source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml for Device.specialization and Device.property. The Device resource is active in source/fhir.ini, so that source file is in current build scope. No prior Issue-Reconcile-Key commit for FHIR-36693 was present on the combined branch.

Additional context:
FHIR-36154 had reintroduced Device.specialization shortly before this clarification. Later Published issues FHIR-39084 and FHIR-39242 renamed Device.specialization to Device.conformsTo and broadened the wording to cover standards, specifications, formal guidance, certification, regulation, and related conformance models; those issues are already represented on the combined branch. A later OO decision recorded in FHIR-39322, while not decided here because it remains Resolved - change required, explains why the current Device.property wording now emphasizes static or essentially fixed characteristics and pushes dynamic settings to DeviceMetric and Observation. OO Confluence minutes for 2022-03-28 match the FHIR-36693 vote, and no direct Zulip discussion was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current Device.conformsTo preserves the specialization standards/certification meaning under the later Published name, and current Device.property plus its comment preserves the intended distinction from DeviceDefinition while incorporating the later static-property clarification. Reapplying the exact 2022 FHIR-36693 text would undo later Device wording decisions rather than improve the current specification.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is for FHIR-36693. Any remaining review of the broader Device.property, DeviceMetric, and Observation boundary should be handled under FHIR-39322 or a successor, not by changing the already-applied FHIR-36693 reconciliation.
