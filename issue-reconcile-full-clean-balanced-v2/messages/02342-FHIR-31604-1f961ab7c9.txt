Additional context:
BR&R minutes from 2021-03-23 record that the ClinicalTrials.gov mapping could handle registration and reporting and identified companion code/extension work. Current ResearchStudy has been refactored by later R5/R6 decisions, so the right current-state test is preservation of the useful semantics rather than literal restoration of the 2021 patch. For example, arm is mapped to comparisonGroup, sponsor/principalInvestigator are represented through associatedParty.party, currentState/statusDate became progressStatus, and R6 moved outcomeMeasure under objective. Extension-related material from the 2021 work is no longer all core-local; researchStudy-siteRecruitment and researchStudy-studyRegistration are present in the FHIR Extension Pack.
