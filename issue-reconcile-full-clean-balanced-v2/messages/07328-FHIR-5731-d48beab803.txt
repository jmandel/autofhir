FHIR-5731: Audit BodySite location collapse

Issue request:
FHIR-5731 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification OO decision for the DSTU1 BodySite resource. It asked to collapse the prior split between specific sites and relative sites, remove the need to choose between specificLocation and relativeLocation, change BodySite.name to BodySite.code, and collapse side, aspect, anatomical plane, and number into one repeating modifier CodeableConcept.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied before this reconciliation in commit 30f165af9ed7458068df3a2b54b7b6b474f9772d, whose subject explicitly says it pre-applied #5716, #5731, and #5852 for the BodySite resource. In the BodySite spreadsheet patch, BodySite.specificLocation was marked deleted, BodySite.code was introduced as a CodeableConcept, and BodySite.modifier 0..* CodeableConcept was introduced with a definition covering laterality, relative location, directionality, number, and plane.

Additional context:
FHIR-5852 is the same-change-family issue from the same OO call and is audited separately in this reconciliation. Later current-build history changed names and shape without reversing the collapse: FHIR-11194 renamed BodySite.modifier to BodySite.qualifier, FHIR-13396 renamed BodySite to BodyStructure, FHIR-33129 restructured BodyStructure into includedStructure/excludedStructure backbones with structure/laterality/qualifier children, and FHIR-45256 split laterality from the general qualifier value set. source/fhir.ini confirms BodyStructure is active while BodySite is removed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative BodyStructure source no longer has separate specificLocation or relativeLocation choices; it has includedStructure.structure as the coded anatomical structure, includedStructure.laterality as the separated laterality qualifier, and includedStructure.qualifier 0..* for other relative-location qualifiers. Current examples and value sets use this collapsed model, and the remaining BodySite.modifier reference is only a legacy rename map.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce retired BodySite location-choice source or edit current BodyStructure under FHIR-5731; any future modeling changes should be handled under current BodyStructure issues rather than reopening this DSTU1 disposition.
