FHIR-12470: Audit MedicationStatement status and adherence

Issue request:
FHIR-12470 was a Published Pharmacy change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2/STU3-era MedicationStatement. It asked to repair the MedicationStatement status plus notTaken/taken comments after element-name, datatype, and status-code changes, and to revisit the table of status/taken combinations including active, completed, intended, stopped, on-hold, entered-in-error, and unknown.

Initial application:
The Jira comments identify git-svn commit 3b901a8b986348f24b8043298ab9a1d288b13d19 as the original TR#12470 application. That commit updated MedicationStatement notes and spreadsheet source, renamed the notTaken binding/element to taken, made taken required with y | n | unk | na, adjusted the reason-not-taken invariant, and added a status-plus-taken interpretation table.

Additional context:
Later Published R5 Pharmacy decisions superseded the old table. FHIR-19468 removed not-taken from MedicationStatement status and directed that taken/not-taken be conveyed through an adherence attribute; FHIR-19470 added the adherence element; FHIR-32060 changed status to recorded/entered-in-error/draft; FHIR-38682 renamed MedicationUsage back to MedicationStatement while keeping the adherence/status redesign; and FHIR-39271 reaffirmed that status reflects the statement record while adherence reflects whether the medication is being taken. Related issue-reconcile commits already exist on the combined branch for FHIR-19468, FHIR-19470, FHIR-32060, and FHIR-39271.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. MedicationStatement is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current source already expresses the correct post-R5 model: MedicationStatement.status is only the status of recording the statement, MedicationStatement.adherence is used for patient taking/not-taking/adherence, effective[x] supplies the time interval for use or non-use, and the adherence code system includes taking, not-taking, on-hold, stopped, and unknown. The only remaining notTaken reference found in fhir.ini is in the historical [r2-renames] section and does not define the current resource.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Do not restore a status/notTaken or status/taken matrix to current MedicationStatement; the later Pharmacy decisions intentionally replaced that model with status plus effective[x] plus adherence. Any future explanatory improvements for MedicationStatement.adherence should be handled under the newer open or post-ballot adherence issues rather than under FHIR-12470.
