FHIR-20380: Audit Flag.subject PractitionerRole support

Issue request:
FHIR-20380 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Care change request raised against STU3 and applied for R5. It asked to add PractitionerRole to Flag.subject so a flag can apply to a practitioner in a specific role, such as unavailable-for-referral as a cardiologist, rather than only to the practitioner identity.

Initial application:
The Jira records GH#d7e990d, and commit d7e990dfb1a7d69472c86bce47f6a2ed933da942 changed the then-authoritative source/flag/flag-spreadsheet.xml from Reference(Patient|Location|Group|Organization|Practitioner|PlanDefinition|Medication|Procedure) to include PractitionerRole and added the GF#20380 committee note. The later source rework carried that target profile into current source/flag/structuredefinition-Flag.xml.

Additional context:
Flag is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini and owned by Patient Care there. Current source/flag/structuredefinition-Flag.xml includes PractitionerRole in the Flag.subject targetProfile list, with the committee note GF#20380, J#37950. FHIR-37950 later added RelatedPerson to the same element and author element; FHIR-56379 later changed Flag.supportingInfo, not this subject target; and FHIR-20183 recorded a no-change decision not to add ServiceRequest or NutritionOrder to Flag.subject. Patient Care minutes for 2019-02-21 list GF#20380 as resolved, and no directly relevant Zulip thread for FHIR-20380 was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-20380 unless a future Patient Care decision revisits the broader Flag.subject target list.
