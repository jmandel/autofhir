FHIR-19334: Audit wiki and tracker link migration

Issue request:
FHIR-19334 was Published/Persuasive for STU3 and requested replacing FHIR specification links that pointed at the old HL7 wiki and gForge tracker with links for Confluence and Jira before R4 publication.

Initial application:
The Jira comment identifies GH#512a4d9c8. That merge, "Merge pull request #270 from HL7/confluence-jira", broadly replaced wiki spreadsheet-authoring links with Confluence links, removed generic gForge tracker references, and introduced the stable http://hl7.org/fhir-issues tracker link for generic issue submission.

Additional context:
A contemporaneous Zulip committers announcement from Lloyd McKenzie explained that generic gForge change-proposal links were intentionally replaced with http://hl7.org/fhir-issues so the redirect could move from gForge to Jira when Jira was ready, while specific tracker items were changed to Jira paths and wiki references were given Confluence placeholders. Current source still contains many intentional Confluence/Jira references and generic hl7.org/fhir-issues tracker links. The only current source hit for wiki.hl7.org/gforge/tracker.fhir.org in the focused scan was a historical Argonaut wiki link in source/history.html, not the FHIR wiki/tracker infrastructure targeted by this issue.
