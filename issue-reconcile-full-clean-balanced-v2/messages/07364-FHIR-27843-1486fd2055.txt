FHIR-27843: Audit search parameter combination cardinality

Issue request:
FHIR-27843 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked that the capabilitystatement-search-parameter-combination extension's required slice allow 0..* instead of requiring at least one required parameter. The approved resolution also required explanatory text that, when no parameters in the group are required, the extension means at least one of the listed optional parameters must be present and that the same rule can be expressed more verbosely by repeating the extension.
