FHIR-46702: Audit NutritionProduct human milk boundary

Issue request:
FHIR-46702 is Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6. It asked that NutritionProduct not be silent about human milk and suggested adding NutritionProduct-side text/linkage corresponding to BiologicallyDerivedProduct guidance, plus asking whether manufacturer/provenance could represent breast milk from a mother or donor.

Initial application:
The issue was applied through a small set of related OO changes rather than a standalone source commit for only FHIR-46702. FHIR-46613 added NutritionProduct-side boundary text for BiologicallyDerivedProduct and donated human milk, FHIR-46604 added the reciprocal BiologicallyDerivedProduct-side text, and commit d0f9f0cb921ca73af9137792711cb1d73df8e5c1 explicitly lists FHIR-46702 while removing the overly narrow NutritionProduct scope wording "for human consumption" and applying the related FHIR-48640 manufacturer/traceability changes.

Additional context:
FHIR-46702's final resolution explicitly delegates the boundary question to FHIR-46613 and FHIR-46604, and delegates the human milk source/manufacturer question to FHIR-48640. FHIR-48640 is already represented on the combined branch by a separate no-change audit commit, so this commit does not duplicate that decision. Later source refactoring in 9c85a7b7d0fb8e2043768ba811c76eb7d5db49a6 consolidated the boundary material into the current Boundaries and Relationships lists while preserving the donated-human-milk ambiguity guidance and active links between NutritionProduct and BiologicallyDerivedProduct.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. NutritionProduct and BiologicallyDerivedProduct are active resources in source/fhir.ini; the current NutritionProduct introduction links to BiologicallyDerivedProduct and says that products for dietary administration are NutritionProduct while transplant, infusion, therapeutic administration, or injection products are BiologicallyDerivedProduct, with donated human milk called out as an ambiguous area for local guidance. The reciprocal BiologicallyDerivedProduct introduction says the same. The current NutritionProduct StructureDefinition also has RelatedPerson as a manufacturer target and keeps instance.biologicalSourceEvent for biological-material traceability.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. The current source satisfies the final FHIR-46702 disposition without adding redundant prose; future work should only revisit this if OO chooses to add more specific nutrition-domain guidance for water, value-set interpretation, or chain-of-custody beyond the bounded FHIR-46702/FHIR-48640 resolutions.
