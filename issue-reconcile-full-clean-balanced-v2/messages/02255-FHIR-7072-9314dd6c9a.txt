FHIR-7072: Audit Composition section title guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7072 was a Published FHIR-core Composition change request with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked whether Composition.section.title should be 1..1 when a section is present; the approved resolution was to add explanatory guidance that sections generally SHOULD have titles, while recognizing documents where a title is unnecessary or inappropriate and noting that most Implementation Guides will require it.
