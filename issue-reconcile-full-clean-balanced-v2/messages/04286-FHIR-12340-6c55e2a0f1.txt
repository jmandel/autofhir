FHIR-12340: Audit MedicationStatement substance-use guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-12340 was a Published Pharmacy change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2. It asked for guidance and examples around substance use in MedicationStatement, with the final guidance saying to use MedicationStatement when substances should participate in active medication use or medication clinical decision support, and Observation when the goal is social history or extra details such as desire to quit, amount per day, or negative health effects.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in 4da69799ea7fe5dff9a28bbb0ca10f6970e9b803, which added a MedicationStatement Scope and Usage paragraph allowing substance abuse, tobacco, and alcohol in MedicationStatement when intended for clinical decision support or active medication lists, and recommending Observation for social history or additional substance-use details. The originating Zulip thread and Jira comments match that applied paragraph.

Additional context:
The same active MedicationStatement introduction has since been narrowed by the later Published Pharmacy issue FHIR-39165. That issue explicitly removed substance-abuse use from scope, directed non-prescribed alcohol/tobacco use to Observation, and allowed MedicationUsage/MedicationStatement only for prescribed agents such as nicotine replacement or long-term-care alcohol. The later decision was historically applied in ccd70e13dace3a42cdffade3560070c4ef2af900 and is already represented on the combined branch by 5a4cf404987a1d31b296878fbed6185a85f360ca, which also removed adjacent stale recreational-drug/substance wording. FHIR-38682 explains the resource name change back from MedicationUsage to MedicationStatement but does not alter this substance-use boundary.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active MedicationStatement source is in current build scope and now says MedicationStatement SHALL NOT be used for substance abuse or non-prescribed tobacco/alcohol, while directing non-prescribed use to Social History Observations. Reinstating the original FHIR-12340 wording would conflict with the later Published FHIR-39165 decision and with the already-published FHIR-39165 reconciliation commit.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future changes to allow non-prescribed substance use in MedicationStatement should be handled as a new Pharmacy work-group decision that explicitly revisits or supersedes FHIR-39165, not as a replay of the older FHIR-12340 guidance.
