FHIR-8210: Audit Goal and CarePlan subject reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-8210 was a Published/Not Persuasive with Modification Patient Care change request raised against DSTU1. It asked why Goal.patient was not 1..1; the approved resolution kept the then-new Goal.subject optional for anonymized care-plan quality evaluation, renamed Goal.patient to Goal.subject and CarePlan.patient to CarePlan.subject with corresponding search-parameter updates, added Group and Organization targets for Goal and Group for CarePlan, and broadened the scope/examples for population, organization, department, team, and herd use cases.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the git-svn history by d6c4981fa8fe7d58cc3e1cc393aa8cc0b6a83c54, whose message corresponds to SVN #6766. That patch changed the old Goal spreadsheet row from Goal.patient 0..1 Reference(Patient) to Goal.subject 0..1 Reference(Patient | Group | Organization), changed the old CarePlan spreadsheet row from CarePlan.patient 0..1 Reference(Patient) to CarePlan.subject 0..1 Reference(Patient | Group), updated many examples from patient to subject, and expanded the Goal and CarePlan introduction text for group, organization, population, and herd scenarios.

Additional context:
Two later Published Patient Care decisions now govern the current cardinality. FHIR-12341 made CarePlan.subject 1..1 because generic or unattached care-plan templates belong in PlanDefinition and de-identified research cases should use de-identified Patient resources; it is already audited on the combined branch in cb56a104bfc153bb2d3d0899d3d8635fb0ecdc94. FHIR-12776 made Goal.subject 1..1 and explicitly rejected omitted subject as an anonymization mechanism; it is already fixed on the combined branch in 45e27de9db1350cd74a70cf95970d1644f164198. Goal and CarePlan are active resources in source/fhir.ini, and the current StructureDefinitions, search-parameter bundles, and introduction pages preserve the FHIR-8210 rename/target/scope work while correctly reflecting the later mandatory-subject decisions.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Reverting Goal.subject or CarePlan.subject to 0..1 would conflict with the later Published FHIR-12776 and FHIR-12341 decisions, while the remaining FHIR-8210 changes are already present in the current active source.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Do not reopen FHIR-8210 as a source fix unless Patient Care adopts a new later decision to make Goal.subject or CarePlan.subject optional again; otherwise the existing current source and the related FHIR-12341/FHIR-12776 reconcile commits should stand.
