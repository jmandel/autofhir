FHIR-8085 was a Published/Persuasive Clinical Genomics change request for the old Observation genetics CIGAR extension. It asked that observed nucleotides differing from the reference be uppercase and nucleotides matching the reference be lowercase, with alignment represented by CIGAR while ObservedAllele and referenceAllele remained plain ATCG sequences.
