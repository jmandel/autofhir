FHIR-7861: Audit Claim scope cleanup

Issue request:
FHIR-7861 was a Published/Persuasive Financial Management change request from the DSTU1/DSTU2 ballot cycle asking that the Claim Scope and Usage text stop describing OralHealthClaim as one of a suite of VisionClaim, PharmacyClaim, ProfessionalClaim, and InstitutionalClaim resources, because those separate claim resources were not in DSTU2.

Initial application:
The current tree has no Jira-tagged FHIR-7861 commit, but the history shows the underlying resource cleanup: commit 80474beb51 moved OralHealthClaim, VisionClaim, PharmacyClaim, ProfessionalClaim, and InstitutionalClaim out of active resources, and commit 92d7d80103 rewrote source/claim/claim-introduction.xml from the obsolete OralHealthClaim suite wording to Claim-centered prose before STU3.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini keeps Claim active in [resources] and lists the old claim resources only in removed-resource/bookkeeping sections. Current source/history.html still records those names in historical release notes, which is appropriate and not Claim scope guidance. Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later or conflicting decision requiring a current source edit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This is an empty audit commit. The active Claim source already says Claim is used by providers and payers/insurers for financial and supporting clinical information, and no active Claim narrative or structure definition retains the obsolete DSTU2-era resource-suite description.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No source follow-up is needed for FHIR-7861 unless the Financial Management work group separately wants broader editorial cleanup of current Claim introductory wording unrelated to the obsolete resource references.
