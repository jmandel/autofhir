FHIR-11304: Audit mean blood pressure note reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-11304 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO change request for the DSTU2 FHIR-core Vital Signs profile table. It asked to replace the mean blood pressure note "Will define this as Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) = 2/3DP + 1/3SP" with "The mean blood pressure is most commonly a direct average taken off of an instrument."

Initial application:
No explicit Jira-keyed source commit for FHIR-11304 was found; a git log search for 11304 only found an unrelated Subversion revision number. The relevant source history shows the issue was effectively pre-applied in commit 0d2d645db3, which changed the mean blood pressure table row from the MAP formula to the direct-average note before the Jira vote/resolution was published.

Additional context:
Observation is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, and implementationguide-Observation-vitalsigns.xml wires observation-vitalsigns-profile-introduction.xml into the current build. Later vital-signs refactoring commented out the standalone mean blood pressure table row while preserving the direct-average note in the commented historical row. FHIR-32788 then directed removal of the blood pressure supplement sentence that still listed 8478-0 Mean blood pressure, and the combined branch already contains the FHIR-32788 reconciliation commit d696144c4669d29fc0a67f35ea28e36e475f93e4. Current active source therefore no longer contains either the rejected formula or active guidance that needs the FHIR-11304 replacement note.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current build-scoped vital signs table has no active mean blood pressure row, has no MAP formula wording, and the remaining mean blood pressure example uses the LOINC code/display without redefining the measurement method.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not re-add the direct-average note unless OO decides to restore an active mean blood pressure row; doing so now would duplicate out-of-scope historical guidance and conflict with the later FHIR-32788 cleanup that removed blood pressure supplement guidance.
