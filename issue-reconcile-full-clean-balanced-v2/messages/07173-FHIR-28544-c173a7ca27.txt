FHIR-28544: Audit CarePlan custodian application

Issue request:
FHIR-28544 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked to rename CarePlan.author to CarePlan.custodian, define custodian as the responsible party to whom the care plan is attributed, note that the custodian may or may not be a contributor, update CarePlan.contributor to include individuals, organizations, or devices that provided the plan contents, and add a custodian search parameter.

Initial application:
The request was applied in git commit decdf89f81, which changed source/careplan/structuredefinition-CarePlan.xml from CarePlan.author to CarePlan.custodian, updated the custodian comment and contributor definition, and added source/careplan/bundle-CarePlan-search-params.xml entry CarePlan-custodian. The later R4-to-R5 migration map in source/fhir.ini maps CarePlan.author to CarePlan.custodian.

Additional context:
The Jira-linked Zulip discussion shows the original distinction between initial authorship, contribution, and ongoing responsibility. Patient Care meeting evidence and the Jira resolution selected the rename rather than adding a new extension. Later FHIR-33954 explicitly declined to redefine the renamed custodian as the person who authored the plan, because that role is represented by contributor, and FHIR-48444 refined only the FiveWs mappings while preserving the CarePlan.custodian and CarePlan.contributor semantics.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current build-scope CarePlan source has CarePlan.custodian with the approved definition and comment, CarePlan.contributor with the approved definition and device target type, and an active CarePlan-custodian search parameter. No current CarePlan.author element or CarePlan-author search parameter remains in the CarePlan source.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-28544 source follow-up is needed in core; future CarePlan ownership/authorship changes should be handled through new Patient Care workflow decisions rather than reopening this applied rename.
