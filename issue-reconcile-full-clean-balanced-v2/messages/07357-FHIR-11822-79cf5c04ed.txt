FHIR-11822: Audit observation grouping guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-11822 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification OO change request for DSTU2 asking for clearer guidance on how to represent observation panels and batteries across DiagnosticReport and Observation. The resolution said best practice varies by use case and realm, but the relevant section should be clarified, with related component-use work tracked in FHIR-10038.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation. Commit 687bf7399e was explicitly tagged [#11822] and expanded Observation notes with panel/battery guidance; the immediately related FHIR-10038 commits d20fec3441, 0909c366e0, and def17105a1 then rewrote that guidance into separate choices for DiagnosticReport.result, Observation.component, and Observation-related grouping. Earlier commit d4505c43ef applied the FHIR-7187 panel/battery terminology in the same guidance family, and later commit 5bdc655967 updated the related-observation mechanism to Observation.hasMember and Observation.derivedFrom. These commits are ancestors of the current worker and combined branches.

Additional context:
Later final OO work refined but did not reverse the seed. FHIR-39168 and FHIR-40540 added the Diagnostics Module parent/child guidance and linked it back from Observation grouping. FHIR-50138 added Observation.organizer for hasMember-based organizer/groupers. R6 no-change decisions FHIR-55019, FHIR-54562, and FHIR-54808 rejected adding List/Group as another DiagnosticReport.result grouping option and preserved the current DiagnosticReport/Observation approach. Confluence minutes show ongoing realm and implementation variation, but no discovered final community or Jira evidence requires a seed-specific source correction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit makes no source change. Observation and DiagnosticReport are active resources in source/fhir.ini, and the current source already gives implementer-facing guidance: DiagnosticReport.result can group report observations under a report or panel code; Observation.component is limited to inseparable component results sharing method/performer/device/time; Observation.hasMember represents panels/batteries whose member observations stand alone and typically have no panel-level value; Observation.derivedFrom covers derived observations; and the Diagnostics Module provides linked parent/child reporting guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current specification already satisfies the FHIR-11822 clarification after later refactors and related OO decisions; future changes to organizer usage or additional grouping patterns should follow separate final Jira decisions rather than reopening this DSTU2 clarification.
