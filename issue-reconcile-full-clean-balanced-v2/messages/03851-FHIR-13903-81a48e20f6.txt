FHIR-13903: Audit CarePlan past activity support

Issue request:
FHIR-13903 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Care change request for STU3, applied for R5, asking CarePlan to support tracking past activities and past interventions by updating CarePlan.activity to cover actions that have occurred as well as planned actions.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in df4a77edaa94a67ae85b9796089ed309a304e6a0, which changed source/careplan/careplan-spreadsheet.xml to the requested short and definition and added the GF#13903 committee note. The 2021 source rework in 33b409a4b6d544598262f2f074ebb4b6dc466157 moved CarePlan from the spreadsheet source into source/careplan/structuredefinition-CarePlan.xml while preserving the GF#13903 note and wording.

Additional context:
CarePlan is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/careplan/structuredefinition-CarePlan.xml is in the current build. Later CarePlan activity work, especially FHIR-26064/J#26064, renamed outcome/reference/detail elements to performedActivity/plannedActivityReference/plannedActivityDetail, and later FHIR-45000 and FHIR-48415 refined performedActivity wording. Those later changes strengthen rather than reverse the FHIR-13903 intent: the current source includes CarePlan.activity.performedActivity for completed or in-progress activities, while CarePlan.activity still says an activity may have occurred or be planned. FHIR-54918 is only Submitted and is not a final conflicting decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The active current source already contains the requested CarePlan.activity short and definition, retains the GF#13903 source note, and has concrete performedActivity support and examples for activities that have occurred.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is for FHIR-13903. No core source follow-up is needed for this published issue; any future wording adjustment to performedActivity should be handled through later issues such as FHIR-54918 if they reach a final workflow state.
