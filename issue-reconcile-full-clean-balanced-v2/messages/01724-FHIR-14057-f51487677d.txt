FHIR-14057: Audit MeasureReport subject generalization

Issue request:
FHIR-14057 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 CQI change request asking to generalize MeasureReport from patient-only reporting to a subject model. The resolution required changing patient to subject, allowing MeasureReport.subject to reference Patient plus Group, Location, Organization, RelatedPerson, Device, and Practitioner, updating $evaluate-measure to use a subject parameter, and adding the analogous Measure subject type indication.

Initial application:
Jira records the issue as applied at SVN 12641. In git, 734c7befc75fc6677ab2dbae8d77bdc07428cc5b is the matching application commit and explicitly says it generalized patient to subject in Measure and MeasureReport. That historical commit changed the then-current Measure and MeasureReport spreadsheets, examples, and $evaluate-measure request text.

Additional context:
Measure and MeasureReport are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini. The current source has MeasureReport.subject rather than MeasureReport.patient, its target profiles include every resource type required by FHIR-14057 and later additions, Measure.subject[x] and Measure.group.subject[x] carry the generalized subject model, and the Measure operations list includes both the current $evaluate operation and retired $evaluate-measure operation. Later related decisions already represented on the combined branch refined the same area: FHIR-21299 audited Measure/MeasureReport subject alignment, FHIR-17355 aligned Group membership wording, FHIR-40422 completed group-level Measure subject support, and FHIR-16822 fixed remaining generic patient-only wording.
