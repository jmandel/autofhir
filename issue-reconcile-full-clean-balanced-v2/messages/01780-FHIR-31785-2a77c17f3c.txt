FHIR-31785: Audit administrable product ingredient clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-31785 was a Published FHIR-core BRR change request for R5, resolved Not Persuasive with Modification. It asked whether AdministrableProductDefinition.producedFrom and AdministrableProductDefinition.ingredient were redundant and requested either an explanation of how they differ or a choice; the approved resolution kept both elements and called for clarifying text that contrasts manufactured items with ingredients and avoids duplicative use.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly tagged FHIR-31785 was found in the source history. The relevant wording appears to have been applied through the 2021 AdministrableProductDefinition documentation changes: producedFrom was clarified as manufactured medication items rather than raw ingredients, and ingredient was clarified as only needed when ingredients are not already supplied via producedFrom/ManufacturedItemDefinition or incoming Ingredient references. The Jira comment then records that changes were applied on 2022-02-02.

Additional context:
AdministrableProductDefinition is active in the current build via source/fhir.ini. Later applied/published decisions in the same change family refined, but did not undo, the distinction: FHIR-31778 replaced the producedFrom wording to distinguish formOf from specific manufactured item components; FHIR-31779 removed misleading language about administrable and manufactured products being the same; FHIR-31808 reversed Ingredient references and made the local ingredient element a coded shortcut; and FHIR-41432 added the current one-to-one producedFrom clarification. A 2020 BRR Zulip thread similarly distinguishes substances/ingredients from products or manufactured components. No Confluence or issue-key Zulip evidence was found that conflicts with this interpretation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already conveys the approved distinction: producedFrom references the specific ManufacturedItemDefinition components used to prepare the administrable form, while ingredient is a CodeableConcept shortcut only needed when ingredients are not specified through those manufactured items or incoming Ingredient resources. Adding duplicate prose for FHIR-31785 would not change implementer understanding and could drift from the later applied wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Future copyediting may clean minor grammar in the ingredient definition under a separate editorial tracker, but FHIR-31785 does not require a current source correction.
