FHIR-26356: Audit qrs-1 invariant wording

Issue request:
FHIR-26356 was Published/Persuasive for R5. It asked FHIR-I/SDC to clarify the QuestionnaireResponse qrs-1 invariant description because "Nested item can't be beneath both item and answer" implied the wrong nesting rule; the approved text was "Item cannot contain both item and answer".

Initial application:
The Jira comment records commit 1da72dddf5b8fcea51b49a0b21e9759c46da6b58. That commit explicitly changed source/questionnaireresponse/structuredefinition-QuestionnaireResponse.xml from the old "Nested item can't be beneath both item and answer" wording to "Item cannot contain both item and answer" while leaving the qrs-1 expression unchanged.

Additional context:
QuestionnaireResponse is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current authoritative StructureDefinition still carries the approved qrs-1 human text next to the unchanged expression (answer.exists() and item.exists()).not(). The originating Zulip discussion confirms the reporter's concern and Lloyd McKenzie's agreement that the intended rule was that an item cannot contain both child item and answer elements. SDC Confluence minutes for 2020-05-21 record the tracker vote. A qrs-1 Jira search found no later final issue reversing or refining this specific invariant wording; related FHIR-31357 nesting guidance is already represented on the combined branch and does not change this decision.
