Initial application:

Later source refactors renamed MedicationPrescription/MedicationOrder to MedicationRequest and moved dosage instructions into shared dosage datatypes while retaining the exemplar binding.

Additional context:
FHIR-8115 is the same change family and explicitly says it is related to FHIR-8018; it covered missing MedicationAsNeededReason reference URLs in MedicationDispense, MedicationPrescription, and MedicationStatement. Current source/fhir.ini lists Dosage and MedicationRequest as active build inputs.
