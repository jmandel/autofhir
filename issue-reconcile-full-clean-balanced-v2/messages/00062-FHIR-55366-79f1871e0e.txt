FHIR-55366: Remove MeasureReport non-modifier metadata

Issue request:
FHIR-55366 was Applied/Persuasive for FHIR-core MeasureReport. The CQI resolution agreed that scoring, dataUpdateType, and improvementNotation do not meet the definition of Is Modifier, and directed the spec to remove Is Modifier and the modifier reason for all three elements.

Initial application:
The issue was partially applied by ddb69d6546, which changed MeasureReport.dataUpdateType, MeasureReport.group.scoring, and MeasureReport.group.improvementNotation from isModifier=true to isModifier=false. That commit left the isModifierReason elements in place and left MeasureReport.group.improvementNotation prose saying the element was labeled as a modifier.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists MeasureReport as an active resource, so source/measurereport/structuredefinition-MeasureReport.xml is in the current build scope. Later MeasureReport refactoring means the current scoring and improvementNotation elements are group-level elements; root-level scoring/improvementNotation are no longer the current targets. FHIR-49670 added Measure-to-MeasureReport population guidance to the improvementNotation comment, and this reconciliation keeps that guidance while removing only the obsolete modifier assertion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit removes the explicit isModifier=false entries and isModifierReason entries from MeasureReport.dataUpdateType, MeasureReport.group.scoring, and MeasureReport.group.improvementNotation, and removes the stale modifier-label sentence from the MeasureReport.group.improvementNotation comment. No unrelated MeasureReport status modifier metadata was changed.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It completes the applied Jira resolution against the active MeasureReport source without changing the substantive interpretation guidance or reopening the broader Measure/MeasureReport refactoring decisions.
