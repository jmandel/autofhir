FHIR-53492: Audit decimal regex syntax correction

Issue request:
FHIR-53492 was Applied with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for FHIR-core datatypes. It reported that the decimal regex had an extra closing brace and proposed also allowing decimals such as .5; the final decision accepted only removal of the extra brace and rejected the leading-zero change as substantive for a normative datatype.

Initial application:
Commit 2a1360e087 applied FHIR-53492 by changing source/datatypes/primitives.xml from the FHIR-22655 regex with `[0-9]{1,9}}` to `[0-9]{1,9}`. There was no prior AutoFHIR audit commit for FHIR-53492 on the combined branch.

Additional context:
The FHIR-22655 reconciliation in the immediately preceding commit changed the decimal regex further to enforce the approved 18-digit mantissa limit, but it preserved the two FHIR-53492 decisions: there is no extra closing brace in the exponent branch, and the regex still requires a leading zero for fractional decimals rather than allowing .5.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit records that no additional FHIR-53492 source change is needed after the FHIR-22655 reconciliation. The active primitive regex and the XML schema override pattern are syntactically balanced and continue to require a digit before the decimal point.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit. FHIR-53492 is satisfied by current source; any future proposal to allow .5 should be handled as a separate normative datatype change, not as part of this syntax correction.
