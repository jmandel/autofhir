FHIR-8551: Audit Observation reference range inclusivity

Issue request:
FHIR-8551 was Published with resolution Not Persuasive with Modification for Observation.referenceRange.low and .high. The issue originally proposed an or-equal-to extension after SimpleQuantity removed Quantity.comparator from the reference-range endpoints, but the final disposition was to make the low/high endpoint descriptions say the bounds are inclusive of the value, e.g. >=5 - <=9, and not to create an inclusive/exclusive extension.

Initial application:
The issue was first pre-applied in August 2015 by commits 138fd1fb, eb4447e, and 8cd2c23 with an inclusive-flag extension approach. Commit 593b20ac then applied the final disposition by changing the Observation.referenceRange.low and .high text to say the endpoints are inclusive and by removing reliance on the extension proposal. Later source refactoring moved the authoritative Observation definition from spreadsheet source into source/observation/structuredefinition-Observation.xml.

Additional context:
FHIR-5165 is the supporting Quantity/SimpleQuantity split that made comparator handling relevant to Observation.referenceRange. FHIR-51566 is a later Applied OO decision for R6 that changed Observation.referenceRange.low and .high from SimpleQuantity to Quantity so explicit exclusive comparators can be represented, but it preserved the FHIR-8551 interpretation by stating that absent low/high comparators remain inclusive. Observation is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/observation/structuredefinition-Observation.xml is in build scope. Zulip and OO minutes from 2025 identify FHIR-8551 as related background for the later exclusive-bounds work and do not contradict the inclusive default.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Observation source already says that when low.comparator or high.comparator is absent, the corresponding reference-range endpoint is inclusive of the value; it also permits explicit >= and <= comparators and uses > or < only when an exclusive endpoint is intended. That preserves the FHIR-8551 no-extension disposition while incorporating the later FHIR-51566 R6 change.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-8551 because the current source carries the inclusive default in the authoritative Observation definition and the later comparator support is a compatible superseding clarification rather than a reversal.
