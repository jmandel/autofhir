FHIR-15941: Audit MedicationRequest not-taken rationale

Issue request:
FHIR-15941 was Published as Persuasive with Modification for STU3 Pharmacy. It asked MedicationRequest to support recording why a medication was not recommended or not ordered, initially framed as a notDoneReason-style need. The approved resolution was to align MedicationRequest to the Request logical model by adding doNotPerform as a modifier flag and updating reasonCode/reasonReference to specify the reason for prescribing or not prescribing.

Initial application:
The Jira comments point to GForge/SVN build r13840. In git history this is 3ca8aaddb2c50c08732cad49f8ab68f086ba8c83, which added MedicationRequest.doNotPerform to the then-current medicationrequest spreadsheet and changed the reason text from a reason for writing the prescription to a reason for ordering or not ordering the medication. The source has since been refactored from spreadsheets into source/medicationrequest/structuredefinition-MedicationRequest.xml.

Additional context:
MedicationRequest is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. Current source keeps doNotPerform as a boolean modifier and points the rationale to MedicationRequest.reason. The historical reasonCode/reasonReference elements have been merged into MedicationRequest.reason as a CodeableReference, and later Pharmacy decisions further refined the same area: FHIR-26594 updated doNotPerform wording, while FHIR-47856 explicitly rejected adding a doNotPerformReason element and clarified that MedicationRequest.reason covers ordering medication to be taken or not taken. Those later issues are already represented on the combined branch, so this seed should not duplicate their commits.
