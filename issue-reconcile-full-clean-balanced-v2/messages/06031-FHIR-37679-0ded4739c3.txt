FHIR-37679: Audit canonical version above/below clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-37679 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for the FHIR-core Search page. It asked how :above and :below should behave when used with canonical business versions, especially whether a value such as |1 is simple text/prefix matching that could also match values like 10.0, 21.0, or 2.1.0. FHIR-I resolved that above and below may be used for versions only when a version-comparison mechanism is available, and then below means less-than and above means greater-than under that algorithm.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied by source commit 1bcd09ca79aba31bbad941f372c0be3f34a54162 on 2022-08-26. That commit changed the active Search page source, source/search.html, adding canonical-reference text under :above and replacing the pending FHIR-37679 placeholder under :below with language requiring a known version scheme, ignored-or-rejected unknown schemes, and greater-than/less-than comparisons under the resource's version scheme.

Additional context:
The related FHIR-26370 and FHIR-27920 decisions evolved the original "version-comparison extension" idea into the current CanonicalResource.versionAlgorithm[x] mechanism and related versioning guidance. Later Applied issues FHIR-51466, FHIR-52895, and FHIR-46311 refined the same search/version family: current source/search.html now allows a specified or inferred version scheme, states that unknown-scheme criteria are ignored or rejected, says :above/:below compare against the version scheme and exclude supplied-version matches, and says unmodified [canonical]|[version] searches are exact token matches. Current source/references.html contains the canonical version matching rules and versionAlgorithm guidance that these search paragraphs link to. Confluence minutes for 2022-06-27 and 2022-07-18 show FHIR-I discussion and approval of the FHIR-37679 resolution; targeted Zulip searches found no conflicting FHIR-37679 discussion.
