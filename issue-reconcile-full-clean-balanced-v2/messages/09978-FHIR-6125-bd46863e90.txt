FHIR-6125: Audit profiling descriptive-element rules

Issue request:
FHIR-6125 was Published as Persuasive with Modification for DSTU1. It asked the profiling page to state that profiles cannot remove definitions, usage notes, rationale, or mappings in a differential; when creating a snapshot directly, definitions SHALL NOT be removed, while usage notes, rationale, and mappings MAY be omitted only as a presentation choice that does not make the inherited guidance untrue.

Initial application:
The Jira comments identify SVN revision 5529. Git commit ad7501455ceb4065e483002477c5ec314f64c615, carrying git-svn build@5529, applied the issue to source/profiling.html by adding comments/usage notes and mappings to the list of profile statements, changing the descriptive-text consistency rule, adding a note that mappings and constraints could not be removed but could be omitted for clarity, and adding snapshot-tool wording about not suppressing mappings or constraints.

Additional context:
Later final FHIR-I decisions deliberately refined the 2015 no-removal model. FHIR-20385, already audited on the combined branch, made the current profiling page say inherited ElementDefinition code, comment, requirements, alias, example, and mapping content can be removed when profile constraints make those descriptive properties invalid or irrelevant. FHIR-31406, already fixed on the combined branch, added the ElementDefinition-suppress mechanism for selected descriptive properties, including comment, requirements, and mapping. FHIR-20500, already fixed on the combined branch, separately documents ElementDefinition.constraint.suppress for warning or best-practice constraint messages while retaining inherited constraints in snapshots. These later decisions supersede an absolute reading of FHIR-6125 for usage notes, rationale, mappings, and warning messages, but preserve its core rule that inherited meaning cannot be invalidated and definitions/constraints are not simply deleted.
