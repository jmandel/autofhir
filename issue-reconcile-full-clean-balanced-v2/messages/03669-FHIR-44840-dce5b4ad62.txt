FHIR-44840: Audit Device owner reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-44840 was an Applied OO FHIR-core change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R6, asking whether Device.owner meant legal ownership or responsibility for provision and maintenance. The approved motion removed Device.owner and its search parameter, moved ownership/custodianship/related roles into DeviceAssociation guidance, renamed DeviceAssociation.category to DeviceAssociation.relationship, added Organization and PractitionerRole to DeviceAssociation.subject, bound relationship extensibly with owner/custodian/patient/operator-style codes, and added a relationship search parameter.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation by FHIR-44840-tagged source commits. Commit cce271e159 corrected the relationship search-parameter description and patient relationship wording. Commit 3cf635fa2b added Device/DeviceAssociation narrative guidance that Device.owner was removed and DeviceAssociation should be used for ownership/custodianship.
