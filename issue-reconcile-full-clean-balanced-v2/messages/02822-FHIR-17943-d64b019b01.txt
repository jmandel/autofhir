FHIR-17943: Audit Coverage copay value datatype

Issue request:
FHIR-17943 was a Published FHIR-core Coverage change request from FM, resolved Persuasive with Modification for STU3. It asked whether Coverage.copay.value should remain Quantity with monetary meaning conveyed by system, and the final resolution was to change the attribute to a choice of SimpleQuantity or Money.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied before this reconciliation in Jira-tagged commit 7fbe7046270ff6857644a6af63207642576dc7ef. Before that commit, source/coverage/coverage-spreadsheet.xml modeled Coverage.copay.value as Quantity; after the commit, the Coverage model used Coverage.costToBeneficiary.value[x] with type SimpleQuantity|Money and added a valueMoney example.

Additional context:
Coverage is in current build scope through the active source/fhir.ini resources entry. The current authoritative source is source/coverage/structuredefinition-Coverage.xml, where the STU3 copay concept is now represented as Coverage.costToBeneficiary with alias CoPay, and Coverage.costToBeneficiary.value[x] permits Quantity constrained to SimpleQuantity and Money. The historical spreadsheet source was later converted into the current StructureDefinition source without losing the datatype choice. Zulip and Confluence searches found no relevant or conflicting community record.
