FHIR-13347: Audit Media and Observation reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-13347 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification STU3 OO change request asking that Media be allowed as an alternative to Observation for photo, video, and audio observations. The accepted resolution removed Observation.value[x] Attachment in that era, added direct Media relationship targets for Observation-derived content, and noted ongoing Media/Observation alignment.

Initial application:
The change was partially applied in the pre-R4 source history by GF#13347 commits: ded8a0f added the ballot note and boundary text explaining removal of valueAttachment and use of Media, and 37fadcd removed Attachment from the Observation.value[x] choice list. No current source file contains the FHIR-13347 key itself.

Additional context:
Later final decisions changed the model. FHIR-13421 transferred Media boundary ownership to OO. FHIR-15145 and FHIR-18304 declined additional Media-as-Observation linkage requests, pointing implementers to DiagnosticReport or derivedFrom DocumentReference where appropriate. FHIR-19250 then merged Media's practical role into DocumentReference and removed Media from the active resource list. FHIR-24693 explicitly restored Observation.valueAttachment for R5/R6 and required guidance on valueAttachment versus Observation.derivedFrom and DiagnosticReport.presentedForm.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. In the current build, source/fhir.ini lists Observation and DocumentReference as active resources but not Media; Observation.value[x] and Observation.component.value[x] include Attachment; Observation.derivedFrom points to DocumentReference, ImagingStudy, ImagingSelection, QuestionnaireResponse, and Observation, not Media; and DiagnosticReport.media.link points to DocumentReference. That current state reflects the later decisions rather than an unapplied FHIR-13347 defect.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Re-applying FHIR-13347 literally would incorrectly reintroduce an out-of-build Media dependency and reverse later final work-group decisions; any future concern about multimedia observations should be handled against the current Observation/DocumentReference/DiagnosticReport model, not by restoring Media references.
