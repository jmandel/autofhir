FHIR-5756: Broaden CarePlan definition authorship

Issue request:
FHIR-5756 is a Published/Persuasive Patient Care ballot issue asking the CarePlan definition to be broadened beyond practitioner-only care planning. The accepted resolution says CarePlan instances can be authored by, and participated in by, the patient and RelatedPersons.

Initial application:
The historical CarePlan rework commit db4ace37c6615c9732f958d34d6286fe739fc657 explicitly listed #5756 and added old-spreadsheet support for CarePlan.author as Reference(Patient|Practitioner|RelatedPerson|Organization), plus patient and RelatedPerson participation/performer support. A follow-up 2015 introduction commit added self-maintained patient or care-giver authored CarePlan examples, and later refactors preserved Patient and RelatedPerson target profiles on the current custodian, contributor, reported, and performer elements. However, the current resource-level description/definition and the active Nutrition module CarePlan row still described only how practitioners intend to deliver care.

Additional context:
CarePlan is active in source/fhir.ini, and nutrition-module.html is an active module page referenced from source/hierarchy.xml and module lists. A 2021 Zulip implementers thread also confirms the current interpretation that CarePlans do not have to come from a clinician and can be created by patients. No direct Confluence reference to FHIR-5756 was found, and no later source evidence was found that narrowed CarePlan authorship back to practitioners only.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the authoritative CarePlan StructureDefinition description and root definition to remove the practitioner-only limitation and state that care may be intended by practitioners, the patient, related persons, or other participants. Updated the active Nutrition module CarePlan summary so it does not preserve the stale practitioner-only wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this source-changing commit as-is. It completes the FHIR-5756 intent in current active source without changing the already-correct Patient and RelatedPerson target profiles on CarePlan participation/contribution elements.
