FHIR-32000 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core BRR change request for R5. It asked to add a reference from RegulatedAuthorization to Ingredient so eAF manufacturer approval for a product ingredient can be represented without treating it as whole-product authorization or abstract substance authorization.
