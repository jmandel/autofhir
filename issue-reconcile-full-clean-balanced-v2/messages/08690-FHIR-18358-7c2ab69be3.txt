FHIR-18358: Audit clarified extensibility wording

Issue request:
FHIR-18358 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core clarification from the STU3 extensibility page. It asked that the "Exchanging Extensions" sentence stop using an unclear "they" in "The more the requirements are shared ..., the less impact they will have" and reword the thought more clearly.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source history. Commit db4aa401e536dc5971c109c0b5139a2e708f2c61 changed the original "less impact they will have" wording to "less of an obstacle they will represent". The Jira comment points to commit 63bfbcb974fa08850e59717ca80301a77bed8daa, which further clarified the referent as "the extensions (and the requirements they represent)".

Additional context:
The active source/fhir.ini type-page mapping routes the Extension type page to extensibility, so source/extensibility.html is current build input. Later source edits touching the same page preserved the clarified sentence, and targeted Zulip/Confluence searches found no contrary community record.

This commit intentionally makes no source change.
