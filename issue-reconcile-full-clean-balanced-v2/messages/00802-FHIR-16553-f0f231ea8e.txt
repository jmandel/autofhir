FHIR-16553: Audit Sequence patient compartment application

Issue request:
FHIR-16553 was Published as Persuasive for STU3. It asked that the then-current Sequence resource be considered part of the Patient compartment because Sequence represented patient genetic data and was missing from the Patient compartment definition.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation. Commit 2e96a94 added Sequence to the Patient column in source/compartments.xml, and the Jira comment records that commit as the applied change. Three days later, FHIR-19453 renamed Sequence to MolecularSequence in commit 78abae0217 while preserving the Patient compartment entry and fhir.ini patient categorization.

Additional context:
The original Zulip discussion already noted that Sequence belonged in the Patient compartment only "as defined right now" and that CG was exploring a definitional model that would no longer be patient-specific. That later model became explicit in FHIR-51227, an Applied/Persuasive with Modification R6 issue replacing MolecularSequence with MolecularDefinition and stating that genetic data was strategically decoupled from patient/encounter context. The combined branch already contains an audit for FHIR-51227, so this commit does not duplicate that decision. Current fhir.ini no longer has Sequence, MolecularSequence, or MolecularDefinition in the active [resources] list; Sequence remains only as a removed-resource name, MolecularSequence has only legacy/commented entries, source/molecularsequence is absent, and source/compartments.xml has no MolecularSequence or MolecularDefinition Patient-compartment row.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The seed request was correctly applied to the resource that existed at the time, and current core source is correct after later CG decisions because there is no active Sequence/MolecularSequence resource to add to the Patient compartment and the successor MolecularDefinition model is not patient-compartment scoped in core.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. Do not re-add Sequence, MolecularSequence, or MolecularDefinition to the Patient compartment in core unless a future CG work group decision reverses the later MolecularDefinition decoupling/replacement model and makes a build-scope resource patient-specific again.
