FHIR-29660: Audit USSG repeat placement

Issue request:
FHIR-29660 was a Published/Persuasive technical correction for the US Surgeon General family-history Questionnaire examples. It asked to fix the USSG Questionnaire because linkId 2 was marked repeating while the repeated answer content was actually under linkId 2.1, and to understand why validation had not caught the mismatch.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records PR https://github.com/HL7/fhir/pull/1120. Local git history shows commit 80b85ada6bb842d6735a97308afb06182f63a820, "Fix FHIR-29660", cherry-picked from c6b8560a9f8f1a6beff0adf4d5280e369c8b8199, moved `<repeats value="true"/>` from the USSG `Questionnaire.item` with linkId 2 to its child item with linkId 2.1.

Additional context:
Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini, and source/questionnaire/list-Questionnaire-examples.xml lists the USSG family-history Questionnaire example. The current populate response keeps one `2` group and repeats `2.1` groups beneath it, which matches the current Questionnaire repeat flag. Zulip and Confluence searches for FHIR-29660, USSG, Questionnaire, linkId, and repeat terms found no relevant conflicting discussion or later reversal.
