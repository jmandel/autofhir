FHIR-34002: Audit multiple-specimen Observation model

Issue request:
FHIR-34002 was a Published / Persuasive with Modification R4 issue asking how a single Observation can identify multiple specimens without changing the normative Observation.specimen cardinality from 0..1 to 0..*. The final motion was to add Group as an Observation.specimen target, constrain such Groups to specimens, and ensure searching works for groups of specimens.

Initial application:
The seed was explicitly applied for R5 in fd8f971f11 / PR #2178 by adding Group as a targetProfile on Observation.specimen and adding invariant obs-9. Later invariant QA commits 55512f8e09, 9b24283fc, and a82569d3be refined the FHIRPath into the current build-safe expression. The related FHIR-35972 application in 7816ce8037 added Specimen to Group.member.entity so a Group can actually contain specimen members.

Additional context:
OO minutes from 2022-03-07 record the approved rationale: link an Observation to either one Specimen or a Group of specimens, keep the invariant on Observation.specimen, and rely on Group.member search behavior after Specimen was added to Group.member.entity. Current source/fhir.ini lists Observation, Group, and Specimen as active resources. Later decisions reinforce the model: FHIR-38732 added Specimen grouping guidance, FHIR-39269 retained Specimen in Group to avoid breaking Observation, and FHIR-54865 was retracted after noting this design came from FHIR-34002.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scoped source already matches the final approved model: Observation.specimen is still 0..1 but can reference Specimen or Group; obs-9 constrains referenced Groups to specimen members; Group includes Specimen as a type/member target; the Group member search parameter covers Group.member.entity; and Specimen notes describe grouping via Observation.specimen.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Revisit only if OO decides to reopen the normative-compatible Group indirection model or to define a new cross-resource Observation search parameter for member specimens; FHIR-34002 itself is already applied correctly.
