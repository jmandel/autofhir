FHIR-43625: Audit DocumentReference date precision

Issue request:
FHIR-43625 was Applied/Persuasive for FHIR-core DocumentReference after a 2024-05-20 OO vote. It requested changing DocumentReference.date from instant to dateTime because historical documents often only have a known date, and adding guidance to use the most precise timestamp available.

Initial application:
The current source already reflects the decision. Commit d63adb6a17972d47bde74e7377febe531b104a9a changed source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml from instant to dateTime and added the requested precision comment, while also removing the Event.recorded mapping-exception type mismatch. There was no previous Issue-Reconcile-Key audit commit for this Jira on the combined branch.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini actively lists documentreference=DocumentReference, so source/documentreference/structuredefinition-DocumentReference.xml and companion DocumentReference source files are in current build scope. The generated SearchParameter remains type=date, which is appropriate for FHIR search date parameters and does not contradict the element datatype. FHIR-43927 and FHIR-25205 distinguish DocumentReference.date from content attachment creation dates; they do not reverse the FHIR-43625 datatype change. Confluence OO WGM minutes record the motion to accept this change, and focused Zulip searches found no conflicting discussion.
