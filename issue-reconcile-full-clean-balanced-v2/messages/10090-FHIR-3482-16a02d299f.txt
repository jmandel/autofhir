A later Published/Persuasive rename decision, FHIR-8480, was applied by c51b8682420300c0f51dd0ad260d0be7f5ae20ab and changed that historical list entry from QuestionnaireAnswers to QuestionnaireResponse.

Additional context:
The old source/clinical.html page that FHIR-3482 changed was deleted in 8b16115d880b33726d98fd973da248d688742494 during later module-page reorganization. The current build has active Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse resource entries in source/fhir.ini, and the current source/hierarchy.xml plus source/foundation-module.html place Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse under the Foundation module's content-management resources. The current source/clinicalsummary-module.html is a narrower Patient Care clinical module list and no longer corresponds to the old all-in-one Clinical Resources page.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Re-adding QuestionnaireResponse to the current Clinical module would duplicate or reinterpret a resource that the current source deliberately lists under Foundation, not repair a remaining omission from FHIR-3482.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. A future work-group decision to reclassify QuestionnaireResponse into the current Clinical module should be handled as a new scope decision, not as unfinished work for FHIR-3482.
