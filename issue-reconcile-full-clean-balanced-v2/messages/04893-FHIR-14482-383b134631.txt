FHIR-14482: Audit Patient gender and sex guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-14482 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 Patient technical correction asking whether Patient.gender may represent other kinds of gender in addition to administrative gender, and asking to fix the old "In addition, to" wording. The approved resolution rewrote the Patient Gender notes as "Patient Gender and Sex", stating that Patient.gender is administrative gender and that other sex/gender properties should be represented through appropriate observations, extensions, or realm-specific mechanisms.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by commit 7fa7a34f97 (#14482), which rewrote source/patient/patient-notes.xml with the approved Patient Gender and Sex section, clarified Administrative Gender, added bullets for Clinical Sex, Clinical Gender, Gender Identity, Sex assigned at Birth, and Legal Sex, and removed the typo. Commit 6432ebd1cc (GF#17586) then refined the Administrative Gender bullet by replacing "attempts to represent" with "represents". Patient is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so the checked Patient source is in current build scope.

Additional context:
Later Patient Administration and Gender Harmony work intentionally updated this same section. Commits 76b1f6db87, 5fa4ec6a53, 74a8d0a430, and 01c44ce75f reframed the guidance around Gender Identity, Sex Parameter for Clinical Use, clinical observations about sex characteristics, Recorded Sex or Gender, Legal Sex, and Sex assigned at Birth, and source/patient/structuredefinition-Patient.xml now links Patient.gender to the Patient Gender and Sex section. The current FHIR Extension Pack contains the referenced genderIdentity, recordedSexOrGender, and sexParameterForClinicalUse extension definitions. Existing combined-branch audits for related Patient sex/gender issues FHIR-14719, FHIR-14755, FHIR-29673, and FHIR-38861 also treat the later Gender Harmony model as the current baseline.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source already says Patient.gender represents administrative gender, warns against inferring specific clinical meaning without pre-coordination, and identifies other mechanisms for gender identity, clinical sex-characteristic observations, SPCU, RSG, legal sex, and sex assigned at birth. Reinstating the exact 2018 bullets would partially reverse later approved guidance and would not improve implementer understanding of the current specification.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-14482 unless PA or Gender Harmony makes a new decision to change the current Patient Gender and Sex model or move additional Gender Harmony IG guidance back into the base Patient narrative.
