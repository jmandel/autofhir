FHIR-14852: Audit _count Bundle.total clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-14852 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core search clarification from fhir-i asking that _count's effect on Bundle.total be made explicit: Bundle.total is the total number of matches, not the number returned in a single Bundle response.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git-svn commit c34eec9f765f018326632732939268293e94d626, subject "GF#14852 - note about _count", with git-svn revision 14268 matching the Jira application comment. That commit added the Bundle.total note to source/search.html. The current source still states that the _count parameter has no impact on Bundle.total.

Additional context:
source/search.html is the current core Search page linked from source/documentation.html. Later Bundle.total clarifications, including FHIR-33942 on estimated totals and _total and FHIR-48485 on the Bundle.total short/constraint rationale, refine adjacent semantics but do not reverse the FHIR-14852 rule. The current Bundle.total definition also says total is the potentially estimated count of match entries across all pages and not a count of Bundle entries.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-14852 unless a future work group decision changes the relationship among _count, _total, and Bundle.total.
