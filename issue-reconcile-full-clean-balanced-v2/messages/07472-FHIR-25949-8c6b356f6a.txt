FHIR-25949: Audit QuestionnaireResponse unanswered-item guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-25949 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5 and asked to make explicit that QuestionnaireResponses do not contain unanswered question items. The approved resolution required QuestionnaireResponse.item.answer.value[x] to be mandatory when an answer is present, a rule that question items are included only when they have at least one answer, no invariant requiring item.answer or item.item because display items can have neither, and guidance that display items MAY be retained in completed responses when Questionnaire extensions require that context.

Initial application:
The Jira comment identifies git fe2213975a3205546ea589efc902f9dfa2022d7e. That commit changed the active QuestionnaireResponse StructureDefinition so QuestionnaireResponse.item is defined as a group or question item for which answers are provided and QuestionnaireResponse.item.answer.value[x] has min=1, and it added the current Validating QuestionnaireResponses note describing incomplete responses, removal of non-answered question items at completed/amended status, and permitted retention of display items.

Additional context:
QuestionnaireResponse is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so the inspected structuredefinition and notes feed the current build. Later current-branch fixes for FHIR-37550 and FHIR-38853 edited the same QuestionnaireResponse guidance around resolvable Questionnaires and nesting, but they do not reverse the FHIR-25949 rule. The linked 2020 Zulip discussion and SDC minutes confirm the WG disposition and the concern about persisted unanswered items. A related Jira search for QuestionnaireResponse unanswered/display/required terms found only FHIR-25949, and the display-category extension evidence now lives in the FHIR Extension Pack rather than needing a core definition edit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative source already preserves the applied issue: answers must have a value, question items in completed/amended QuestionnaireResponses must not remain unanswered, incomplete QuestionnaireResponses may temporarily contain unanswered/non-enabled content, and display items may be retained when needed for interpretation.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. A future work group request could add a more direct cross-link to a specific extension-pack display extension if desired, but that would be an editorial discoverability improvement rather than a required FHIR-25949 source correction.
