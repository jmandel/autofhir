Additional context:

Later Published issue FHIR-26904 refined the same boundary by adding the nursing problem-list example and the distinction between a Condition instance and categorical patient state. Later 2026 issues FHIR-56690 and FHIR-57327 propose further Condition/Observation wording, but their workflow states are Submitted and Triaged, so they are not final source-edit authority for this reconciliation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Condition Scope and Usage and Boundaries and Relationships text already explains that Condition covers conditions, problems, diagnoses, and concerns that have risen to a level of concern; that symptoms and signs are typically Observation; that persistent or managed symptoms can be Condition; and that Observation should be used for resolved or diagnostic-contributing symptoms.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Revisit the Condition/Observation boundary only after the later R6 proposals reach an Applied or Published workflow state, or if the work group explicitly directs a current-build change.
