FHIR-13953: Audit Patient match resource parameter wording

Issue request:
FHIR-13953 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 Patient Administration change request for [base]/Patient/$match. The reporter noted that the input parameter named "resource" was mandatory while its documentation still said, "If a patient record is not provided, then one or more of the other parameters must be provided," implying the patient resource could be optional. The accepted resolution said that sentence came from the previous version of the operation, was incorrect, and would be removed.

No separate Jira-keyed commit was found on the combined branch before this reconciliation.
