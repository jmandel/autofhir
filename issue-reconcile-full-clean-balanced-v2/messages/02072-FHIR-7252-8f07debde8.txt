FHIR-7252: Audit ConsentDirective internationalization

Issue request:
FHIR-7252 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 CBCC change request saying the 2015May ConsentDirective material appeared US-specific and that US-specific legislation references should either be removed or balanced with examples from other jurisdictions.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-7252 was found in the current repository history. The source history shows the older ConsentDirective profile was later complemented by a standalone Consent resource and Canadian privacy-consent examples, and the Contract-hosted ConsentDirective profile was restored and maintained as legacy Contract profile material.

Additional context:
The current build still has active Consent and Contract resource entries in source/fhir.ini, while the old Privacy Consent Directive IG entry in source/igs.xml is commented out and fhir.ini notes that ConsentDirective became Consent. The current Consent resource prose is jurisdiction-neutral and its privacy-consent examples include Canada. The Contract examples list includes several ConsentDirective examples based on Canadian policy and one 42 CFR Part 2 example; the remaining HIPAA/42 CFR phrases in consentdirective.profile.xml are illustrative examples, not a current conformance rule requiring US law.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source no longer presents ConsentDirective/Consent as solely US-specific, and removing isolated illustrative US examples would not improve current conformance meaning after considering the Canadian examples and the jurisdiction-neutral Consent resource.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. If the work group wants the legacy Contract/ConsentDirective profile definitions to avoid naming any US laws at all, that should be handled as a new editorial cleanup or as part of the separate Contract removal work, not as a required FHIR-7252 reconciliation fix.
