FHIR-8406: Audit immunization protocol split history

Issue request:
FHIR-8406 is a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core Immunization issue from the Public Health work group. It asked to remove the vaccination protocol content from Immunization and model the evaluation/protocol information as a separate resource; the final 2017-11-30 Public Health decision was to move forward for R4 with Immunization plus ImmunizationEvaluation.

Initial application:
The split was applied in December 2017 by f561c880f8, with a follow-up adjustment in 03306070c0. Those commits added ImmunizationEvaluation to source/fhir.ini and removed the old Immunization.vaccinationProtocol content from the Immunization source.

Additional context:
Later Published Public Health issues deliberately narrowed that original split. FHIR-14536 and FHIR-16520 found that removing dose sequence/protocol context from Immunization broke jurisdictional recording needs and approved a new Immunization.protocolApplied backbone. FHIR-18868 then clarified that protocolApplied captures the provider's intent at administration time, while independent schedule evaluation belonged in ImmunizationEvaluation. Still later, current R6 work moved ImmunizationEvaluation and ImmunizationRecommendation out of active core source: e073fb45ec removed both resources from source/fhir.ini and deleted their core resource folders, and current narrative/resource guides point those resource names to the Immunization Incubator IG instead.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit makes no source change. The seed issue was applied for R4, then superseded in part by later Published Jira decisions that reintroduced Immunization.protocolApplied, and finally by current R6 governance that removed ImmunizationEvaluation from active core build scope. Re-removing protocolApplied or re-adding ImmunizationEvaluation to core would reverse later decisions rather than complete FHIR-8406.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Any renewed desire to split provider protocol intent out of Immunization, or to return ImmunizationEvaluation from the Immunization Incubator IG to core, should be handled as a new Public Health/FMG proposal rather than as FHIR-8406 reconciliation.
