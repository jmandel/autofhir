FHIR-12443: Audit medication category reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-12443 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request, raised and applied for DSTU2, asking to harmonize medication category support by adding 0..1 CodeableConcept category elements to MedicationAdministration and MedicationDispense, binding them to MedicationRequestCategory, and changing the MedicationRequestCategory binding to preferred across the four medication resources using category.

Initial application:
The request was applied in historical SVN/Git commit a437487f804c183e181dbec98864b65bf2a86923, which explicitly references TR #12443 and added category to MedicationAdministration and MedicationDispense, changed category binding strength to preferred on four resources, reordered category in MedicationRequest and MedicationStatement, and changed MedicationStatement.category to CodeableConcept. Later source-format conversion preserved the category elements in current StructureDefinition XML source.

Additional context:
The active current resources are MedicationAdministration, MedicationDispense, MedicationRequest, and MedicationStatement. Their current category elements are all CodeableConcept and 0..*, with example bindings. The differences from the 2017 text are later intentional changes: FHIR-15819 aligned MedicationDispense.category with MedicationRequest and changed the value set to exemplar/example; FHIR-25555 updated the category descriptions and exemplar value-set handling; FHIR-20673 records that MedicationAdministration.category had already been changed from 0..1 to 0..* and declined adding more structure for category type; FHIR-31632/FHIR-36259 and recent reconciliations restored/corrected FHIR-owned example category terminology. Unresolved FHIR-55333/FHIR-55334 may later move MedicationAdministration/MedicationDispense category terminology to THO, but they do not reverse the current core build.
