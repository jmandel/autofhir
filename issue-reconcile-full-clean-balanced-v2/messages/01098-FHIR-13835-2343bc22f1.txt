FHIR-13835: Audit DNA variant change name as external IG content

Issue request:
FHIR-13835 was a Published FHIR-core Observation change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking to add Observation-geneticsDNAVariantChange.Name as a 0..1 CodeableConcept for the gHGVS genetics DNA change using LOINC 81290-9. The accepted resolution said the spirit of the change was already applied in the Genomics Reporting IG, specifically at the described-variant genomic DNA change component.

Initial application:
No current FHIR-core source contains Observation-geneticsDNAVariantChange, DNAVariantChange, obs-described-variant, genomic-dna-chg, or LOINC 81290-9. Core history does not show a FHIR-13835-specific commit; instead, Clinical Genomics minutes approved FHIR-13832, FHIR-13833, and FHIR-13835 together as already applied in the Genomics Reporting IG but not in Observation-genetics in FHIR Core. Later core history updated genetics notes to point to the Genomics Reporting IG and then removed outdated clinical genomics artifacts from Observation, DiagnosticReport, and ServiceRequest.

Additional context:
Current FHIR core has Observation in build scope, but no active Observation-genetics profile entry. The core genomics, Observation, and DiagnosticReport narrative points implementers to the Genomics Reporting IG for structured genomic results. The FHIR Extension Pack's legacy observation-geneticsVariant definition is retired/deprecated and says it has been replaced by profiles and extensions in the Genomics Reporting Guide. The current Genomics Reporting IG has evolved the old described-variant component into the active Variant profile's genomic-hgvs component, with LOINC 81290-9, CodeableConcept value, required HGVS binding, and 0..1 component cardinality.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no FHIR-core source change. Adding or restoring Observation-geneticsDNAVariantChange.Name in core would be outside current build scope and would contradict the work-group resolution that the requested semantics belonged in the Genomics Reporting IG rather than FHIR core.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future corrections to the gHGVS/genomic DNA change component should be made in HL7/genomics-reporting or the published Genomics Reporting IG, not in this FHIR core checkout, unless Clinical Genomics explicitly moves the content back into core.
