FHIR-7236: Audit genomics narrative guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7236 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 Clinical Genomics issue against the old Observation genetics example. It noted that the generated narrative for obs-genetics-example1-somatic did not include clinically essential structured variant information and requested general guidance about how much genetic-reporting information must be represented.

Initial application:
The requested guidance was applied in 2015 to source/observation/observation-genetics-introduction.xml. Git history shows commit 45025abf6c99388591b956ef20357479b9a0e7ab adding the exact sentences about semantic equivalency, the HL7 Clinical Sequencing Domain Analysis Model, and the limitation around clinically relevant negative or wildtype results. The old obs-genetics-example1-somatic example and Observation genetics profile were later carried forward for a time.

Additional context:
The specific Observation genetics profile and related examples that FHIR-7236 targeted are no longer current build artifacts. Commit 3cfe12f1b4037e4fe922af288ba96fa49faf1435 removed outdated clinical genomics artifacts under Observation, DiagnosticReport, and ServiceRequest and streamlined genomics.html. The current active Observation notes now say genomic reporting uses DiagnosticReport and Observation for structured, computable genomic data and points implementers to the Genomics Reporting IG; source/hierarchy.xml includes genomics.html under Diagnostics, and source/fhir.ini keeps Observation as an active resource. Community searches for FHIR-7236 and the distinctive resolution wording found no conflicting Zulip or Confluence evidence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Re-adding the old DSTU1 profile-specific caveat to current Observation or genomics pages would be redundant and partly stale because the current source has replaced those artifacts with broader Genomics Reporting IG guidance and current structured genomic data references.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. If the Clinical Genomics Work Group wants stronger requirements for narrative completeness or negative/wildtype result reporting in current genomic reports, that should be raised as a new current-scope issue against the Genomics Reporting IG or the current Observation/DiagnosticReport genomic guidance rather than reviving the obsolete Observation genetics profile wording.
