FHIR-3746: Audit digital signature guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-3746 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Security change request asking for advice on how to create and validate Provenance.signature, how signatures interact with moved/imported resources, rewritten references, metadata, versioned references, server relocation, and whether counter-signatures or document signatures should be used. Its final resolution recommended W3C/XML Digital Signatures, Provenance as a detached digital-signature carrier through the Signature datatype, document signing through enveloped/IHE DSG mechanisms, and preserving room for other signature approaches.
