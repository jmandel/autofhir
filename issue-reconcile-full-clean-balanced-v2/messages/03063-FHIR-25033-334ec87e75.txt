FHIR-25033: Audit Practitioner specialty clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-25033 is a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration change request raised against STU3 and applied for R5. It asked the specification to clarify that education-style specialties belong in Practitioner.qualification, while PractitionerRole.specialty represents the functional role a practitioner is practicing for a given organization or location.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git commit 59f25a099c07759b6ab65c4ac1287ef30fd0ba6f in the Patient Administration batch later merged as PR #2156 by merge commit 40cbf110b3833370acb337e5b5fb02aaee8b5bdf. That commit updated Practitioner.qualification with a comment pointing to PractitionerRole.specialty and replaced the PractitionerRole.specialty definition/comment with the role-vs-qualification clarification. Practitioner and PractitionerRole are active current-build resources because source/fhir.ini lists both resources and marks both approved.

Additional context:
The originating Zulip implementers thread contains the same distinction and Brian Postlethwaite's request to log it for R5. PA minutes from the 2021 WGM record discussion of qualification and specialty placement, and the 2022-08-10 PA minutes record the reopening/clarified vote that matches the final Jira resolution. A later combined-branch reconciliation for FHIR-32497 corrected the only meaningful stale detail in the FHIR-25033 text, changing the non-existent Practitioner.qualifications reference in PractitionerRole.specialty to Practitioner.qualification while preserving the later FHIR-25033 wording.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active source now conveys the approved distinction: Practitioner.qualification explains that PractitionerRole.specialty defines the practiced functional role and is not defined on the practitioner, while PractitionerRole.specialty explains that qualifications may or might not relate to or be required by that practicing specialty and references the real Practitioner.qualification element.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current build source is already aligned with FHIR-25033 after the earlier application and the FHIR-32497 property-path correction; further wording polish such as may/might not differences would not change implementer understanding and should not be made as reconciliation work.
