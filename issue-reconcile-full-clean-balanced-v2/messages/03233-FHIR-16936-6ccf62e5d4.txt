FHIR-16936: Audit coverage eligibility rename

Issue request:
FHIR-16936 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution to rename EligibilityRequest and EligibilityResponse to CoverageEligibilityRequest and CoverageEligibilityResponse, while leaving EnrollmentRequest and EnrollmentResponse unchanged until a business need and appropriateness for FHIR or HL7 were determined.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in git commit b165b5fdd3ce35681359e96682ece8e7cd377591, which added the CoverageEligibilityRequest and CoverageEligibilityResponse source folders and registered those resources. Three days later, commit 8107744062a751ab36ef2a170bae67172c5ea511 removed the active EligibilityRequest and EligibilityResponse resource registrations and module/navigation mentions.

Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini actively builds coverageeligibilityrequest=CoverageEligibilityRequest and coverageeligibilityresponse=CoverageEligibilityResponse, and keeps enrollmentrequest=EnrollmentRequest and enrollmentresponse=EnrollmentResponse at FMM 0. The old source/eligibilityrequest and source/eligibilityresponse folders still exist as historical source residue, but they are not active resources in source/fhir.ini or current financial navigation. Later FHIR-17473 work, already represented on the combined branch, adjusted CoverageEligibilityRequest scope using the renamed resource.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scope source already implements the FHIR-16936 decision: the coverage eligibility pair is renamed and active, the old eligibility resources are excluded from active build configuration and navigation, and enrollment remains present but draft/stub as expected.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not edit the out-of-build historical EligibilityRequest/EligibilityResponse folders for this issue; future enrollment generalization or replacement should be handled by a separate FM work-group decision.
