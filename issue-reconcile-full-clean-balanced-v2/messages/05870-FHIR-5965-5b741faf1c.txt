FHIR-5965: Audit MedicationStatement status/effective handling

Issue request:
FHIR-5965 was a Published Pharmacy change request with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It raised that MedicationStatement.status values describing whether medication use was in progress or completed could conflict with MedicationStatement.effective[x], and asked whether the element needed different semantics or an invariant. The final resolution reviewed Pharmacy resource statuses, updated the MedicationStatement.status definition, and moved the invariant question to FHIR-8010.

Initial application:
Jira comments record the initial application in svn 6556 and svn 6745. Git history has #5965 commits 097af202a4, 534313123a, and cf7b12afe7; cf7b12afe7 specifically changed the MedicationStatement.status definition to the approved patient/source judgment wording. The related invariant thread was handled in FHIR-8010 rather than as part of FHIR-5965.

Additional context:
Later MedicationStatement decisions materially changed the source model. FHIR-8010 was Published/Not Persuasive with Modification and ultimately clarified status/wasNotTaken semantics rather than requiring an invariant. FHIR-19468 and FHIR-19470 moved taking/not-taking information into MedicationStatement.adherence; FHIR-32060 changed the R5/R6 status codes to recorded, entered-in-error, and draft; and FHIR-39271 reaffirmed that MedicationStatement.status reflects the status of recording the statement while adherence and effective[x] carry medication-use assertions. The combined branch already contains the FHIR-39271 reconciliation commit d5e940beb2 correcting the remaining current-source wording. Later FHIR-55338 and FHIR-54315 discussions are unresolved or triaged and do not supersede the applied/published status model.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. MedicationStatement is an active current build resource, and the active StructureDefinition now defines status as "the status of recording the medication statement", limits it to recorded | entered-in-error | draft, points taking/not-taking semantics to MedicationStatement.adherence, and leaves effective[x] to describe the medication-taking interval. That current model removes the timing conflict identified by FHIR-5965 and leaves no approved invariant to add.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future work should be driven by final decisions on newer unresolved MedicationStatement status/no-known-medication issues such as FHIR-55338 or FHIR-54315, not by reopening the already-superseded DSTU1 status/effective conflict.
