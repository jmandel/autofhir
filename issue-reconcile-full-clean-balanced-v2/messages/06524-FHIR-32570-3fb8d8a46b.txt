FHIR-32570: Audit Device subject relocation

Issue request:
FHIR-32570 was a Published R4-raised Device change request with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked why patient was not represented inside the Device association-status work, accepted the FHIR-36214 associationState.subject direction, and required removal of Device.subject because it was superseded by Device.associationState.subject while excluding Practitioner from that then-current choice.

Initial application:
The Jira applied comment points to PR #2014 commit 6a6ee72b710a54bcab359fd05e64db63365a12f3, which only made minor Device association text cleanups. The substantive nearby source history for this issue is commit 5ffc19505fc915435cfd5d60c903cb4912672bec, which commented out Device.subject and redirected the Device subject search parameter to Device.association.humanSubject, followed by 67a7976ec9caae4ba35221b3a089835a8f7a7fa5 updating the Device patient search expression.

Additional context:
FHIR-36214 created the associationState backbone that FHIR-32570 relied on, FHIR-34134 later renamed that Device backbone to association, and FHIR-38904 later superseded the whole Device-level association approach by creating the active DeviceAssociation resource and removing patient-identifying association data from Device. FHIR-39009 also records the overlapping Device subject trackers and points to the FHIR-38904 disposition. Those related issues are already represented on the combined branch, so this commit does not publish duplicate decisions for them.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current build-scoped source/fhir.ini lists both Device and DeviceAssociation as active resources. Comment-stripped and XML-parse checks show no active Device.subject, Device.association, Device.associationState, Device.associationStatus, Device-subject, or Device-patient definitions. The active replacement surface is DeviceAssociation: it has subject, associationStatus, period, patient/subject search parameters, patient compartment membership, and Device narrative guidance pointing ownership/custodianship relationships to DeviceAssociation. Later inclusion of Practitioner on DeviceAssociation.subject is governed by the published DeviceAssociation/overlap decisions rather than the narrower FHIR-32570 R5 wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce Device.subject or edit the commented legacy Device association block; any future refinement of subject target choices or relationship semantics belongs to DeviceAssociation-specific follow-up rather than late application of FHIR-32570.
