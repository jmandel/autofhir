FHIR-11373: Remove stale QUICK Claim missingTeeth metadata

Issue request:
FHIR-11373 is a Published/Persuasive with Modification FM issue from the DSTU2 ballot. It asked whether Claim MissingTeeth should instead be represented as an Observation placed in the proposed Claim.information.clinical area; the accepted resolution was that missing teeth can be represented in Claim.information.

Initial application:
The historical application appears in commit aaa1e5693e365bca976f7aa2115c85a0f2293a38, which handled Tracker #7901, #11373, #11395, and #12356 together. For Claim it disabled the old Claim.missingTeeth, .tooth, .reason, and .extractionDate spreadsheet rows, expanded Claim.information.value[x] to support Attachment and Reference(Any), and added Claim.information.reason with the MissingReason binding. Later Claim refactoring renamed information to supportingInfo while preserving the replacement information pattern.

Additional context:
FHIR-7901 is the older Claim issue with the same accepted rationale to remove Claim.missingTeeth because missing teeth can be represented in Claim.information. FHIR-11372 and FHIR-12356 supplied the supporting information payload/resource support, and they are already represented on the combined branch. The active Claim resource source and FHIR ModelInfo already expose Claim.supportingInfo, and the oral Claim example represents a missing tooth through supportingInfo. The remaining stale surface was limited to the active Library QUICK model-definition example, which still declared QUICK.Claim.missingTeeth and QUICK.Claim.MissingTeeth.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Removed the stale QUICK.Claim.missingTeeth element and QUICK.Claim.MissingTeeth ClassInfo from source/library/library-quick-model-definition-content.xml. Claim, the FHIR ModelInfo, and the dental Claim example were left unchanged because they already reflect the information/supportingInfo replacement representation.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. No further core Claim change is needed for FHIR-11373 unless maintainers decide the legacy QUICK example should be regenerated from an external model source rather than maintained directly in core.
