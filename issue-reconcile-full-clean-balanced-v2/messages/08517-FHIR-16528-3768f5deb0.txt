FHIR-16528: Audit safety checklist reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-16528 was a Published FHIR-core change request with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked that the clinical safety page reference the security page and avoid maintaining a separate duplicate security/privacy checklist; the accepted modification was to approve the proposed checklist in principle while Grahame worked with the security editors to fine tune the result.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by 68f6cca29ae4d06f089f338982a2b98269e1a48a, "GF#16528 - updates to safety page per discussion with committee." That commit rewrote source/safety.html into an Implementer's Safety CheckList, explicitly said that safety was interpreted loosely and covered security and privacy issues, and added privacy/security checklist sections with links into the security and security/privacy material.

Additional context:
The current source/safety.html remains a current top-level source page and still states that the checklist covers security and privacy issues, exposes the checklist as the safety-entries CodeSystem, and links individual security checklist entries to source/security.html, security-labels.html, secpriv-module.html, AuditEvent, Provenance, and related security/privacy pages. The current source/security.html is the dedicated normative security page. The December 2018 Zulip thread shows Grahame and Security participants fine tuning the proposed security/privacy checklist entries before Grahame posted the reconciled safety page, which matches the Jira resolution more closely than the issue title alone. Later safety checklist commits maintain and extend the checklist rather than reversing that model.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Removing the safety-page security/privacy checklist or replacing it with only a pointer to source/security.html would undo the modified work-group decision and later checklist maintenance; adding another generic pointer would be redundant with the existing item-level links and would not materially change implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Revisit only if FHIR-I and Security decide to change the product model from one implementer safety checklist with security/privacy coverage to separate authoritative checklists; that would require coordinated edits to source/safety.html and the safety-entries CodeSystem rather than a small cleanup.
