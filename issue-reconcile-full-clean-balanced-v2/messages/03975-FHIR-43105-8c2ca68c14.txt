Issue request:
FHIR-43105 is an Applied FHIR-core OO change request for ServiceRequest. It initially asked for a coded reject-reason extension, but the final Not Persuasive with Modification resolution said rejection reasons belong on Task.statusReason and that ServiceRequest should instead gain ServiceRequest.statusReason, a 0..* CodeableConcept with an example binding to ServiceRequest status-reason codes including no-longer-clinically-necessary and pre-approval-denied, plus guidance about overlap with ServiceRequest.reason early in request performance.
