FHIR-20770: Audit Communication payload CodeableConcept support

Issue request:
FHIR-20770 was Published/Persuasive and asked to add CodeableConcept as a Communication payload content choice for machine-generated communications, with the final resolution also adding the same choice to CommunicationRequest.payload.content and clarifying that contentCodeableConcept is the communicated content rather than metadata about the content.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in R5 by GF#20770 commits f7c3c1d and 8b4d465, which added CodeableConcept to Communication.payload.content and CommunicationRequest.payload.content, removed string from those choice lists, updated comments, and changed examples from contentString to contentCodeableConcept.text. The later source rework preserved the change in the current StructureDefinition XML.

Additional context:
FHIR-32763 later requested the CommunicationRequest half of the same payload choice and was resolved as a duplicate of FHIR-20770, with Patient Care minutes in August 2023 listing the duplicate disposition. Current source/fhir.ini lists Communication and CommunicationRequest as active resources in the build.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Current source/communication/structuredefinition-Communication.xml and source/communicationrequest/structuredefinition-CommunicationRequest.xml both include Attachment, Reference(Resource), and CodeableConcept for payload.content[x], include the FHIR-20770 explanatory comment, and no longer expose contentString in the Communication or CommunicationRequest source folders.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. The current build-scope source already reflects the published FHIR-20770 decision, and the duplicate FHIR-32763 does not require any additional source edit.
