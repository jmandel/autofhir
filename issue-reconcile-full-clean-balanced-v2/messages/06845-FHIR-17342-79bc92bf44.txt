FHIR-17342: Audit Task boundary clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-17342 was Published with resolution Persuasive for STU3. It asked OO to review and clarify the boundaries among Task, Procedure, ServiceRequest, and Encounter after Procedure's scope broadened, including the distinction between administrative workflow tasks and clinical/physical activities and the expectation that an office visit is represented as Encounter rather than Procedure.

Initial application:
The Jira issue linked the implementers Zulip thread "Task vs Procedure Boundaries." Commit 5686ff744511d305f4c9602e3423018133e3fa6c (GF#17342) applied the seed by adding Boundaries and Relationships prose to active Task and ServiceRequest narrative source. That prose distinguished Task as spanning intent/event and tracking administrative workflow execution, Procedure as an event/activity resulting in change to or for the subject, and ServiceRequest as the request/authorization that a Task may fulfill. The seed commit did not directly edit Procedure or Encounter source.

Additional context:
The same boundary discussion continued through tightly related Published issues FHIR-17359 and FHIR-19109, which added and refined the Task/Procedure boundary text now present in Procedure and Task. Later Applied R6 issues FHIR-46344, FHIR-46341, and FHIR-47461 refined the same Task and ServiceRequest paragraphs with Request-pattern fulfillment and ServiceRequest authorization guidance; those related issues already have Issue-Reconcile-Key commits on the combined branch. Current fhir.ini lists Task, Procedure, ServiceRequest, and Encounter as active resources, and current source now covers the relevant boundaries across their narrative pages.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Task, ServiceRequest, Procedure, and Encounter source already conveys the requested boundary guidance when the seed and later related decisions are considered together, and adding duplicate prose would not change implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future changes to these boundaries should be handled as a new OO/PC workflow-boundary decision, not as a remaining FHIR-17342 application gap.
