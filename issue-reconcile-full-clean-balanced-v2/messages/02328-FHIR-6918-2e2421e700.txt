The recorded resolution says existing changes for other tasks had already made the relationship and purpose clear.

Additional context:
Composition remains an active resource in source/fhir.ini. Later FHIR-17555 renamed Composition.class to Composition.category and increased cardinality to 0..*, with an active migration alias Composition.class=Composition.category. FHIR-12652 later answered a related value-set concern by noting class had become category and that the binding is only example. Current source describes Composition.type as the required precise kind of composition and Composition.category as optional, repeatable categorization that may be implied by or derived from Composition.type. A 2019 Zulip discussion reached the same coarse-category versus fine-type interpretation, and Confluence search found no direct FHIR-6918 reference that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

Recommendation:
