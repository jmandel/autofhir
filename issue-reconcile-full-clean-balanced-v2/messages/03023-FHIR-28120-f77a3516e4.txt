It asked section 4.8.7/4.8.8 Code System Fragments to say that fragments cannot contain codes, concepts, or properties outside a complete representation of the code system, while noting that such a complete representation might not exist in one authoritative publication such as SNOMED International with all modules/extensions.

Initial application:
