Issue request:
FHIR-5707 was a Published Patient Care DSTU1 change request about status values for ProcedureRequest and Procedure, especially whether ProcedureRequest should carry proposed/planned-style states and whether Procedure needed its own status. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution added aborted as a Procedure status, recorded that in-progress, entered-in-error, and completed were also added, and deferred further ProcedureRequest status discussion.

Initial application:

No direct ProcedureRequest status source change was expected from FHIR-5707 because the Jira resolution explicitly deferred that part.

Additional context:
Procedure and ServiceRequest are current active resources in source/fhir.ini; ProcedureRequest is recorded only as merged into ServiceRequest. Related FHIR-6877 is already audited on the combined branch as satisfied by this Request/Event workflow split.
