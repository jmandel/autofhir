FHIR-7370: Audit narrative context guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7370 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core narrative clarification from the DSTU1 ballot. It asked how generated resource narrative can avoid redundant referenced-resource summaries in lists, documents, or multi-resource contexts, and the approved resolution was to add a note that systems may generate narrative intelligently but SHALL NOT assume any particular rendering context.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git commit 209b86b3855c85093735f059ae3be5ce06855664, which is the git-svn mirror of svn revision 6212 cited in the Jira comment. That commit added the current guidance to source/narrative.html. Later QA and normative-language refactors adjusted surrounding wording and paragraph structure while preserving the SHALL NOT assume-context requirement.

Additional context:
The narrative page remains in current build scope via source/hierarchy.xml, which lists narrative.html and its related narrative pages. Current source/narrative.html still tells systems that they may choose how much dereferencing to perform but SHALL NOT assume that the resource is rendered in any particular context when generating narrative. FHIR-17463 later touched the same section for normative-language cleanup and explicitly retained this sentence as normative guidance. Zulip and Confluence searches found no directly relevant community record changing the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current source already carries the approved FHIR-7370 requirement in the active narrative page, and adding further list/document-specific prose would be redundant rather than a necessary correction.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-7370 unless the work group reopens the broader narrative generation policy; the active specification already preserves the ballot resolution.
