FHIR-18853: Audit AdverseEvent participant reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-18853 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for STU3/R5 Patient Care feedback that AdverseEvent.contributor's short description implied participation in causing or being involved in an event while its definition described contribution of information. The accepted change replaced contributor with an AdverseEvent.participant BackboneElement, with participant.actor identifying who/what was involved and participant.function distinguishing involvement such as contributor, informant, participant, or witness.

Initial application:
The Jira comment links commit ea5ae1cea9a64a1156a791cb99ae4b4ed7922d06, which changed the legacy AdverseEvent spreadsheet from AdverseEvent.contributor to AdverseEvent.participant/function/actor and added the adverse-event-participant-function value set. The 2021 source rework carried that applied content into source/adverseevent/structuredefinition-AdverseEvent.xml with GF#18853 committee notes, and source/fhir.ini now records the R4-to-R6 rename map from AdverseEvent.contributor to AdverseEvent.participant.actor.

Additional context:
FHIR-13894 is the predecessor that had renamed eventParticipant to contributor; FHIR-18853 superseded that naming by introducing participant plus function. Later final AdverseEvent issues legitimately refined the same area: FHIR-24684 folded detector into participant and added AUT to the example participant-function value set, FHIR-38823 clarified when subject/recorder should not be duplicated as participant and removed ResearchSubject actor targets, and FHIR-41402 added Group as a participant.actor target only for family/household groups. Confluence Patient Care minutes show GF#18853 was deferred in 2018 and discussed again on 2019-09-12; no direct Zulip thread was found that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The active current AdverseEvent source already has AdverseEvent.participant 0..*, participant.function 0..1 CodeableConcept bound to the adverse-event-participant-function example value set, and participant.actor 1..1 Reference with the accepted participant wording. The remaining differences from the 2019 resolution text are later approved refinements, not incomplete application of FHIR-18853.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not remove AUT, Group, or the later participant comments while reconciling FHIR-18853; those belong to later AdverseEvent decisions and can be audited independently if their own issues are selected.
