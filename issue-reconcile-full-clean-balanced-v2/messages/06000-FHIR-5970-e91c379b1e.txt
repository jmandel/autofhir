FHIR-5970: Audit CarePlan/EpisodeOfCare boundary

Issue request:
FHIR-5970 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification DSTU1 Patient Care clarification asking the spec to differentiate EpisodeOfCare and CarePlan because both can involve care teams, conditions, and activities. The accepted resolution said to clarify the Boundaries and Relationships text, with the basic distinction that EpisodeOfCare is a tracking resource rather than a planning resource and does not require a CarePlan.

Initial application:
No Jira-keyed commit for FHIR-5970 was found in the current git history. A pickaxe/blame check identifies 210464f33855dbfab3be8305d006cb34f638cf87, committed on the same day the issue was created, as adding the exact EpisodeOfCare-versus-CarePlan clarification to source/episodeofcare/episodeofcare-introduction.xml. That wording remains in the current active source.

Additional context:
CarePlan and EpisodeOfCare are active build resources in source/fhir.ini. Current CarePlan Scope and Usage describes CarePlan as a request/planning resource that captures intended actions and groups activities, goals, and participants, while current EpisodeOfCare Boundaries and Relationships says EpisodeOfCare is the tracking resource, usually exists before CarePlan, and can be used without a CarePlan. The already-published FHIR-6131 audit and later FHIR-54402 No Change decision cover the separate question of direct CarePlan/EpisodeOfCare linkage and support leaving that linkage to the workflow-episodeOfCare extension rather than adding new core text here. A 2020 Zulip thread discussed the nuance of the wording but did not identify a final work-group reversal of the applied distinction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current build-scope source already conveys the accepted FHIR-5970 distinction in the EpisodeOfCare Boundaries and Relationships section and complementary CarePlan scope text. Adding duplicate prose to the CarePlan page or changing the direct linkage model would be redundant or would conflict with later decisions rather than complete FHIR-5970.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If Patient Care wants richer examples or a more nuanced comparison of CarePlan and EpisodeOfCare behavior, that should be handled as a new work-group decision rather than as an unapplied remainder of FHIR-5970.
