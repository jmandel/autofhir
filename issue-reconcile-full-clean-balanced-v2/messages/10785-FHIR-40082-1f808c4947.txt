Issue request:
FHIR-40082 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification FM change request for R5 that explicitly completed FHIR-22662 by adding missing reviewOutcome cardinalities, assigning the reason value set, and renaming category to decision. Its approved structure was reviewOutcome.decision 0..1, reviewOutcome.reason 0..*, reviewOutcome.preAuthRef 0..1, and reviewOutcome.preAuthPeriod 0..1 at item, detail, and subItem levels for both main and addItem adjudications in ClaimResponse and ExplanationOfBenefit.

Initial application:
Git commit 9524a45683 ("FHIR-22662 and FHIR-40082") implemented reviewOutcome in ClaimResponse and ExplanationOfBenefit and reused the item-level definition through contentReference at the nested levels. Git commit 72084b0406 ("FHIR-22662") then added claim-decision-reason terminology and corrected the reviewOutcome.reason binding from claim-decision to claim-decision-reason.

FHIR-40082 is not an independent semantic reversal; it is a later same-family completion of FHIR-22662.
