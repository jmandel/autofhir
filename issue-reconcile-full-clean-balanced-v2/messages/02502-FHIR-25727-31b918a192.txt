FHIR-25727: Audit RelatedPerson scope clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-25727 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification PA change request asking to clarify the scope of RelatedPerson for birth-defect reporting use cases, including parents who may be deceased or otherwise not participating in patient care. Its resolution states that the related FHIR-30313 update to include people involved in a patient's health allows this use, while the remaining modeling choice among RelatedPerson, Observation, or FamilyMemberHistory may need Patient Care discussion.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied together with FHIR-30313 in git commit 81f8b30cb34e87c76cffca4218d55c7c48d53799. That commit message says it covered FHIR-25727 and changed the RelatedPerson StructureDefinition description and root definition to include people involved in a patient's health or care.

Additional context:
The PA January 2022 WGM minutes discussed FHIR-25727 immediately before FHIR-30313, noted their relationship, and recorded the FHIR-30313 persuasive vote. Current source keeps the shared health-scope wording in active RelatedPerson source. Later FHIR-41566 and FHIR-41559 changed adjacent boundary wording, but neither narrows the health-scope clarification that FHIR-25727 relied on.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change because the current RelatedPerson definition already allows the broader class of people relevant to a patient's health. The unresolved modeling advice in the Jira resolution is guidance for use-case profiling or Patient Care discussion, not an unapplied core source requirement.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Do not add core elements or extensions for FHIR-25727 as part of this reconciliation; any further representation guidance for specific parent-history data should be handled by the relevant IG or a new work-group decision.
