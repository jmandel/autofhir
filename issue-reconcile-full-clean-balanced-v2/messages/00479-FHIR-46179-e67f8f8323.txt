FHIR-46179 was an Applied/Persuasive CDS change request for PlanDefinition.action.condition. It asked to clarify that an action with no applicability conditions is applicable, that multiple conditions of the same kind use AND semantics, that different condition kinds are considered separately, and that an example show actions with no applicability criteria, multiple applicability criteria, and applicability/start/stop criteria.
