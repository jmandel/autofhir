FHIR-30587: Audit CapabilityStatement actor guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-30587 was a Published FHIR-core change request with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked for CapabilityStatement.implementationGuide to explain that IG-level declaration is insufficient for IG actor or option support, and that IG-defined CapabilityStatements should be referenced with CapabilityStatement.instantiates to identify the supported role(s).

Initial application:

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists CapabilityStatement as an active resource, so the checked StructureDefinition source is in current build scope. Later FHIR-39433 replaced the terse FHIR-30587 note with fuller guidance explaining that CapabilityStatement.implementationGuide asserts adherence to IG-level global definitions and general textual requirements but not to IG-defined CapabilityStatements; CapabilityStatement.instantiates is required for those conformance claims. Later FHIR-42975 was resolved No Change because implementationGuide already says it is not the actor-specific mechanism, with actor references handled separately by FHIR-43899.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Recommendation:
