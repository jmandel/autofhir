FHIR-30872: Audit Patient financial-use scope wording

Issue request:
FHIR-30872 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration change request for R5 asking the Patient Scope and Usage section to clarify that Patient can be used in financial contexts where the person may be a subscriber or policy holder rather than an actual care recipient. The approved resolution was to add a scope bullet for tracking financial services, such as an insurance subscriber or policy holder.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git commit 715cfb05586732de6e57c8f9b0bf5b15b622159e, which added the exact approved bullet to source/patient/patient-introduction.xml. No prior Issue-Reconcile-Key audit commit for FHIR-30872 was present on robo-spec-combined-issue-reconcile-full-clean-balanced-v2 before this reconciliation.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists patient=Patient as an active resource, so source/patient/patient-introduction.xml is in current build scope. Later Patient work, especially FHIR-40295 and FHIR-42732, did not reverse the FHIR-30872 clarification; it reinforced it by adding Boundaries and Relationships text that Patient can be the right resource for terms such as subscriber or guarantor and that financial contexts may use distinct Patient resource instances when demographics differ.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Patient introduction already contains the approved financial-services bullet and now also contains additional financial-context clarification, so another source edit would be redundant and could create maintenance drift.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No FHIR core follow-up is needed for FHIR-30872 unless the work group later chooses to revise the broader Patient financial-context guidance introduced by subsequent issues.
