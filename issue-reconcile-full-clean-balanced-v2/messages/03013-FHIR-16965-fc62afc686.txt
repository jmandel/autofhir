FHIR-16965: Audit Annotation Group author rejection

Issue request:
FHIR-16965 was a Published/Not Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core datatypes request from MnM, raised against STU3 and applied for STU3. It asked to add Group to Annotation.authorReference and also asked why Annotation.text was not a markup type. The final resolution rejected Group because Group is intended as a subject and is not scoped to take responsibility or act collectively, but agreed to make Annotation.text markup/markdown.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by svn/git commit f5365429a97de1065e0df7297f110f707e198aa0, whose message says GF#16965 and which changed source/datatypes/annotation.xml so Annotation.text changed from string to markdown with matching definition text. No historical or current source evidence shows Group being added to Annotation.author[x] for this issue.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists Annotation as an active datatype and normative artifact, so source/datatypes/annotation.xml is current build input. Current source has Annotation.author[x] as Reference(Practitioner|PractitionerRole|Patient|RelatedPerson|Organization|Device)|string and still omits Group, while Annotation.text is markdown. Later related author-target issues broadened Annotation.author[x] with Organization, PractitionerRole, and Device, but none reverse the FHIR-16965 decision to exclude Group. FHIR-15743 separately requested the same Annotation.text markdown change and Jira comments say it was already done under GF#16965. MnM 2018-07-17 minutes corroborate the Not Persuasive with Modification vote, and community searches found no relevant conflicting discussion.
