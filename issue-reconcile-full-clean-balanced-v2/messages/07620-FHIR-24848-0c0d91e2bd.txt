FHIR-24848: Audit Group evidence collection scope

Issue request:
FHIR-24848 was Published as Not Persuasive with Modification for R5. The request asked to add an `information` Group.type code and Evidence as a Group.member.entity target so meta-analyses could model a group of Evidence resources. The accepted decision rejected that expansion, treated it as Group scope creep, and instead aligned Group around participant-type members while pointing other collection use cases toward List.

Initial application:
The applied source commit 272e3fb71ecb0d87630a48101b00015f0f18b038 (PR #2358) explicitly includes `J#24848: Aligned group type with participant pattern`. In Group source it changed the group-type CodeSystem and Group StructureDefinition by removing the then-present non-participant medication/substance/bodystructure entries and adding participant-like targets such as CareTeam, HealthcareService, Location, Organization, RelatedPerson, and Specimen. It did not add an `information` code or an Evidence target, and pickaxe checks found no historical addition of those rejected seed terms in the Group source.

Additional context:
The linked 2019 Zulip discussion records the rationale: Group should stay focused on passive participants, and evidence/information collections should not be modeled by expanding Group. FHIR-I minutes from 2019-11-25 record the 7-0-0 vote to align Group membership with the Participant pattern. Later issues refined the same area without reversing the seed decision: FHIR-26648 confirmed Organization support through FHIR-24848; FHIR-35972 retained Specimen support; FHIR-32318 restored BodyStructure; FHIR-49851 clarified Group and Organization boundaries; and FHIR-52918 later allowed medication/substance definitional resources. Those related decisions are already represented on the combined branch and explain why the current allowed member list is broader than the exact 2019 participant-pattern wording.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active current Group source still has no `information` group-type concept and no Evidence targetProfile for Group.member.entity, while the authoritative Group CodeSystem, StructureDefinition, and introduction reflect the applied seed plus later published/applied Group decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not add Evidence or `information` to Group unless a future work-group decision reopens that modeling question; related Group member-type issues already have their own combined-branch reconciliation commits.
