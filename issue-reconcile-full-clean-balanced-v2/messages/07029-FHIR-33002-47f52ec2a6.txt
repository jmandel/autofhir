FHIR-33002: Audit _summary=text clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-33002 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5 and asked how _summary=text should treat top-level mandatory elements such as Observation.code: whether their whole subtrees, nested optional children, and extensions must be returned. The approved clarification says _summary=text returns text, id, meta, and top-level mandatory elements to keep the payload valid, while servers MAY omit elements within those subtrees as long as the resulting payload is valid; extensions may be returned, but clients should not rely on them.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by commit 4387db82e028ca74f3cea49b90392e0c77940128, which rewrote the _summary value table in source/search.html and added the approved mandatory-subtree clarification plus the extension guidance. The current source still contains that text in the FHIR search page.

Additional context:
The original Zulip implementer thread raised the same Observation.code/CodeableConcept subtree question and led to the Jira. FHIR-I WGM minutes for January 2022 record the same concern, note that servers MAY omit elements from the subtree, and show the 17-0-1 Persuasive with Modification vote. Related issues FHIR-33148 and FHIR-35865 address ElementDefinition.isSummary and modifierExtension summary behavior; both are already represented on the combined branch by separate audit commits and do not require a FHIR-33002 source change.
