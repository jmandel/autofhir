Additional context:

A later invariant cleanup commit for FHIR-24860 removed a redundant condition marker from Goal.target but preserved the gol-1 constraint and its FHIRPath expression. Jira and community searches found no later final core decision reversing or narrowing this invariant; the only direct Zulip hit was the tracker bot announcement.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative Goal StructureDefinition contains gol-1 on Goal.target with human text "Goal.target.measure is required if Goal.target.detail is populated" and expression "(detail.exists() and measure.exists()) or detail.exists().not()", which is equivalent to the requested implication. Both Goal.target.measure and Goal.target.detail[x] carry condition gol-1, and an existing failing invariant test exercises detail without measure.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-12585 unless a future Patient Care decision changes the Goal.target measure/detail relationship.
