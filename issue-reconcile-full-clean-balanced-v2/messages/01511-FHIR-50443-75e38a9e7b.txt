The final resolution was to include all proposed relationships, including Practitioner.

Initial application:

Additional context:
The Device module page is active source through source/hierarchy.xml and publish.ini, and publish.ini registers DeviceModule.svg as a published image. FHIR-50445 and FHIR-50444 are already represented on the combined branch with no-change audits for the same diagram update family.
