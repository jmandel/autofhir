FHIR-7859: Audit Condition recordedDate clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-7859 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification DSTU1 Patient Care issue asking that Condition.dateAsserted stop overlapping onset and instead reflect when the condition statement was documented. The approved resolution renamed assertedDate to recordedDate and changed the definition to "Date the Condition statement was documented".

Initial application:
The issue was applied in historical commit c6b154ca1b, which explicitly cited #7615, #7855, and #7859, renamed Condition.dateAsserted to Condition.dateRecorded in the then-current Condition spreadsheet and examples, and changed the definition/usage note away from detected/suspected onset semantics toward a documented/record-created date.

Additional context:
FHIR source later changed naming again: the R2-to-R3 rename map preserves Condition.dateRecorded as the predecessor of Condition.assertedDate, and the R3-to-current rename map preserves Condition.assertedDate as the predecessor of Condition.recordedDate. Later Condition work, including GF#14643/GF#17285 and J#27808/J#34392/J#34394, moved the old assertedDate semantics into the current recordedDate element and clarified that recordedDate is when this Condition record was created/first recorded, often system-generated, while recorder is the most recent author. Condition is an active resource in source/fhir.ini. No relevant Zulip or Confluence evidence was found that changes this conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active source defines Condition.recordedDate with short text "Date condition was first recorded," a definition saying it is when the Condition record was created in the system, and comments preserving the record-created/date-preservation semantics. That conveys the approved FHIR-7859 correction without reintroducing obsolete dateAsserted/dateRecorded names.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core follow-up is needed for FHIR-7859 unless the Patient Care work group wants to reopen the later, broader recordedDate wording established by subsequent Jira decisions.
