Issue request:
FHIR-44106 was an Applied FHIR-core search issue, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking whether Meta.lastUpdated and _lastUpdated should explicitly allow false positives and false negatives for facade servers or whether a new flexible mechanism was needed. Its final resolution points to the FHIR-45012 disposition rather than requiring a separate new core search parameter.

Initial application:

Later, 993291c03c for FHIR-45047 refined the Meta.lastUpdated comment with more explicit facade-server examples and a preference for updating too often over missing updates.

FHIR-45047 has already been represented on the combined branch, so this reconciliation does not duplicate that issue.
