FHIR-7303: Audit Encounter dietPreference repeatability

Issue request:
FHIR-7303 was a Published/Persuasive PA change request for FHIR-core Encounter, raised against DSTU1, asking that Encounter.hospitalization.dietPreference change from 0..1 to 0..* because an encounter may record more than one dietary preference or restriction. The approved resolution agreed to change the cardinality to 0..* and gave dairy-free plus nut-free as a reasonable example.

Initial application:
Commit 06c7ab85137432aeaa608312554b0f2f73e0dd7c, "Tracker# 6912, 7303, 7304, 7654 Ballot recon", changed the historical Encounter spreadsheet row for Encounter.hospitalization.dietPreference from 0..1 to 0..* and added the dairy-free/nut-free explanatory example. Later source refactoring moved the element out from Encounter.hospitalization to Encounter.dietPreference while source/fhir.ini retained an explicit mapping from the old path.

Additional context:
FHIR-7654 is a Published/Persuasive duplicate of the same request. In the current build, Encounter is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, and the active StructureDefinition source defines Encounter.dietPreference with max="*" plus an example comment for simultaneous dairy-free and nut-free preferences. The current requirements text also points full nutrition needs to NutritionOrder, addressing the only Jira comment found during this investigation. No relevant Zulip or Confluence evidence was found that changes this conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
