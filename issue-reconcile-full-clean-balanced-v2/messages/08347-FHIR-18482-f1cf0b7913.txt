FHIR-18482: Audit narrative resource rules wording

Issue request:
FHIR-18482 was a Published FHIR-core narrative change request with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked whether resource narrative rules could still vary based on how a resource is referenced and resolved that they do not; the specific source request was to change the narrative example from "Some resources (e.g. List, Composition) can provide specific rules..." to "Some resources (e.g. List) can provide specific rules...".
