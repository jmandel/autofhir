FHIR-13601: Audit patient search parameter target scope

Issue request:
FHIR-13601 is a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration change request raised against STU3 and applied for R5. It reported that the common clinical-patient search parameter appeared to target both Patient and Group even though patient search parameters should target only Patient. The approved resolution was to remove "or Group" from the Encounter-patient and ClinicalImpression-patient search parameter descriptions because their expressions already filtered to Patient and the separate subject search parameters cover Patient or Group.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by commit f3156be190fa6b0dcc3acec5f7ef59db4d052b27, which changed source/clinicalimpression/bundle-ClinicalImpression-search-params.xml from "Patient or group assessed" to "Patient assessed" and source/encounter/bundle-Encounter-search-params.xml from "The patient or group present at the encounter" to "The patient present at the encounter". Later source history preserved the FHIR-13601 scope: Encounter-patient now says "The patient related to this encounter" after FHIR-20479, and the patient search parameter still filters Encounter.subject to Patient and declares target Patient.

Additional context:
Related final issues FHIR-15903 and FHIR-28219 confirmed the broader rule that patient search parameters should target Patient only and use subject search parameters when Group is also searchable. FHIR-31714 addressed the generated/downloaded SearchParameter.target problem by requiring target-constrained reference search parameters to declare their narrowed target, and FHIR-32876 was closed as a duplicate of that work after the reporter confirmed the build showed target ["Patient"]. ClinicalImpression was later mirrored/renamed as ClinicalAssessment, and both retained patient-only patient search parameter wording and target declarations. source/fhir.ini actively lists Encounter and SearchParameter as current build inputs; it no longer actively lists ClinicalImpression, so the current-build check focuses on active Encounter/common SearchParameter source while noting the retained/renamed clinical assessment sources are also consistent.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source/searchparameter/common-search-parameters.xml has no Group target in the clinical-patient block, source/encounter/bundle-Encounter-search-params.xml defines Encounter-patient with a Patient-only description, Patient-filtered expression, and target Patient, and the retained ClinicalImpression plus renamed ClinicalAssessment patient search parameter bundles also use "Patient assessed", Patient-filtered expressions, and target Patient. The subject search parameters remain Patient-or-Group as intended and are not part of the FHIR-13601 correction.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. The current source already satisfies the published FHIR-13601 decision and later related target-scope fixes; no additional core source edit is needed unless a future build-scope review reactivates or removes the retained ClinicalImpression source directory.
