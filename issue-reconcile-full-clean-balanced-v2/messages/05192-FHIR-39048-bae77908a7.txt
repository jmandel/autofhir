FHIR-39048: Audit Device patient relationship

Issue request:
FHIR-39048 was a Published/Persuasive OO change request asking how to best model the relationship between Device and Patient for implantable or attached devices and for devices reused across patients. Its final resolution linked the disposition to FHIR-38904.

Initial application:
No source commit was found that mentions FHIR-39048 directly. The linked FHIR-38904 work appears to have applied the change: commit 620b8fa10c added the active DeviceAssociation resource, commit 97e59d026d commented out the old Device.association backbone and Device subject/patient search parameters, and commit 7a135eb348 performed FHIR-38904 QA on DeviceAssociation.

Additional context:
FHIR-38904's accepted motion created DeviceAssociation and removed Device.association from Device to avoid patient-identifying association history in reusable Device instances. FHIR-39009, FHIR-39226, FHIR-39243, and FHIR-38637 were part of the same Device Association discussion; FHIR-39226 and FHIR-39243 already have no-change reconciliation commits on the combined branch. The OO Device Association Confluence page and the #devices WGM tracker thread group FHIR-39048 with the FHIR-38904 DeviceAssociation work, while later implementer discussion points R5 users to DeviceAssociation for linking a device to a patient.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active build configuration includes both Device and DeviceAssociation, classifies DeviceAssociation as patient-scoped, records Device.patient as moved to DeviceAssociation, leaves Device without an active Device.association or Device.patient search surface after XML comments are stripped, and provides DeviceAssociation.subject, DeviceAssociation.period, and patient/subject search parameters for the relationship model.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. No FHIR-39048 core source follow-up is needed; future refinements should be handled as DeviceAssociation-specific issues rather than by reintroducing patient relationship data into Device.
