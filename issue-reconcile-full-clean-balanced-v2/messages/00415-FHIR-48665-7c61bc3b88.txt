Initial application:

PR HL7/fhir#3201 was merged after review on 2024-11-25.

Additional context:
EvidenceVariable is active in source/fhir.ini, and the fhir.ini hierarchy now lists the FHIR-48665 elements as active EvidenceVariable children. Later Applied issues refined the same area: FHIR-49755 intentionally changed several shorts/definitions and the missingDataMeaning cardinality, while FHIR-49077 and FHIR-49875 added dataStorage.component and dataStorage.delimiter. FHIR-55066 is a later terminology migration/background issue and is not a separate decision in this commit.
