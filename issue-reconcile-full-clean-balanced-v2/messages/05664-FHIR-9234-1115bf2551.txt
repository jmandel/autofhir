FHIR-9234: Audit Account coverage payment linkage

Issue request:
FHIR-9234 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration FHIR-core Encounter change request raised against DSTU2. It asked to identify the primary group responsible for payment of services rendered. The approved resolution added a reference from Account to Coverage, noted that list order could be significant, and recorded that the work was related to FHIR-7881's Encounter/EpisodeOfCare-to-Account linkage.

Initial application:
The historical Jira-tagged commit 519a0de34da28045c1031f016cb4540e9ca0f3be ("Tracker# 9234") added Account.coverage 0..* Reference(Coverage) to the spreadsheet-era Account source with payment/insurance/self-pay comments. The already-applied FHIR-7881 commit d79e721591063158c7da0b6ec54d09b1dd3fac91 supplied Encounter.account and EpisodeOfCare.account, giving Encounter payment responsibility an Account-mediated path. Later source refactors preserved the model in the current StructureDefinition XML files.

Additional context:
FHIR-12658 later changed Account.coverage from a direct repeating Coverage reference into a repeating BackboneElement with Account.coverage.coverage 1..1 Reference(Coverage) and Account.coverage.priority for coordination-of-benefit ordering. The current combined branch already contains the FHIR-12658 reconciliation that restores the lower-value-first priority wording. Direct Confluence searches found no FHIR-9234 references, and Zulip searches found no conflicting human discussion; the only directly relevant Zulip hit was a FHIR tracker bot notification for the later FHIR-12658 ordering issue.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active source already supports the approved responsibility-for-payment path: Encounter.account and EpisodeOfCare.account point to Account, while Account.coverage contains the nested Coverage reference and priority ordering guidance required by later decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. FHIR-9234 is satisfied by the current Account-mediated model, and adding a direct payment-responsibility field to Encounter would be broader than the approved resolution and inconsistent with the later Account.coverage backbone design.
