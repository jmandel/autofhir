FHIR-6938: Audit Appointment planned encounter support

Issue request:
FHIR-6938 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU1. It asked how to communicate planned encounters that were not yet scheduled when Appointment required start/end date-times, and the final resolution said to use Appointment's new proposed status from tracker 5713, make start/end optional for proposed or cancelled appointments, add minutesDuration, and use comments/notes for scheduling guidance.

Initial application:
Historical commit 44a2cf49ea applied the Appointment/AppointmentResponse reconciliation for trackers including 6938 and 5713 by adding proposed status support, changing Appointment.start and Appointment.end to 0..1, adding minutesDuration, and expanding notes/usage guidance. Commit 8db2dceb19 then specifically referenced tracker 6938 and corrected app-3 so proposed and cancelled appointments can be missing start/end dates.

Additional context:
FHIR-5713 is the supporting proposed-status decision named in the FHIR-6938 resolution. FHIR-5702, the related proposed-Encounter discussion, was closed no-change, which supports not adding a proposed Encounter status for this issue. FHIR-18824 later added waitlist as another Appointment status that may omit start/end; that later broadening explains why current app-3 includes waitlist in addition to the proposed/cancelled rule from FHIR-6938.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Current build-scope Appointment source already has proposed status, start/end cardinality 0..1, app-3 requiring start/end except for proposed/cancelled/waitlist statuses, minutesDuration, requestedPeriod guidance, and Appointment.note guidance for planned appointments whose start/end are not set. Encounter is active source but does not need a proposed status change under the final FHIR-6938/FHIR-5702/FHIR-5713 decision trail.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. The current specification conveys the intended way to represent unscheduled planned encounters through Appointment rather than Encounter, and further appointment waitlist or encounter-request modeling questions should stay with later dedicated trackers.
