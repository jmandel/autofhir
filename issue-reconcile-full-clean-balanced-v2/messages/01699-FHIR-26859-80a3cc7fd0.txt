Issue request:
FHIR-26859 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It requested keeping Procedure.subject at 1..1 while expanding the allowed reference targets beyond Patient and Group to include Device, Practitioner, Organization, and Location, and updating the Procedure definition, subject short/definition, and scope text for non-patient procedures such as inspections and licensing.

Initial application:

The later source rework in 33b409a4b6 preserved the same target list and text in source/procedure/structuredefinition-Procedure.xml and source/procedure/procedure-introduction.xml.

Additional context:
The active source/fhir.ini resource entry includes procedure=Procedure, so the Procedure resource folder is in current build scope. The current Procedure source also includes later Procedure.focus wording in the subject definition, but that does not reverse or narrow the FHIR-26859 target expansion. The linked Zulip discussion and Confluence references align with the Jira rationale that Procedure needed to support non-patient subjects; no conflicting community evidence was found.
