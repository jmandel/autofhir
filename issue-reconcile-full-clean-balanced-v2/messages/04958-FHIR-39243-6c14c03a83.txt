FHIR-39243: Audit Device association relocation

Issue request:
FHIR-39243 was a Published/Persuasive OO change request, applied for R5, asking to move patient association out of Device and explicitly resolving by linking to the FHIR-38904 disposition. The concern was that the R5 ballot Device.association[0..*].humanSubject approach could retain multiple patients' identifying information in a Device instance and leak PHI through the Device patient compartment.

Initial application:
No source commit was found that mentions FHIR-39243 directly. The linked FHIR-38904 work appears to have applied the change: 620b8fa10c added the draft DeviceAssociation resource, 97e59d026d commented out Device.association and the Device subject/patient search parameters, and 7a135eb348 applied QA changes to DeviceAssociation. Current source still contains some old Device association XML inside comments, but those comments are ignored by the build and do not define active elements or search parameters.

Additional context:
FHIR-38637 and FHIR-39009 were also resolved by the FHIR-38904 disposition, and FHIR-39226 is a companion ballot issue with the same request. The OO Device Association project page and the #devices WGM tracker thread group FHIR-39243 with FHIR-38904 and the other Device association tickets. Current source/fhir.ini lists Device and DeviceAssociation as active resources, classifies Device as business and DeviceAssociation as patient, and records the R4-to-current mapping that Device.patient moved to DeviceAssociation. Later DeviceAssociation work changed details of the new resource, but did not reintroduce an active Device patient association.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. Comment-stripped checks of source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml and source/device/bundle-Device-search-params.xml found no active Device.association, Device.association.humanSubject, Device-subject, or Device-patient definitions. Active DeviceAssociation source provides the subject, period, and patient/subject search-parameter surface needed for the FHIR-38904/FHIR-39243 model, and Device's narrative points ownership/custodianship relationships to DeviceAssociation.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is for FHIR-39243. Any future cleanup of ignored historical XML comments or generated-looking diagrams should be handled separately because the authoritative current source already implements this issue's required Device-to-DeviceAssociation relocation.
