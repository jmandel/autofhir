FHIR-13855: Audit Composition summary settings

Issue request:
FHIR-13855 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for STU3. It asked to review Composition _summary behavior against DocumentReference FHIR-12949, then updated the accepted Composition disposition to keep summary flags on status, type, subject, author, title, event, identifier/masterIdentifier, confidentiality, encounter, date, and custodian, while removing flags from non-Composition content fields, attester and children, relatesTo, section.author, and section.mode.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git-svn commit 1ed44d057f3aa04de302c0203b78b3c9dce9fd66 (svn revision 14131), which changed the then-authoritative source/composition/composition-spreadsheet.xml and removed summary marks from attester, attester children, relatesTo, relatesTo children, section.mode, and section.author. The old spreadsheet source was later replaced by the current StructureDefinition source, but the same summary/no-summary pattern is preserved in source/composition/structuredefinition-Composition.xml.

Additional context:
Composition is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. Later Composition evolution explains differences from the STU3-era element names and exact list: FHIR-17555 renamed Composition.class to Composition.category and kept it aligned with DocumentReference category behavior; commit f3e7ac34683c8671c4394b99790aa156564d279e removed Composition.confidentiality in favor of Bundle.meta.security; section.mode has since been removed; and newer current-build metadata such as url, version, name, useContext, and classification have their own summary flags from later changes. Zulip implementation discussion for GF#13855 records that event.detail was intentionally left as-is and that class/category should be a summary element.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit made no source changes. Current source already has summary flags on the still-current counterparts of the requested kept elements (identifier, status, type, category, subject, encounter, date, author, title, custodian, event, event.period, and event.detail) and no summary flag on the requested removals that still exist (attester and children, relatesTo and children, section.author). The removed or renamed STU3 elements are accounted for by later source history rather than by a current-spec omission.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Do not restore the STU3 literal list or remove later summary flags while reconciling FHIR-13855; revisit only if the Structured Documents work group opens a new current-version issue to redefine the R6 Composition summary set.
