Initial application:

Git history shows a pre-application commit for Condition and a later GF#15583 commit that updated modifier reasons across CarePlan, AllergyIntolerance, and Condition and added entered-in-error to the CarePlan.activity.detail.status value set. The affected resources are active in source/fhir.ini. Related Jira FHIR-15579 confirmed that CarePlan activity status should stay a modifier because FHIR-15583 added entered-in-error. Zulip and Patient Care minutes show the issue originated from the cross-resource missing-isModifierReason cleanup and that PC discussed retaining the modifier semantics.
