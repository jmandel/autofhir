FHIR-47463: Audit ServiceRequest requisition guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-47463 was Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6. It asked for guidance explaining why both ServiceRequest.requisition and RequestOrchestration can group related requests, and when each mechanism should be used. The approved resolution was to add ServiceRequest Scope and Usage text saying that simple grouping, such as for billing, can use the requisition element, while coordinated execution may need another resource such as RequestOrchestration.
