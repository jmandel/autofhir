Issue request:
FHIR-16972 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core datatypes issue from the 2018-May Core Norm Infrastructure ballot. It asked to clarify Attachment.data by replacing wording that said "In XML, represented using base64" with representation-neutral wording that the attachment data is a sequence of bytes, base64 encoded.
