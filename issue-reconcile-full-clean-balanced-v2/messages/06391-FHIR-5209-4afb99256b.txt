FHIR-5209: Audit Encounter and EpisodeOfCare referral links

Issue request:
FHIR-5209 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Jan 2015 ballot issue for Encounter and EpisodeOfCare. The ballot comment questioned whether EpisodeOfCare should link directly to referralRequest or whether the link should go through Encounter; the final resolution was modified to have EpisodeOfCare and Encounter point to the referral, with discussion of an incomingReferralRequest/fulfilsReferral name and 0..* cardinality for multiple referrals.

Initial application:
The explicit Jira-tagged application was commit 27302910ae49c6d1fd5544682e5f48f9e365b347, which added Encounter.incomingReferralRequest as 0..* Reference(ReferralRequest) plus an incomingReferral search parameter. The EpisodeOfCare side already had referralRequest, and the closely related March 2015 EpisodeOfCare reconciliation commit f659c3fbc16870414aac80f798d75afcb5480bf8 made EpisodeOfCare.referralRequest repeatable and defined it as incoming referrals fulfilled by the EpisodeOfCare. Later refactors preserved the same semantics while ReferralRequest became ServiceRequest and Encounter.incomingReferral was renamed to Encounter.basedOn.

Additional context:
Encounter and EpisodeOfCare are active resources in source/fhir.ini. Current Encounter.basedOn is 0..* and targets ServiceRequest among other request resources, with an incomingReferral alias and a definition that covers incoming referrals and procedure requests. Current EpisodeOfCare.referralRequest remains 0..* Reference(ServiceRequest) and is defined as incoming referrals fulfilled by the EpisodeOfCare. The related FHIR-5210 referral-direction issue has already been audited on the combined branch and explicitly treats FHIR-5209 as the same change family. Zulip and Confluence searches found no direct FHIR-5209 discussion that conflicts with the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative source already satisfies the FHIR-5209 decision: EpisodeOfCare still carries repeatable incoming referral requests, and Encounter carries the referral/request link through the renamed basedOn element.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR core follow-up is needed for FHIR-5209 unless Patient Administration later reopens the broader question of whether EpisodeOfCare should continue to hold a direct incoming referral reference.
