FHIR-7414: Audit OperationOutcome business-issue boundary

Issue request:
FHIR-7414 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification clarification asking how OperationOutcome represents business issues, using lack of insurance coverage as an example. The accepted resolution removed "Business and" from the old Alert/Contraindication boundary text and changed "Administrative" to "Technical" so the spec would not say OperationOutcome covers business issues.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records svn revision 5790. Git history shows commit 192e425a5dfe7e0bf1684dd8dc97176c9fe4ec7a, whose git-svn-id is build@5790, changing source/contraindication/contraindication-introduction.xml from "administrative issues" to "technical issues" and from "business/technical issues" / "Business and technical issues" to "technical issues" / "Technical issues". The old Contraindication/Alert area has since been refactored into DetectedIssue plus OperationOutcome narrative in the current build.

Additional context:
Current source/fhir.ini actively includes both DetectedIssue and OperationOutcome. Current OperationOutcome narrative says OperationOutcome is for attempted system operations and is not used for clinical or workflow issues, which are handled by DetectedIssue or other resources; it also explains that an OperationOutcome can accompany a DetectedIssue when a requested action is rejected due to a clinical issue. The OperationOutcome StructureDefinition definition remains about messages from a system action, while DetectedIssue defines the clinical issue role and retains Contraindication and Alert as aliases. Focused Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later decision or community discussion that contradicts this interpretation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current authoritative source no longer asserts that business issues are conveyed using OperationOutcome, and it preserves the technical/system-vs-clinical/workflow boundary requested by FHIR-7414 without needing redundant wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If the community wants new guidance for financial, coverage, or other business outcomes, that should be handled as a new substantive workflow/administrative-domain decision rather than as a FHIR-7414 cleanup.
