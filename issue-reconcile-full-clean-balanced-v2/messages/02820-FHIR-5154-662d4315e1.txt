FHIR-5154: Audit document wholeness guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-5154 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification. The January 2015 ballot comment asked the Structured Documents work group to identify or add specification language addressing the wholeness of FHIR documents and the status of narrative content when a Composition and its section-supporting resources are wrapped into a Document.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git-svn commit 11e32d8670a345d8c934c76c3d204222b3716303, which added wording to the then-current Composition introduction saying that when a Composition and its Lists are wrapped into a Document, the List narrative is part of the attested content and an indivisible part of the document. Later Composition refactors replaced the old section.content/List model with Composition.section.text and Composition.section.entry.

Additional context:
Later final document decisions changed how the same rule is expressed. FHIR-12963 added general clinical-document principles, including wholeness, to the clinical document guidance. FHIR-15723 clarified the Documents guide's attested-content wording and distinguished presented document content from other included resources. FHIR-7404 moved section narrative into Composition sections and, in the already-published combined branch, removed remaining stale List/section.content language in favor of Composition.section.text. FHIR-17109 also confirms that document wholeness means authentication applies to the whole document in context, not that every referenced resource must be inlined.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Active current source already explains document wholeness and attested content in the maintained model: clinical document guidance states that a document is a whole unit, the Documents guide says omitted referenced resources can compromise wholeness and defines the attested presentation, Composition notes point attesters to subject, Composition, and Composition.section.text narratives, and List notes now say List.text is not part of the attested document presentation unless incorporated into Composition narrative or section.text.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. Reintroducing the old List narrative wording would conflict with later Composition.section.text decisions; any future change should be handled as a new document-presentation policy issue rather than as additional FHIR-5154 application work.
