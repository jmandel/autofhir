FHIR-16894: Audit Bundle grouping list wording

Issue request:
FHIR-16894 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for STU3 after a May 2018 Core Norm Infrastructure ballot comment that the Bundle boundaries section listed List, Group, and Composition as grouping resources but omitted other grouping mechanisms such as CareTeam and DocumentManifest. The accepted resolution was to indicate that the list is not exhaustive.

Initial application:
The application is explicit in git history: commit 7b9cb0c7cff88bdf34b84ed4f74e52b8a74205e7, matching Jira's svn revision 14362 comment, added a "This list is not exhaustive; other resources also provde grouping functionality." paragraph to source/bundle/bundle-introduction.xml. Later typo-cleanup commits c3155bc601c17a8d7a5a272d31b562091699fd53 and 14537b3d6fc2504ae41c2de6340f30ceb69e9bac corrected the accidental surrounding wording and misspelling, leaving the intended non-exhaustive statement in current source.

Current source still lists List, Group, and Composition and immediately states that "This list is not exhaustive; other resources also provide grouping functionality." A later combined-branch reconciliation for FHIR-17036 changed adjacent Composition wording on the same page but preserved the non-exhaustive sentence, and no direct Zulip or Confluence evidence was found that conflicts with the Jira/source conclusion.
