FHIR-29930: Audit subject alignment

Issue request:
FHIR-29930 was Published with a Persuasive OO resolution for R5 after being raised against R4 and intended for R4B. It replaced the retracted FHIR-29652 placeholder and asked to synchronize Observation.subject and DiagnosticReport.subject so both included Patient, Group, Device, Location, Organization, Procedure, Practitioner, Medication, and Substance, in the context of FHIR-29266 adding Medication and Substance to DiagnosticReport.subject.

Initial application:
The Jira records application in fe81540c84 for R4B and 11b15d7ecc for master. The master commit added Medication and Substance to Observation.subject and added Organization, Procedure, and Practitioner to DiagnosticReport.subject; FHIR-29266's f6109edb34 had already added Medication and Substance to DiagnosticReport.subject on the R4B path. The current active resource definitions are still in build scope through source/fhir.ini.

Additional context:
FHIR-29031 is part of the same Observation subject expansion family and its Published resolution broadened Observation.subject to Medication, Substance, NutritionProduct, and BiologicallyDerivedProduct; the current Observation source includes all of those plus the FHIR-29930 baseline targets. FHIR-32575 is a later Published OO decision that supersedes the Procedure part of FHIR-29930 for DiagnosticReport.subject: it explicitly removed Procedure from DiagnosticReport.subject and directed non-subject focal components to separate modeling. That later issue is already represented on the combined branch, and FHIR-49614 later added the current DiagnosticReport.procedure 0..* Reference(Procedure) element.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current Observation.subject includes Patient, Group, Device, Location, Organization, Procedure, Practitioner, Medication, Substance, BiologicallyDerivedProduct, and NutritionProduct. Current DiagnosticReport.subject includes Patient, Group, Device, Location, Organization, Practitioner, Medication, Substance, and BiologicallyDerivedProduct, and current DiagnosticReport.procedure references Procedure. Restoring Procedure to DiagnosticReport.subject or forcing exact target equality under FHIR-29930 would reverse FHIR-32575 rather than complete the seed issue.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. The seed's Medication/Substance alignment is present, and the only apparent current divergence is explained by later published decisions. Any future desire to add NutritionProduct to DiagnosticReport.subject or otherwise re-align the two subject elements should be handled as new OO work rather than as a FHIR-29930 fixup.
