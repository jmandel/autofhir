FHIR-5781: Audit ElementDefinition binding strength model

Issue request:
FHIR-5781 was Published/Persuasive for DSTU1 and asked that the two ElementDefinition.binding controls, conformance and extensibility, be rationalized into a single element, with an extension considered for edge-case binding variations.

Initial application:
The Jira comments point to FHIR-3187 and record "svn 4300 for 3187". The corresponding git-svn commit f45d3ec3d1 added ElementDefinition.binding.strength, marked the old isExtensible and conformance rows inactive, and updated terminology narrative for the new strength codes.

Additional context:
FHIR-3187 records the detailed Vocab motion to adopt one strength element with required, extensible, preferred, and example codes, plus a value-set extension mechanism. The current build keeps ElementDefinition active and normative, models binding through ElementDefinition.binding.strength plus ElementDefinition.binding.additional, and documents that additional bindings replace the former max/min value set extensions. The extension-pack copies of elementdefinition-maxValueSet and elementdefinition-minValueSet are retired/deprecated and point users to additionalBinding instead.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The authoritative current source already has one active ElementDefinition.binding.strength element bound to BindingStrength, no active binding.conformance or binding.extensibility elements, and terminology prose that describes strength and additional bindings consistently.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-5781 unless a future work group decision changes the current additional-binding model.
