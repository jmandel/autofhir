FHIR-38646: Audit BodyStructure scope wording

Issue request:
FHIR-38646 was a Published/Persuasive R5 OO change request asking that BodyStructure Scope and Usage clarify the post-FHIR-33129 ability to represent complex or multiple body structures/locations with included and excluded elements, qualifiers such as laterality/directionality, and use of BodyStructure outside the context of another resource.

Initial application:
The Jira comment points to ef5a412, but the relevant source application in this checkout is commit 16ae4fdaa9, which added the requested two-paragraph BodyStructure Scope and Usage text to source/bodystructure/bodystructure-introduction.xml. The active current build still registers BodyStructure in source/fhir.ini, and the current StructureDefinition keeps includedStructure at 1..* and excludedStructure at 0..*, matching the structural premise from FHIR-33129.

Additional context:
The OO Specimen minutes on 2022-11-14 record the motion to find FHIR-38646 persuasive. Later BodyStructure edits refined nearby wording: FHIR-48789 replaced the pre-coordination phrasing while preserving the complex included/excluded structures sentence; FHIR-54844 removed the duplicate "the" typo; and FHIR-54872 replaced the Boundaries and Relationships section with CodeableReference guidance without weakening the Scope and Usage clarification.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current Scope and Usage text says BodyStructure can represent complex body structures and/or locations using included and excluded elements, can indicate laterality and directionality when not indicated in corresponding codes, and can be tracked outside another resource, including as a Procedure or Observation target. That is the needed current-spec expression of FHIR-38646 after later accepted edits.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core follow-up is needed for FHIR-38646 unless the work group later reopens BodyStructure scope wording more broadly; related R6 BodyStructure issues should be reconciled under their own keys if selected.
