FHIR-16517: Audit Observation subject and focus guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-16517 was a Published/Persuasive OO change request raised against the STU3 Observation page. It asked to clarify the distinction between the person or entity whose record contains the Observation, the subject/focus of information, and the purpose of Observation.focus. The approved resolution specifically changed the Observation.focus short display from "The focal point of the observation" to "What the observation is about, when it is not about the subject of record (e.g. fetus observations in a mother's record)."

Initial application:
The change was pre-applied in the historical Observation spreadsheet by commit 99532b4c0a6c05d7f6d3176df90ba33f1940abbc, which replaced the old "focal point" short display with "What the observation is about, when it is not about the subject of record." A subsequent GF#16517 commit carried the spreadsheet through a larger Observation update. The current build no longer uses that spreadsheet as the authoritative source, but the active Observation StructureDefinition preserves the approved short wording and adds subject/focus examples.

Additional context:
The 2018 Zulip follow-up and OO minutes confirm the intended model: Observation.subject is the record association and the default focus when no other focus is declared; Observation.focus is used when the Observation is about another person, device, location, or other entity in that record context. Later applied work, including FHIR-29031 and FHIR-43357, updated the same Observation.subject and Observation.focus text to cover newer subject target types and DeviceMetric-as-focus examples without reversing the FHIR-16517 interpretation. Those later issues already have their own Issue-Reconcile-Key commits on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. source/fhir.ini lists Observation as an active resource, and the active Observation source already states that Observation.subject is the patient/group/location/device/etc. this observation is about and into whose or what record it is placed. Observation.focus has the requested short display, defines focus as the actual focus when it is not the subject of record, and includes examples for a parent in a child's record, a fetus in a mother's record, body structures, implanted devices, and DeviceMetric settings. The Observation notes page also explains that subject is the record association and focus records the other entity when the Observation is about someone or something other than the subject.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current source satisfies FHIR-16517 after considering the initial application and later Observation refinements; adding the parenthetical fetus example back into the short display would duplicate examples already present in the focus comment and notes rather than changing implementer understanding.
