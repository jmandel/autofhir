FHIR-37550: Clarify stand-alone QuestionnaireResponse guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-37550 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5. It asked FHIR-I/SDC to fix QuestionnaireResponse's stand-alone concept because QuestionnaireResponse.item.linkId is required, and the approved resolution made QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire 1..1 while allowing non-resolving or extension-only questionnaire metadata for legacy or stand-alone responses.

Initial application:
The historical Questionnaire-fixes commit ab0ef5c3d2e5d0f0ab7672dfd6012f39ea567865 applied the main structural change by making QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire required, adding the approved non-resolving/extension-only comment to the element, and updating examples including an extension-only questionnaire element. That application did not update the active QuestionnaireResponse notes section that originally caused the confusion: it still said a QuestionnaireResponse "may be stand-alone or may point" to a Questionnaire and conditioned linkage guidance only on referring to a Questionnaire.

Additional context:
The #questionnaire Zulip thread behind the issue records the intended distinction: linkIds are not computably meaningful without questionnaire context, QR.questionnaire should generally be present even if it does not resolve, and an extension-only questionnaire element can convey display name, identifier, or data-absent-reason-like metadata. Confluence minutes show FHIR-I moving the issue to SDC, seeking Zulip review, and then accepting it as Persuasive with Modification. QuestionnaireResponse is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. Later FHIR-38853 edits moved the linkage diagram into this same notes section but did not supersede the FHIR-37550 stand-alone/cardinality decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated source/questionnaireresponse/questionnaireresponse-notes.xml so the active narrative now says QuestionnaireResponse has a required questionnaire element, scopes the consistency/linkId guidance to resolvable Questionnaires, and explicitly explains how legacy or stand-alone responses can use a non-resolving canonical or extension-only questionnaire element and what that means for linkId interpretation.

Recommendation:
Keep this source correction. It preserves the approved R5 structural behavior and example pattern while removing stale narrative that could lead implementers to think QuestionnaireResponse.questionnaire remains optional.
