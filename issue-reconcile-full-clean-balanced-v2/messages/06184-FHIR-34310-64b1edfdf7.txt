FHIR-34310: Audit Device.parent hierarchy wording

Issue request:
FHIR-34310 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification R5 Orders and Observations change request for FHIR-core Device. It asked to restore Device.parent to a hierarchical parent-device relationship by replacing "The device that this device is attached to or is part of." with "The higher level or encompassing device that this device is a logical part of." and adding the vital signs monitor/module example as a comment.

Initial application:
