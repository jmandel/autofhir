FHIR-17375: Audit Medication strength reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-17375 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification STU3 Pharmacy change request asking whether Medication.amount needed removal or revised wording because it described an "amount of drug in package" after package concepts had been removed. The final resolution required changing Medication.ingredient.amount to Medication.ingredient.strength, changing the datatype to Ratio, and fixing related definitions.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the historical GF 17375 commits. Commit 783b9b0cce changed the Medication spreadsheet element from Medication.ingredient.amount to Medication.ingredient.strength, changed the datatype from SimpleQuantity to Ratio, and updated medication examples from <amount> to <strength>; merge commit 7a4277462af4c3e0a9f5bb22a07ec31cbeaed149 recorded the applied Jira implementation.

Additional context:
Medication is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. The current authoritative Medication StructureDefinition no longer has Medication.ingredient.amount or active Medication example <amount> content. It defines Medication.ingredient.strength[x] and its definition/comment now incorporate later final Pharmacy decisions: FHIR-19439 added codeable strength for sufficient/trace quantity, FHIR-25122 added Quantity when the denominator is implicit, FHIR-23733 clarified the ingredient item as the basis of strength, FHIR-25944 later renamed/reworked the separate package amount concept, and FHIR-36499 added current narrative and comments about ingredients, strength, and packaging. Those later decisions explain why current source has packageSize/package narrative and more than just Ratio for ingredient strength.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Medication source already preserves the FHIR-17375 semantic change from ingredient amount to ingredient strength, while later applied issues intentionally refined the strength datatype and packaging language. Editing current packageSize or narrative merely to remove package wording would contradict later published/applied Pharmacy decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Future work on Medication packaging or ingredient strength should be handled under the later issue family, not by reopening FHIR-17375's already-applied STU3 rename.
