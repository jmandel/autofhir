FHIR-15906: Audit patient compartment provenance links

Issue request:
FHIR-15906 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for the Patient CompartmentDefinition. It reported that Provenance.target.subject and Provenance.target.patient were confusing invalid chained parameters because Provenance.target can point to any resource, and the resolution asked to remove AuditEvent and Provenance from the compartment while noting their usefulness for $everything and access control.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git-svn history as b63ba456ef969b5e173fc94623f34884301e68cc, which removed the indirect chained compartment links rather than deleting the resource rows: AuditEvent changed from patient | agent.patient | entity.patient to patient, and Provenance changed from target.subject | target.patient | patient to patient. That application eliminated the reported target.subject and target.patient ambiguity in the compartment source.

Additional context:
Later published/applied decisions changed the correct current interpretation. FHIR-33155 added explicit AuditEvent.patient and Provenance.patient elements and direct patient search parameters to enable deterministic patient-compartment discovery. FHIR-43373 then updated the Patient $everything text because Provenance and AuditEvent are now in the Patient compartment, and the related Zulip discussion recorded that this inclusion was intentional even if servers apply policy to returned resources. The active build includes CompartmentDefinition, AuditEvent, and Provenance source, so these current direct patient links are in scope.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current CompartmentDefinition source no longer contains target.subject, target.patient, agent.patient, or entity.patient links, and it lists AuditEvent and Provenance only through the direct patient parameter. The corresponding AuditEvent.patient and Provenance.patient elements and search parameters now exist in the current source, so removing the rows would conflict with later final work-group decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. Do not re-apply the original removal wording literally; the confusing chained links from FHIR-15906 are gone, while the later FHIR-33155 and FHIR-43373 decisions supersede the broader idea that AuditEvent and Provenance should be outside the Patient compartment.
