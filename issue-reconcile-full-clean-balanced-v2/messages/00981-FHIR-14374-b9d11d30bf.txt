FHIR-14374: Audit Immunization reason value sets

Issue request:
FHIR-14374 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for STU3 Immunization. It asked whether the collapsed Immunization.reason element could support separate value sets for reasons a vaccine was given and reasons a vaccine was not given, and the final Jira resolution explicitly deferred to FHIR-14377.

Initial application:
The FHIR-14374 ticket was closed after FHIR-14377 was applied. The February 2018 source commit f451f3fc did not name FHIR-14374 in the subject, but it implemented the linked resolution by moving not-done reasons from the old Immunization.reason element to Immunization.statusReason, renaming the performed-reason element to reasonCode, adding reasonReference, and adding separate ImmunizationReason and ImmunizationStatusReason value sets.

Additional context:
FHIR-14377 is already represented on the combined branch by commit 77723ba4ef568134e5cd8f32bae854fb4964fb97, which refreshed the active Immunization.statusReason comment to point at the current R6 Immunization.reason element. Later model refactoring merged R4 reasonCode and reasonReference into Immunization.reason CodeableReference, while source/fhir.ini records the compatibility mappings from reasonCode to reason.concept and reasonReference to reason.reference. FHIR-17811 and FHIR-54325 later refined statusReason wording and constraints without reversing the FHIR-14374/FHIR-14377 distinction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current active Immunization StructureDefinition has Immunization.statusReason bound to http://hl7.org/fhir/ValueSet/immunization-status-reason for reasons an immunization was not performed or was entered in error, and Immunization.reason bound to http://hl7.org/fhir/ValueSet/immunization-reason for why an immunization occurred. The already-published FHIR-14377 reconciliation also fixed the remaining stale reasonCode wording in the current source.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No additional FHIR-14374 source change is needed in core; future changes to broaden or constrain Immunization.statusReason should be handled under newer statusReason tickets rather than reopening this STU3 value-set split.
