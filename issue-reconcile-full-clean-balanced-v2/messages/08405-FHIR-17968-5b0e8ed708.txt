No directly relevant Zulip or Confluence record was found that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The active CoverageEligibilityResponse introduction already contains the requested Boundaries and Relationships section, directs use of CoverageEligibilityResponse for responses, distinguishes related eClaim resources, and describes CoverageEligibilityRequest as requesting a CoverageEligibilityResponse. Adding a new literal "only response" sentence would be redundant and could overstate the boundary beyond the applied historical wording and current optional request-reference model.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Revisit only if FM wants new normative wording about exclusive response semantics; that would be a new work-group decision rather than a missing application of FHIR-17968.
