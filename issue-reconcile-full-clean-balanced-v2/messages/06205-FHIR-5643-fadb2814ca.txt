FHIR-5643: Audit body-site choice reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-5643 was a Published Patient Care change request with resolution Persuasive. It followed FHIR-3600 and asked to change Procedure.bodySite and Condition.location to a bodySite choice of CodeableConcept or Reference(BodySite).

Initial application:
The Jira comments record svn 4981. The git-svn import is f734eb20e9cea5803c519475780be919724f148f, whose subject cites {#5643}; it changed Condition.location.code to Condition.location.site[x] with type CodeableConcept | Reference(BodySite), removed the old location.detail row, and updated the Condition location search path. Procedure already had Procedure.bodySite.site[x] CodeableConcept|Reference(BodySite) before and after that commit, while the same commit's Procedure change was Procedure.used.

Additional context:
FHIR-3600 established the broader cross-resource body-site harmonization intent. Later source history moved Condition away from Condition.location to Condition.bodySite, renamed BodySite to BodyStructure, and added separate Condition.bodyStructure and Procedure.bodyStructure Reference(BodyStructure) elements. The later Applied Patient Care issues FHIR-39630 and FHIR-39288 explicitly chose the separate bodyStructure element pattern rather than a choice/CodeableReference in order to preserve compatibility. The sibling follow-up issues FHIR-5644 and FHIR-5645, plus the Procedure target-site clarification FHIR-7630, are already represented on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Condition and Procedure are active in source/fhir.ini, and the current authoritative StructureDefinitions no longer contain Condition.location. They expose bodySite as CodeableConcept and the BodyStructure reference path as bodyStructure with mutual-exclusion invariants, which is the current compatible form of the body-site choice requested by the DSTU1 issue family after later decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not convert these elements to CodeableReference or a literal choice unless a later triaged issue is resolved to make that breaking model change.
