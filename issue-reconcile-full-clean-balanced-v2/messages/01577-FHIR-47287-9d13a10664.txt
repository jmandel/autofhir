FHIR-47287: Clarify Subscription status diagram labels

Issue request:
FHIR-47287 is Applied/Persuasive for R6 and asked the Subscriptions workflow examples to avoid ambiguous shorthand such as `status=active` by saying `Subscription.status=active` when the status being changed is on the Subscription resource.

Initial application:
The prior Jira-tagged implementation commit 53e52b22414708ad884b48ca485813ea62939f15, merged via HL7/fhir#3967, updated two labels in `images/subscriptions/subscription-workflow-general.*`. The current source still had one `status = "requested"` label in the same in-band workflow and two `status: requested` labels in Subscription error-recovery diagrams.

Additional context:
The FHIR-I block vote approved the subscription tracker set 17-0-0. `source/subscriptions.html` embeds the affected diagrams, and `publish.ini` publishes their checked-in SVGs, so the PlantUML sources and SVGs are in the current build surface. The `SubscriptionStatus.status` label in the broken-communication diagram is intentionally unchanged because that response is a SubscriptionStatus resource, not the Subscription resource.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the remaining Subscription-resource status labels in the in-band workflow and error-recovery diagrams to say `Subscription.status`, and mirrored those changes in the checked-in SVGs referenced by the build.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is; it completes the applied Jira clarification without changing unrelated SubscriptionStatus wording or broader subscription behavior.
