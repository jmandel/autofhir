FHIR-37736: Audit Observation.focus related-person guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-37736 was an Applied/Persuasive with Modification Orders and Observations change request for R6. It asked for clarification of what it means when Observation.subject is present and Observation.focus is a RelatedPerson, using a caregiver with back strain as the motivating case, and it resolved to update Observation.focus guidance and make Observation.focus a modifier. The resolution also noted that Patient Care would make the parallel Procedure.focus modifier change in FHIR-41268.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation. Jira's final comment points to PR merge history, and the source-changing commit found by git history is 7a5dceb8125349335cb7532470282bc4b609f368, which made Observation.focus a modifier, added an isModifierReason, added a FiveWs modifier mapping exception, and inserted the caregiver back-strain use case into the Observation.focus definition.

Additional context:
Later Observation work for FHIR-43357, already represented on the combined branch, deliberately rewrote the Observation.focus definition/comment to be subject-general and to list examples. That later text removed the exact caregiver-back-strain sentence but retained the substance that focus is the actual target when it is not the subject of record, represents someone or something associated with the subject, and changes who the observation is about. The Observation notes page also says that when an Observation is about another person but collected in a patient context, the patient remains the subject and the other person is recorded in focus. Confluence records for the original PC and OO discussions support that the intended clarification was this subject/focus split and modifier treatment. No Zulip record was found that conflicts with that interpretation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit intentionally makes no source change. Current authoritative source/observation/structuredefinition-Observation.xml is in build scope, has Observation.focus isModifier="true" with a modifier reason, and its current definition/comment plus the Observation notes page answer the RelatedPerson/caregiver scenario without re-adding the exact older example text. Re-adding the back-strain sentence would be redundant and would partially undo the later FHIR-43357 cleanup.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core FHIR source follow-up is needed for FHIR-37736 unless OO specifically wants to restore an explicit RelatedPerson caregiver example in addition to the current subject-general guidance.
