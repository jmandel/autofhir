FHIR-16948: Align primitive seconds discretion wording

Issue request:
FHIR-16948 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for FHIR-core datatypes. It asked whether seconds in dateTime and time may always be ignored or only when zero-filled, and the accepted resolution was to clarify that ignoring seconds is up to the receiver.

Initial application:
The historical application commit 4479281e5a updated source/datatypes.html for both dateTime and time to say seconds may be ignored "at receiver discretion." That wording remains present on the current datatype narrative page.

Additional context:
The active primitive spreadsheet source in source/datatypes/primitives.xml still used the older dateTime wording without "at receiver discretion," and after the FHIR-5871 reconciliation the time primitive carried the same older sentence. This spreadsheet feeds generated primitive StructureDefinitions such as datetime.profile.json and time.profile.json, so the gap affected current build-scope conformance artifacts even though the narrative datatype table was correct.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Updated the dateTime and time primitive source descriptions to include "at receiver discretion," matching the published FHIR-16948 resolution and the current narrative datatype table.

Recommendation:
Keep this source fix. It synchronizes generated primitive profile descriptions with the already-applied datatype page wording and requires no regex or normative behavior change.
