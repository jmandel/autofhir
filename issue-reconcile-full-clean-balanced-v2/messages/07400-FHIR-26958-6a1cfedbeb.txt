FHIR-26958: Audit subscription notification normativity note

Issue request:
FHIR-26958 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I change request raised for R4 and applied for R5. It asked for the Bundle page to note that Subscription Notification bundle details were non-normative/trial-use, including details such as the subscription-notification bundle type, constraints, and notification-specific guidance.

Initial application:
No git commit in the inspected history explicitly mentions FHIR-26958. The application appears to have been implicit in the Subscription R5 work: the R5 source contains a StandardsStatus trial-use macro directly under the Bundle "Subscription Notification" subsection, and the 2020 subscriptions R5 feedback thread lists FHIR#26958 as merged with a link to bundle.html#subscription-notification. The Bundle subscription-notification section itself had been added immediately before the Jira was filed.

Additional context:
Bundle, Subscription, SubscriptionStatus, and SubscriptionTopic are active resources in source/fhir.ini. In current source, Subscription, SubscriptionStatus, and SubscriptionTopic are normative after the R6 normative-status change, and the old Bundle subsection trial-use macro was explicitly removed by the later "sort out trial-use notes" commit. Current Bundle source still defines the subscription-notification section, the bdl-13 first-entry constraint, and the subscription-notification bundle profile, but no longer carries the R5 trial-use note.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Re-adding a non-normative/trial-use note to the current Bundle page would now conflict with the later R6 normativity changes and the explicit removal of the old trial-use macro. The historical R5 request is therefore already represented in the R5 source/history, and the current build is correct without the note.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce a Bundle non-normative/trial-use note for subscription notifications unless FHIR-I makes a new current-version decision that the subscription-notification Bundle type, constraint, or profile should remain trial-use despite the later normative Subscription artifacts.
