FHIR-7870: Audit Goal author usage note

Issue request:
FHIR-7870 was a Published Patient Care change request from the DSTU1 ballot asking to add Goal.Informant because the person responsible for the goal can differ from the person who entered it. The final Not Persuasive with Modification resolution directed adding a usage note on Goal.author that author is who is responsible for creating the goal, not who typed it into the system, with data entry handled through Provenance.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the old SVN-era Goal tracker commit 8bd3bd917d at svn revision 6806, matching the Jira comment. That commit added a Goal.author comment saying the element is the individual responsible for establishing the goal and not necessarily who recorded it, using Provenance for recording.

Additional context:
The current build uses source/goal/structuredefinition-Goal.xml for the active Goal resource. Current migration metadata maps DSTU1 Goal.author through Goal.expressedBy to current Goal.source, and the current Goal.source comment preserves the responsibility-vs-recording distinction. Later R6 issue FHIR-48366 deliberately kept Goal.source, removed the old Provenance-only aside after adding Goal.recorder, and added recorder prose explaining that the recorder might or might not be the source who established the goal. FHIR-41409 only broadened Goal.source to Group/family use and does not alter the FHIR-7870 distinction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current active StructureDefinition already conveys the requested distinction through Goal.source and the newer Goal.recorder, so adding Goal.Informant or duplicating a Provenance-only note would be stale relative to later applied decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-7870 unless Patient Care reopens the broader Goal.source/Goal.recorder modeling question; any such work should be handled under a newer tracker such as FHIR-48366 rather than the 2015 DSTU1 author note.
