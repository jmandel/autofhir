FHIR-11061: Audit Consent party terminology

Issue request:
FHIR-11061 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification DSTU2 FHIR-core Consent change request. It asked to replace Consent.consentor with Consent.consentingParty and define that party as either the grantor responsible for granting Consent Directive rights or the grantee responsible for complying with the directive, because a Consent Directive needs both sides of the agreement to be named or implied.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git commit 687d10304f, whose message explicitly lists GF#11061. That commit changed the active Consent spreadsheet source from Consent.consentor to Consent.consentingParty, kept consentor as an alias, and replaced the patient-only definition with the approved grantor-or-grantee definition.

Additional context:
Later Consent modeling moved from Consent.consentingParty to Consent.performer and then, through the Published FHIR-31906 decision, split performer into the current Consent.grantor and Consent.grantee elements. Current source/fhir.ini keeps Consent in the active build, maps old Consent.performer to Consent.grantee, and source/consent/structuredefinition-Consent.xml now defines grantor and grantee separately. The FHIR-27779 clarification, already reconciled on the combined branch, adds that a Consent without listed grantor or grantee may be a subject preference statement and is not in force, preserving the seed issue's distinction without requiring obsolete element names or new cardinality constraints.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current build-scope Consent source already represents the approved FHIR-11061 semantics through explicit grantor and grantee elements, narrative describing healthcare consumer grantors and authorized entity grantees, and a grantee comment that distinguishes fully computable consent from non-force preference statements. Reintroducing Consent.consentingParty or adding a redundant constraint would conflict with later approved modeling.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-11061 unless the work group wants a new policy decision about cardinalities or enforceable invariants for current Consent.grantor and Consent.grantee.
