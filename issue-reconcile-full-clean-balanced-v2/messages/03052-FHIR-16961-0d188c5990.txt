FHIR-16961: Audit Identifier.use official guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-16961 was Published with resolution Not Persuasive with Modification. The request challenged the IdentifierUse code "official" because "official" can imply formal or government standing, and asked to rename it to "primary" or "main". The accepted modification kept the code name, added that "primary" and "main" are aliases, and clarified that deciding what is "official" is subjective and that implementation guides may provide use guidelines.
