Issue request:
FHIR-23710 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to update the Timing.repeat.timeOfDay comment because FHIR-12460 made timeOfDay mutually exclusive only with when, not with frequency or period.

Initial application:
The R5 MnM source commit 5cbc14c7d0f361941099bf72066b75c3f74f6d4f explicitly listed FHIR-23710 and changed source/datatypes/timing.xml from the old frequency/period prohibition to the approved "If there's a timeOfDay, there cannot be a when, or vice versa" wording. A later combined-branch FHIR-25289 reconciliation commit further clarified the first sentence of the same comment without changing the FHIR-23710 exclusivity rule.

Additional context:

The current timeOfDay comment and tim-10 human/expression are consistent with FHIR-23710. Zulip discussion confirmed the FHIR-23710/FHIR-25289 correction path, and Confluence searches found no separate or conflicting FHIR-23710 record.
