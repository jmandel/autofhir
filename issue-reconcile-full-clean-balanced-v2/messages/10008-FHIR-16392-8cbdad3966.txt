FHIR-16392: Audit Bundle.signature definition

Issue request:
FHIR-16392 was a Published FHIR-core STU3 change request, resolved Not Persuasive with Modification. It asked to remove Bundle.signature or move it to an extension because the purpose was unclear, Provenance could satisfy the use case more completely, and the Bundle.signature wording was narrower than the Signature datatype. The approved resolution kept Bundle.signature trial-use, corrected the definition to align with Signature, and encouraged further digital-signature connectathon work.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied in svn r14403, now git commit 39a558ee42ccaa31f374cad2491b0a717e544253, which changed the spreadsheet-era Bundle.signature text from a Bundle-specific signing explanation to the broader Signature wording covering electronic representations, cryptographic signatures, graphical signatures, documents, messages, transactions, and search responses. The old source/bundle/bundle-spreadsheet.xml source was later replaced by source/bundle/structuredefinition-Bundle.xml during the current build-source refactor.

Additional context:
Later applied signature work intentionally changed the right current-state interpretation. Commit 9fe4e8ba2ec241ab35dc18046c102c38872e1d50 deprecated Bundle.signature, removed the old element-local rationale, and moved Bundle signing guidance to the digital signatures page. FHIR-51099, already represented on the combined branch, confirms the later FHIR-I decision not to relax Bundle.signature cardinality and instead use Provenance resources in Bundle entries for Bundle signatures. FHIR-18440 and FHIR-38939 are also already represented on the combined branch and corrected the current Bundle signature note/link and deprecation metadata. FHIR-53914 remains Triaged and is broader unresolved follow-up, not a final decision requiring source changes for this audit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. Current active Bundle source keeps Bundle.signature as a Signature element but marks it deprecated, points to Provenance based Signatures, and the active Bundle notes and signatures page direct implementers to Provenance resources carrying Signature elements. Re-adding the old broad Bundle.signature rationale to the deprecated element would promote stale guidance that later applied decisions intentionally superseded.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Future wording work should happen under later final signature issues, not by reviving FHIR-16392-era Bundle.signature prose unless the work group reverses the current Provenance/deprecation direction.
