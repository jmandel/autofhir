FHIR-39614 later changed sopClass back from Coding to oid because DICOM did not supply a usable CodeSystem/ValueSet. The remaining AppointmentResponse mention found in source/imagingstudy/imagingstudy.svg is a generated-looking diagram artifact and is not the authoritative source for the current target profiles.
