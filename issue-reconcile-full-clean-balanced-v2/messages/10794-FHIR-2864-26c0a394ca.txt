FHIR-2864: Audit narrative grouping guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-2864 is Published/Persuasive with Modification. It asked for clearer guidance that the Narrative for grouping constructs such as documents, standalone Compositions, Lists, and DiagnosticReports should not simply include the narrative for the resources being grouped. The accepted disposition was Lloyd McKenzie's narrative.html wording: resource narratives should include only summary information about referenced resources as needed for safe understanding, with resource-specific exceptions and limits because referenced resources can change independently.

Initial application:
The Jira record says the decision was applied in SVN revision 5034 for DSTU1. In the current Git source, commit 52b5232e9d ("2025Sep: FHIR-2864: explain narrative guidance for contained resources and Bundle entries") added the current narrative.xml guidance that matches the accepted wording and adds explicit contained-resource/Bundle-entry handling. That commit is already reachable from the combined branch, and no prior Issue-Reconcile-Key commit exists for FHIR-2864.

Additional context:
source/page-table.xml lists narrative.html as an active Types and References page, and source/fhir.ini actively maps the documents page plus Composition, List, and DiagnosticReport resources. Current documents, Composition, and List source text does not contradict the general rule: document presentation assembles narratives from referenced resources, Composition.section.text is the attested human-readable section content tied to section entries, and List text describes the list rather than requiring pasted item narratives. Neighboring published narrative issues FHIR-7370, FHIR-17463, and FHIR-9826 were checked as later/background context and do not require a FHIR-2864 follow-up source edit.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active narrative source already states that containing-resource and Bundle narratives are not expected to include each contained resource or Bundle entry narrative, that referenced-resource information should be summarized only as needed for safe understanding, that List and Composition may impose resource-specific rules, and that copied referenced-resource content should be limited.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source change is needed for FHIR-2864 unless the work group wants a broader future rewrite of narrative guidance, which would be a new editorial/specification decision rather than reconciliation of this published issue.
