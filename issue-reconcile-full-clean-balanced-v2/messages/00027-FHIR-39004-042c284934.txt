The THO 7.1.0 Study Design ValueSet includes SEVCO codes from https://fevir.net/sevco.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Follow-up content changes for the Study Design ValueSet should occur in THO/UTG; FHIR core should continue to reference the THO canonical ValueSet.
