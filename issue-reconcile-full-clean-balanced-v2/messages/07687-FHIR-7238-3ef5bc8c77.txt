FHIR-7238: Audit diagnostic and questionnaire boundaries

Issue request:
FHIR-7238 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification DSTU1 clarification asking for the difference between DiagnosticReport, Observation, and QuestionnaireAnswers. The accepted resolution said the DiagnosticReport boundaries section had already been updated as the duplicate/same-change-family work of FHIR-6199, and that the Questionnaire section had been updated to be more explicit that information may exist in multiple forms depending on purpose.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-7238 was found. The DiagnosticReport portion appears to have been applied through commit 1a1a9868d5186613a0e3aa02a2676638d36968f8, whose subject was "6199, preapply 6200" and whose patch added the Boundaries and Relationships section with the proposed DiagnosticReport-versus-Observation text. The Questionnaire portion appears to have been applied through commit cacb4626a33e26cbc231a2b66d1e53eb0ce31c4a, which added the paragraph saying data may exist in both QuestionnaireAnswers and other resources, followed by commit c51b8682420300c0f51dd0ad260d0be7f5ae20ab renaming QuestionnaireAnswers to QuestionnaireResponse and commit 52dbc1c47868e26651369ea4109008738f17ce5e preserving and refining the traceability wording.

Additional context:
DiagnosticReport, Observation, Questionnaire, and QuestionnaireResponse are active resources in source/fhir.ini. Current source still has DiagnosticReport Boundaries and Relationships text contrasting DiagnosticReport with Observation, Observation boundaries text contrasting Observation with DiagnosticReport and QuestionnaireResponse, Questionnaire boundaries and notes explaining the role of Questionnaire/QuestionnaireResponse and extraction into other resources, and QuestionnaireResponse boundaries text contrasting answers with Observation. Recent AutoFHIR no-change audits for FHIR-6313 and FHIR-7193 independently confirmed the same QuestionnaireResponse and diagnostic Procedure/Observation boundary families. Bounded Zulip and Confluence searches for FHIR-7238, FHIR-6199, and the relevant boundary terms found no directly relevant community record or later decision that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The active current source already conveys the FHIR-7238 resolution: DiagnosticReport and Observation boundaries are explained in the DiagnosticReport and Observation pages, and Questionnaire/QuestionnaireResponse guidance explicitly permits the same information to exist in questionnaire responses and more specific resources depending on purpose, traceability, query, and analysis needs.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No FHIR core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-7238 unless a future OO or FHIR-I decision intentionally revises the broader DiagnosticReport/Observation/QuestionnaireResponse boundary guidance.
