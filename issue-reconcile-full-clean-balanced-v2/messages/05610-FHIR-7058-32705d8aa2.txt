FHIR-7058: Audit AllergyIntolerance physical-stimuli guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7058 was a Published FHIR-core AllergyIntolerance change request, resolved Not Persuasive with Modification, asking why sunlight and similar physical-agent reactions were excluded from AllergyIntolerance instead of following the C-CDA design. The approved resolution replaced the old misuse sentence with guidance that the allergy/intolerance list is a patient-safety decision-support tool and that reactions triggered by physical stimuli such as light, heat, cold, pressure, and vibration should be recorded as Conditions on the problem list, not as AllergyIntolerance.

Initial application:
The change appears to have been applied in git commit 3ea42ecce8fc3a87379eb817328e7bdabebf8a5a on 2015-08-21, which replaced the older physical-agent/sunlight misuse sentence in source/allergyintolerance/allergyintolerance-introduction.xml with the approved decision-support/problem-list wording. A later 2015 cleanup commit only adjusted capitalization and nearby wording without changing the substance.

Additional context:
AllergyIntolerance is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and the current AllergyIntolerance introduction still carries the same guidance in the Misuse section with a Condition link. Related DAF/US Core issue FHIR-8140 challenged the value-set alignment but was resolved as no-change and later comments recognized that the AllergyIntolerance text had removed the explicit sunlight example in favor of the broader physical-stimuli language. Zulip and Confluence searches found no directly relevant community record that conflicts with the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build source already conveys the final FHIR-7058 rule in the authoritative AllergyIntolerance narrative, and rewording or duplicating it elsewhere would not change implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core follow-up is needed for FHIR-7058 unless Patient Care later reopens the broader clinical-modeling question or a separate IG such as US Core requests value-set alignment work in its own repository.
