FHIR-15615 was Published/Persuasive for STU3. It asked FHIR-core Resource Identity guidance to improve the scattered descriptions of logical, literal, business, and canonical identifiers by giving canonical URLs more prominence, mentioning the canonical data type and use, and pointing readers to the Canonical URLs section of references.html.

Initial application:
