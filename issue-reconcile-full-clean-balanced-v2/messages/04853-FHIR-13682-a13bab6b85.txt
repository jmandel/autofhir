FHIR-13682: Audit PractitionerRole reference reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-13682 was a Published Patient Administration change request for STU3. It asked either to add organization information back to Practitioner or to make sure work groups updated resources that only referenced Practitioner after role/organization details moved into PractitionerRole. The final resolution was Not Persuasive with Modification: during R4 closeout, work groups and resources addressed the issue by ensuring PractitionerRole references were present in all relevant resources.

Initial application:
No source commit was found that names FHIR-13682 directly. The R4-era application appears in the coordinated PractitionerRole reference sweep: PA minutes approved changing relevant Reference(Practitioner) targets to include PractitionerRole, and git history includes commits such as 710e005513, 3e01cea0, eff65f2a33, 2d9d71ed42, and 7475edc72c/cf7c8a6188 adding PractitionerRole to patient-care, pharmacy, messaging/research, and OO resources.

Additional context:
Current fhir.ini actively includes both Practitioner and PractitionerRole. The current PractitionerRole narrative explains that many resources intentionally allow either Practitioner or PractitionerRole, that PractitionerRole supplies organization context when needed, and that PractitionerRole may represent an organizational role even when a specific practitioner is not preallocated. Later FHIR-23935 made this guidance explicit and is already represented on the combined branch; FHIR-21166 was retracted with PA comments noting that the STU3 referencing-resource issues had been resolved in R4/R5. FHIR-19688 remains unresolved and concerns search/compartment behavior for indirect PractitionerRole references, not whether the reference targets should be changed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. A current active StructureDefinition audit found the expected broad use of PractitionerRole alongside Practitioner in role/actor/performer/author/requester/participant references. The remaining Practitioner-only targets are subject/identity/workflow/authorization-style elements or PractitionerRole.practitioner itself, so they do not show a missed implementation of FHIR-13682.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not add organization back to Practitioner and do not broaden Practitioner-only targets without a new work-group decision for a specific element; use FHIR-19688 or a future targeted issue if the remaining problem is search behavior across indirect PractitionerRole references.
