FHIR-11503: Audit definitional resource template wording

Issue request:
FHIR-11503 was a Published CDS change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking the Clinical Reasoning scope text not to imply that definitional resources must be profiled or templated resources. The approved resolution was to clarify that the resources allow, but do not require, the use of profiles.

Initial application:
The Jira comments identify SVN 10927 as the application. The corresponding git-svn commit c03a8aca84 changed source/clinicalreasoning-module.html from "template resources" to wording that says definitional resources are resources not defined on a specific patient and "can be used directly, or with profiles to provide intended structure for specific types of resources."

Additional context:
The current Clinical Reasoning module remains an active source page linked from the module list and module fragment, and it links to the definitional resources topic. That topic still uses "templates" as a general metaphor for creating patient- or context-specific resources, but it does not restore the rejected implication that definitional resources require profiles. Corrected key and phrase searches found no directly relevant Zulip discussion, Jira neighbor, or Confluence minutes that conflict with the Jira/source-history conclusion; one broad CDS minutes hit only used general ClinicalReasoning/profiles terminology.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current scope sentence already implements the approved clarification, and further edits to remove every metaphorical use of "templates" would be broader than FHIR-11503 and could remove useful explanatory text without changing implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. No FHIR core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-11503 unless the CDS/CQI work groups separately decide to revisit the explanatory template metaphor in the detailed definitional resources topic.
