The Jira resolution text contains a copied "$expand" typo, but the issue summary, description, linked discussion, and target artifact are consistently CodeSystem $lookup.

Initial application:

The terminology service narrative also includes an instance-level example, GET [base]/CodeSystem/loinc/$lookup?code=1963-8. The original Zulip thread confirms the requested change was considered an oversight and compatible; Vocabulary minutes on 2022-04-04 record FHIR-20205 as resolved change required.
