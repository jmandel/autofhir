Issue request:
FHIR-12179 was Published/Persuasive for DSTU2 and asked that the official build snapshot inherit all applicable constraints from applied base and type profiles, with ele-1 applied to all elements as the example. The Jira comments also clarified that mappings, aliases, and other aggregable ElementDefinition content should be inherited, with full snapshot guidance to follow.

Initial application:
Jira records svn revision 10025 as the application. In this Git history that is commit 82e1ca4af69d33f527a180c7bfa003e811feb4c0, whose subject names Tasks 12180, 12182, and 12179. The visible source change added ElementDefinition.constraint.source so generated snapshots can retain inherited constraints while preserving their originating StructureDefinition for rendering and traceability.

Additional context:
Current build scope still includes ElementDefinition as an active datatype in source/fhir.ini and StructureDefinition as an active resource. Current StructureDefinition source says specialization snapshots include inherited elements and rules, profiling guidance says generated snapshots are complete and retain inherited constraints, and the StructureDefinition notes explain that inherited interface constraints are propagated into snapshots with ElementDefinition.constraint.source. A current CI-build check of Patient.name in patient.profile.json shows ele-1 inherited from http://hl7.org/fhir/StructureDefinition/Element, and inherited mappings are also present.

This commit intentionally makes no source change.
