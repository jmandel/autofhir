FHIR-11298 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU2 correction asking that the 2016-09 vital signs profile table show the LOINC short name somewhere, because otherwise the table's LOINC codes were hard to verify and LOINC terms of use were a concern.

Initial application:

The same vital signs table was changed shortly after the recorded vote by fad3d2f2fa366aa12069eef046c3c52aeb09226a, labelled for FHIR-11301 and partial FHIR-11300, which added the "LOINC Name and Comments" column and LOINC names next to the listed codes. Jira comments on FHIR-11298 then recorded "fixed" on 2017-02-26.

Additional context:
FHIR-11300 and FHIR-11301 are the same vital signs table change family and their resolutions also directed adding LOINC names to the table. The current source/fhir.ini keeps Observation active, the Observation vital signs ImplementationGuide includes observation-vitalsigns-profile-introduction.xml, and that current introduction still has the LOINC-name column with names/comments for the vital-sign codes. Quoted Zulip and Confluence searches found no contrary community record for this issue.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current authoritative build source already satisfies the Jira request by showing LOINC names alongside the vital signs LOINC codes; changing the heading to assert "short name" now would be a low-value editorial change and could be inaccurate for entries that use current LOINC display/common-name wording rather than a guaranteed ShortName field.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Revisit only if the Orders and Observations work group wants the table to distinguish a specific LOINC naming field such as ShortName versus display/common name; that is broader terminology presentation policy, not a missing application of FHIR-11298.
