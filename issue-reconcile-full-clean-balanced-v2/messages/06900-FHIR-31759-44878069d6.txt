FHIR-31759: Audit CodeableReference constraint placement

Issue request:
FHIR-31759 was a Published FHIR-core R4B change request resolved Persuasive with Modification. It asked whether profiles could bind CodeableReference.concept or constrain CodeableReference.reference directly when CodeableReference itself also carries binding or targetProfile constraints. The approved disposition was to declare that profiles SHALL NOT constrain targetProfile at CodeableReference.reference or binding at CodeableReference.concept, and to enforce the rule in validation if possible.

Initial application:
The Jira application comment points to master commit bb07b227109ce945918994d616a37171b3ba46f2 and R4B commit e54acba3221b3015925ae860c64bc41966700740. The master commit added StructureDefinition constraints sdf-24 and sdf-25, which prohibit targetProfile on CodeableReference.reference and binding on CodeableReference.concept. Current source still contains those constraints, with later expression cleanup, and applies them to StructureDefinition.snapshot.element.

Additional context:

The current CodeableReference narrative says that targetProfile and binding constraints declared on CodeableReference apply to CodeableReference.reference and CodeableReference.concept respectively, and SHALL NOT be specified directly on the child elements.
