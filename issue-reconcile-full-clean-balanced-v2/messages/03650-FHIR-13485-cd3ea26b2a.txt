FHIR-13485: Audit current addItem tiering

Issue request:
FHIR-13485 was Published/Persuasive for STU3. It asked ClaimResponse and ExplanationOfBenefit to reduce payer addItems from a two-tier structure to a single tier, add detail and subDetail sequence links so added items can identify the replaced claim detail/sub-detail, and remove addItem revenue/category because those are specified on the replaced item.

Initial application:
The request was applied in the historical spreadsheet era by 0f5969cd4d for ClaimResponse and 1666da603a for ExplanationOfBenefit. Those commits added top-level addItem detail/subdetail sequence link elements and disabled the then-existing addItem.detail subtree and addItem revenue/category rows. The current XML source was later regenerated during the 2021 source rework, and current naming maps the old sequenceLinkId/detailSequenceLinkId/subdetailSequenceLinkId family to itemSequence/detailSequence/subDetailSequence.

Additional context:
ClaimResponse and ExplanationOfBenefit are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini. Current StructureDefinitions and the FHIR ModelInfo content already expose addItem.itemSequence, addItem.detailSequence, and addItem.subDetailSequence for both resources, and the ClaimResponse addItem example uses the current itemSequence/detailSequence elements. Current source also contains addItem.detail and addItem.detail.subDetail. That nesting should not be removed during this reconciliation because later Published FM decisions for R5, including FHIR-29689 and FHIR-37588, explicitly discuss the addItem.detail and addItem.detail.subDetail surfaces. FHIR-29689 is already represented on the combined branch, and FHIR-17929 has already corrected the ClaimResponse subDetailSequence casing on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The sequence-link portion of FHIR-13485 is present in the active current source, while the original STU3 request to remove nested addItem details has been overtaken by later current-build decisions that preserve and use those nested structures.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not remove addItem.detail or addItem.detail.subDetail from current ClaimResponse/EOB without a new FM work-group decision that explicitly reverses the later R5 addItem.detail-related changes.
