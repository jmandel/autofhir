FHIR-2964: Audit Observation and Condition boundary text

Issue request:
FHIR-2964 was a Published FHIR-core Patient Care change request for the Initial Draft/DSTU1 Observation artifact. It asked that the introductions for Observation and Condition clearly differentiate when each resource should be used. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution directed Eric Haas to enhance the Observation introduction and Stephen Chu to enhance the Condition introduction, including the November 2014 Patient Care motion to enhance both introductions.

Initial application:
The historical application is present in source history rather than a commit explicitly tagged FHIR-2964. Commit a84a0c5884fdbcb5dcb003820bb86641e52d448e updated the Condition introduction on 2014-12-04 with problem/diagnosis/concern scope text and initial Observation boundary guidance. Commit 3ecf22e65d42355b0c19a59ecb149bfa6bc5ea5a updated the Observation introduction on 2014-12-07, removed the diagnosis example from Observation use cases, changed APGAR to a clinical assessment tool example, and added the Observation Boundaries and Relationships section differentiating Observation from Condition and other specialized resources. Commit 07aa93633f66b8d2d0b94692528feae41659d399 later separated and normalized the Patient Care introduction material.

Additional context:
Observation and Condition are active resources in source/fhir.ini, so their introduction XHTML files are current build inputs. Later final Jira decisions in the same boundary family refined rather than reversed the FHIR-2964 direction: FHIR-8872 clarified symptom/complaint guidance in Condition, FHIR-26904 added further Condition-vs-Observation clarification, and FHIR-39190 aligned the Observation boundary text with Condition language. Patient Care minutes and a later implementer Zulip thread confirm the boundary remains nuanced and sometimes overlapping, not a simple one-resource-only rule.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current Observation source already explains that Observation is for measurements and subjective point-in-time assessments, includes clinical assessment tools rather than diagnoses in the use-case list, and says clinical diagnoses typically belong in Condition while Observations may support Condition assertions. Current Condition source already describes conditions, problems, diagnoses, concerns, and tracked health issues, and its Boundaries and Relationships section explains when subjective/objective findings and symptoms are typically Observations versus Conditions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current source satisfies FHIR-2964 after considering the original application and later boundary clarifications; additional wording would duplicate actively maintained boundary guidance and risks drifting from later Patient Care and OO decisions.
