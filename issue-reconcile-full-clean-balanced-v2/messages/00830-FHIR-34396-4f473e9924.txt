FHIR-34396: Audit AllergyIntolerance recordedDate alignment

Issue request:
FHIR-34396 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to align AllergyIntolerance.recordedDate and AllergyIntolerance.recorder with recent Condition recordedDate/recorder definition, comments, and mappings work. The approved text changed the recordedDate short and comment and added a recorder comment clarifying that the recorder is the most recent author while recordedDate is when the allergy or intolerance was first recorded.

Initial application:
The request was applied in source history by e729df8464 (J#34396 AllergyIntolerance recorder and recordedDate clarifications), which updated AllergyIntolerance.recordedDate short text, added the recordedDate preservation/onset comment with the recorder contrast sentence, and added the recorder comment. The Jira comment cites the GitHub PR/merge commit path for that application.

Additional context:
FHIR-34395 was applied shortly afterward to both Condition and AllergyIntolerance, replacing recorder/asserter with participant and explicitly striking the final recordedDate sentence, "By contrast, the recorder is the most recent author." Later R6 decisions FHIR-39513, FHIR-44517, and FHIR-39602 reverted the participant model back to recorder/asserter, and FHIR-48804 clarified the recorder definition in both Condition and AllergyIntolerance to say the recorder takes responsibility for accurately recording the content. The current build includes AllergyIntolerance as an active resource, and the current Condition and AllergyIntolerance sources are aligned: recordedDate keeps the onset/preservation guidance without the struck contrast sentence, while recorder carries the most-recent-author/first-recorded contrast.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. Re-adding the recordedDate contrast sentence only to AllergyIntolerance would break alignment with Condition, and re-adding it to both resources would reverse the later FHIR-34395 decision without a new work-group decision. The current source already preserves the meaningful FHIR-34396 clarification through the recordedDate short/comment and recorder definition/comment/mappings.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. If Patient Care wants the recordedDate comment itself to restate the recorder contrast again, handle that as a new paired Condition and AllergyIntolerance decision because the sentence was deliberately removed by FHIR-34395 and the current recorder comments already express the distinction.
