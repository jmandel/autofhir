FHIR-46763: Audit _maxresults match-only clarification

FHIR-46763 was an Applied/Persuasive FHIR-I change request for R6 asking the search page to clarify that _maxresults limits returned match resources, not resources returned through _include or _revinclude. The approved resolution said to update the documentation so this extension refers only to matches and does not cover included resources or operation outcomes.
