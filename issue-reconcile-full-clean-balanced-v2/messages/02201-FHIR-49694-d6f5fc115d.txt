FHIR-49694: Audit ServiceRequest additionalRecipient removal

Issue request:
FHIR-49694 was an Applied/Persuasive with Modification R6 OO change request stating that the FHIR-46425 addition of ServiceRequest.additionalRecipient was inappropriate. The final decision was to revert FHIR-46425 while preserving the intent by using CommunicationRequest for copy-results workflows.

Initial application:
FHIR-46425 had added ServiceRequest.additionalRecipient in 4a989ec22d019ba21c4b07cffa34088127162f46. FHIR-49694 was then applied in 204fbe04fc47f3358206780add3cca78e00f061f and merged via PR #3288, removing the element and replacing requester-comment guidance so it no longer points to additionalRecipient.

Additional context:
ServiceRequest and CommunicationRequest are active build resources in source/fhir.ini. The current ServiceRequest source has no ServiceRequest.additionalRecipient element and its requester comment says to use a CommunicationRequest based on the ServiceRequest for additional result recipients. FHIR-51696 later applied the Patient Care scope example for CommunicationRequest, and the OO Jira comments state that no further edits are required on FHIR-49694 after the ServiceRequest revert and separate CommunicationRequest tracking.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current source already reflects the approved reversal, and adding more ServiceRequest prose would duplicate or reopen guidance that has been split to CommunicationRequest and implementation-guide work.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If the work group later wants to refine whether the ServiceRequest requester comment should mention CommunicationRequest.category or CommunicationRequest.reason, that should be handled as a new targeted guidance/terminology decision rather than as a missing FHIR-49694 application.
