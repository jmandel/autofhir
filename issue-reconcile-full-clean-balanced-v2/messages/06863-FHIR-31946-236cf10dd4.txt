Issue request:
FHIR-31946 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for FHIR-core Subscription. It asked how a server declares Subscription support and resolved that R5 should match the R4 Backport conformance requirements: support Subscription read/write, Subscription $status, SubscriptionTopic read/search, at least one channel type, at least one payload type, and document non-CapabilityStatement discovery out of band.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied by git commit d35d9e104bc474133430758027a6da4ad7b1b667, which added a Conformance section and Discovery text to source/subscriptions.html. That source page is in the current build through source/hierarchy.xml and current navigation, and Subscription, SubscriptionStatus, and SubscriptionTopic are active resources in source/fhir.ini.

Additional context:
The referenced backport issue FHIR-31459 contains the same conformance requirements. Later applied FHIR-46495 intentionally split Subscription Framework conformance into in-band and out-of-band management styles, relaxing some resource/operation requirements for out-of-band management while preserving the in-band requirements and preserving the channel, payload, and discovery guidance. FHIR-47288 later declined to force out-of-band subscriptions to follow the in-band rules, confirming that the current split should not be collapsed back into the original blanket wording.
