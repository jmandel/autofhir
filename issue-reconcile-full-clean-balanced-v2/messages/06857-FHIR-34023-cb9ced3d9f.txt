FHIR-34023 was a Published FHIR-core-search issue with resolution Not Persuasive with Modification. It asked to clarify the chained-search example for Patient?general-practitioner.name=Joe&general-practitioner.address-state=MN so readers would understand that the two chained parameters need not match the same Practitioner; the approved change was a rewording, not a change to search semantics.
