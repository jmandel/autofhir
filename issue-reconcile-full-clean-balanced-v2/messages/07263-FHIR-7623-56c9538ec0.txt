FHIR-7623: Audit Supply resource relocation

Issue request:
FHIR-7623 was Published/Not Persuasive with Modification for DSTU1. It asked for distinct patient-specific supply request/statement resources versus stock-supply resources, using examples such as requesting or supplying a wheelchair for a patient; the approved response said that the patient-specific use case was covered by DeviceUseRequest and DeviceUseStatement, with a required clarification to SupplyRequest and SupplyDelivery scope and boundaries.

Initial application:
No Jira-tagged FHIR-7623 source commit was found. Historical source changes split the old Supply resource into SupplyRequest and SupplyDelivery under the GForge #6067 family, and the 2016 pre-ballot SupplyRequest boundary update removed the unresolved TODO and directed patient-focused medication/device requests to DeviceUseRequest or MedicationOrder instead of SupplyRequest. Later FHIR-24936 and FHIR-27941 source/audit history refined that same boundary for DeviceRequest versus SupplyRequest.

Additional context:
The current core build no longer owns SupplyRequest, SupplyDelivery, DeviceUsage, or DeviceDispense as active resources: source/fhir.ini has DeviceRequest and DeviceAssociation entries but no active supplyrequest, supplydelivery, deviceusage, or devicedispense entries after the 2025 commit that removed OO resources and pointed references to the OO Incubator. The active core DeviceRequest and DeviceAssociation pages cover the patient-focused device request/association side, while the relocated OO Incubator continuous build serves SupplyRequest, SupplyDelivery, DeviceUsage, and DeviceDispense StructureDefinitions. Later related issues FHIR-24936, FHIR-27941, FHIR-37980, FHIR-46342, and FHIR-51689 are already represented on the combined branch and do not need duplicate commits.
