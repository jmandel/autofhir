Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-16246-specific conflict; the only relevant Zulip trail was later MolecularSequence referenceSeq redesign discussion, which supports treating current MolecularDefinition/MolecularSequence history as a later redesign rather than a missed application of the old Sequence note.
