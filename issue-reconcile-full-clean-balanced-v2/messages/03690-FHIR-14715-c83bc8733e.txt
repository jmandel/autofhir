FHIR-14715: Audit ClinicalUseDefinition improvement

Issue request:
FHIR-14715 is a Published/Persuasive BR&R FHIR-core change request raised against the old MedicinalProductClinicals resource, with MedicinalProductAuthorization also listed as a related artifact. The ballot comment said the original MedicinalProductClinicals Description of Resource, Scope and Usage, and Boundaries and Relationships were insufficient, questioned why AdverseEvent text appeared there, and recommended removing the resource from ballot until a complete proposal and relationships to medication resources existed. The later Jira comments record BR&R agreement that substantial R5 improvements were planned and that so many changes had been applied that the issue could be regarded as change applied.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly mentions FHIR-14715. Source history shows the problematic starting point and indirect application path: 8d6d0fa613 added MedicinalProductClinicals with AdverseEvent-focused narrative; baa0da382d removed that incorrect AdverseEvent text; 85df8c065b added settings for separate resources broken out of MedicinalProductClinicals; 115735b9c4 then removed the old MedicinalProductClinicals source folder. Subsequent R5/R4B medication-definition work introduced ClinicalUseIssue, renamed it to ClinicalUseDefinition, and replaced MedicinalProductAuthorization with RegulatedAuthorization.

Additional context:
The current build actively includes ClinicalUseDefinition and RegulatedAuthorization. MedicinalProductClinicals appears only as a removed resource, and legacy MedicinalProductContraindication, MedicinalProductIndication, MedicinalProductInteraction, and MedicinalProductUndesirableEffect map to ClinicalUseDefinition; MedicinalProductAuthorization maps to RegulatedAuthorization. Current ClinicalUseDefinition source describes one typed issue per instance for indications, contraindications, interactions, undesirable effects, and warnings; distinguishes potential undesirable effects from actual AdverseEvent records; links to the Medication Definition module; and includes the restored rationale that cross-workgroup consultation combined originally separate resources into one typed definition. FMG Confluence records approved resource proposals for ClinicalUseIssue and RegulatedAuthorization, and later Jira decisions FHIR-31847, FHIR-31852, and FHIR-26540 refined the same active successor resource family.
