FHIR-6304: Audit W5 and FiveWs alignment machinery

Issue request:
FHIR-6304 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It asked for an abstract way to extract action who, what, when, where, and why metadata attributes across all FHIR resources for Record Lifecycle Event use. The final resolution closed the issue because W5 classifications, build tools, and QA processes had been introduced to drive ongoing alignment, with future discrepancies to be tracked as new tasks.

Initial application:
No Jira-tagged FHIR-6304 implementation commit was found in the source history or on the combined branch. The relevant application appears to be the broader W5/FiveWs machinery: the W5 subsystem was introduced before the issue in January 2015, a SPARQL QA check for missing W5 classifications was present by June 2015, and the FiveWs logical model was added to the active logical-model list in November 2017.

Additional context:
Current source keeps the legacy W5 map page in the build hierarchy but marks it deprecated in favor of the FiveWs pattern. The active FiveWs logical model defines a pattern, not an instance resource, and maps identifier, status, what, subject/who, timing, where, and why concepts. FHIR Workflow minutes from 2017 record the plan to turn the W5 framework into a pattern/logical model, tie patterns to ontology, and use pattern-alignment reports and QA for consistency. Later Jira and community search results show separate W5/FiveWs mapping issues, including applied and unresolved resource-specific fixes, but no later decision reversing the broad FHIR-6304 disposition.
