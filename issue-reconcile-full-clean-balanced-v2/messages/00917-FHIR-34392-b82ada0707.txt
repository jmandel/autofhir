FHIR-34392 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification R5 Patient Care change request asking to clarify Condition.recordedDate. The approved resolution changed the short description to "Date condition was first recorded" and added comments explaining that when onset is unknown, recordedDate can establish that the condition was present on or before a given date, should be preserved from a sending system when known, and may use the receipt timestamp when not provided.
