FHIR-26879: Audit Binary patient compartment treatment

Issue request:
FHIR-26879 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked that Binary not be excluded from the Patient compartment, specifically noting Binary.securityContext references to a patient.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly tagged FHIR-26879 was found. The contemporaneous Zulip discussion identified the old exclusion as a tooling artifact, and the Jira issue was later annotated "see 22269".

Additional context:
FHIR-22269 later revisited the same Binary/compartment question and changed the application path: rather than making Binary formally computable as a Patient compartment member through Binary.securityContext, the work group removed the old "never in this compartment" style exclusion and clarified that resources outside formal compartments can still be relevant for access control. FHIR-51725 subsequently clarified that listed resources without .param elements are outside the formal compartment, while still permitting non-compartment resources to be returned by _include or local convention.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active CompartmentDefinition source and compartment page template no longer exclude Binary as "never" in the Patient compartment; they state that resources outside a compartment may still be subject to access restrictions and explicitly use Binary as the example of a non-listed resource that may still be relevant for access-control compartment uses.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not add Binary.securityContext as a Patient compartment search parameter without a new work-group decision, because the later FHIR-22269 and FHIR-51725 decisions intentionally distinguish formal computable compartment membership from access-control treatment of Binary.
