FHIR-26381: Audit Organization and Location scope clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-26381 was an Applied PA change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking the Organization resource to better distinguish Organizations from Locations. The final resolution moved Organization examples into Scope and Usage, kept the formal/informal grouping definition focused on collective action, and added service-provision guidance that the Organization provides services, HealthcareService describes them, and Location describes where they are offered.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git commit cbfbf7a063222a467608db5c97ac61143678be8b, which added the Organization examples to source/organization/organization-introduction.xml, added the requested service-provision paragraph to the Organization Boundaries and Relationships section, and removed the examples from the Organization StructureDefinition description/definition. Current source still contains those changes.

Additional context:
Organization and Location are active resources in source/fhir.ini, so the inspected resource source is in current build scope. The closely related FHIR-26027, already Published, added the Event-pattern clarification that Location is where a service is performed and Organization can represent who performed it; that related text remains in both Organization and Location boundaries. PA Confluence minutes record the January 2022 disposition and the May 2023 motion to apply this issue. A later Zulip Location Service thread shows continuing implementation ambiguity around facilities, but it reinforces rather than contradicts the current responsibility/location distinction and does not provide a formal reversal of FHIR-26381.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Organization source contains the requested definition shape, example placement, and service-provision boundary paragraph, and the parallel Location/Event guidance belongs to the already-published FHIR-26027 change rather than an unapplied part of FHIR-26381.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Further modeling work for facilities, healthcare services, or multi-hierarchy directories should be handled by separate PA decisions rather than by reopening this applied clarification.
