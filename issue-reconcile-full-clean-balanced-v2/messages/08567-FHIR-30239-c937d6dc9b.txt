FHIR-30239: Audit Address.country ISO 3166 wording

Issue request:
FHIR-30239 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked to align the Address.country short description and comment by changing the comment to allow ISO 3166 2- or 3-letter codes, using MAY language, and retaining the human readable country name wording.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in 5cbc14c7d0f361941099bf72066b75c3f74f6d4f, "First round of R5 MnM changes," which changed source/datatypes/address.xml to "Country (e.g. may be ISO 3166 2 or 3 letter code)" and "ISO 3166 2- or 3- letter codes MAY be used in place of a human readable country name."

Additional context:
FHIR-30239 refines the same Address.country row previously clarified by FHIR-16982. The linked Zulip discussion records implementer confusion about the old 3-letter-only comment and Grahame Grieve's statement that the comment did not mean to discourage 2-letter ISO codes. MnM minutes for 2021-02-03 record the Persuasive with Modification vote. Address is an active normative datatype in source/fhir.ini.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit makes no source change. The current source/datatypes/address.xml text exactly reflects the FHIR-30239 resolution, and the stale 3-letter-only comment is absent from source.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-30239 unless a future work group decision changes the broader policy for coding string-valued Address parts.
