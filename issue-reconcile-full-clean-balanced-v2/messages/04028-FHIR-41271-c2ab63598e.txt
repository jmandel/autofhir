FHIR-41271: Audit MessageHeader REST clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-41271 was an Applied/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core messaging issue raised for R6. It asked whether MessageHeader is transient in a messaging workflow and whether a receiver should discard it after processing; the final decision was to add a "MessageHeader and REST" subsection to the messaging page clarifying that MessageHeader is necessary to understand a message bundle, but once message resources are processed into another context, MessageHeader content SHALL NOT be required to interpret those resources.

Initial application:
Git commit 48603ec9f67f91177955f8470fed7caad80596e4 applied FHIR-41271 together with FHIR-40294, FHIR-42716, and FHIR-43942. That commit added the requested "MessageHeader and REST" subsection and implementation note to source/messaging.html, with minor punctuation/link/uppercase rendering differences from the Jira resolution text. The current source still contains that semantically matching text.

Additional context:
The linked FHIR-I Zulip thread shows Grahame's draft for FHIR-41271 and discussion that the point was not whether implementers may persist MessageHeader, but that systems SHALL NOT have to retain or consult MessageHeader after message processing to understand persisted resources. FHIR-I minutes on 2023-06-26 record the Persuasive with Modification vote and intent to take input from Zulip. InM minutes on 2023-06-27 record the downstream implication that information needing to persist should be represented in another resource such as Task or Provenance, not as required retained MessageHeader context. The top-level messaging page is in build scope through source/hierarchy.xml and source/navigation.xml.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This is an empty audit commit. No source change is needed because source/messaging.html already includes the approved MessageHeader/REST clarification and implementation note. Moving the subsection to exactly precede the asynchronous REST subsection, or changing capitalization/punctuation to match the Jira prose literally, would not improve implementer understanding and would be unnecessary churn.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future MessageHeader or messaging work should preserve the distinction captured here: MessageHeader may be persisted for audit or other local purposes, but post-processing resource interpretation must not depend on looking up a prior MessageHeader; persistent interpretive context belongs in the payload or another appropriate resource.
