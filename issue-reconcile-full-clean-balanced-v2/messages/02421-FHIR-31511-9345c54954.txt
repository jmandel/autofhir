Issue request:
FHIR-31511 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to rename ResearchSubject.individual because research subjects can be humans, specimens, substances, devices, and other entities. The accepted request was to rename the element to subject and update the short/definition from person/animal-only wording.

Initial application:

A same-day committers Zulip thread shows the editor asking how to update the patient compartment reference after the rename, and the current compartments source now uses subject.

Additional context:
ResearchSubject is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/researchsubject/structuredefinition-ResearchSubject.xml, source/researchsubject/bundle-ResearchSubject-search-params.xml, examples, and source/compartments.xml are in current build scope.
