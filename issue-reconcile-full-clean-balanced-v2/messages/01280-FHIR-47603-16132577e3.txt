FHIR-47603: Audit Group collective action definition

Issue request:
FHIR-47603 was Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6 and asked FHIR-I to clarify whether Group can represent Practitioners, CareTeams, or Organizations taking collective action. The approved resolution replaced the old Organization-exclusion definition with language saying Groups are not typically expected to act collectively or be formally recognized, while family and household Groups are limited exceptions and practitioner/organization collectives should use Organization or CareTeam when collective action is needed.
