Issue request:
FHIR-30204 was a Published/Persuasive CDS change request for R5 asking to eliminate the CQF Questionnaire profile and update CQF documentation to point users to SDC Library/questionnaire guidance. The Jira resolution agreed to remove the CQF Questionnaire profile.

Initial application:

The same commit replaced the local documentation-template guidance with a reference to the Structured Data Capture implementation guide.

Additional context:
FHIR-21005 is the same change family and explains that the old CQIF/CQF Questionnaire profile merely pointed to a CQL library and should be withdrawn in favor of SDC questionnaire guidance. FHIR-20411 is the upstream harmonization issue and records that the CQF example was migrated to SDC. Current build-scope evidence shows the Clinical Reasoning pages are active in source/hierarchy.xml, and the current Extension Pack cqf-library definition points questionnaire users to SDC behavior guidance for cqf-library.

Recommendation:
