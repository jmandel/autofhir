FHIR-31829 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core BR&R clarification request for the R4 SubstanceSpecification/SubstanceDefinition moiety.amountType element, saying the existing definition was not understandable and needed a better description. The Jira resolution accepted that a better description was needed, and the applied wording explained the measurement type of the quantitative value, including mole ratio and weight ratio examples.

Initial application:

No source gap remains from the original application.

Additional context:
