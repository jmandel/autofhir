FHIR-16685: Audit CarePlan created date

Issue request:
FHIR-16685 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Care change request from the STU3 ballot asking that CarePlan include the time and date it was created. The accepted resolution was to add CarePlan.created as a 0..1 dateTime with the definition "Represents when this particular CarePlan record was created in the system, which is often a system-generated date."

Initial application:
