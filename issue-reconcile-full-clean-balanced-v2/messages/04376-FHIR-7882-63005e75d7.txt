FHIR-7882: Audit Coverage beneficiary application

Issue request:
FHIR-7882 is a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Patient change request from the 2015May ballot asking to add Coverage so benefit coverage could be captured for a patient. The final resolution clarified that the real request was to add the patient to Coverage rather than add coverage to Patient, and recorded that this was accommodated in STU3 as Coverage.beneficiary.

Initial application:
No prior source commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-7882 was found. The implicit application appears in historical Coverage work: git commit 9874f360497fb610204cb1aea808e8d78419c5ed added Coverage.beneficiary[x] and Coverage.relationship while updating Coverage and Claim in March 2016, and later Coverage refactoring carried the model forward as Coverage.beneficiary in the current source. Current source/fhir.ini actively builds both Patient and Coverage.

Additional context:
FHIR-7880 is the tightly related relationship-to-subscriber ballot item named in FHIR-7882's comments; current Coverage.relationship covers the beneficiary/patient relationship to the subscriber. FHIR-7862 is related background about Coverage.dependent and the reporter's query concern, but it is a separate field/type issue and was not decided here. No direct Zulip or Confluence FHIR-7882 references were found.
