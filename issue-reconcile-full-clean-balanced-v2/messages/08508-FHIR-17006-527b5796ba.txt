FHIR-17006 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core references-page clarification from the STU3 ballot cycle. It asked to change the reverse-reference sentence from saying the reverse relationship is not explicitly represented in the target resource to saying it is not typically represented explicitly, because circular resource references can be valid.
