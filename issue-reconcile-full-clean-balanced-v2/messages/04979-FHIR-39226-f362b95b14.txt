FHIR-39226: Audit Device patient association move

Issue request:
FHIR-39226 was a Published/Persuasive R4-raised Device issue asking to move patient association out of Device, with its final resolution explicitly linking to the FHIR-38904 disposition.

Initial application:
The change was applied through the FHIR-38904 work: commit 620b8fa10c added the active DeviceAssociation resource, commit 97e59d026d commented out the old Device.association elements and Device patient/subject search parameters, and commit 7a135eb348 completed FHIR-38904 QA on DeviceAssociation. The current build configuration actively lists Device and DeviceAssociation, and its R4-to-R6 change notes record Device.patient as moved to DeviceAssociation.

Additional context:
FHIR-38904's accepted motion created DeviceAssociation to avoid placing patient-identifying association history in reusable Device instances. FHIR-38637 and FHIR-39009 were resolved by reference to the same FHIR-38904 disposition. Later R6 work, including FHIR-46011 and subsequent DeviceAssociation refactoring, clarifies the relationship in Device boundaries and keeps association data on DeviceAssociation rather than Device.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active source already removes Device patient association from Device, provides DeviceAssociation with subject, period, patient search, and patient compartment membership, and links Device to DeviceAssociation in the Device boundaries narrative.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-39226 source follow-up is needed in core; future clarifications about DeviceAssociation details should be handled through later DeviceAssociation-specific Jira issues rather than by reintroducing patient association content into Device.
