FHIR-13716: Audit TriggerDefinition event-prefix rename

Issue request:
FHIR-13716 was a Published/Persuasive STU3 change request from CDS asking to remove the redundant "event" prefix from TriggerDefinition element names. The approved resolution was to drop the prefix from the TriggerDefinition elements.

Initial application:
The Jira comment points to SVN 12254. In the git-svn import, commit 068f0b3582368d21cac3b59b0da22d37bd42edcb carries git-svn build@12254 and changed the authoritative TriggerDefinition datatype spreadsheet from TriggerDefinition.eventName, TriggerDefinition.eventTiming[x], and TriggerDefinition.eventData to TriggerDefinition.name, TriggerDefinition.timing[x], and TriggerDefinition.data. Later commit 1d4d221110faed79a9a19bb0c58abeb8e9a07916 added R3 diff/rename mappings from the old names to the new names.

Additional context:
TriggerDefinition remains an active metadata datatype in source/fhir.ini and is marked normative. The current datatype spreadsheet defines TriggerDefinition.type, name, code, subscriptionTopic, timing[x], data, and condition, with no event-prefixed element paths. The only current source hits for TriggerDefinition.eventName, TriggerDefinition.eventTiming[x], and TriggerDefinition.eventData are the intentional [r3-renames] compatibility mappings in source/fhir.ini, not live element definitions. Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later or conflicting decision about this rename.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change because the current build-scope source already reflects the requested rename, and removing the R3 rename mappings would damage historical diff/compatibility behavior rather than further applying FHIR-13716.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-13716 unless a future build-scope check shows generated artifacts diverging from the authoritative datatype spreadsheet.
