FHIR-49012 was Applied as Persuasive with Modification for R6. It requested clearer CanonicalResource.copyright wording about intellectual property ownership, a corrected copyrightLabel typo, updated comments explaining that copyrightLabel should not include copyright prefixes or symbols, and rollout to resources implementing CanonicalResource or MetadataResource that carried the old pattern.

Initial application:

Additional context:
FHIR-I Madrid WGM minutes record approval of the modified wording, including the decision to describe copyright as a notice about intellectual property rights and to clarify use of the '(c)' character in copyrightLabel.
