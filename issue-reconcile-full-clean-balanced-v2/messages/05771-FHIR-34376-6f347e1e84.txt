Additional context:
OO minutes for 2023-03-20 record FHIR-34376 as pre-applied and published in R5, while noting that the Clinical module home page still lists ServiceRequest in its index and ServiceRequest is assigned to Workflow. The later FHIR-44611 workflow-module update reinforces that domain-specific modules should be consulted for relationships and does not reverse FHIR-34376.
