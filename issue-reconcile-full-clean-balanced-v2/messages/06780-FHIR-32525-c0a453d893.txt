FHIR-32525: Confirm que-1 group rule split

Issue request:
FHIR-32525 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core Questionnaire change request raised against R4. It asked that draft questionnaires be allowed to contain a group item without child items, and the voted resolution split the old que-1 error into que-1a, que-1b, and que-1c: an error for group items without children when the Questionnaire was considered complete, a warning that groups should have items, and an error that display items cannot have child items.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source/questionnaire/structuredefinition-Questionnaire.xml by Jira-tagged commits 520d040e6a00c79ed674439f077bdd3bcc261776 and 2bbb1b812ec2ed83d43c4bded1b1c84b809ce58b. Those commits removed the single que-1 error, introduced que-1a/que-1b/que-1c, and attached the split constraints to Questionnaire.item.

Additional context:
The literal FHIR-32525 resolution used %resource.status='complete', but Questionnaire.status has draft | active | retired | unknown rather than complete. Later Applied/Persuasive technical correction FHIR-43667 identified that error and said the que-1a status should be active, not complete; source commit 5fa59a80deca6414b4dbd79a5a21941c537e1a13 made that change, and combined-branch audit commit ec8545fa2377e7fb04429ec211d5f6fbcef76f62 already represents FHIR-43667. Questionnaire is active in source/fhir.ini, and the current build-scoped StructureDefinition source contains que-1a as an active-only error, que-1b as the general warning, and que-1c as the display-child error. Community searches found no conflicting Zulip or Confluence evidence.
