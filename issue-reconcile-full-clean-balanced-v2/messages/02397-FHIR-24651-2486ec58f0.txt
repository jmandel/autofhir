FHIR-24651: Audit MedicationKnowledge ingredient role

Issue request:
FHIR-24651 was a Published Pharmacy change request raised from STU3 asking to update MedicationKnowledge to include both Basis of Strength Substance (BOSS) and Precise Active Ingredient (PAI). The accepted resolution was Not Persuasive with Modification: change the MedicationKnowledge ingredient isActive representation to a CodeableConcept with an exemplar value set, while deferring the remaining BOSS/PAI and Ingredient-resource role/entity questions to follow-up Pharmacy/BR&R work.

Initial application:
Commit bb4b0e41393b1023027fc45c9e53d5d941c95ff3 explicitly included FHIR-24651 and changed MedicationKnowledge.ingredient.isActive from boolean to CodeableConcept with an example binding to v3 RoleClassIngredientEntity. Commit 6422f2f98a0fcf03140c15fe93bfd6c765fc6377 later moved the ingredient elements under MedicationKnowledge.definitional.ingredient and preserved the CodeableConcept role/type representation and binding.

Additional context:
MedicationKnowledge is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. The current source has MedicationKnowledge.definitional.ingredient.item as a CodeableReference to SubstanceDefinition and MedicationKnowledge.definitional.ingredient.type as a CodeableConcept bound, at example strength, to v3 RoleClassIngredientEntity. September 2020 Pharmacy WGM minutes confirm the 13-0-0 Not Persuasive with Modification decision and explicitly record that BOSS/PAI representation needed BR&R coordination rather than a direct MedicationKnowledge BOSS/PAI addition. Later BR&R work added Ingredient.substance.strength.basis for basis-of-strength needs and changed Ingredient link direction; those related issues already have combined-branch audit commits.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. The active authoritative source already contains the accepted CodeableConcept ingredient-role change, and adding separate BOSS/PAI fields or narrative to MedicationKnowledge now would exceed FHIR-24651's approved scope and risk conflicting with later Ingredient/BR&R modeling decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core FHIR source follow-up is needed for FHIR-24651 unless Pharmacy and BR&R make a new current decision requiring MedicationKnowledge-specific BOSS/PAI modeling.
