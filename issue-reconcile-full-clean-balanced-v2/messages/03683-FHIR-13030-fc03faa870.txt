FHIR-13030: Audit ClaimResponse payment clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-13030 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2 by Financial Management. It asked ClaimResponse to clarify how a consumer knows whether the related Claim was processed as a traditional claim, preauthorization, or predetermination, and to state that an adjudicated traditional claim can indicate intended payment while the actual funds transfer occurs separately; preauthorization and predetermination responses do not imply payment is pending. The final resolution said Claim.use identifies the claim/preauth/predetermination nature, that the ClaimResponse introductory text had been updated, and proposed also adding ClaimResponse.use, ClaimResponse.type, and ClaimResponse.subType for clarity.

Initial application:
No local git commit explicitly tagged FHIR-13030 was found. The local history shows the key ClaimResponse introductory payment wording was already added in commit 2acb5fd597 before Jira migration, and the 2021 source rework preserved the active ClaimResponse StructureDefinition with ClaimResponse.type, ClaimResponse.subType, and ClaimResponse.use.

Additional context:
ClaimResponse is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. The paired Claim issue FHIR-13029 records that Claim.use explicitly identifies claim, preauthorization, or predetermination, and the current Claim introduction and Claim.use definition do that. Later FHIR-36101 was Resolved - No Change and kept Claim/ClaimResponse covering preauthorization and predetermination rather than splitting resources. The already-published FHIR-17936 reconciliation further constrains ClaimResponse.payment to use='claim' with a non-error outcome, which is consistent with this issue's payment clarification.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current active source already tells implementers that only an actual claim may be expected to result in actual payment, while ClaimResponse.use/type/subType and Claim.use provide the requested machine-readable context. Adding more prose would be redundant and could create maintenance drift.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-13030; any broader redesign of Claim/ClaimResponse scope or payment modeling should be handled under a new or existing FM work-group issue rather than this already-applied clarification.
