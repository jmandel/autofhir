FHIR-44517: Audit AllergyIntolerance participant removal

Issue request:
FHIR-44517 is Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6. It asked Patient Care to remove the AllergyIntolerance.participant backbone element, because participant/function described involvement in reactions rather than the risk assessment, and to restore AllergyIntolerance.recorder plus AllergyIntolerance.asserter to align with R4.

Initial application:
The Jira comments cite GH commits ed99dff, a4fa606, and 1f388a4 from HL7/fhir#3141. Commit ed99dff directly reintroduced AllergyIntolerance.recorder and AllergyIntolerance.asserter in source/allergyintolerance/structuredefinition-AllergyIntolerance.xml, commented out the participant backbone, and updated AllergyIntolerance examples and the asserter search parameter. Those cited commits are ancestors of the current combined branch.

Additional context:
FHIR-39513 is the same AllergyIntolerance change family and records the typed participant/function anti-pattern rationale; its resolution explicitly defers to FHIR-44517. source/fhir.ini has AllergyIntolerance as an active resource and includes R5-to-R6 migration hints from participant to recorder/asserter. Current source still has a checked-in allergyintolerance.svg showing participant, but the authoritative StructureDefinition, examples, and search parameter source no longer expose an active participant element. No Zulip hit or Confluence minute changed that conclusion; the May 2024 Patient Care minutes say FHIR-44517 and FHIR-39513 were resolved because participant was removed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scope source already implements the applied Jira decision: recorder and asserter are active, participant is not active after stripping XML comments, and examples/search parameters no longer use participant. The remaining SVG-only stale diagram was not edited under the AutoFHIR generated-diagram guardrail.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If maintainers want visual parity, refresh the AllergyIntolerance diagram through the normal diagram generation path, but no normative core source correction is needed for FHIR-44517.
