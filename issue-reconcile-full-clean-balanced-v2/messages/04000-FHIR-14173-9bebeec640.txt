The Jira resolution records that the change was made and published in the Dec 2017 FHIR ballot.

Initial application:
No current git commit subject or message explicitly mentions FHIR-14173. Historical R4 evidence shows the request was applied: origin/r4 ResearchStudy source defines ResearchStudy.arm.type rather than arm.code, and the R3/R4 StructureMap maps src.code to tgt.type and src.type back to tgt.code for ResearchStudy.arm.

Additional context:
Later ResearchStudy modeling decisions changed the surrounding artifact. FHIR-31681 renamed ResearchStudy.arm to ResearchStudy.comparisonGroup for R5, and that related reconciliation is already represented on the combined branch by e53a02e41ceb338508d42078dd95a8f03ce9f14e. Later R6 work, especially FHIR-48736 with FHIR-46491, removed the comparisonGroup.type child altogether and replaced the comparison-group child shape with targetNumber, actualNumber, eligibility, observedGroup, name, and description. Current source/fhir.ini records ResearchStudy as active, maps the old R4 arm backbone to comparisonGroup for R4-to-R6 metadata, and intentionally leaves arm.type and comparisonGroup.type deleted.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The active current ResearchStudy StructureDefinition has no ResearchStudy.arm.code, no ResearchStudy.arm.type, and no ResearchStudy.comparisonGroup.type; the historical rename is preserved where R4 history is available, while current R6 source reflects later applied decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. No current FHIR-core source correction is needed for FHIR-14173; any future change to reintroduce a comparison-group categorization element would need a new BRR decision rather than this STU3 rename issue.
