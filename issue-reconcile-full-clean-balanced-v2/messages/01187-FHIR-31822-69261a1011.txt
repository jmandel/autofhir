FHIR-31822: Audit SubstanceDefinition 80 percent guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-31822 was a Published FHIR-core BRR change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for R5, asking for a Here Be Dragons warning on SubstanceDefinition and guidance that users distinguish Ingredient from SubstanceDefinition because SubstanceDefinition might change significantly as its 80 percent fit was reviewed.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git commit 59ffd1065202cf6338c52ccb557c07637464edea, "added better scope and warning for FHIR#31822", which expanded source/substancedefinition/substancedefinition-introduction.xml with SubstanceDefinition scope text, Ingredient/Substance/Medication boundary guidance, and the then-approved dragon warning. No separate Ingredient-page commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-31822 was found, but later Ingredient documentation and definitions now explain the Ingredient context and its relationship to SubstanceDefinition references.

Additional context:
Ingredient and SubstanceDefinition are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini under BRR. Current SubstanceDefinition scope text says it is for rich definitional substance descriptions, is not generally used directly as an ingredient or patient product, and that Ingredient supplies the product-specific strength and role context while using SubstanceDefinition to describe the substance. Current Ingredient scope text explains that Ingredient is a product-context resource and its StructureDefinition permits references to SubstanceDefinition for the substance and reference substance. Later Published/Applied issues refined the same area: FHIR-31841 and FHIR-39138 are already represented on the combined branch as no-change audits for the broader SubstanceDefinition and Ingredient guidance, FHIR-48405 refined the Substance versus SubstanceDefinition distinction, and FHIR-50941 later approved removing the dragon warning because SubstanceDefinition had stabilized.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Restoring the dragon warning would contradict the later BRR-approved FHIR-50941 decision, and adding another local Ingredient note would duplicate current build-scope guidance that already distinguishes Ingredient's contextual role from SubstanceDefinition's definitional role.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future changes to Ingredient/SubstanceDefinition guidance should be handled through new BRR decisions, not by restoring the superseded FHIR-31822 warning language.
