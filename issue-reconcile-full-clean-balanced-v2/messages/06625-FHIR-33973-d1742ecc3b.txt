FHIR-33973: Audit SubscriptionTopic notification shape focus wording

Issue request:
FHIR-33973 was Published/Persuasive for R5. It asked to clarify that SubscriptionTopic.notificationShape.resource is the focus of the subscription and aligns with SubscriptionTopic.eventTrigger.resource and SubscriptionTopic.canFilterBy.resource when those elements are present.

Initial application:
The Jira comment points to commit 0f0727da15874cb2bcec967a805c088f1370cdc1, which updated the SubscriptionTopic StructureDefinition and notes to describe notificationShape.resource and canFilterBy.resource as focus resources linked to resourceTrigger.resource or eventTrigger.resource.

Additional context:
Later R6 work in FHIR-46492 collapsed resourceTrigger and eventTrigger into SubscriptionTopic.trigger, and moved canFilterBy and notificationShape under trigger. Later FHIR-39029/FHIR-39030 clarification work and current notes preserve the same focus/root-resource rule using the current trigger.resource path. source/fhir.ini keeps old R5 element names mapped to the active R6 trigger paths.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The active SubscriptionTopic definitions already state that trigger.canFilterBy.resource and trigger.notificationShape.resource are focus/root resources and are the same, a generality, or a specificity of SubscriptionTopic.trigger.resource when present; this is the current-model equivalent of the requested eventTrigger/canFilterBy clarification.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Reintroducing old eventTrigger/resourceTrigger names or editing generated diagram artifacts would be less correct than relying on the active StructureDefinition, notes, and fhir.ini path mappings.
