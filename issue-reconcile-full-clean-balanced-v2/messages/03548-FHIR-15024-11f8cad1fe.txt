The active AllergyIntolerance page still contains narrative saying refuted allergies indicate a reaction has been ruled out with high clinical certainty, but that text was explicitly added or preserved by later Published issue FHIR-28458 about clinicalStatus guidance for refuted AllergyIntolerance/Condition records.
