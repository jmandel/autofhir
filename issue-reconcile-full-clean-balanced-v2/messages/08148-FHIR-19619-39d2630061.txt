FHIR-19619: Audit history response status wording

Issue request:
FHIR-19619 was Published with a Persuasive resolution for the RESTful history interaction. It reported that the _history guidance implied Bundle.entry.response.status could be omitted, while the Bundle definition made that element 1..1, and the approved resolution was to replace the optional-status sentence with: "Servers may choose to only record successful interactions. Servers may choose to only use 200 OK instead of other success codes."

Initial application:
The issue was applied by e8104dd10d, which changed source/http.html from the sentence about not populating response.status to the approved successful-interactions/200 OK wording. The current source/http.html still contains that replacement wording, and the old optional-status sentence is absent from current source.

Additional context:
The current history section now also says each history entry SHALL contain a response element, and later history examples include response.status values. The Bundle source still defines Bundle.entry.response.status as 1..1, so the original contradiction has been resolved by clarifying history guidance rather than changing the Bundle cardinality. The Jira-linked Zulip discussion supports that interpretation: participants identified the contradiction, noted that changing cardinality was not appropriate for R4, and asked for a change proposal to clarify expectations. No relevant Confluence record was found. The related FHIR-23013 reconciliation is already represented on the combined branch and further confirms the current history/status guidance is consistent.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current built source already matches the approved FHIR-19619 wording and no longer implies that required Bundle.entry.response.status may be omitted.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. Future changes to whether history Bundles must carry response.status should be handled as a new conformance decision, not as a reapplication of FHIR-19619.
