FHIR-19575: Audit CapabilityStatement conformance language

Issue request:
FHIR-19575 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core issue asking whether a conformance/capability statement is required for FHIR conformance. The approved resolution was to add guidance that non-server systems may publish a CapabilityStatement to assert formal conformance, and that conformance assertions without a CapabilityStatement have little meaning because they cannot be tested or verified.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git 8c2e1da030578313093b7b0267c6e9953e257156 on 2022-08-25. That commit changed source/conformance-rules.html from only saying that systems can claim FHIR conformance for functionality described in an applicable CapabilityStatement to also say that RESTful servers must provide a CapabilityStatement on request and that other systems or applications asserting formal conformance may publish one.

Additional context:
source/conformance-rules.html is an active narrative page in the current build tree, listed under the Foundation documentation hierarchy and linked from source/conformance-module.html and source/documentation.html. The current wording uses "Other systems or applications" instead of the Jira resolution's "Non-server systems", but preserves the intended distinction between RESTful servers and other conforming systems. Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later FHIR-core decision or community discussion that reverses or narrows this language.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Made no source change. The current source already contains the requested CapabilityStatement conformance guidance in the active conformance rules page, and the small wording difference from the resolution does not change implementer understanding.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. No FHIR-19575 source follow-up is needed in core unless a future work-group decision changes how non-server conformance assertions should be documented.
