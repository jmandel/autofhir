FHIR-11077: Audit Condition absence guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-11077 is a Published Patient Care change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification for DSTU2, asking how to assert that a subject does not have a condition. The approved resolution added an "Assertions of Condition Absence" usage section explaining that electronic records generally do not contain assertions of conditions a patient does not have, with exceptions for refuted Condition records and checklist-style questions that should be represented with QuestionnaireResponse or Observation rather than Condition.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation in git commit 87f224c94c, whose subject explicitly says "Condition #11077 added section about asserting the absence of a condition" and whose patch added the requested section to source/condition/condition-notes.xml. The section survived later source restructuring and restoration work, including 005f3df50a restoring condition-notes.xml after it had been unintentionally deleted.

Additional context:
Condition is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so source/condition/condition-notes.xml is current build-scope source. Later related decisions refined but did not reverse the seed guidance: FHIR-11026 updated the refuted-condition bullet to point to Condition.verificationStatus and Condition.evidence, FHIR-14871 rejected adding a distinct absent status and reiterated that checklist absence should use QuestionnaireResponse or Observation, and FHIR-26904 clarified the Condition/Observation boundary for patient states and was already audited on the combined branch. Zulip discussion on "Assertions of Condition Absence" shows broader debate about negation but no final decision requiring a source change for this seed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Condition notes already contain the requested "Assertions of Condition Absence" heading and both approved exceptions, with later wording that more precisely distinguishes a refuted Condition record from a general assertion of absence and preserves the QuestionnaireResponse-or-Observation guidance for checklist questions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR core source change is needed for FHIR-11077; broader questions about information-model negation or absent-status codes should remain under their own Jira decisions rather than reopening this applied DSTU2 clarification.
