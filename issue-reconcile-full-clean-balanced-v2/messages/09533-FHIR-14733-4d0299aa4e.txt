FHIR-14733: Audit Observation focus guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-14733 was a Published, Persuasive with Modification STU3 Observation issue asking how to represent observations about entities or workflow processes associated with a patient, such as an implanted device, while retaining the patient link. The resolution pointed to the focal-subject construct, said the subject definition should clarify when to use that construct versus Observation.subject, and left any move into core to a separate tracker.

Initial application:
No source commit in the local history explicitly mentions FHIR-14733. The same application family was implemented through the Observation focal-subject work: d06b52856c894 added focalSubject/focal-subject guidance to Observation, and 5c093b94a97 renamed that construct to Observation.focus and updated the subject/focus text. Those commits touched the historical Observation spreadsheet and notes rather than carrying the FHIR-14733 key.

Additional context:
FHIR-14863 records the final WG decision to promote the focal-subject extension to a DSTU Observation element, and the linked Zulip thread shows the core modeling choice: keep the patient or other subject of record in Observation.subject and add a separate focus reference for the actual target. Later changes refined but did not reverse that result: FHIR-37736 made Observation.focus a modifier and added related-person guidance; FHIR-43357 changed wording from patient-of-record to subject-of-record and added device/DeviceMetric focus examples; FHIR-44333 deprecated the remaining observation-focusCode extension in the Extension Pack because Observation.focus and referenced-resource elements are the preferred representation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Observation is active in source/fhir.ini, and the current build-scoped Observation StructureDefinition and notes already explain that Observation.subject is the record subject while Observation.focus carries the actual focus when it differs, including examples for a parent/caregiver, fetus, body structure/tumor, implanted or patient-attached device, and DeviceMetric. Adding old focal-subject extension wording back into core would be stale and less correct than the current Observation.focus guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-14733 unless the work group later reopens the broader Observation.focus modeling; current source is consistent with the applied/published issue family and later R6 refinements.
