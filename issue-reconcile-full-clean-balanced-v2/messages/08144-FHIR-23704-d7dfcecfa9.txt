FHIR-23704: Audit Device patient compartment decision

Issue request:
FHIR-23704 was a Published FHIR-core Device change request, resolved Persuasive for R5. It asked why Device, which then had a patient reference, was not in the Patient compartment. The Jira discussion recorded the OO rationale that many Device instances are reusable and do not uniquely belong to one patient, while patient-specific device use/association belongs in device-use or association artifacts.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-23704 was found. The R5 source history shows the question was initially reflected through the broader patient-compartment cleanup: commit 49328521 added a Device row entry of subject to the Patient compartment as part of FHIR-39386, matching the R5-era state where Device carried patient association information.

Additional context:
Later Published issues FHIR-38904, FHIR-39226, and FHIR-39243 superseded the R5-era approach by moving patient association out of Device to avoid reusable Device instances carrying patient-identifying history and by introducing DeviceAssociation for the patient/device relationship. Commit 97e59d026 removed Device subject from source/compartments.xml and commented out Device.association plus Device subject/patient search parameters. Later Applied issue FHIR-51498 updated the current Device-related compartment matrix without adding Device back to the Patient compartment; it added the Device root to the Device compartment and added/kept patient-relevant mappings on resources such as DeviceAssociation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active build source already matches the later decisions: source/fhir.ini keeps Device, DeviceAssociation, and CompartmentDefinition in the current build; source/compartments.xml has no Patient-compartment parameter for Device, while DeviceAssociation is in the Patient compartment via patient | subject | focus; source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml has the old Device.association backbone commented out; and source/device/device-introduction.xml directs patient/device ownership and custodianship to DeviceAssociation while noting that devices may be reused.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Re-adding Device itself to the Patient compartment would conflict with the later FHIR-38904/FHIR-39226/FHIR-39243 decision family and the FHIR-51498 compartment update unless OO/FHIR-I make a new explicit decision to reverse the DeviceAssociation-based model.
