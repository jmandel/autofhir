FHIR-10352: Audit Communication reason reference scope

Issue request:
FHIR-10352 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for Patient Care and asked that Communication.reasonReference and CommunicationRequest.reasonReference support references beyond Encounter-limited context by changing the reasonReference target to Reference(Any) with 0..* cardinality.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by fd021062fc5a010ce4ff3cab2d6b8127527811f2, which changed the then-authoritative Communication and CommunicationRequest spreadsheet rows for reasonReference from Reference(Condition|Observation|DiagnosticReport|DocumentReference) to Reference(Any) and tagged the rows with GF#10352. Later source refactoring removed the spreadsheet files and preserved the modeled intent in current StructureDefinition XML.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists Communication and CommunicationRequest as active current-build resources and records the R5 merge of reasonCode and reasonReference into the current reason element, with reasonReference mapping to reason.reference. A later Patient Care no-change issue, FHIR-25795, explicitly confirmed that Appointment was already possible because the current build had reason as CodeableReference(Any), and its comment traced that intentional change back to FHIR-10352. Community searches found no Zulip or Confluence record that conflicts with the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. In the current active StructureDefinitions, Communication.reason and CommunicationRequest.reason are both 0..* CodeableReference with targetProfile Resource, which is the current-source representation of the old reasonReference Reference(Any) requirement after the R5 reason merge.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source fix is needed for FHIR-10352; any future cleanup of unrelated stale comment wording around reasonCode/reason.concept should be handled under the tracker that owns the R5 reason merge or a dedicated editorial issue, not as a semantic FHIR-10352 change.
