FHIR-29673: Audit Patient sex and gender guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-29673 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration change request for R5 to update the Patient Gender and Sex section for Gender Harmony, add Sex for Clinical Use and Recorded Sex guidance, update sex assigned at birth language, and define the related standard extensions for gender identity, pronouns, sex for clinical use, and recorded sex or gender.

Initial application:
The work was applied before this reconciliation through the FHIR-29673 gender-harmony branch and merge commit 59f7e56b6c3ddae75195e82628fabe15328556b1, with branch commits such as 76b1f6db879c785cf4ee1a3b98897ebc0576cefb and 5fa4ec6a53c15a2a9b401ad4cb2867b2b458a7f1 updating the Patient narrative and extension definitions. Patient Administration approved the Jira disposition 3-0-0 on 2021-12-22, and Gender Harmony minutes later recorded approval of the current branch build for merge into the FHIR CI trunk.

Additional context:
Later R5 ballot follow-up issues refined the same section: FHIR-38861 added use-case guidance for Patient.gender versus RSG, FHIR-39202 removed the problematic openly-known gender identity language, FHIR-39201 made clinical observations a sibling bullet, and FHIR-39219 renamed Sex for Clinical Use to Sex Parameter for Clinical Use. Core extension definitions were subsequently removed from the R5 source and moved to the FHIR Extension Pack; current core narrative uses extension-pack links, and the extension-pack checkout contains the current individual-genderIdentity, individual-pronouns, individual-recordedSexOrGender, and patient-sexParameterForClinicalUse definitions.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Patient source already contains the Patient Gender and Sex section with Administrative Gender, Gender Identity and pronouns, Sex Parameter for Clinical Use, clinical observations, Recorded Sex or Gender, Legal Sex, and Sex assigned at Birth guidance, and Patient.gender links readers to that section. The remaining extension definitions are maintained in the FHIR Extension Pack rather than this core checkout, and no missing core-source correction was found.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future changes to the extension definitions should be made in the FHIR Extension Pack; future core changes should be limited to concrete divergences from the current Gender Harmony guidance rather than restoring pre-R5 SFCU/RSG wording.
