FHIR-7633: Audit ProcedureRequest alignment

Issue request:
FHIR-7633 was a Published Patient Care change request from the DSTU1 era asking to realign Procedure and ProcedureRequest after Procedure had evolved. The Persuasive with Modification resolution and follow-up Jira comment called for the two resources to use aligned subject/patient naming, include Group on the subjects, rename performer person/individual style elements to actor with Organization support, make notes 0..* Annotation, and keep the resource ordering consistent.

Initial application:
The issue was applied historically in commit 27f73740e4afc943ade51df795872e6e5c3865ee, which explicitly mentions #7633 and changed the Procedure source from Procedure.patient to Procedure.subject, changed Procedure.performer.individual to Procedure.performer.actor, expanded Procedure.subject to Patient|Group, expanded Procedure.performer.actor to include Organization, and expanded the ProcedureRequest subject to Patient|Group. The note-related shape was subsequently maintained through workflow alignment work, including FHIR-10293 commits that renamed notes to note while keeping the 0..* Annotation datatype.

Additional context:
ProcedureRequest is no longer an active current-build resource. Later final decisions merged ReferralRequest into ProcedureRequest (FHIR-13196) and renamed the merged resource to ServiceRequest (FHIR-13867); source/fhir.ini now actively lists Procedure and ServiceRequest and records ProcedureRequest only as a deleted/merged resource. Later Procedure work, especially FHIR-26859, deliberately broadened Procedure.subject beyond the original Patient|Group alignment, so the current broader reference list is expected and should not be narrowed for this old DSTU1 issue. No directly relevant Zulip or Confluence evidence was found that conflicts with the Jira/source/git trail.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. The active current source already preserves the substance of FHIR-7633 in its successor resources: Procedure.subject and ServiceRequest.subject are active subject elements that allow Group, Procedure.performer.actor and ServiceRequest.performer allow Organization, and both Procedure.note and ServiceRequest.note are 0..* Annotation. A search of the active Procedure and ServiceRequest source found no stale Procedure.patient, performer.individual, ProcedureRequest, or notes element usage requiring cleanup.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. Do not recreate ProcedureRequest or narrow later broadened subject/performer scopes; any future work should be driven by newer Procedure or ServiceRequest issues rather than by this already-applied DSTU1 alignment request.
