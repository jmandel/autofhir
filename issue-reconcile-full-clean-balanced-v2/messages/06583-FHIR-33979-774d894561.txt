Issue request:
FHIR-33979 was a Published FHIR-core change request with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked that the Developer Introduction Framework bullet stop implying that every resource intrinsically has a URL identifier and instead say that resources have an identifier, typically a URL.

Initial application:

No source gap remains in the current checkout.
