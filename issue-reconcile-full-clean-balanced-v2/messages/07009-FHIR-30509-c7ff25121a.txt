FHIR-30509: Audit derived OperationDefinition parameters

Issue request:
FHIR-30509 is Published/Persuasive for R5. It asked that an OperationDefinition derived from a base OperationDefinition be allowed to introduce new parameters in addition to the base parameters, with the accepted wording changing the guidance to say a constrained profile can make optional parameters required or not used, introduce new parameters (required or optional), and clarify documentation.

Initial application:
Commit ecd177722cbd27b6abf4e8610489a0ea592ca0a7 applied the issue by updating the OperationDefinition.base comment in source/operationdefinition/structuredefinition-OperationDefinition.xml to include the accepted "introduce new parameters (required or optional)" wording. The same guidance was later moved out of the element comment and into OperationDefinition usage notes rather than left on OperationDefinition.base.

Additional context:
OperationDefinition is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. FHIR-38034, already represented on the combined branch, moved and expanded the derived OperationDefinition guidance into source/operationdefinition/operationdefinition-notes.xml. FHIR-48481, also already represented, later added detailed SHOULD-level derivation rules including optional-parameter compatibility guidance; it did not remove the explicit statement that a derived operation can introduce new required or optional parameters. FHIR-I minutes for 2021-02-22 record the FHIR-30509 Persuasive vote, and targeted Zulip searches found no conflicting issue-specific discussion.
