Issue request:
FHIR-7673 was a Published CDS change request from the 2015 May ballot asking why the Contraindication resource existed, who submitted it, and whether it recorded patient-specific drug-drug interaction alerts/responses rather than general knowledge. The final Persuasive with Modification resolution says the resource originated from CDS and points to the FHIR-7118 resolution for the substantive clarification.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly mentions FHIR-7673. The referenced FHIR-7118 application commit 40d7256c82 added the patient-specific, non-knowledge clarification and workflow-alert-fatigue warning to source/contraindication. The FHIR-8043 rename commit 1408563d74 then carried that text into the active DetectedIssue resource and fhir.ini records that Contraindication became DetectedIssue.

Additional context:
DetectedIssue is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. Current DetectedIssue source states that it represents a specific instance of a potential issue for a particular patient, is not general patient-independent knowledge, distinguishes ClinicalUseDefinition/Flag/AllergyIntolerance/Condition/Procedure/DeviceAlert boundaries, and retains the warning about clinically irrelevant alerts causing alert fatigue. Later Published/Applied issues such as FHIR-21633 and FHIR-52315 refine these boundaries but do not reverse the FHIR-7673/FHIR-7118 answer.
