FHIR-28221: Audit Goal.source CareTeam support

Issue request:
FHIR-28221 was a Published FHIR-core Goal change request with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked to rename Goal.expressedBy to Goal.source, add CareTeam as an allowed reference target so a care team can establish goals for collaborative care, and update the comment to say the individual or team establishes the goal rather than necessarily recording it.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by a0156c2d5b (J#28221 Rename Goal.expressedBy to source), which renamed the Goal example element from expressedBy to source, renamed the StructureDefinition element to Goal.source, added CareTeam as a targetProfile, and changed the comment to say individual or team. No prior combined-branch reconciliation commit exists for FHIR-28221.

Additional context:
Goal is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. Current source/goal/structuredefinition-Goal.xml still defines Goal.source with CareTeam in the reference targets and the individual-or-team comment. Later applied R6 issues changed the same element: FHIR-41409 added Group and a Group-use comment, and FHIR-48366 added Goal.recorder and intentionally removed the old parenthetical Provenance guidance from Goal.source because recorder now handles who recorded the goal. Patient Care Confluence records confirm FHIR-28221 was part of the 2020 Goal/care-plan discussion and no Zulip search result was found that contradicts the Jira/source history.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

Recommendation:
