FHIR-31793: Audit manufactured item naming clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-31793 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification BRR change request asking that ManufacturedItemDefinition acknowledge that "manufactured" is not itself the distinguishing characteristic. The accepted resolution was to add wording explaining that the item is represented in the state it comes from manufacturing, before preparatory steps such as crushing or mixing, and before any separate administrable transformation.

Initial application:
The approved wording was applied before this reconciliation to the active ManufacturedItemDefinition scope-and-usage narrative. Git history shows commit fbfbf287b1ed7a1add0a216ec02cd2d6daab062c added the exact paragraph on 2021-10-07, and commit 4b8f3f05771e5fc759182d5d0cb02253fa273ef5 carried the same change the next day.
