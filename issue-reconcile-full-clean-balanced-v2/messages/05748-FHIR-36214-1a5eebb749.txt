FHIR-36214: Audit Device association rename

Issue request:
FHIR-36214 was a Published R5 Device change request with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked to revise Device.associationStatus into a backbone named associationState containing status, statusReason, and subject so the state and patient association details were grouped together.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied through PR #1774, merged as 246a31f1c76d7c93679f83e2b73847895473621a. The Jira comment points at PR-head commit 235bdc99b4a8d2094926152516a4b3923c930ea5; the substantive source change is in that branch history at f97a700790904dd18c9b22d002021a75d9f61f16, which replaced Device.associationStatus with Device.associationState and added status, statusReason, and humanSubject.

Additional context:
FHIR-32570 used the FHIR-36214 backbone to remove Device.subject. FHIR-34134 then changed associationState to association and added bodyStructure. FHIR-38904 later superseded the Device-level association backbone by moving patient association tracking out of Device into the active DeviceAssociation resource to avoid patient-identifying information in reusable Device instances. Current fhir.ini includes both Device and DeviceAssociation as active resources, and FHIR-38904 is already represented on the combined branch.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. In current source, the old Device.association block is entirely XML-commented, `xmllint` reports zero active Device association/associationState/associationStatus elements, and the active build-scope model is DeviceAssociation. Editing the commented legacy Device block would not change the current specification and could obscure the later published move to DeviceAssociation.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not resurrect Device.associationState or rename DeviceAssociation.associationStatus for this R5 Device issue; any further status modeling work belongs to the later DeviceAssociation issue family.
