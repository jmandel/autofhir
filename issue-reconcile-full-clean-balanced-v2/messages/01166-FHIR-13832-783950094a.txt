FHIR-13832: Audit DNA variant change rename as external IG content

Issue request:
FHIR-13832 was a Published FHIR-core Observation change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking to rename Observation-geneticsVariant to Observation-geneticsDNAVariantChange and clarify the definition from generic variant information to DNA variant change information. The accepted resolution said the spirit of the change was already applied in the Genomics Reporting IG at the described-variant variation-code component.

Initial application:
No FHIR-13832-specific core source commit was found. Clinical Genomics minutes approved FHIR-13832 together with FHIR-13833 and FHIR-13835 as already applied to the Genomics Reporting IG, but not to Observation-genetics in FHIR Core. Historical core source retained the older observation-geneticsVariant profile/extension surface until later genetics guidance was redirected to the Genomics Reporting IG and outdated clinical genomics artifacts were removed from Observation, DiagnosticReport, and ServiceRequest.

Additional context:
Observation is active in the current core build, but the legacy Observation-genetics profile and Observation-geneticsDNAVariantChange target are not active core build-scope content. Current core narrative directs genomic result implementers to the Genomics Reporting IG. The FHIR Extension Pack contains the legacy observation-geneticsVariant extension as retired/deprecated content and says it has been replaced by profiles and extensions in the Genomics Reporting Guide. The current Genomics Reporting IG has evolved the old described-variant material into the Variant profile and includes a variation-code component for variant database accessions.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no FHIR-core source change. Reintroducing or renaming the retired Observation-genetics variant profile in core would be outside current build scope and would contradict the work-group disposition that the requested naming/definition change was handled in the external Genomics Reporting IG.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future corrections to the variant change naming or variation-code semantics should be made in HL7/genomics-reporting or the owning external artifact, not by reviving legacy Observation-genetics content in FHIR core.
