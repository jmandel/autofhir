Initial application:

Additional context:
Later approved changes changed the target surface. FHIR-18860 removed ClinicalImpression.investigation and merged its meaning into supportingInfo. FHIR-19250 merged Media into DocumentReference and changed the ClinicalImpression references from Media to DocumentReference while removing Media from the active resource list. FHIR-46596 renamed ClinicalImpression to ClinicalAssessment, and a later fhir.ini cleanup removed ClinicalAssessment from the active current-build resources. The checked-in ClinicalImpression and ClinicalAssessment source folders are now out of build scope; their inactive finding item definitions retain the GF#14150 note but use DocumentReference rather than Media, and supportingInfo is Reference(Resource).

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

Recommendation:
