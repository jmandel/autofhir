FHIR-28101 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification for FHIR-core Provenance. It asked that confusing W3 PROV-O-style Provenance Entity Role definitions be rewritten from the perspective of each entity role in the activity, and that the unnecessary derivation code be removed.

Initial application:

The later QA cleanup 91ff45e5413e7a2baef06fa5814fe403edf4d8fc removed generated narrative text while preserving those concept definitions.

Current source still contains instantiates as a distinct role and does not contain the removed provenance-entity-role derivation code. Zulip search found no direct discussion of FHIR-28101 or conflicting Provenance Entity Role rationale; Confluence references show the issue on Security WG agenda/minutes pages without changing the Jira/source conclusion.
