FHIR-20652: Audit Timing repeat definitions

Issue request:
FHIR-20652 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification MnM change request, raised against STU3 and applied for R5, asking that the duplicate Timing.repeat.frequency and Timing.repeat.period Description & Constraints text "Event occurs frequency times per period" be made distinct. The approved resolution set frequency to "Indicates the number of repetitions that should occur within a period. I.e. Event occurs frequency times per period" and period to "The duration to which the frequency applies. I.e. Event occurs frequency times per period".
