FHIR-7901: Audit Claim missing teeth removal

Issue request:
FHIR-7901 was a Published FHIR-core Claim change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking why dental-specific elements such as Claim.missingTeeth were not extensions or otherwise outside the core Claim resource. The accepted resolution was to remove the MissingTeeth backbone because missing teeth can be represented in Claim.information.

Initial application:
The application appears in commit aaa1e5693e365bca976f7aa2115c85a0f2293a38, "Tracker #7901, 11373, 11395, 12356", which added Claim.information.reason and commented the Claim.missingTeeth rows out of the Claim spreadsheet source. No commit message used the later Jira key spelling, but the historical tracker number and patch content match the Jira request.

Additional context:
Claim is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. Later financial-management work renamed Claim.information to Claim.supportingInfo for Request-pattern consistency, so the current representation uses Claim.supportingInfo rather than the historical Claim.information name. The current Claim StructureDefinition still supports missing-tooth reasons through Claim.supportingInfo.reason, and an oral Claim example represents a missing tooth through Claim.supportingInfo category/code/timing/reason. Related Jira issues FHIR-11373, FHIR-11395, and FHIR-12356 are already represented on the combined branch, so this seed audit does not duplicate them.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Claim source no longer defines Claim.missingTeeth, and the remaining missing-tooth terminology/examples are the intended Claim.supportingInfo representation rather than a stale Claim.missingTeeth backbone.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-7901 unless the Financial Management work group later decides to remove or remodel the current supportingInfo-based missing-tooth example and binding.
