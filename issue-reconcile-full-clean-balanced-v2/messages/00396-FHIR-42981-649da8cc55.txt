FHIR-42981: Audit Condition.evidence clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-42981 was an Applied/Persuasive Patient Care change request to clarify Condition.evidence. The approved resolution changed the short description to "Supporting evidence for the condition", changed the definition to "Supporting evidence / manifestations that are the basis for determining the Condition", and added guidance not to use Condition.evidence for causality, pointing instead to AdverseEvent.suspectEntity.causality, pre-coordinated Condition.code values, or the condition-dueTo extension.
