The accepted resolution was to make .indexed 0..1 and rename it to "date"; Jira comments clarified that the element records when the DocumentReference, not the underlying document, was created.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in git commit 94e532a611046fcdc40df2d7d28457eeb7dad3a6, whose San Diego WGM block-vote message explicitly lists GF#12653. In the then-authoritative DocumentReference spreadsheet and examples, that commit changed DocumentReference.indexed 1..1 to DocumentReference.date 0..1, retained indexed as an alias, and updated example tags from indexed to date.

Additional context:
DocumentReference is active in source/fhir.ini.
