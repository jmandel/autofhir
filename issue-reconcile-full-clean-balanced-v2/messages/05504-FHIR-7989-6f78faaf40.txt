FHIR-7989: Audit Encounter admitted status guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7989 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 Patient Administration issue asking which Encounter.status corresponds to an admitted patient. The accepted resolution said admission is not determined by one fixed status; local business rules may treat arrived or in-progress as admission start, onleave may or may not remain part of admission, location is likely present, and at minimum an in-progress encounter means the patient is admitted. The requested source action was to add guidance to the Encounter Status Management section.
