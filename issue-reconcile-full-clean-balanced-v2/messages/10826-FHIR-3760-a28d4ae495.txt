FHIR-3760: Audit OperationOutcome boundary guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-3760 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core change request for DSTU1 asking to clarify when OperationOutcome is appropriate instead of an application-oriented resource such as Contraindication. The accepted resolution was to copy the Contraindication boundary language into OperationOutcome and remove the word "specifically" from the usage introduction.

Initial application:
Git-svn commit f0f6a05d8a7599d9a4be1f64d962e8f24ee884c3 maps to SVN revision 5796 cited in Jira and explicitly applied #3760. It removed "Specifically" from the OperationOutcome usage list and added a Boundaries and Relationships section saying clinical/workflow issues should use ContraIndication or other resources, that OperationOutcome is not designed to be persisted or referenced from workflow, and that both resources can appear together when OperationOutcome reports rejection and Contraindication carries the clinical details.

Additional context:
OperationOutcome is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and fhir.ini records that Contraindication became DetectedIssue. Later commit cc54b56e72272bed97b5c33459fc90bc2bfe52d8 updated the FHIR-3760 boundary text from Contraindication to DetectedIssue, commit db084c99bb4f40bbd48893ec3b4fae6d64298362 refined adjacent OperationOutcome scope text without reversing the boundary, and the existing FHIR-5872 reconciliation commit linked the same paragraph to the standard operationoutcome-detectedIssue extension. FHIR-3663 is the broader related OperationOutcome role issue referenced by Jira, but its snapshot says it was closed out in other tasks and does not require a separate decision here.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change.
