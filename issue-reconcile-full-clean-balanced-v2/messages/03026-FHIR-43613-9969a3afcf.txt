Initial application:
No prior Issue-Reconcile-Key commit for FHIR-43613 was present on the combined branch. PR #3967 also merged a large set of later R6 subscription clarifications, and many of those individual core trackers already have their own reconcile commits on the combined branch.

Additional context:
The original 2024 Subscriptions Backport block vote was against the Backport IG and included several Resolved - change required IG issues. Those IG decisions were not separate FHIR-core Applied issues to decide here; the relevant core import decisions were later recorded as Applied FHIR-core tickets, especially FHIR-46493, FHIR-46494, and FHIR-46495. Current source/fhir.ini actively includes Subscription, SubscriptionStatus, and SubscriptionTopic, and source/hierarchy.xml includes subscriptions.html, so the inspected subscription source is in build scope.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
