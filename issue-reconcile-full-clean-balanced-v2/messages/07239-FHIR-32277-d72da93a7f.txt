FHIR-32277: Audit Device boundary updates

Issue request:
FHIR-32277 was a Published, Persuasive OO technical correction for R5 asking that the Device Boundaries and Relationships section be updated before normative consideration. The specific request was to add DeviceUsage and DeviceRequest with appropriate definitions and to clarify the boundary between Device and Medication when medications are packaged or administered with devices.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by commit 3c23148747a7d0fead21ff9afaab2fc3bb65eae9 ("apply #20574"), which explicitly listed FHIR-32277 and replaced the Device introduction/boundaries text in source/device/device-introduction.xml. That application added DeviceRequest and DeviceUsage to the device-related resources list and rewrote the medication boundary paragraphs.

Additional context:
The Jira resolution for FHIR-32277 points to the same 2022-06-13 OO motion used for FHIR-20574, FHIR-32382, FHIR-32276, FHIR-32408, and FHIR-32681: accept the Device Scope-Boundary-Relationships document as published and disposition those trackers accordingly. The 2022-06-13 OO minutes record the motion and 10-0-0 vote. Device is active in source/fhir.ini, and later Device source changes, including existing AutoFHIR commits for FHIR-32382, FHIR-32408, and FHIR-32681 on the combined branch, refine the same section without removing the FHIR-32277 substance.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active Device source already includes DeviceRequest and DeviceUsage with explanatory text in the Boundaries and Relationships section, and the medication boundary now states that device-packaged medication workflows typically focus on medication while Medication must not represent implanted devices when the actual device needs tracking.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No additional core source change is needed for FHIR-32277 unless OO later decides to replace the remaining Device page boundary prose wholesale; such future work should be tracked by a newer Jira rather than by reopening this R5-applied correction.
