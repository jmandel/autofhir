FHIR-36784: Audit ImagingSelection performer wording

Issue request:
FHIR-36784 was a Published/Persuasive R5 Imaging Integration change request asking to replace ImagingSelection.performer short and definition with "Selector of the instances (human or machine)" and "Selector of the instances - human or machine." The request was non-substantive clarification for the ImagingSelection detailed descriptions.

Initial application:
The request was applied before this reconciliation. Jira cites merge commit c138fb1b9ed930251866ab1da3be7d524b3fb1d4 for PR #2000, and its side-branch commit e89adf0784a54c08ffd911a3263cf2c60ef430cb changed source/imagingselection/structuredefinition-ImagingSelection.xml from the older "Author" wording to the requested "Selector of the instances" wording.

Additional context:
ImagingSelection is active in the current build through source/fhir.ini. The R5-applied wording was later modified by FHIR-44974 to pluralize 0..* element descriptions, then superseded by the Applied FHIR-51645 cleanup aligning ImagingSelection definitions with current ImagingStudy text. FHIR-50542 later asked whether the performer/actor descriptions were unclear and was resolved No Change because the wording would be handled through FHIR-51645. Current source now says ImagingSelection.performer is "Who performed imaging selection and what they did" and defines it as who/what performed the imaging selection and how they were involved; performer.actor is required and separately says who performed the selection.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. Restoring the exact R5 "Selector of the instances" text would undo later WG-approved R6 clarification work and reintroduce the ambiguity called out by FHIR-50542.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. The current source should remain aligned with FHIR-51645/FHIR-50542 rather than being reverted to the older FHIR-36784 wording.
