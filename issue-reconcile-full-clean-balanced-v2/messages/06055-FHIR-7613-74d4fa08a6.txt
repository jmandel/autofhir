FHIR-7613: Audit Encounter diagnosis handling

Issue request:
FHIR-7613 is a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration issue from the DSTU1 ballot asking how Encounter diagnoses support primary/secondary sequence and facility versus professional billing distinctions. The accepted resolution kept reason/indication as separate multi-cardinality properties after a technical correction, used a positiveInt primaryDiagnosis standard extension to express sequence, and requested implementer feedback on whether a richer approach was needed.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git commit 0983925cea, whose subject explicitly lists tracker 7613 and says it handled Encounter diagnosis and the primaryDiagnosis standard extension. That commit added the encounter-primaryDiagnosis extension for Encounter.reason, Encounter.indication, and Encounter.hospitalization.admittingDiagnosis, and added comments explaining that primary diagnosis order used a sequence value with 1 as primary. Later source history moved from the DSTU1 reason/indication-plus-extension shape to Encounter.diagnosis with role/rank and then to the current Encounter.reason, Encounter.diagnosis, and Account.diagnosis model.

Additional context:
The original extension path is intentionally no longer current: FHIR-25215 removed stale Encounter.reason references to the deleted primaryDiagnosis extension. FHIR-16148 redesigned Encounter.reason and Encounter.diagnosis so reasons are expected problems and diagnoses are conditions addressed during the encounter. FHIR-19285 moved ranked billing diagnoses and procedures from Encounter to Account, where Account.diagnosis.sequence now supplies the primary/secondary/tertiary ordering needed for billing. FHIR-38807 and the combined-branch FHIR-19285 reconciliation have already corrected remaining current-source wording in this same design family. Encounter, Account, and Condition are active build resources in source/fhir.ini, and current source contains no primaryDiagnosis extension in core or the extension-pack checkout.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit makes no source change. The current specification already gives implementers the necessary guidance: Encounter.reason and Encounter.diagnosis distinguish expected reasons from addressed conditions, Encounter.diagnosis.use classifies encounter diagnoses, Account.diagnosis.sequence ranks billing-related diagnoses, and Condition notes explicitly direct primary/secondary/tertiary billing rank to Account.diagnosis.sequence.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not reintroduce the retired primaryDiagnosis extension or Encounter.diagnosis.rank for this DSTU1 issue; any future non-billing diagnosis ranking requirement should be raised as a new work-group decision against the current Encounter/Account model.
