FHIR-12914: Audit CompartmentDefinition title and jurisdiction

Issue request:
FHIR-12914 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I change request from DSTU2 asking to remove CompartmentDefinition.jurisdiction and CompartmentDefinition.title because compartment definitions are HL7-defined and tied to the resource they describe.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in SVN revision 13984, represented in git as db6d6b7c65, which removed CompartmentDefinition.title and CompartmentDefinition.jurisdiction rows from the historical CompartmentDefinition spreadsheet source.

Additional context:
The current CompartmentDefinition resource is active in source/fhir.ini and now derives from CanonicalResource. Current source still has no CompartmentDefinition.jurisdiction element. A later final FHIR-I decision, FHIR-37455, explicitly added title back to CompartmentDefinition and GraphDefinition for R5 because title was considered useful on these CanonicalResource resources. FHIR-I minutes and a methodology Zulip thread support the same split: title is acceptable for CompartmentDefinition, while jurisdiction remains useless/fixed.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source reflects the surviving part of FHIR-12914 by omitting CompartmentDefinition.jurisdiction, and the apparent title mismatch is superseded by the later Published/Persuasive FHIR-37455 decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Do not remove CompartmentDefinition.title unless FHIR-I revisits FHIR-37455 or issues a newer decision; no core-source follow-up is needed for FHIR-12914.
