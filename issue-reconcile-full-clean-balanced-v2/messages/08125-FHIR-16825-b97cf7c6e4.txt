FHIR-16825: Audit Observation grouping guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-16825 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for STU3 by OO. It reported that Observation.component could not support complex, inter-related findings and asked whether Observation.hasMember should be used; the approved resolution says components do not nest recursively, hasMember is used for such complex findings, and the panel/battery wording should make clear those are examples rather than the only hasMember use.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in git-svn commit 69761b715e5c64e5f02da9d3cab64b40f1b00d1b, whose subject was "GF#16495, GF#16733, GF#16825". That commit changed the Observation grouping note from grouping related observations "as" a panel or battery to grouping related observations "such as for" a panel or battery. No prior commit on the combined branch carried Issue-Reconcile-Key: FHIR-16825.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists Observation as an active resource and normative from 4.0.0, so the Observation source inspected here is current-build source. The current hasMember definition says a group Observation includes targets such as a battery, panel of tests, or set of vital-sign measurements. The current Observation grouping notes distinguish component results that cannot reasonably be interpreted outside the containing Observation from hasMember/derivedFrom relationships for supporting results that can stand alone, and they explicitly say hasMember permits nested grouping. Later FHIR-51724 reached a Resolved - No Change decision because the current component/hasMember text already covers the requested distinction. FHIR-54887 is a later Triaged R6 grouping/organizer proposal and is not final enough to supersede this Published STU3 issue.
