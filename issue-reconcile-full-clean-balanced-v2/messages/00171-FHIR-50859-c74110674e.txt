FHIR-50859: Audit Imaging bodySite laterality application

Issue request:
FHIR-50859 is Applied/Persuasive with Modification for R6 ImagingStudy and ImagingSelection. The initial request was to make ImagingStudy.series.bodySite and ImagingSelection.bodySite BodyStructure-only references and remove laterality ambiguity, but the final Jira comments and referenced resolution page narrowed this to keeping bodySite as CodeableReference, removing ImagingStudy.series.laterality, and adding guidance on representing laterality through bodySite codes or BodyStructure.

Initial application:
The change was applied before this reconciliation through the imaging October 2025 work. Commit c8ab401837 removed the ImagingStudy XR example laterality value, commit db64fafb15 removed ImagingStudy.series.laterality from the authoritative StructureDefinition, updated ImagingStudy and ImagingSelection bodySite definitions and DICOM mappings, and added Body Site and Laterality notes, and PRs HL7/fhir#3735 and HL7/fhir#3729 merged that work into the imaging branch and then master.

Additional context:
The July 2025 Zulip announcement shows the original BodyStructure-only proposal and implementer concern about search and patient-specific BodyStructure use. OO WGM minutes and the Jira comments record the later conclusion to keep CodeableReference, remove laterality, and provide guidance. FHIR-46918 is a supporting no-change decision that explicitly points to FHIR-50859 for not adding ImagingSelection.laterality. FHIR-54747 is a later Submitted request for more detailed guidance, so it is not a final decision for this reconciliation.
