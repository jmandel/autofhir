FHIR-18994: Audit Subscription security guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-18994 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to expand Subscription security considerations beyond the general safety text, especially around authorization/user privileges, payload minimization, and subscription criteria that can reveal sensitive information through the notification alone. The Jira resolution says to handle it as part of FHIR-18993.

Initial application:
The historical application is commit 3a6fa70bed0cb56521fe1f91749e4e4e95f39c55, "FHIR-18994, FHIR-18993, FHIR-19621 - Additional security considerations.", which updated source/subscriptions.html. Later Subscription framework edits, including fc659be95ed, reformatted/refined the section but preserved the security guidance in the current build.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists subscription=Subscription and the current resource-specific Safety and Security section in source/subscription/subscription-notes.xml links to the framework Safety and Security section at source/subscriptions.html#safety. FHIR-18993 is the same change family for subscriber-side security; duplicate FHIR-19621 raised REST-hook HTTPS/TLS concerns and links the Zulip REST Hook payload discussion. No Confluence references for FHIR-18994, FHIR-18993, or FHIR-19621 were found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current framework safety section tells applications to evaluate SubscriptionTopic and filters when authorizing creation, re-check authorization when sending notifications, limit payload types to reduce disclosure, avoid generating notifications that would reveal confidential results, protect sensitive Subscription data such as privileged endpoints, and validate incoming notifications/guard against denial-of-service. This covers the concern that a specific subscription criterion can leak PHI even without a payload.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as the reconciliation record. Further source edits would be redundant unless the work group wants a new, more prescriptive policy beyond the published FHIR-18994/FHIR-18993 resolution.
