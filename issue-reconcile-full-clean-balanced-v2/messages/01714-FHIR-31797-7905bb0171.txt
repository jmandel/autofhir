FHIR-31797: Audit containedItemQuantity guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-31797 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for R5 and requested clearer PackagedProductDefinition.containedItemQuantity guidance because a repeatable list of quantities without item context was ambiguous. The accepted resolution was to add usage-note text and clarify that multiple repetitions must use disjoint units.

Initial application:
The request was applied before this reconciliation in source commits a244714665 and 3c738e3beb, then refined and synced by 091c15f2de and c582702619. The current active StructureDefinition keeps the requested complete-count wording, the 21+7 tablet aggregation example, the repeat-by-item-type rule, the different-units requirement, and the warning that repeats are not alternative pack sizes.
