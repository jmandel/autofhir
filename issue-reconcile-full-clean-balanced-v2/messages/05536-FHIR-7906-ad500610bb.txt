FHIR-7906: Audit Encounter diagnosis relationship

Issue request:
FHIR-7906 is a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration DSTU1 issue asking Encounter to represent which conditions or diagnoses were related to an encounter, with a role code for concepts such as principal, working, preliminary, confirmed, and final diagnoses. Its resolution constrained the then-current Encounter.indication references to Condition or Procedure, made the reason/indication concepts multi-cardinality, and proposed a standard positiveInt extension for diagnosis sequence.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in git commit 0983925cea, whose message explicitly lists tracker 7906 and the Encounter diagnosis/primaryDiagnosis change family. That commit constrained Encounter.indication from Reference(Any) to Reference(Condition|Procedure), added aliases and primaryDiagnosis comments to reason/indication, and introduced the encounter-primaryDiagnosis extension in examples. Later history changed the representation: 5ba2142889 applied FHIR-10544 by replacing Encounter.indication and the Encounter-specific diagnosis extensions with Encounter.diagnosis.condition, Encounter.diagnosis.role, and Encounter.diagnosis.rank; c1e9ac08c9 removed the redundant primaryDiagnosis extension after FHIR-14758; and FHIR-25215 later removed remaining stale primaryDiagnosis prose.

Additional context:
Subsequent final PA decisions intentionally superseded the 2015 representation while preserving the substantive capability. FHIR-16148 redesigned Encounter.reason and Encounter.diagnosis for R5: reason is for expected medical reasons and diagnosis is for medical conditions addressed during the encounter. FHIR-38784 made reason.use and diagnosis.use repeat, supporting multiple classification axes. FHIR-19285 moved billing-oriented diagnosis ranking to Account.diagnosis.sequence. FHIR-38807 is already represented on the combined branch and corrected the remaining Encounter reason/diagnosis wording to match the R5 redesign. Encounter remains an active build resource in source/fhir.ini.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change because the current active source already satisfies the seed issue at the current specification level. Encounter.diagnosis.condition represents the condition addressed during the encounter, Encounter.diagnosis.use represents the role/use of that diagnosis, Encounter.reason.value still covers admitting/reason diagnoses where appropriate, and Account.diagnosis.sequence carries the numeric billing rank that replaced the old primaryDiagnosis extension. Re-introducing the 2015 extension or re-constraining reason/indication literally to the old DSTU1 shape would contradict later applied PA decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Future work should not re-add encounter-primaryDiagnosis or Encounter.diagnosis.rank to Encounter without a new PA decision; if implementers need non-billing primary/secondary diagnosis ranking in the current model, that should be raised as a new issue against Encounter or Account rather than treated as an unapplied FHIR-7906 gap.
