Issue request:
FHIR-7761 was Published/Persuasive for DSTU1 ConceptMap/$translate. It asked to allow multiple translation outcomes with distinct codings and cited placing equivalence/message details under a new outcome-style structure, with final resolution "see 7760, 7457. Endorse as edited."

Initial application:
No discovered source commit explicitly names FHIR-7761, but the Jira resolution points to the same DSTU1 edit family as FHIR-7457 and FHIR-7760. Commit b1e826a4b8 (svn revision 5518) implemented the multiple-return change by replacing the former single translate.outcome with repeatable translate.match and nested equivalence/concept/product parts; FHIR-7760 supplied the related dependency/product shape, and FHIR-9131 later corrected the response example from outcome to match.

Additional context:
Current ConceptMap is an active resource in source/fhir.ini and its operation list references OperationDefinition/ConceptMap-translate. Later R5 vocabulary decisions, especially FHIR-22632/FHIR-22636, renamed the output relationship part from equivalence to relationship to align with the ConceptMap resource; that later rename supersedes the DSTU1 wording without changing the multiple-outcome semantics.
