FHIR-5896: Audit superseded medication resource naming

Issue request:
FHIR-5896 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request from the DSTU1 ballot noting that MedicationPrescription covered more than prescriptions and orders, including pharmacy-created encoded orders, MAR entries, decision-support recommendations, and care plans. The issue suggested MedicationRequest with an alias of Prescription; its final resolution changed the name to MedicationOrder, updated the definition, and added Prescription as an alias.

Initial application:
The request was applied historically by commit 9d6493a912, whose message explicitly cites #7224 and #5896 and mechanically renamed MedicationPrescription to MedicationOrder in source/fhir.ini and related medication source files. The Jira comment also states FHIR-5896 was a partial duplicate of FHIR-7224 and that the unique alias work was done.

Additional context:
Later Published issue FHIR-10456 intentionally changed MedicationOrder to MedicationRequest for workflow alignment and broader request semantics. Current source follows that later decision: source/fhir.ini has MedicationRequest as the active resource and maps MedicationOrder to MedicationRequest, while source/medicationrequest/structuredefinition-MedicationRequest.xml names the resource MedicationRequest, explains why it is not MedicationPrescription or MedicationOrder, and includes Prescription and Order aliases.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. The current MedicationRequest source already satisfies FHIR-5896's underlying concern that MedicationPrescription was too narrow, and reverting to MedicationOrder would contradict the later final FHIR-10456 decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Treat FHIR-5896 as historically applied through the MedicationOrder rename and alias work, then superseded by the current MedicationRequest naming; no current core source edit is needed.
