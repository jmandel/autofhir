FHIR-50194: Audit vital signs applicability clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-50194 was an Applied OO R6 ballot change request, resolved Not Persuasive with Modification. It asked that the Observation vital signs profile conformance requirement be made crystal clear for cases such as de-identified vital signs data and average blood pressure, and the final resolution specifically required the vital signs introduction to state that de-identified data or measurements directly from a device that are not yet associated with a patient are not required to conform to the Vital Signs profiles.

Initial application:
The issue was not applied by a separate FHIR-50194 commit. Jira comments say the related PR for FHIR-50194 would not be merged and that the content would be included as part of FHIR-41426. Git history confirms commit 6cb9883195, "FHIR-41426 - Updates to clarify the Vital Signs profile introduction text (includes the updates from dbarwin-50194)," added the current vital signs introduction paragraphs, including the de-identified/device exception.

Additional context:
The Observation resource is active in source/fhir.ini, and source/observation/observation-vitalsigns-profile-introduction.xml is the active narrative source for observation-vitalsigns.html. The current source also clarifies that the profiles apply to the vital signs observations listed in the table and their bound value sets, and a later section states that calculated observations such as averages based on multiple point-in-time vital signs cannot be based on the Vital Signs profile and must use separate profiles/codes. Related Jira and meeting evidence for FHIR-43960 records the work group decision that average blood pressure is not within the scope of the point-in-time vital signs profile, while FHIR-46061 and FHIR-50075 remain background/no-change decisions rather than separate source edits for this issue.

This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active current source already includes the final FHIR-50194 clarification in the requested location and is consistent with the same-family FHIR-41426 application and the related average-blood-pressure scope decision.

Keep this audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-50194 unless the work group later chooses to reopen the broader FHIR-41426/Observation core table wording question or to create a new profile family for calculated vital-sign-related observations.
