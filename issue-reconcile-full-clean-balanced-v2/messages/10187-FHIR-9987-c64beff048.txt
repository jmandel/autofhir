FHIR-9987 was a Published FHIR-core StructureDefinition change request, resolved Persuasive, asking to clarify how StructureDefinition.abstract should be interpreted for logical models because the earlier definition was written before logical models existed.

Initial application:
Jira records svn revision 10363 as the application. In the current git-visible source history, the 2021 source rework imported StructureDefinition.abstract with a logical-model sentence saying the implication of abstract rests with the author depending on logical-model use.

Additional context:

The current source also preserves the FHIR-3941 distinction that abstract constraint structures express design intent only. The original Zulip discussion linked from FHIR-9987 concluded that logical models can be abstract or concrete at the model author's discretion and that the pre-logical-model wording needed improvement.
