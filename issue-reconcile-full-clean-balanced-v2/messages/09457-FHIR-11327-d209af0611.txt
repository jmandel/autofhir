FHIR-11327: Use patient portal in architecture overview

Issue request:
FHIR-11327 was Published/Persuasive for the DSTU2 FHIR-core overview. It asked that the architect overview stop describing a patient accessing their record in an EMR as a personal health record (PHR), because a PHR is generally patient-owned/controlled rather than an EMR-backed patient view; the resolution agreed and said Patient portal could be used instead.

Initial application:
The issue was initially applied in svn revision 10270, represented in git as c450e7fbd1179e0f29a46dbe260300aebe7db5d6, with commit subject "#11327 - fix PHR -> portal". That commit changed "personal health record (PHR)" to "personal health record (portal)", but left the misleading "personal health record" wording in the current overview source.

Additional context:
source/hierarchy.xml actively lists source/overview-arch.html as the Architect's Introduction page, so this narrative source is in the current build scope. Jira neighborhood and community searches found no later final FHIR-core decision, Zulip thread, or Confluence minutes that changed the Published/Persuasive intent for this specific overview sentence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit updates source/overview-arch.html so the example describes a patient accessing a patient portal enabled by an underlying EMR, removing the remaining personal-health-record terminology from the sentence while preserving the existing example and EMR/FHIR-server framing.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as-is. It completes the original non-substantive terminology correction in the active source page without broadening the issue to unrelated current uses of PHR where the term may be intentional.
