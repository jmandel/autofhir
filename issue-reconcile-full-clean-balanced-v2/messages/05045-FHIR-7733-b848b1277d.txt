FHIR-7733: Audit MedicationAdministration performer actor scope

Issue request:
FHIR-7733 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Pharmacy change request from the DSTU1 ballot asking how to document a patient or someone other than a practitioner administering medication. The approved resolution was to expand what the former MedicationAdministration.practitioner element could reference, including patient/agent-style performers, and update the definition to discuss the performer of the administration.

Initial application:
The Jira-tagged source commit b11981d13acc78914bac8350efcaa2654cda7848 applied the immediate DSTU1 change by changing MedicationAdministration.practitioner from Reference(Practitioner) to Reference(Practitioner | Patient | RelatedPerson) in the then-authoritative spreadsheet source. It did not separately rewrite the definition in that spreadsheet. Later source history moved the old practitioner element into the Event-pattern performer backbone; commit 7003eba639b96a6cb1198300f293effe3f4bda1d introduced MedicationAdministration.performer.actor as Reference(Practitioner | Patient | RelatedPerson | Device), and the current XML source preserves and extends that model.

Additional context:
MedicationAdministration is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and source/fhir.ini still records the historical rename from MedicationAdministration.practitioner to MedicationAdministration.performer. Current source/medicationadministration/structuredefinition-MedicationAdministration.xml defines MedicationAdministration.performer as who or what performed the medication administration, and performer.actor now accepts Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Patient, RelatedPerson, Device, and Group through a CodeableReference. The already-published FHIR-37631 reconciliation clarified the distinct device-as-performer guidance and does not conflict with this conclusion. Direct Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-7733 discussion or work-group record that changes the Jira/source interpretation.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit intentionally makes no source change. The active authoritative source already allows patient and non-practitioner performers and describes the performer/actor role clearly enough for current implementers; adding extra local prose would be redundant and risk drifting from the generated element definition.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up core source work is needed for FHIR-7733 unless the Pharmacy work group wants a separate modern policy change to further broaden or narrow MedicationAdministration.performer.actor.
