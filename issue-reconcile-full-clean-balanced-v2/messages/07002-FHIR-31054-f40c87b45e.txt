FHIR-31054: Confirm sliced cardinality clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-31054 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for the FHIR-core profiling page. It asked to clarify the Slice Cardinality rules by rewording the per-slice maximum rule, adding that the sum of slice minimum cardinalities SHOULD be less than or equal to m, and preserving that slice minimums remain separate constraints that validators must evaluate.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by PR #1274 in commit 9029921047, which changed source/profiling.html. That commit replaced the ambiguous maximum-cardinality bullet with "The maximum cardinality on each slice cannot exceed n" and added the slice-minimum SHOULD sentence requested by the final resolution.

Additional context:
source/profiling.html is build-scoped through the Conformance/Profiling FHIR hierarchy and publish.ini page list. FHIR-I WGM minutes recorded the intended modification as SHOULD rather than SHALL and explained that the sum of slice minimums and the base element minimum are separate constraints. The originating Zulip thread led directly to FHIR-31054; later Zulip discussion in 2023 treated the profiling rule as already covering the behavior and recorded that publisher/snapshot-generator hint behavior had been updated. Jira phrase searches found no later core tracker that changes this profiling rule.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current source and current build output already contain the applied R5 clarification: the per-slice maximum cannot exceed n, the sum of minimum cardinalities must be less than or equal to n, and the sum of slice minimums SHOULD be less than or equal to m while remaining independently enforceable.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. A new core source edit would only restate the already-applied rule; any further tooling behavior belongs to validator/publisher maintenance rather than this FHIR-core profiling page.
