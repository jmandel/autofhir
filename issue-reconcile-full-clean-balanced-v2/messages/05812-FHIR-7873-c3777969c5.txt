FHIR-7873: Audit Period future-end clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-7873 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for DSTU1. It asked whether a Period with a missing or future end could still represent an ongoing interval, and whether Encounter.period could carry estimated arrival or discharge dates. The final resolution was to clarify that Period dates can be in the future, without using the word "ongoing" as the datatype rule.

Initial application:
The Jira comment cites svn revision 6033, and git-svn commit 52e42a45025236aaa9bd539343f2b1ab138c9f8b is the matching application. That commit updated the Period datatype narrative and spreadsheet source to add the future/expected end-date clarification. Later Period datatype edits retained and refined that rule.

Additional context:
FHIR-38819 later corrected the adjacent Period.end missing-meaning text so a missing end means no end date/time was known or planned when the instance was created, while preserving explicit future-end guidance. For Encounter, later PA decisions FHIR-22766 and FHIR-32351 superseded the older suggestion that Encounter.period itself carry forecast discharge timing: current source uses Encounter.actualPeriod for actual timing and Encounter.plannedStartDate/plannedEndDate for planned timing. A Zulip thread that explicitly cites GF#7873 shows implementers wanted estimated dates separated from actual dates, matching the later source direction. No direct Confluence reference for FHIR-7873 or conflicting community decision was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Period datatype inputs already say that a known or planned future end may be represented explicitly, and current Encounter source separates actual period timing from planned start/end timing. Restoring the old Encounter.period forecast interpretation would conflict with the later published Encounter decisions.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current specification correctly incorporates the seed's datatype clarification and the later Encounter-specific refinement; future changes to Encounter forecast or actual timing should be handled through the PA-owned Encounter issue family rather than by re-editing FHIR-7873.
