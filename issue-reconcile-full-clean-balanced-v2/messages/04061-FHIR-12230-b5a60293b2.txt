FHIR-12230: Audit Coverage policyHolder RelatedPerson support

Issue request:
FHIR-12230 was Published/Persuasive for DSTU2 Coverage and asked to add RelatedPerson as a choice for PlanHolder/planholderReference because a person can be the insurance plan holder without being a patient at the treating practice.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in git commit 43806174288e821cb06173a72cde96317186412b, a 2016 Coverage commit whose subject explicitly listed tracker 12230. That commit changed the old Coverage.planholder row to Coverage.policyHolder with Reference(Patient|RelatedPerson|Organization) and added Coverage.subscriber with Reference(Patient|RelatedPerson). Later Coverage source refactors retained the RelatedPerson target profiles in the current StructureDefinition source.

Additional context:
Related Coverage decisions FHIR-12067 and FHIR-7772 explain the same redesign: PlanHolder was split/renamed into subscriber and policyHolder concepts, Person was rejected as a direct Coverage reference, and RelatedPerson was added for non-patient personal roles. A Zulip implementers thread records the same use case and discussion; Confluence and exact-key Zulip searches found no conflicting later community evidence. Coverage is active in source/fhir.ini, so source/coverage/structuredefinition-Coverage.xml is current build input.
