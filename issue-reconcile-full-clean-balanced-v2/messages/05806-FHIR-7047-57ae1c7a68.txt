FHIR-7047: Audit AllergyIntolerance/RiskAssessment relationship

Issue request:
FHIR-7047 was Published/Persuasive for the Patient Care work group and asked the specification to explain the relationship between AllergyIntolerance and RiskAssessment. The adopted resolution was to update both resources to say that AllergyIntolerance is a specific risk, namely propensity to reaction to a substance, while RiskAssessment covers general risks to a subject that are not generally based on a reaction.

Initial application:
The AllergyIntolerance side was initially applied by a30496dcda, which added an "AllergyIntolerance and RiskAssessment" subsection to source/allergyintolerance/allergyintolerance-introduction.xml. The RiskAssessment side was then applied by 3ea42ecce8, which added the matching subsection to source/riskassessment/riskassessment-introduction.xml. Later source refactors preserved the relationship text in the current resource introduction pages.

Additional context:
Both AllergyIntolerance and RiskAssessment remain active resources in source/fhir.ini, so their introduction pages are in current build scope. Jira neighborhood searching found no later issue competing with FHIR-7047 for this relationship text, and Zulip/Confluence searches found no community discussion or meeting evidence that changes the published Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current AllergyIntolerance introduction states that AllergyIntolerance describes a specific type of risk, propensity to reaction to a substance/product, while RiskAssessment describes general risks to a subject. The current RiskAssessment introduction carries the parallel wording. That satisfies the published FHIR-7047 request without adding redundant prose.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-7047 unless a future Patient Care decision revisits the resource boundary.
