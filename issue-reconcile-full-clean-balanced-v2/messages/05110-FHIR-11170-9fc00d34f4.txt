FHIR-11170: Audit CarePlan risk addresses guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-11170 was a Published Patient Care change request with a Not Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked to include risk in the CarePlan structure, and the final decision was to update the usage notes for CarePlan.addresses so the element can identify risks addressed by the plan as well as active conditions, with Condition able to represent examples such as "at risk for hypertension" or "fall risk".

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git commit c124072d7b, which updated the old CarePlan spreadsheet requirements for CarePlan.addresses with the requested risk/active-condition wording and marked the row GF#11170. Later source migrations moved that content into the current StructureDefinition XML, and FHIR-20168 refactored CarePlan.addresses into code/reference guidance while preserving the risk semantics.

Additional context:
CarePlan is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and source/careplan/structuredefinition-CarePlan.xml is the authoritative current element source. Current CarePlan.addresses says coded concerns can include condition, problem, diagnosis, or risk, and its requirements say the element can identify risks addressed by the plan as well as concerns. Later CarePlan.addresses work, including the recent FHIR-13743 allergy/intolerance restoration and the FHIR-32512 target-profile expansion, does not supersede the FHIR-11170 risk guidance. Quoted FHIR-11170 Zulip and Confluence searches found no conflicting community record; broader CarePlan risk hits concerned a separate possible RiskAssessment linkage question.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current active source already conveys the accepted FHIR-11170 outcome, and adding a separate risk element or duplicating the older examples would go beyond the final resolution and create maintenance drift.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Any future expansion of CarePlan.addresses to directly reference RiskAssessment or other risk-specific artifacts should be handled as a separate Patient Care decision, not as a FHIR-11170 reconciliation.
