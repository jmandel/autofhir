Issue request:
FHIR-18514 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core OperationOutcome change request raised for STU3. It asked to remove wording that suggested an OperationOutcome could be sent as part of a request message, because that clinical or workflow issue context should instead be handled by DetectedIssue or another resource.

Initial application:

Later source rework in 33b409a4b6d544598262f2f074ebb4b6dc466157 preserved the remaining response-message wording in structuredefinition-OperationOutcome.xml.

Additional context:
OperationOutcome is an active current build resource in source/fhir.ini and also listed as a special resource. Current narrative says OperationOutcome can be part of a message response, and the boundaries text says clinical or workflow issues associated with proposed or ongoing actions are handled with DetectedIssue or other resources. The active StructureDefinition comment says OperationOutcome may be part of the response message returned from a request message; this is response guidance, not the removed request-message suggestion. Jira searches found no neighboring final issue changing that interpretation, and Zulip/Confluence searches found no directly relevant conflicting community decision.
