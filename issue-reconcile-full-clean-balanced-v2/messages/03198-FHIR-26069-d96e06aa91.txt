The final Jira resolution says this ticket is a duplicate of FHIR-26550, which had already been applied, so no technical change was needed from FHIR-26069 itself.

Later, b89a83b4b7 updated the active ValueSet.status comment to link to the renamed workflowStatusDescription extension. These commits show the requested rename was applied before FHIR-26069 was closed as a duplicate.

The current FHIR Extension Pack checkout contains input/definitions/ValueSet/StructureDefinition-valueset-workflowStatusDescription.xml with the renamed canonical, and also retains the retired valueset-workflowStatus artifact with a replacement note.
