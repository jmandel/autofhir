FHIR-20348 was Published/Persuasive for R5 and asked to drop the DocumentReference.context backbone element, move its child elements to the resource root, and allow the resulting breaking change plus workflow-oriented reordering.

Initial application:

Later source refactoring converted the spreadsheet into the current StructureDefinition source. Commit d41daf2 for FHIR-29801 then renamed the promoted encounter element/search parameter from DocumentReference.encounter to the current top-level DocumentReference.context while adding Appointment and EpisodeOfCare support.

Additional context:
DocumentReference is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. The former sourcePatientInfo child was later removed from the resource by FHIR-32582 and is represented as the documentreference-sourcepatient extension; that related issue is already represented on the combined branch. FHIR-54170 later clarified the top-level DocumentReference.context wording without reintroducing a backbone.
