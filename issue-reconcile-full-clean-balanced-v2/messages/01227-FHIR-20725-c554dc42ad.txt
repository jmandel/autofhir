FHIR-20725: Audit Measure reporting frequency application

Issue request:
FHIR-20725 was an Applied/Persuasive CQI change request asking for a first-class Measure element that indicates how frequently measure results are reported or released. The approved resolution specified Measure.reportingFrequency as Quantity 0..1, with examples such as 1 '/a' for yearly and 4 '/a' for quarterly, and later Jira comments requested guidance that reporting frequency is often determined by a quality program or initiative rather than the Measure itself.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by HL7/fhir PR #3337 and commit 8da66e5fc984715edb8bbe4f8b30b7b1d09617af, which added Measure.reportingFrequency to source/measure/structuredefinition-Measure.xml as a Quantity 0..1 element. The commit subject says FHIR-29725, but the Jira FHIR-20725 comments cite that commit and PR, and the patch content is the requested reportingFrequency element.
