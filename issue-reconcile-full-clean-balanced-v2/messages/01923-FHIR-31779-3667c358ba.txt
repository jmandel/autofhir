FHIR-31779: Audit administrable/manufactured product wording

Issue request:
FHIR-31779 was a Published FHIR-core BRR change request, applied for R5 and resolved Persuasive with Modification. It objected to the MedicinalProductPharmaceutical wording "Note that often the administrable product is the same as the manufactured item" because administrable and manufactured products are conceptually different resources even when no preparation or transformation is needed.

Initial application:
The wording appears to have been applied in R5 source history when fbfbf287b1ed7a1add0a216ec02cd2d6daab062c changed the AdministrableProductDefinition introduction from "same as" to "needs no processinng or transformation" in a commit subject listing FHIR#31779. Commit 3ea769b93017e9f6c4500fab63522d37ee6446ba then corrected the typo to "processing", matching the 2022 Jira comment that the new text had been applied. The old MedicinalProductPharmaceutical artifact maps to the current AdministrableProductDefinition resource, which is active in source/fhir.ini.

Additional context:
Later FHIR-41432 wording in AdministrableProductDefinition.producedFrom explicitly allows references from complex many-to-many through simple one-to-one manufactured-to-administrable cases even when there is no transformation or combining involved. This preserves the seed correction that the resources are not literally the same while making clear how unchanged manufactured forms can still be linked consistently. FHIR-31778 and FHIR-31785 are same artifact family context; FHIR-31785 already has an audit commit on the combined branch. BRR minutes from 2021-07-06 record the earlier concern that the use case/definition needed more work, and no directly relevant Zulip or conflicting Confluence record was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The active introduction now says that in some cases the administrable product needs no processing or transformation from the manufactured item, and the active producedFrom definition covers simple unchanged administration cases. Adding more local prose for FHIR-31779 would not change implementer understanding and could duplicate later accepted wording.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. No current FHIR-core source follow-up is needed for FHIR-31779; related producedFrom/formOf clarifications can be audited under their own Jira keys when selected.
