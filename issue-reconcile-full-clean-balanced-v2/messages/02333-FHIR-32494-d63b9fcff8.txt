PractitionerRole.identifier remains a role/location identifier, and PractitionerRole.specialty says role qualifications are represented in Practitioner.qualification. Related FHIR-32493 asked for Practitioner identifier-placement guidance, FHIR-36680 was later recorded as a duplicate search issue, and FHIR-36681 later relied on the existing Practitioner search behavior.
