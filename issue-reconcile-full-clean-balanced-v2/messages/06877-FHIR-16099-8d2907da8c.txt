FHIR-16099: Audit BDP collection source definition

Issue request:
FHIR-16099 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for STU3. It asked to clarify BiologicallyDerivedProduct.collection.source because the old definition, "Person or entity providing product", was unclear about whether the source was a vendor or the entity from which the product was extracted. The approved wording was: "The patient or entity, such as a hospital or vendor in the case of a processed/manipulated/manufactured product, providing the product."

Initial application:
The Jira comments cite commits c1f2872 and 842cb7b. The substantive BDP application is 842cb7bc8b171a6af5eaa68bc34e7e4a3817feb4, which changed the source spreadsheet short from "Person or entity providing product" to "Who is product from" and changed the detailed definition to the approved patient-or-entity wording. The c1f2872 merge commit touched MedicinalProductIngredient only and does not appear to be a BDP application. The approved definition then carried into the generated/reworked StructureDefinition source.

Additional context:
BiologicallyDerivedProduct is active in source/fhir.ini. Later applied BDP issues refined the same element family: FHIR-36696 changed the short display to "The patient or entity providing the product"; FHIR-37953 rejected adding RelatedPerson and clarified the source as either the patient who underwent collection or the facilitating organization; and FHIR-51939 replaced the single collection.source element with collection.sourcePatient and collection.sourceOrganization. The current authoritative StructureDefinition now has sourcePatient defined as "The patient providing the product" and sourceOrganization defined with the FHIR-16099 entity/hospital/vendor wording. Current source search found no active obsolete "Who is product from" or old ambiguous definition outside historical release files and transition maps.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. The current R6 source already preserves the FHIR-16099 meaning after later final decisions split the old element into patient and organization-specific references.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. No FHIR-16099 source edit is needed; future cleanup of any residual legacy path wording from the FHIR-51939 split should be considered under that later issue family, not this STU3 definition clarification.
