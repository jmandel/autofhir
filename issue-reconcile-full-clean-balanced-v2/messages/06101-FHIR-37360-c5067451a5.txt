FHIR-37360: Audit Questionnaire answer matching guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-37360 is Published/Persuasive with Modification. It asked FHIR-core Questionnaire guidance to clarify validation of Questionnaire answer choices for complex answers, especially Coding and Reference values: computable parts should be checked, while display-oriented or extension data should not require exact matching unless those are the only populated elements.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by the Jira-cited Questionnaire-fixes commit a5e43fab4e7185d48bbc4b61907edcb002079442, which added a comment to source/questionnaireresponse/structuredefinition-QuestionnaireResponse.xml on QuestionnaireResponse.item.answer.value[x]. A later formatting/cleanup commit preserved the same substantive guidance in the current source.

Additional context:
Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse are active, approved resources in source/fhir.ini, so the checked StructureDefinition source feeds the current build. The originating Zulip thread records the validation question and Lloyd McKenzie's guidance that validation should check Coding.code/system, be loose on human-readable fields such as display/unit, and clarify this in a tracker. FHIR SDC minutes record the FHIR-37360 persuasive-with-modification vote. A nearby same-family issue, FHIR-37468, later changed QuestionnaireResponse quantity answers to SimpleQuantity and is already represented on the combined branch; it does not change the FHIR-37360 rule that Quantity.unit-style display text should not be part of answer-choice matching unless it is the only populated data.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source/questionnaireresponse/structuredefinition-QuestionnaireResponse.xml already says that comparison against Questionnaire answerOption, answerValueSet, or answerExpression SHALL NOT pay attention to Coding.display, Reference.display, or Quantity.unit unless those are the only elements present; it also says systems are not required to check extensions and may enforce extension matching when present in both places. That satisfies the approved clarification without adding redundant prose elsewhere.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The active QuestionnaireResponse source already implements the published FHIR-37360 decision, and the remaining minor wording roughness is editorial rather than a semantic gap that would justify a new source-changing reconciliation commit.
