Issue request:
FHIR-5709 was Published/Persuasive for FHIR-core DSTU1. It reported that ProfileUtilities.sortDifferential() could incorrectly order a sparse differential when child elements appeared later in the list, causing snapshot generation to miss nested constraints such as mustSupport flags.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the old in-repository Java implementation.
