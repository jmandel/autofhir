FHIR-16960: Audit Device UDI reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-16960 was a Published, Persuasive with Modification STU3 ballot issue for FHIR-core Device asking OO to work with the Orders and Observation UDI team on recommendations for Device changes covering device kind versus instance and multiple device identifiers. The final resolution says that collaboration had occurred and led to numerous updates improving representation of UDI and its components.

Initial application:
No source commit in the local history explicitly mentions FHIR-16960. The issue appears to have been applied through the broader Device/DeviceDefinition rework and related ballot issues: the 2018 Device rework added DeviceDefinition and changed Device, FHIR-15812 recorded that the Device kind/instance/subcomponent work was already done, and FHIR-13965 recorded that multiple-UDI complex-device concerns were already satisfied because Device and DeviceDefinition UDI data were repeatable.

Additional context:
Device and DeviceDefinition are active resources in source/fhir.ini. Current source separates DeviceDefinition as the kind/model definition from Device as the actual instance; Device can reference DeviceDefinition, carry Device.parent for component hierarchies, expose Device.udiCarrier as 0..*, and keep serial/lot/manufacture/expiration production identifiers separate from Device.identifier. DeviceDefinition carries repeatable udiDeviceIdentifier, packaging UDI identifiers, regulatory identifiers, and classification. Later R5/R6 issues, including FHIR-20575, FHIR-34356, and FHIR-47419, refined UDI-DI and regulatory identifier wording; FHIR-34356 and FHIR-47419 already have reconciliation commits on the combined branch. FMG minutes only show FHIR-16960 being returned to OO, and targeted Zulip/Confluence searches found no conflicting implementation rationale.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build-scoped source already reflects the seed's broad resolution: the Device/DeviceDefinition split distinguishes kind from instance, Device supports component relationships and multiple UDI carriers, and later accepted clarifications have refined the UDI surfaces.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit as-is. Future work should address only specific later Device or UDI trackers; FHIR-16960 itself does not identify a remaining current-spec source defect.
