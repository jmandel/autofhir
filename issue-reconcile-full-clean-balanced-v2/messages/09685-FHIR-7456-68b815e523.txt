FHIR-7456: Audit DomainResource abstract wording

Issue request:
FHIR-7456 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core DomainResource correction from the DSTU1 ballot. It reported that the DomainResource introduction sentence "As an abstract resource, this resource never exists directly, only one of its descendent resources" was a fragment and unclear, and asked for wording that clearly points readers to descendant resources.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in git-svn commit 8e8c5b20b161b669967700dc213ac4e727eca143, matching the Jira comment "svn revision 6457". That commit changed the fragment to say DomainResource is never created directly and that one of its descendent resources is created instead. A later typo-only commit d8565d81844634c7138e99e85b3616945d2246f6 changed "descendent" to "descendant".

Additional context:
The current build configuration lists DomainResource as an active abstract resource in source/fhir.ini, and the active source/domainresource/domainresource-introduction.xml still contains the clarified sentence with the later spelling correction. Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches did not find a later conflicting decision for this narrow wording issue.
