FHIR-17035: Audit Bundle container semantics

Issue request:
FHIR-17035 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Feed change request from the STU3 ballot asking the Bundle page to make clear that packaging resources in a Bundle does not itself provide List, Group, or Composition semantics. The approved resolution was to add a note under the List/Group/Composition enumeration saying those resources represent meaningful groupings, while a Bundle is only a container for transfer and storage.
