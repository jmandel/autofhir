FHIR-13364: Audit Encounter PV1-10 service mapping

Issue request:
FHIR-13364 was a Published/Persuasive PA change request for STU3 Encounter. It said v2 PV1-10 Hospital Service was wrongly represented by Encounter.serviceProvider, asked to remove that misleading mapping, and resolved to add Encounter.serviceType 0..* with an example ServiceType binding and a PV1-10 mapping.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the historical spreadsheet source by f4b6c9c7 ("Encounter changes applied: GF#14140 GF#13364 GF#13155 GF#12888 GF#13020"), which added Encounter.serviceType as CodeableConcept with the service definition and v2 Mapping = PV1-10. The 2021 source conversion commit 33b409a4 carried the element and PV1-10 mapping into source/encounter/structuredefinition-Encounter.xml.

Additional context:
Later Published PA decisions changed the same element without reversing the PV1-10 decision: FHIR-22687 updated Encounter.serviceType to CodeableReference(HealthcareService), and FHIR-25794 updated its cardinality to 0..*. The active current Encounter resource now has Encounter.serviceType 0..* CodeableReference(HealthcareService), example binding to ValueSet/service-type, and v2 map PV1-10, while Encounter.serviceProvider maps to PL.6 and PL.1 instead of PV1-10. Focused Zulip and Confluence searches found no conflicting community decision.
