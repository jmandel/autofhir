FHIR-11370: Audit Claim package-code term reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-11370 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification DSTU2 Financial Management change request for Claim. It asked for a consistent term for DRG, bundle, or equivalent aggregate billing/package codes, and the final resolution was to rename drg in Diagnosis to packageCode.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly mentioning FHIR-11370 was found. Git history shows d06f8787e65d9eb23bd903d44c86c650b2fac541, committed 2016-11-19, changed the historical Claim spreadsheet source from Claim.diagnosis.drg to Claim.diagnosis.packageCode and updated the wording from diagnosis-specific DRG text to package billing code text. That is the apparent original application of this issue family for Claim.

Additional context:
FHIR-11371 is the paired Published Claim tracker for the same diagnosis DRG rename and is already represented on the combined branch. Later Published issue FHIR-29688 explicitly changed the model again: add diagnosisRelatedGroup 0..1 CodeableConcept at the Claim, ClaimResponse, and ExplanationOfBenefit roots and remove resource.diagnosis.packageCode. The current build-scope source lists Claim as an active resource in source/fhir.ini and defines Claim.diagnosisRelatedGroup at the Claim root; Claim.diagnosis no longer has drg or packageCode children. Zulip discussion after R5 also refers implementers to Claim.diagnosisRelatedGroup for DRGs, and no Confluence evidence was found that conflicts with the Jira/source history.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The old drg child requested by FHIR-11370 was renamed to packageCode historically, and the intermediate packageCode child was later removed by a final R5 work-group decision. The current Claim source carries the DRG/package concept as Claim.diagnosisRelatedGroup, so reintroducing Claim.diagnosis.packageCode would contradict later applied intent.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. Do not reintroduce Claim.diagnosis.packageCode for FHIR-11370; any future change to model DRG/package billing differently should come from a newer final Financial Management decision, not from this DSTU2 rename.
