FHIR-25928 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I clarification request to add Subscription to the "Exchanging Resources" section, with the approved resolution to add it as a sibling to Messaging and Documents.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied in the R5 work stream before this reconciliation. Commit 1d47e8413b042c27e2a30b811679a43350c6a35e added a Subscriptions Framework link to source/documentation.html, and later FHIR#25928 commits ef0558f0c6008b9fd15ed93c302b484ab1c67f10 and dcaf7d32918612a69679db4f6850009f332ce42c expanded and reorganized source/subscriptions.html and the Subscription resource introduction. The later Jira comment listed additional subscription documentation commits, but those were typo/documentation follow-ups rather than the initial sibling link.

Additional context:
The February 2020 FHIR-I minutes show the initial subscriptions-and-messaging discussion was tabled because the placement and relationship to REST/Messaging/Documents needed clarification. The April 2020 FHIR-I minutes record the final approved resolution: add Subscription as a sibling to messaging and documents. The linked Zulip thread confirms the implementer uncertainty and the emerging answer: create a page analogous to Messaging and have documentation and subscription-related resources point to it. Current source now does that: source/documentation.html links Subscriptions Framework immediately after Documents and Messaging, source/exchange-module.html has a Subscriptions Framework section, source/exchanging.html and source/exchanging-subscription.html include subscription in the exchange guidance, and source/hierarchy.xml plus publish.ini include those pages in the build.
