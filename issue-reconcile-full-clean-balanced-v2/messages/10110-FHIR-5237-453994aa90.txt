FHIR-5237: Audit modifier-extension example guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-5237 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core FHIR-I ballot issue for DSTU1 about the Extensibility page's modifier-extension examples. The commenter objected that examples such as using Condition to say a patient does not have a diagnosis, no-known-allergies, and rule-out diagnoses needed positive standardized guidance rather than only advice to avoid modifier extensions. The approved resolution was narrower: change the modifier-extension examples, with Grahame's comment explaining that the page is the general fallback position and resource-specific comments belong on the specific resources.

Initial application:
No commit explicitly mentions FHIR-5237. The closest historical application is the 2015-03-18 source/extensibility.html change c844cc1d382c0df547670aa996e24347ac204cdf8, made before the Jira comment saying the work had been done, which removed weaker modifier-extension examples and revised the modifier-extension example block. Later, FHIR-7386 directly corrected the same Condition example through commit fbb5823018ed5a3b9736b8a17c67c503f34a26b8 by replacing the confusing "doesn't have a diagnosis/refuted" wording with family-history wording.

Additional context:
The current build-scope Extensibility page is active through source/fhir.ini and source/hierarchy.xml. It now uses Patient.contact do-not-contact, Condition family-history, Procedure performer-not-involved, and value-set subsumption examples, and its conformance rules require special handling for unrecognized modifier extensions. Resource-specific pages now carry positive clinical absence guidance, including Condition assertions of absence/refuted status, AllergyIntolerance no-known-allergies guidance, and FamilyMemberHistory negation examples. FHIR-7386 and FHIR-18347 are already represented on the combined branch and should not be duplicated.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
