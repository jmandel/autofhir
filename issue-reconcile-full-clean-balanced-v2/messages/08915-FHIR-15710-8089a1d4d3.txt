FHIR-15710: Audit Definition pattern title wording

Issue request:
FHIR-15710 is a Published/Persuasive STU3 technical correction for the FHIR-core workflow page asking that the Definition logical model detailed-descriptions page stop calling the artifact a resource. The requested final wording was to change the section title from "Resource definition - Detailed Descriptions" to "Pattern definition - Detailed Descriptions" and the paragraph from "definition resource" to "definition pattern".

Initial application:

That commit is labeled GF#15711 because it also applied the adjacent mappings-page issue, but its patch explicitly changed template-logical-definitions.html in the exact title and paragraph requested by FHIR-15710.

Published-surface checks show the wording was wrong in STU3, corrected by R4, and remains correct on build.fhir.org. The adjacent FHIR-15711 mappings wording issue is already represented on the combined branch by commit 93a8fe814f0934f91b30011a14b69a2deb77dfc7.
