The recorded resolution first discussed a Device DI element and removal of DI references from Device.type, then reopened on 2017-02-05 with a 9-0-1 vote to apply the attached UDI/device presentation; the Jira related section is Device.name.

Initial application:

The exact historical spreadsheet has since been replaced by generated StructureDefinition XML and related terminology sources.

Later published Device issues refined this same area: FHIR-20639 improved Device.name.type definitions, FHIR-39162 renamed Device.deviceName to Device.name and added Device.name.display, and later work added the current commercial-brand-family name type.
