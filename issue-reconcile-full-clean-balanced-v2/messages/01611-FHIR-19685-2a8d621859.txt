FHIR-19685: Audit DocumentReference creationTime mapping

Issue request:
FHIR-19685 was Published/Persuasive for STU3 DocumentReference. It requested that XDS DocumentEntry.creationTime map to DocumentReference.content.attachment.creation and that DocumentReference.date have no XDS mapping.

Initial application:
The current history shows the attachment aggregate XDS mapping carrying DocumentEntry.creationTime introduced during the late-2018 DocumentReference QA work and preserved through the 2021 source rework. No FHIR-19685-tagged source commit was found, but the current source reflects the issue's requested placement.

Additional context:
FHIR-12892 is the earlier DSTU2 mapping issue that had put creationTime on the indexing/date element. FHIR-43408 is the later Applied R6 mapping update that explicitly leaves date unmapped and maps content.attachment, including creationTime, repositoryUniqueId, and URI. DocumentReference remains an active resource in source/fhir.ini.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current StructureDefinition has no XDS mapping on DocumentReference.date and carries DocumentEntry.creationTime in the DocumentReference.content.attachment XDS mapping, matching FHIR-19685 and later FHIR-43408.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If a future mapping page expands Attachment subelements under DocumentReference, ensure content.attachment.creation remains the explicit location for DocumentEntry.creationTime and do not map DocumentReference.date to XDS.
