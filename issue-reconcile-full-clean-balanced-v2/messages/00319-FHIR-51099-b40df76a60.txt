FHIR-51099: Audit Bundle.signature deprecation

Issue request:
FHIR-51099 was Applied with resolution Persuasive with Modification. It requested changing Bundle.signature from 0..1 to 0..* for multiple document or persisted-Bundle signatures, but the approved resolution instead kept Bundle.signature at 0..1 and deprecated it in favor of Bundle.entry Provenance resources for multiple Bundle signatures.

Initial application:
No commit message in the local source history explicitly mentions FHIR-51099. The substance appears to have been applied by 9fe4e8ba2ec241ab35dc18046c102c38872e1d50, which marked Bundle.signature deprecated in source/bundle/structuredefinition-Bundle.xml and added the central Signing Bundles guidance in source/signatures.html directing applications to Provenance-based Bundle signatures.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists bundle=Bundle, so source/bundle/structuredefinition-Bundle.xml is active current-build source. The FHIR-I 2025-08-04 minutes record a 12-0-0 motion to deprecate Bundle.signature for FHIR-51099 based on the signature discussion, and the Zulip Bundle.signature thread records the rationale: changing the wire format to 0..* was avoided for compatibility, while Provenance offers the long-term multiple-signature path. FHIR-53914 later proposes broader edits to the Signing Bundles section but is still Triaged/Unresolved and does not reverse the applied FHIR-51099 decision.
