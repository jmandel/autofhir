Issue request:

It asked to clarify the semantics of the old genomic source class wording, "Source of sample used to determine the sequence in sequencing lab -- germline, somatic, prenatal," and its resolution said to point to the LOINC definition, work with LOINC to redefine the term, and then edit the FHIR definition accordingly.

Initial application:

Those old genetics artifacts were subsequently removed from current core source as outdated clinical genomics content.
