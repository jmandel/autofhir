FHIR-5263: Audit RelatedPerson contact wording

Issue request:
FHIR-5263 was a Published FHIR-core RelatedPerson ballot issue with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked to replace wording that directed patient contacts to Patient.contact "instead" with wording that allows the same individual to be represented both as a Patient's Contact and as a Related Person.

Initial application:
The request was applied by git commit 0205e036d4b18587f5b89829b458d61586308807, "Tracker #5263 RelatedPerson notes updates - Ballot Reconciliation comments", which changed source/relatedperson/relatedperson-introduction.xml to remove "instead" and add the approved sentence about individuals serving as both a Patient's Contact and a Related Person.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists relatedperson as an active resource, so source/relatedperson/relatedperson-introduction.xml is in the current build scope. Later RelatedPerson issues FHIR-25727 and FHIR-30313 addressed broader scope/definition questions for R5; they preserve the contact-overlap sentence and are already represented on the combined branch, so they do not require a duplicate decision here. Direct community searches found no FHIR-5263-specific contrary record, and broader RelatedPerson discussions reinforced rather than contradicted the Patient.contact versus RelatedPerson distinction.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current active source already says, "For keeping information about people for contact purposes for a patient, use a Patient's Contact element" and "Some individuals may serve as both a Patient's Contact and a Related Person", which satisfies the Jira resolution.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit as-is. No follow-up source work is needed for FHIR-5263 unless PA later reopens the Patient.contact versus RelatedPerson boundary.
