FHIR-21236: Audit Device patient association relocation

Issue request:
FHIR-21236 was Published with a Persuasive with Modification resolution for R5. It asked how to indicate the patient assigned to a non-affixed device, and directed the Device.patient definition to cover devices affixed to or associated to a patient for specific use, with requirements noting implanted or otherwise patient-used devices such as wheelchairs and oxygen tanks.

Initial application:
The requested Device.patient wording was present in the R5-era Device source imported by commit 33b409a4b6, and FHIR-30243 later renamed Device.patient to Device.subject while preserving the FHIR-21236 definition and requirements text. FHIR-32570 then moved that patient/subject association into Device.association.humanSubject before the broader DeviceAssociation relocation.

Additional context:
Later published/applied Device issues, especially FHIR-38904 and FHIR-39226, superseded the local Device.patient/Device.subject placement by moving patient association history out of Device and into the new DeviceAssociation resource to avoid patient data leakage across reusable devices. The current build includes DeviceAssociation as an active resource, maps Device.patient to DeviceAssociation in fhir.ini, and defines DeviceAssociation.subject plus a patient search parameter for the patient associated with the device. Current DeviceAssociation narrative explicitly includes implantables, wheelchairs, mobility assistance, and assignment or dissociation with a patient.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Reintroducing or editing Device.patient would target an element that is no longer active in the current build, while the current DeviceAssociation source already carries the patient-association semantics needed for non-affixed devices.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Future work should not restore Device.patient for this issue; if the work group wants more explicit examples, that should be a new DeviceAssociation editorial decision against the current R6 model rather than a FHIR-21236 fix.
