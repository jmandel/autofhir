FHIR-5316: Audit specification search support

Issue request:
FHIR-5316 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I DSTU1 change request from the January 2015 ballot asking for a way to search across the FHIR specification, not just from the home page, and noting that home-page search returned results outside the developmental ballot scope. Grahame Grieve's recorded response was that a much better search was already on the work list, and the issue was later commented as resolved for another task.

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly tagged FHIR-5316 was found. The combined branch already includes the source-changing same-family FHIR-8151 reconciliation that routes the current home page and footer search links through the build's configured search-location target.

FHIR-5316 is closely related to the repeated DSTU1 search-capability family represented by Published/Persuasive FHIR-8151, FHIR-7712, and FHIR-6849, plus Duplicate FHIR-7748. FHIR-7712, FHIR-6849, and FHIR-11438 already have no-change audits on the combined branch, while FHIR-8151 carries the necessary source fix. Current source includes an active DuckDuckGo-backed CI search page in source/search-build.html, registers that page in publish.ini and source/hierarchy.xml, and uses [%search-location%] from the navbar, home page, and footer. Later live search.cfm reports such as FHIR-50549 and FHIR-54549 are unresolved and therefore background only for this applied DSTU1 ballot issue.
