FHIR-31357: Audit QuestionnaireResponse nesting guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-31357 was Published/Persuasive for R5. It asked FHIR-I/SDC to surface QuestionnaireResponse guidance explaining that nested items under questions go beneath each answer, nested items under groups go directly beneath item.item, and the hierarchy is retained so multi-answer questions can carry child items per answer.

Initial application:
The Jira comment points to c6f0f9424d, which added a "Nested Items" section to source/questionnaireresponse/questionnaireresponse-notes.xml. That section remains in the active QuestionnaireResponse notes and states the two mechanisms, item.item and item.answer.item, and why answer-level nesting is needed for questions with multiple answers.

Additional context:
QuestionnaireResponse is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. The active StructureDefinition also carries matching comments on QuestionnaireResponse.item, QuestionnaireResponse.item.answer.item, and QuestionnaireResponse.item.item. Older related FHIR-10481 and FHIR-20768 decisions kept both recursion points and asked for clearer guidance rather than removing item.answer.item; FHIR-26356 clarified the nearby qrs-1 invariant; later FHIR-38853 moved a linkage diagram and clarified repeating groups without reversing the FHIR-31357 rule. Confluence minutes confirm the SDC vote accepting FHIR-31357 as Persuasive. Zulip searches found historical implementer confusion about the same two recursion points and no conflicting later community decision.
