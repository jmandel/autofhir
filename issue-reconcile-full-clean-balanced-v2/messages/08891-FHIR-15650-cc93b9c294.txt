FHIR-15650: Audit ElementDefinition eld-2 invariant

Issue request:
FHIR-15650 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core technical correction for STU3. It asked to correct ElementDefinition invariant eld-2 because the expression `min.empty() or max.empty() or (max = '*') or (min <= max.toInteger())` assumes short-circuit behavior and can evaluate `max.toInteger()` when `max = '*'`; the requested expression guards the numeric comparison with `iif(max != '*', min <= max.toInteger())`.

Initial application:
