FHIR-7563: Audit Provenance reference guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-7563 was a Published Security WG change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking to expand Provenance guidance rather than mandate broader policy. The final resolution asked to add Background and Context text explaining that Provenance depends on references to the involved resources, entities, and agents; that those references need not be resolvable but must identify the item unambiguously; that versions must be included when versions matter; and that using Provenance can introduce versioning and unique-identification requirements.

Initial application:
Historical commit 2522cb1492a0be6fa89becaaa0611cf6490cbd20, "Updates for CP 9037, 8638, 7570, and 7563," added the requested paragraph to source/provenance/provenance-introduction.xml. That initial application carried the Jira resolution typo "unique and ambiguous identification"; later commit 84f8086d21facb3551bfb28481156250510ae48b corrected it to "unique and unambiguous identification," and commit 87b00c9c0de977f8559e0f99694e8664e03a06cd uppercased the remaining MAY conformance keyword.

Additional context:
The current Provenance resource is active in the build: source/fhir.ini lists provenance=Provenance under [resources], assigns provenance to the sec work group, and marks Provenance approved. Current source/provenance/provenance-introduction.xml keeps the FHIR-7563 guidance in Background and Context. Searches of Zulip and Confluence for the issue key and distinctive reference wording found no directly relevant or conflicting community records.
