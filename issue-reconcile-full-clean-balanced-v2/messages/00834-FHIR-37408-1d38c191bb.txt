FHIR-37408: Audit ImagingSelection subset exclusivity

Issue request:
FHIR-37408 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Imaging Integration change request for R5 asking that ImagingSelection.instance.frameList, observationUid, segmentList, and roiList be made mutually exclusive. The final Jira resolution directed editors to apply the linked Imaging Integration Confluence update.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in the 2022 ImagingSelection update series. Direct commit 5c5a620527e102650cad53df57ff594d1706fe2a, later merged by 471fbc468b8fd5427fcebad076569e16590ac707/PR #2019 and linked from Jira, removed the four separate list elements from source/imagingselection/structuredefinition-ImagingSelection.xml and represented their approved alternatives through ImagingSelection.instance.subset with SOP-class-specific meaning.

Additional context:
Later Applied ImagingSelection work in FHIR-52900 found that the generic instance.subset representation was confusing and split it into explicit elements: instance.frameNumber, instance.referencedContentItemIdentifier, instance.segmentNumber, instance.regionOfInterest, and instance.waveFormChannel. That same change family added isl-10 to require that only one of those explicit subset elements be present, and FHIR-53211 later added the missing condition links for verification tooling. FHIR-52900 is already represented on the combined branch by Issue-Reconcile-Key: FHIR-52900.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. ImagingSelection is active in source/fhir.ini, the current authoritative StructureDefinition has no occurrences of the original frameList/observationUid/segmentList/roiList elements, and the current isl-10 invariant enforces mutual exclusion among their present-day explicit replacements.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No further core source change is needed for FHIR-37408 unless a future work-group decision changes the later FHIR-52900/FHIR-53211 model.
