Issue request:
FHIR-33096 is Published/Persuasive and asked BR&R to correct the research-study-status code "completed" because it duplicated the closed-to-accrual-and-intervention definition; the resolution also called for the applicable binding to be preferred and for completed to mean the study closed according to plan with no further treatments, interventions, or data collection.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by dcb559de39, which changed source/researchstudy/codesystem-research-study-status.xml from the duplicated R4/R5 wording to the requested completed definition and changed the then-current ResearchStudy study-status binding from extensible to preferred.

Additional context:
The originating Zulip thread confirms the problem was a cut-and-paste error in the status value set and that R5 had separated the resource publication status from study progress status. Current source now binds ResearchStudy.progressStatus.state to the THO research-study-status ValueSet with preferred strength, while ResearchStudy.status is explicitly the publication state of the resource. Later FHIR-50644 was resolved no-change and reinforces that study conduct status belongs on progressStatus.state, not ResearchStudy.status.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
