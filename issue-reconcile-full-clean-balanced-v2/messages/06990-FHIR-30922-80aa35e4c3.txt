FHIR-30922: Audit Questionnaire answerOption definition

Issue request:
FHIR-30922 was Published with a Persuasive resolution for Questionnaire.item.answerOption. The issue reported that answerOption.value[x] had expanded beyond choice/open-choice/Coding answers while the element definition still said it was for a "choice" or "open-choice" question; the approved resolution was to change the definition to "One of the permitted answers for the question."

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by commit fbefca63b6bb145c724eb88563fc935cf7710f6e, which changed source/questionnaire/structuredefinition-Questionnaire.xml from the stale choice/open-choice definition to the approved question-level wording. The Jira comment also records the same short commit hash.

Additional context:
Questionnaire is an active resource in source/fhir.ini, so the StructureDefinition source is in current build scope. FHIR-20396 later removed choice/open-choice from Questionnaire.item.type and added answerConstraint, reinforcing that the answerOption definition should not be limited to those retired item types. Later answerOption-related work, including FHIR-40780, adjusted constraints and value types without reverting the FHIR-30922 definition. Zulip discussion before and after FHIR-30922 confirms the stale wording caused the reported implementer confusion, and FHIR SDC minutes record the persuasive vote.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
