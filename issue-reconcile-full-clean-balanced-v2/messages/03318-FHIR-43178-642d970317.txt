FHIR-43178: Audit StructureMap constant evaluation clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-43178 was an Applied/Persuasive FHIR-core StructureMap mapping-language clarification for R6. It asked whether StructureMap map-level constants can depend on source/context variables and when those expressions are evaluated. The approved resolution was to replace the ambiguous sentence about constants referring to other constants with text saying expressions are evaluated each time a map is evaluated, are FHIRPath expressions, but map-level constants can refer only to map-level scope such as other constants and cannot access source or target variables.

Initial application:
FHIR-21642 originally added the constants section to source/mapping-language.html in commit cfe0c27ccf77885830ace90028056c9567b969d1 with the ambiguous sentence. FHIR-43178 was then applied in commit 4ca72903b8bb6bbe73fb451536a6a5c69fda4d36, which replaced that sentence in source/mapping-language.html with the approved clarification. No prior Issue-Reconcile-Key commit for FHIR-43178 was present on the combined branch.

Additional context:
source/mapping-language.html is an active page in source/hierarchy.xml, and StructureMap is active in source/fhir.ini. The linked Zulip topic gathered limited community feedback and did not identify a conflicting interpretation. FHIR-I WGM minutes record discussion of what constants are and how they are evaluated, with the same 13-0-3 vote recorded on the Jira. Later mapping-language commits on the combined branch did not remove or contradict the FHIR-43178 clarification.
