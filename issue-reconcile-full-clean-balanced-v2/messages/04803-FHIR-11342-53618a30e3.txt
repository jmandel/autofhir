Issue request:
FHIR-11342 was a Published Patient Care change request, resolved Persuasive with Modification, asking for a CareTeam.relatedTeam or equivalent composition relationship so care teams could contain or relate to other care teams. The accepted resolution was to support Reference(CareTeam) on CareTeam.participant.member, without adding a separate relatedTeam/replaces element.

That source was later refactored into the current StructureDefinition XML.
