FHIR-8021: Audit MedicationDispense dosage clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-8021 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-core Pharmacy clarification, raised for DSTU1, asking to differentiate MedicationDispense.dosageInstructions from the prescription dosage instructions. The accepted resolution was to clarify that the pharmacist reviews the medication prescription/order before dispense and updates MedicationDispense.dosageInstruction based on the actual product being dispensed.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied before this reconciliation by commit 1cb9689e725c06210bbeaa920648410591a426d4, whose subject named gForge change request 8021 and added the pharmacist-review wording to the MedicationDispense dosageInstruction spreadsheet source. The 2021 source rework commit 33b409a4b6d544598262f2f074ebb4b6dc466157 carried that wording into source/medicationdispense/structuredefinition-MedicationDispense.xml, and the current combined source still keeps the clarification on MedicationDispense.dosageInstruction.

Additional context:
MedicationDispense is active in source/fhir.ini, and fhir.ini records the historical MedicationPrescription artifact as now MedicationRequest, so the current MedicationRequest comparison is the relevant successor check. Current MedicationRequest.dosageInstruction does not carry the pharmacist-review dispense comment, preserving the intended distinction. Later FHIR-52892 added MedicationDispense.doseAdministrationAid and initially copied the neighboring dosageInstruction comment onto that unrelated dose-aid element, but the already-published FHIR-8502 reconciliation on the combined branch removed that copied comment and updated the remaining MedicationDispense.dosageInstruction wording to use current R6 terms.
