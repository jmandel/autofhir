FHIR-7533: Audit Consent resource replacement

Issue request:
FHIR-7533 was a Published/Persuasive 2015May FHIR-core profiles issue from CBCC asking that ConsentDirective stop being modeled as a profile on Contract: Consent should be its own resource, focused on consent, with any contract about the consent represented separately. The final Jira resolution says, "We now have Consent as a Resource."

Initial application:
No source commit explicitly citing FHIR-7533 was found. History shows the old ConsentDirective profile-on-Contract path in dormant source, then acc3f3137b introduced Consent as a resource rather than a Contract profile, with ba3db96597 and 1264e32b13 expanding documentation and examples. Current source/fhir.ini lists Consent as an active resource, has no active profiles entry for consentdirective, and records in deleted-resource notes that ConsentDirective became Consent.

Additional context:
FHIR-7253 and FHIR-7871 are closely related 2015May ConsentDirective/Contract issues already represented on the combined branch with no-change audits; both were resolved by the same Consent-resource migration. FHIR-19529 later refined the Contract relationship: where Consent applies, Consent should be used, and if both a Contract and Consent exist for a consent directive, the Consent references the Contract to avoid circular Contract-to-Consent references. May 2016 Zulip discussions confirm CBCC and implementer discussion around moving from the Contract profile/IG path to a standalone Consent resource.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build has Consent as an active resource with Consent-specific scope, status, decision, provision modeling, and examples. The old ConsentDirective Contract profile and PCD IG references are not active build entries, so editing them would not be a current-spec correction for FHIR-7533. The remaining Contract/Consent relationship text is later intentional guidance from FHIR-19529 rather than an unapplied part of FHIR-7533.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Future cleanup of dormant consentdirective source, OID registry entries, or broader Contract removal should be handled under a specific current build-scope issue, not this historical profile-to-resource issue.
