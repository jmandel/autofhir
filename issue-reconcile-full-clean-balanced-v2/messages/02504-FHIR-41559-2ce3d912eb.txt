FHIR-41559: Audit RelatedPerson primary-target wording

Issue request:
FHIR-41559 was an Applied/Persuasive with Modification PA change request asking to clarify that a person represented as a RelatedPerson can also be a Patient in their own right, but is not the patient who is the primary target of the healthcare context represented by the RelatedPerson resource. The resolution updated the definition to say "primary target of healthcare" and added Boundaries and Relationships text explaining interaction with Patient.link.

Initial application:

Git commit ac19f96ea99363caf67b40b42f035d7ee1ddf553 then applied the same "primary target" wording to the root element definition.
