Issue request:
FHIR-14412 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification STU3 financial-module technical correction asking whether the phrase "encouraged as are the use" in the Financial module Security and Privacy section was confusing and whether "are" was redundant. The relevant Jira comment recommended changing "as are" to "in addition to".

Initial application:
The issue was first applied in git-svn commit cb4678a111, corresponding to svn revision 12999, which changed the sentence from singular "is" to plural "are" and inserted a comma before "as are". The final clarification was applied in git-svn commit c6368844f5, corresponding to svn revision 13149, which changed the sentence to "encouraged in addition to the use of whatever measures...".

Additional context:
The current source/financial-module.html is an active module page referenced from source/hierarchy.xml. The current Financial module text already contains the clarified "in addition to" wording and no longer contains the confusing "as are" phrase in that section.
