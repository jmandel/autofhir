FHIR-19012: Audit HL7 IG publisher guidance scope

Issue request:
FHIR-19012 was Published/Persuasive for STU3 and asked that implementation guides identify their sponsoring work group, preferably on the landing page. The accepted resolution said to use ImplementationGuide.publisher as "HL7 International - <workgroup name>", ImplementationGuide.contact as the work group homepage, and to add those expectations to the HL7 IG guide quality criteria.

Initial application:
No HL7/fhir source commit mentioning FHIR-19012 was found. The Jira comments say the immediate application was an update to the former FHIR Implementation Guide Publishing Requirements wiki page, and that FHIR-19632 was filed separately to have IG Publisher enforce those requirements.

Additional context:
Current HL7 Confluence publication requirements now say all FHIR implementation guides SHALL declare publisher and contact and expose them from the home page, and specifically say HL7-published IGs shall identify ImplementationGuide.publisher as "HL7 International - <workgroup full name>" and ImplementationGuide.contact as the work group home page. ImplementationGuide is active in the current core build, but its base publisher and contact elements remain optional general metadata because the HL7-specific rule is a publication-policy/tooling requirement, not a universal base-resource cardinality. The closely related enforcement issue FHIR-19632 is already represented on the combined branch as an external-repo audit.
