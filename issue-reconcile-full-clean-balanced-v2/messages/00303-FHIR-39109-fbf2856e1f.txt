Issue request:
FHIR-39109 was Published/Persuasive for R5 Clinical Genomics. It asked GenomicStudy to avoid representing trio participants through DocumentReference.subject Group alone, to use analysis.focus references to the relevant RelatedPerson participants, to restrict GenomicStudy.subject to realistic subject types, and to make similar MolecularSequence.subject limitations.

Initial application:
The requested R5 changes were applied in commit 12431eb8236aebd47261a4341d52f74c4327735b and merged by HL7/fhir#2712. The residual source/genomicstudy definition now limits GenomicStudy.subject to Patient, Group, Substance, BiologicallyDerivedProduct, and NutritionProduct; GenomicStudy.analysis.focus is 0..* Reference(Resource); and the trio example includes Patient plus RelatedPerson focus references.

Additional context:
MolecularSequence.focus was handled by the related FHIR-39110 change, which is already represented on the combined reconciliation branch. Later current-build changes removed GenomicStudy and MolecularDefinition from active core fhir.ini entries, and FHIR-54345 records that current core links should point to the CG Incubator resources. The CG Incubator continuous-build GenomicStudy StructureDefinition currently carries the same subject target list and 0..* focus semantics.
