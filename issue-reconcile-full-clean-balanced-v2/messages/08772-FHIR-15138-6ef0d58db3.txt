That commit added the FHIR namespace to the old source/resource/$meta-delete-request.txt and source/resource/$meta-delete-response.txt examples, along with neighboring Resource meta operation examples.

Direct Zulip and Confluence searches found no FHIR-15138 discussion that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

Recommendation:
