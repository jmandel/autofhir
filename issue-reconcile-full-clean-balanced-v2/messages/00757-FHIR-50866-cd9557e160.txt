FHIR-50866: Audit DiagnosticReport procedure clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-50866 was an Applied FHIR-core DiagnosticReport change request from OO asking whether DiagnosticReport should use a partOf element instead of procedure for the Procedure from which a report was produced. The final Not Persuasive with Modification resolution kept DiagnosticReport.procedure and required its short display and definition to clarify that this is the performed procedure, distinct from requested procedure context available through basedOn.ServiceRequest.

Initial application:
The issue was applied before this reconciliation through HL7/fhir#3492. Commit f6388f822b updated DiagnosticReport.procedure to say performed procedure, commit d5d652b14f fixed the introduced short-display typo, and merge commit 86ea9f38df/53e6e707 brought the change into the build history referenced by the Jira comment.

Additional context:
DiagnosticReport is an active build resource in source/fhir.ini. The May 2025 OO WGM minutes record the same motion: keep procedure, do not decide the broader event-pattern/partOf question in this ticket, and update short display and definition to make performed procedure clear. The directly linked FHIR-50887 later resolved Not Persuasive because FHIR-50866 kept DiagnosticReport.procedure.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current active source already has DiagnosticReport.procedure short value "The performed procedure(s) from which the report was produced" and definition "The performed procedure(s) that are reported on in the DiagnosticReport." The source also keeps DiagnosticReport.basedOn as the requested-service link, matching the Jira rationale.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No additional FHIR-core source change is needed for FHIR-50866; future work about broader Procedure.report, workflow pattern mapping, or circular-reference guidance should be handled by the separate tickets that already cover that broader topic.
