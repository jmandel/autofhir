FHIR-6176: Audit document subject wording

Issue request:
FHIR-6176 was Published/Persuasive for the FHIR Documents page. It asked to replace the confusing phrase "The Subject resource" with wording that identifies the subject as Composition.subject, because there is no standalone Subject Resource.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records svn revision 6187. Git history maps that to 95b22dc12b (2015-08-08), which changed source/documents.html from "The Subject resource" to a lower-case linked "subject resource" pointing to composition-definitions.html#Composition.subject and added matching links for the adjacent Composition and section.text entries.

Additional context:
source/documents.html is in the current build page list via publish.ini. The current source still has the linked subject-resource presentation item and, after later FHIR-39121 editorial changes, also states that the narrative is of the subject resource, typically a Patient, referenced in Composition.subject. The Composition.subject definition says who or what the composition is about and notes that clinical documents are usually about the patient. Community checks found implementation questions and IG Publisher tooling context, including a 2017 Zulip response explaining that subject means Composition.subject, but no conflicting WG or community evidence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
No source change was made. The current specification already implements the requested clarification, and later edits reinforce rather than contradict it.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit as-is. No follow-up is needed for FHIR-6176 unless a future documents-page rewrite removes the link or Composition.subject explanation.
