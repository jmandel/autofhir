FHIR-8027: Audit document stylesheet reference clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-8027 was a Published FHIR-core DSTU1 clarification request for the Documents page. It reported that the old guidance saying internal stylesheet references should be used when a document was not safe to view without the stylesheet appeared to contradict the adjacent rule that stylesheets SHALL NOT change clinical meaning, and it asked whether a common stylesheet was supplied. The final Persuasive with Modification disposition required replacing the unsafe-to-view rationale with wording explaining that internal stylesheet references are preferred because viewers may be unable to resolve external content due to technical problems or local policy decisions.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by git-svn commit b39b208cfd750898e66e5b1a68cad49f5be5b2a7, whose message says '#8027 - adjust language about stylesheets' and whose git-svn id records SVN revision 5824, matching the Jira application comment. That commit replaced the contradictory unsafe-to-view sentence in source/documents.html with the resolved rationale. Later QA commits normalized encoding, whitespace, and wording details, leaving the same substantive rule in the current source.

Additional context:
The current source/documents.html page is in build scope through source/hierarchy.xml. It now states that relative internal references SHOULD be used for stylesheets because the viewer may be unable to resolve external content at viewing time due to technical problems or local policy decisions, and it keeps the adjacent SHALL NOT rule preventing stylesheets from changing clinical meaning. Related document-page work, including FHIR-5248 and FHIR-7388, refined the surrounding presentation-integrity language; an existing combined-branch audit commit for FHIR-5248 already documents that adjacent context.
