FHIR-16906: Audit Bundle timestamp clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-16906 was a Published FHIR-core Feed change request from the STU3 ballot with a Persuasive with Modification resolution. It asked to rename or redefine Bundle.timestamp because "timestamp" was unclear, questioned whether searchset pages were required to have different timestamps, and the final resolution was to clarify that different pages MAY have different timestamps but need not and that Bundle.timestamp is the time the Bundle was assembled.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in SVN revision 14360, now git commit 722bf2f4e92f86bb0907717922669ee10fb36d9c, which changed the Bundle.timestamp searchset usage note in source/bundle/bundle-spreadsheet.xml from "different pages will have different timestamps" to "different pages MAY have different timestamps but need not". The assembled-time short/definition was already present in the Bundle source lineage and was preserved through the later spreadsheet-to-StructureDefinition refactor.

Additional context:
FHIR-17055 is the immediately related Bundle.timestamp searchset tracker from the same ballot family. It later added the separate clarification that different page timestamps do not imply subsequent pages include changes made since the initial query, and that audit is already represented on the combined branch. Current source/fhir.ini lists Bundle as an active resource, so source/bundle/structuredefinition-Bundle.xml is current build input. No relevant Zulip or Confluence evidence was found that changes the Jira/source conclusion.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active Bundle.timestamp source already says the short is "When the bundle was assembled", defines the value as the date/time the bundle was assembled, and says searchset pages MAY have different timestamps but need not.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. The current build source preserves the approved FHIR-16906 clarification, and further rewording would be redundant rather than corrective.
