FHIR-20848: Audit dependent rule combination syntax

Issue request:
FHIR-20848 was Published/Persuasive for the FHIR mapping language and StructureMap. It asked whether a `then` clause can combine dependent group invocations with nested local rules, and its resolution said to document that `then` consists of zero or more invocations followed by at most one group of dependent rules, with the ANTLR grammar changed as much as possible.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by the FHIR#20848/FHIR#20840 application commits 562bf9f82d7 and a81d5848a6, merged by PR #984 as b3a5ebd9c3. Those commits changed source/mapping-language.html to say that multiple group invocations may be comma-separated and that the last group invocation may be followed by dependent rules, and changed images/mapping.g4 so a `then` clause can parse group invocations followed by nested rules. A later grammar refactor in db673c36e8 renamed the rules but preserved the same shape as `groupInvocation (',' groupInvocation)* mapRules? | mapRules`.

Additional context:
The mapping-language page is in current build navigation, `images/mapping.g4` is the grammar linked by that page, and StructureMap is an active current resource in source/fhir.ini. FHIR-20840 is the same resolution family for repeated dependent invocations. Later related decisions matter only for the execution-order words in the old resolution: FHIR-23010 established the general mapping-language guidance that rules may be applied in any order, and the FHIR-25220 application thread explicitly questioned and dropped equivalent `Groups are executed in order` wording for R5.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This empty audit commit makes no source change. Current build-scope source already permits and documents the combination FHIR-20848 requested: comma-separated group invocations may be followed by one nested dependent-rules block, and the grammar accepts that form. Adding new `executed sequentially` prose now would reopen the later order question rather than safely completing FHIR-20848.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. If FHIR-I wants deterministic execution order for group invocations after `then`, handle that as a new ordering decision that explicitly reconciles FHIR-20848, FHIR-20840, FHIR-25220, and FHIR-23010.
