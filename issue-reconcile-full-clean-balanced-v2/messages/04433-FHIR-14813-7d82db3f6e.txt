FHIR-14813: Audit Account active removal

Issue request:
FHIR-14813 was a Published/Persuasive with Modification Patient Administration change request for Account from STU3. It asked to remove Account.active, rename Account.period to Account.servicePeriod, update the period definition to "The date range of services associated with this account", and clarify that transactions may be posted outside the service period when the service occurred within it.

Initial application:
The issue was explicitly applied by e88e991a574185bed8cad539e6ab012cab0cca51, which removed Account.active from the historical Account spreadsheet source, renamed Account.period to Account.servicePeriod, updated the requested definition/comment text, and changed Account examples from period/active to servicePeriod. The current XML source was later regenerated/refactored from the spreadsheet-era source, preserving the requested Account.servicePeriod element and examples.

Additional context:
Account is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. Related FHIR-14811 separately made Account.status mandatory and added status codes; later FHIR-14963 added Account.billingStatus for billing/lifecycle processing and is already represented on the combined branch by f18430c5af6d4524b83c05e5d87df7ebfc161dfd. Those later/same-family decisions explain why current Account still has status/billingStatus fields, but they do not reintroduce the removed Account.active period element or conflict with FHIR-14813.
