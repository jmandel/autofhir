FHIR-14273: Audit Sequence strand split

Issue request:
FHIR-14273 was a Published/Persuasive Clinical Genomics change request for STU3 asking to split Sequence.referenceSeq.strand so the absolute strand and gene-relative sense/orientation concepts were no longer conflated. The accepted resolution kept Sequence.referenceSeq.strand as watson|crick and added Sequence.referenceSeq.orientation as sense|antisense.

A separate git-svn revision whose body contains @14273 is unrelated build-number noise and changed Specimen only.

Additional context:
The current core source no longer has an active Sequence resource or source/sequence folder; source/fhir.ini lists Sequence under removed resources and has no active MolecularSequence entry in the [resources] section. Sequence was renamed to MolecularSequence in 2018, later strand guidance was clarified by FHIR-37407 to use watson/crick rather than +1/-1, and the MolecularSequence folder was deleted in 2025. Current diagnostics narrative says MolecularSequence is being replaced by MolecularDefinition, an Additional Resource with a redesigned model. Applying the old Sequence.referenceSeq split to MolecularDefinition.location.sequenceLocation.strand would be a new CG modeling decision rather than completion of FHIR-14273.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit commit. If Clinical Genomics wants MolecularDefinition to distinguish absolute strand from gene-relative orientation/sense, track that as a new or successor MolecularDefinition issue rather than reopening this already-applied Sequence STU3 tracker.
