FHIR-7373: Audit instant datatype constraint wording

Issue request:
FHIR-7373 was Published/Persuasive for the DSTU1 FHIR-core datatypes page. It asked whether the instant datatype was necessary rather than just a restriction on dateTime, and the resolution was to make the constraint-ness of instant more explicit.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by SVN-backed commit c9c65030b163975d5736803f5a8d9da2c2de9d99, with message "#7373 - clarify use of instant" and git-svn revision 6032. That commit added the note that instant is intended for precisely observed times rather than human-reported times and explicitly stated that "instant is a more constrained dateTime." The current active datatype source still carries that constraint wording.

Additional context:
Later instant datatype issues refined adjacent wording without reversing FHIR-7373. FHIR-16347 clarified that instant is suitable anywhere a work group requires a full instant of time and is already represented on the combined branch. FHIR-16944 addressed grammar/text replacements in the same instant row; this reconciliation fixes its remaining "SHALL specified" typo in a separate per-issue commit while preserving the FHIR-7373 constraint statement. FHIR-24564 later changed nearby time-zone wording to UTC offset. The datatype page is current build source: source/fhir.ini maps primitive datatype pages to datatypes.html by default.
