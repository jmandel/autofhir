FHIR-27083: Audit Group preference guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-27083 is a Published/Persuasive with Modification FHIR-core FHIR-I change request raised against R4 List and Group. It asked whether List and Group should be merged because patient-list implementers found overlap, but the final decision did not merge them; it directed adding guidance to the Group boundaries-and-relationships section that Group should be preferred over List when Group is suitable, such as collections of patients that may be subjects of measures, observations, communications, or similar actions.

Initial application:
The issue was applied in source history before this reconciliation. Commit 50a8a1a8be added the approved guidance to source/group/group-introduction.xml on 2022-08-10, merge commit a78d8bbb76 brought the apply-FHIR-27083 branch into the R5 ballot staging history, and commit c47ed18f2c reapplied the same guidance with minor line wrapping on 2022-08-30. Current blame still points the substantive guidance lines to c47ed18f2c, with later punctuation-only cleanup on one line.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini lists Group and List as active resources and approved artifacts, so source/group/group-introduction.xml is current build-scope source. FHIR-I minutes from 2020-06-01 record the work group discussion and 11-0-0 motion: Group is an actor and can be the subject of Observation and similar resources, while List is a view of a collection of data. Zulip discussions before and after the vote repeat the same distinction and do not reveal a later reversal. FHIR-27903 is a closely related, already-published List/Group clarification audit on the combined branch; it supports keeping separate List and Group boundary guidance rather than adding redundant text here.
