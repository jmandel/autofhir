FHIR-6906: Audit questionnaire answer nesting

Issue request:
FHIR-6906 was a Published FHIR-core QuestionnaireAnswers change request from the 2015 May SDC ballot asking how to nest questions under answers and whether QuestionnaireAnswers.group.question.answer should have a group child. The final resolution was Not Persuasive with Modification: use Questionnaire-side enable-when behavior for answer-dependent questions and add Questionnaire notes calling out the extension and other complex-workflow aids, while keeping the linkage on the Questionnaire side unless evidence showed that was insufficient.

Initial application:
The Jira comment records SVN #5941. Git commit 26e8b597d5034f4372a848e5660a88268f4b664c is the corresponding git-svn commit and explicitly lists {#6906}; it added Questionnaire notes saying the conditional display extension [enable-when] can enable groups nested beneath a question based on a specific answer, giving the behavior of questions under answers. Later commit 52dbc1c47868e26651369ea4109008738f17ce5e refactored Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse for {#8986}, replacing the old QuestionnaireAnswers shape with the current item model and carrying forward explicit nested-question support and QuestionnaireResponse.item.answer.item.

Additional context:
Questionnaire and QuestionnaireResponse are active resources in source/fhir.ini, so their source definitions and notes are in current build scope. Current Questionnaire now has core Questionnaire.item.enableWhen and notes that questions may be nested under groups or questions, with SDC guidance for advanced question flow. Current QuestionnaireResponse explicitly distinguishes item.item for group children from item.answer.item for children of a question answer. Later Published issues FHIR-10481 and FHIR-20768 challenged the dual nesting points and were resolved by retaining item.answer.item with clarification; FHIR-34184 added diagram guidance for the same nesting concept, and FHIR-32665 tightened answer.value cardinality when answers contain child items. Zulip discussion around those later issues records the same rationale, and direct FHIR-6906/community searches found no conflicting evidence.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The current build already satisfies the seed issue's final decision and subsequent refinements: Questionnaire-side enableWhen governs answer-dependent enablement, Questionnaire permits nested child items, and QuestionnaireResponse preserves per-answer child items where repeated answers need their own follow-up questions.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit as a no-change audit. Do not reintroduce the old DSTU1 enable-when extension wording into current narrative; the mechanism is now a core Questionnaire element and the later item.answer.item issue family confirms the current representation. Any future work should be driven by new Questionnaire/SDC tracker decisions rather than FHIR-6906.
