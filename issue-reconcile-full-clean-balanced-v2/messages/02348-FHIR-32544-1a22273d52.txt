FHIR-32544 was Published/Persuasive for R5. It asked why ConceptMap.group.unmapped.url could be present when unmapped.mode was not other-map and requested either explanatory text or a constraint prohibiting that case. Its final resolution says to see FHIR-32545 because that resolution covers this ticket.

Initial application:

Commit 8623473ddfcef0cb88b9abf5241abfe6e28ea542 later migrated that ConceptMap2 source into the current ConceptMap source.
