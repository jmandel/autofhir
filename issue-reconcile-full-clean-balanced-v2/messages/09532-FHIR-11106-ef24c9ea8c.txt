FHIR-11106: Audit Device UDI jurisdiction representation

Issue request:
FHIR-11106 was a Published, Persuasive with Modification OO change request from the DSTU2 ballot asking how a sender or receiver can know the UDI jurisdiction from the carrier string. The request was to make Device UDI guidance convey both the jurisdiction/repository authority and the assigning authority, rather than leaving recipients to infer jurisdiction from the raw UDI string.

Initial application:
No current-history commit names FHIR-11106 exactly, but the February 2017 Device UDI application commit 2bd8c956 explicitly covered the contiguous tracker family [#11105]-[#11109] plus #11049/#11050 and changed UDI examples from the old Identifier-style udiCarrier system/value representation to the newer UDI backbone shape with separate jurisdiction, issuer, carrierHRF/carrierAIDC, and entryType fields. The old source-generation spreadsheet also captured Device.udi.jurisdiction with requirements saying recipients can know which database contains UDI-associated metadata.

Additional context:
Device is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini, and source/device/structuredefinition-Device.xml is the authoritative current Device StructureDefinition. Current source defines Device.udiCarrier.jurisdiction as the authoritative source for UDI generation within a jurisdiction and Device.udiCarrier.issuer as the UDI issuing organization. Later Published issue FHIR-32553 refined the issuer and jurisdiction URI examples and is already represented on the combined branch; FHIR-35973 later made issuer mandatory and is also already represented. Later FHIR-51533 and Zulip discussions identify unresolved THO/NamingSystem registration and validation questions for these URIs, but that issue is Waiting for Input and does not reverse the seed's requirement to convey jurisdiction separately.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current build already provides the semantic separation requested by FHIR-11106: Device.udiCarrier carries both jurisdiction and issuer fields, their definitions explain the jurisdictional repository/authority distinction, and the Device UDI examples include jurisdiction and issuer values. Changing current URI values, adding a binding, or registering NamingSystems would exceed the seed and belongs to FHIR-51533 or a future OO/terminology decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is for FHIR-11106. Do not infer additional URI registration or binding changes from this DSTU2 ballot item; follow up under FHIR-51533 or a new final OO/terminology tracker if the unresolved NamingSystem/THO concern is approved.
