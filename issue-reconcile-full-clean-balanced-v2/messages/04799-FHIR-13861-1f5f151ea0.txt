FHIR-13861: Audit Appointment v2 mapping reconciliation

Issue request:
FHIR-13861 was a Published/Persuasive PA change request asking for clearer HL7 v2 mappings on Appointment: ARQ-7 and SCH-7 should both map to Appointment.appointmentType, not Appointment.reason, and ARQ-8 and SCH-8 should map to Appointment.serviceType.

Initial application:
The issue was applied by historical commit 7b08cd473fbd0b8f8834494c9545fb1c7f1ac565, which added ARQ-8/SCH-8 to Appointment.serviceType, changed Appointment.appointmentType from ARQ-7 to ARQ-7/SCH-7, and removed SCH-7 from the Appointment.reason v2 mapping. Later R5 refactors kept Appointment active in the build and retained the Appointment.reason v2 mapping as AIS-3.

Additional context:
FHIR-41607 is a later Applied/Not Persuasive with Modification PA decision on the same Appointment v2 mapping confusion. After PA and InM review of v2 user-defined tables 0276 and 0277 and real-world mapping practice, it intentionally removed the core Appointment.appointmentType mapping to ARQ-7/SCH-7 and moved the core guidance to Appointment.serviceType, allowing ARQ-8/SCH-8 or ARQ-7/SCH-7 with a trading-partner review note. Current combined-branch source matches that later decision.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. Re-adding the FHIR-13861 appointmentType v2 mapping would contradict the later FHIR-41607 source and work-group decision; the current source already preserves the non-reason mapping intent in the updated serviceType guidance.

Recommendation:
Keep this no-change audit. Do not revert the FHIR-41607 mapping unless PA reopens the Appointment/v2 alignment question; any remaining v2-to-FHIR IG mapping updates should be handled in that IG or a separate issue, not by changing core Appointment source here.
