FHIR-28096: Audit ObservationDefinition specimen cardinality

Issue request:
FHIR-28096 was a Published/Persuasive OO change request for R5 asking to change ObservationDefinition.specimen from 0..1 to 0..* Reference(SpecimenDefinition). The accepted resolution set the cardinality to 0..*, preserving a zero minimum because not all observations use specimens, and recorded that multiple specimen references mean logical AND: all listed specimen kinds are needed.

Later structural/refactoring commits, including 49b86f8a6978650d8f8a6d36d27dee0433e455bc, preserved those semantics.
