FHIR-26939: Audit SubstanceDefinition representation wording

Issue request:
FHIR-26939 was Published/Persuasive for R5 by BRR. It asked to change the SubstanceDefinition.structure.representation definition from "Molecular structural representation" to wording that described a depiction of the substance structure and/or characterization, with examples such as a molecular structure graphic, InChI string value, JCAMP file, or AnIML file.

Initial application:
The request appears to have been applied in the 2020 SubstanceDefinition spreadsheet source even though the implementation commit did not mention FHIR-26939 directly. Commit ae0d9e06 changed SubstanceDefinition.structure.representation from "Molecular structural representation" to "A depiction of the structure or characterization of the substance," and also broadened the representation string/document child definitions to include characterization examples such as JCAMP and AnIML.

Additional context:
SubstanceDefinition is an active current-build resource in source/fhir.ini. FHIR-26940 was approved in the same BRR motion and applied the related representation.type characterization-technique wording; it is already represented on the combined branch as an empty no-change audit. Later Published/Persuasive BRR issues changed the model: FHIR-32115 removed characterization-technique wording from representation.type, and FHIR-39596 added SubstanceDefinition.characterization as the separate home for characterization details, including characterization.technique and characterization.file. The current source now keeps SubstanceDefinition.structure.representation focused on structural depiction while characterization.file carries the JCAMP/JDX/ADX and chromatogram/spectrum examples.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Restoring the original FHIR-26939 "structure and/or characterization" wording to SubstanceDefinition.structure.representation would conflict with the later FHIR-39596 separation of characterization from structure. The current source preserves the substance representation examples where still appropriate: InChI/SMILES-style logical formats remain under structure.representation.format, structure graphics and AnIML/JCAMP attachments remain under structure.representation.document, and analytical characterization files now belong under SubstanceDefinition.characterization.file.

Recommendation:
Keep this empty audit commit. Do not reintroduce characterization wording into SubstanceDefinition.structure.representation; future characterization terminology or example refinements should be raised as separate BRR issues against SubstanceDefinition.characterization or its terminology rather than reopening this R5 representation wording request.
