FHIR-37871: Remove constraint xpath from ModelInfo

Issue request:
FHIR-37871 was a Published/Persuasive FHIR-I R5 change request to remove the trial-use ElementDefinition.constraint.xpath element because XPath constraints were not executed during the build, were often unreliable, and were difficult for authors to maintain. The accepted resolution said to ballot the intent to remove it, then drop it from the spec, and update tooling so Schematron no longer exposes constraints.

Initial application:
Commit 6551c7b8c82e77ff82fa8937a63a69916f06fd5a explicitly mentioned FHIR-37871 and added the ballot warning to source/elementdefinition.html. Commit 1e1665b9352f33a691326fdf3079bc19865a8681 later changed the active ElementDefinition spreadsheet row from ElementDefinition.constraint.xpath to !ElementDefinition.constraint.xpath, and commit cdc3309868ccd5579919ded93d861e90b0701332 added the R5 conversion note ElementDefinition.constraint.xpath=XPath removed due to lack of use and maintenance.

Additional context:
StructureDefinition and Library are active current-build resources in source/fhir.ini, and the FHIR/QUICK ModelInfo sidecar files are linked from source/library/library-examples-header.xml and copied by publish.ini. The authoritative ElementDefinition source no longer has an active constraint.xpath element, but the active FHIR and QUICK ModelInfo sidecars still exposed ElementDefinition.Constraint.xpath. A commented-out Consent constraint still contains an XPath example, and SearchParameter.xpath remains as a separate historical SearchParameter surface; neither is the ElementDefinition.constraint.xpath element resolved by this issue.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
Removed ElementDefinition.Constraint.xpath from source/library/library-fhir-model-definition-content.xml and QUICK.ElementDefinition.Constraint.xpath from source/library/library-quick-model-definition-content.xml, leaving unrelated SearchParameter.xpath entries intact. Refreshed source/library/library-fhir-model-definition.xml so its embedded base64 payload exactly matches the updated FHIR ModelInfo sidecar.

Recommendation:
Keep this commit. It completes the FHIR-37871 removal across the active ModelInfo publication surfaces while preserving the intentional deleted-element marker and unrelated SearchParameter XPath metadata. Any broader cleanup of Schematron narrative or SearchParameter.xpath should be handled by separate Jira decisions.
