FHIR-16315: Audit Observation final status clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-16315 was Published with a Not Persuasive with Modification resolution for an STU3 Observation ballot comment asking whether Observation status `final` means complete, verified, done, or something else. The work group rejected reinstating the older DSTU2 "complete and verified by an authorized person" wording and decided that complete means the event is done; verification, release, signing, and similar acts may occur, but those acts belong in Provenance and the Observation remains preliminary until completion, then final.

Initial application:
The issue appears to have been applied by git 2ebd7c6f7f, a GF#16315-tagged Observation spreadsheet commit that changed the `final` code definition from "The observation is complete." to the current clarification about no further actions, Provenance for released/signed acts, and preliminary-until-final handling. The 2021 source restructuring commits carried that text into source/observation/codesystem-observation-status.xml, and later QA/source maintenance preserved it in the current CodeSystem source.

Additional context:
The cross-referenced companion FHIR-16349 asked to make Observation.status extensible for states such as released and signed; its final decision kept Observation.status as a required code-list binding and used the same Provenance guidance. FHIR-16349 is already represented on the combined branch by 627f2c5146dd5af54dc84e017b49379884b68dc2, which fixed a stale Vital Signs profile summary link while leaving the base required binding and final-status guidance unchanged. FHIR-34055 later recorded a no-change disposition for R4 markdown rendering around the same Provenance link and noted the rendering was fixed in R5. A Zulip tracker thread only lists GF#16315 in the Observation block vote, and no conflicting Confluence or Zulip rationale was found.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. Current source/observation/codesystem-observation-status.xml already says `final` means the observation is complete with no further actions needed, explains that released/signed-style acts are represented with Provenance, and states those act states remain associated with preliminary status until all are completed and final is applied. Current source/observation/structuredefinition-Observation.xml also keeps the required binding to ObservationStatus, which is consistent with the companion decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. No additional core source edit is needed for FHIR-16315; future work should preserve the current semantics that `final` does not require a universal verification step and that release/signing details are Provenance information rather than extra Observation.status codes.
