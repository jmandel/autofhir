FHIR-5201: Audit EpisodeOfCare organization-period guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-5201 was a Published/Persuasive Patient Administration DSTU1 change request for EpisodeOfCare. It asked whether EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization and EpisodeOfCare.period should be more tightly coupled or whether multiple managing organizations should be allowed, and the final resolution required guidance explaining transfers of care and how multiple organizations would have their own EpisodeOfCare instances.

Initial application:
No explicit FHIR-5201 source commit was found. The likely initial application was the March 2015 EpisodeOfCare ballot reconciliation work, especially git commit f659c3fbc16870414aac80f798d75afcb5480bf8, which added the active Scope and Usage subsection "Multiple Organizations and Transfer of Care" and the status/history period notes. That guidance was preserved through later source refactors into source/episodeofcare/episodeofcare-introduction.xml and source/episodeofcare/episodeofcare-notes.xml.

Additional context:
FHIR-5208 depends on this tracker and confirms the period question was about a single organization's tracking period. Later FHIR-20612 refined the original absolute wording that each organization will have its own EpisodeOfCare, keeping EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization 0..1 while clarifying it as the primary managing organization and allowing care teams from different organizations. The current combined branch also contains FHIR-6957's careTeam path correction, so the current managingOrganization comment points to EpisodeOfCare.careTeam rather than the retired EpisodeOfCare.team path.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This audit commit makes no source change. The active EpisodeOfCare source already has the requested guidance: EpisodeOfCare.managingOrganization identifies the primary organization assuming responsibility, EpisodeOfCare.period remains the interval for that responsibility, the resource narrative explains transfers of care, and the narrative says other organizations may have their own EpisodeOfCare instances for tracking their activities. Reintroducing a stronger "will have" rule or changing cardinality would conflict with the later approved FHIR-20612 refinement.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit as-is. The current source satisfies FHIR-5201 after considering FHIR-5208 and later EpisodeOfCare decisions; broader cross-organization modeling should be handled by a new work-group decision rather than by changing this historical tracker.
