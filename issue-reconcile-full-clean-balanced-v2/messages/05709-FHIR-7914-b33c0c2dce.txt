FHIR-7914: Confirm Timing duration clarification

Issue request:
FHIR-7914 was Published/Persuasive with Modification for the DSTU1 datatypes page. It asked for clarification of the Timing.repeat.duration comment that IV infusion duration may be part of the event definition, with the accepted resolution to express that infusion duration is inferred from quantity and rate rather than requiring a Timing duration.

Initial application:
The Jira comments identify svn revision 6036. In the git-svn history, commit a2677428ec3e34f6d9095bcf7e8566ede5a3e9e0, "#7914 - clarify definition about Timing duration", changed source/datatypes/timing.xml to add the IV infusion parenthetical "where the duration is implicit in the specified quantity and rate" to the Timing.repeat.duration guidance, and the same inherited guidance is present for Timing.repeat.durationMax.

Additional context:
source/fhir.ini routes datatypes through the active datatypes page machinery and marks Timing normative, so source/datatypes/timing.xml is current build-scope source. The current source still contains the applied clarification at Timing.repeat.duration and Timing.repeat.durationMax. Later formatting/refactoring commits preserved the wording, and targeted Jira, Zulip, and Confluence searches found no later or community decision that contradicts this issue's application.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The current Timing source already clarifies the IV infusion example by explaining that the duration is implicit in the specified quantity and rate, which satisfies the non-substantive clarification without introducing redundant or potentially over-specific arithmetic prose.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Revisit only if MnM wants a new, separate clarification about exactly how infusion dose quantity and rate representations imply duration; that would be broader than the 2015 published issue and should be decided separately.
