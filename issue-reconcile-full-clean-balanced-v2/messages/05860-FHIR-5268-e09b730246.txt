Issue request:
FHIR-5268 was a Published/Persuasive DSTU1 Patient Administration ballot clarification for Practitioner. The issue asked to remove the note saying Practitioner.organization is optional and can represent self-employed professionals, because absence of an Organization should not imply that a practitioner is self-employed.

Zulip and Confluence searches found no conflicting discussion or later decision for this issue.
