FHIR-8781: Audit FamilyMemberHistory list guidance

Issue request:
FHIR-8781 was a Published FHIR-core Patient Care change request with resolution Not Persuasive with Modification. It asked to return to a patient-centric FamilyHistory model; the accepted resolution kept the FamilyMemberHistory/List approach and required a note like Condition's no-known-problems guidance to draw attention to List.emptyReason for No Known Patient History, while referring member-level negation to GF#8782.

Initial application:
Historical commit bd3f8a7ba5 explicitly tagged #8781 and #8782 and added source/familymemberhistory/familymemberhistory-notes.xml content for whole-list states: List.emptyReason="notasked" when family history was not asked or no family-member information is present, and List.emptyReason="nilknown" after reasonable investigation finds no known family-history items. The same commit added the FHIR-8782 condition.code note for individual-family-member no-known/negated conditions.

Additional context:
FamilyMemberHistory remains an active approved resource in source/fhir.ini. Current notes preserve the requested List.emptyReason guidance and also explain the complementary individual-family-member condition.code pattern. Later FHIR-17887 added a concrete member-level negation example and later FHIR-43306 was Resolved - No Change, recommending a precoordinated condition.code or text rather than a new negation indicator; neither later decision undermines the FHIR-8781 List guidance.

AutoFHIR reconciliation:
This commit intentionally makes no source change. The active FamilyMemberHistory notes already contain the patient-level List.emptyReason guidance requested by FHIR-8781, and keeping that guidance avoids reopening the broader patient-centric versus family-member-centric model decision.

Recommendation:
Keep this audit commit as-is. Revisit only if Patient Care makes a new final decision to remodel family history away from the current List plus FamilyMemberHistory pattern.
