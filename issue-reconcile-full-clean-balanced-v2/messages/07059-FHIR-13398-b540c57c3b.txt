A later Device Scope and Usage refactor in commit 3c23148747a7d0fead21ff9afaab2fc3bb65eae9 preserved the UDI link against the current anchor. The combined branch already contains same-family reconcile records for FHIR-15774 and FHIR-15566, and current source contains no remaining device.html#5.11.3.2.2 or old 8.14.3.2.3 Device UDI section target.
