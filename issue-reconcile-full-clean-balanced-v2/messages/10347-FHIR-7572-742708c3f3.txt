FHIR-7572 was Published with resolution Persuasive with Modification. The May 2015 DSTU1 issue asked for business-specific details about when update behavior may be appropriate or disallowed, and the final resolution was to add a reference from the HTTP update section to updates.html.

Initial application:

Additional context:
